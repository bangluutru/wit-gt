**COACHING AND CONSULTATION FORMULAS OF INNER COACHING AND CONSULTATION EXPERTS**

**INTRODUCTION – THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN CONSULTATION AND COACHING**
To become a true Inner Coaching and Consultation Expert (Inner Coach), we must comprehend the difference between "Consultation" and "Coaching."
* **Consultation:** A "retail" process. We work 1-on-1 or in small groups sharing the same query, directly addressing their specific desires and adversities. Doing only consultation is time-consuming and risks getting dragged into the client's problems.
* **Coaching:** A "wholesale" process. We do not focus on resolving a specific individual's adversity, but offer general, universal awareness. Once learners receive this awareness, they resolve their own issues.
The pinnacle of an Inner Coach is the perfect blend: **Through 1-on-1 consultation to answer a personal query, we simultaneously coach and resolve the common adversity of thousands of people at once.**
To achieve this, we do not act on instinct but follow **4 Core Formulas** strictly.

**THE 4 FORMULAS OF THE INNER COACH**
* **Formula 1: Release - Self-anchor - Release - Self-anchor (Buông - Định thân - Buông - Định thân):**
  This formula protects the expert's inner state and recalls the correct total karma. We must understand two concepts:
  * **Role Attachment (Sĩ thân):** Attaching a title, status, or identity to oneself, mistakenly believing that one is that identity and living with it.
  * **Self-anchoring (Định thân):** The state of inner cognition of who one is, focusing all resources to become that person.
  * **Operating Principle:** *"Being nobody - Self-anchoring who you are - Being nobody."* Before sharing, the Coach must **"Release"** (recognizing they are nobody, dismantling all role attachments). Then, we **"Self-anchor"** as someone who carries appreciation and gratitude, or anchor as an expert who brings deep, thorough clarity (thấu suốt) on a specific topic.
  * *Cause and Effect:* Humans only know things when they know who they are. When we "Release," we are not bound by role attachment. When we "Self-anchor," the corresponding total karma immediately flows, untaught wisdom (vô sư trí) is activated, letting us know exactly what to say. After sharing, we immediately **"Release"** back to being nobody to protect ourselves from arrogance.
  *(Note: Concepts and Mental State in training will be elaborated in topics: Standard Concepts in Training and Right Mental State in Training).*

* **Formula 2: Transferring Knowing - Believing - Understanding (Chuyển Biết - Tin - Hiểu):**
  This formula structures lectures to synchronize the awareness of thousands of people. The sequence forms a loop: **Topic ➔ Definition ➔ Explanation ➔ Examples ➔ Summary ➔ Discussion ➔ Share Lessons/Realizations ➔ New Queries ➔ Back to Topic**.
  Every concept must be clearly defined, using examples to materialize information, then creating contexts for learners to discuss and share. This logic helps the audience shift their Knowing into Believing and deep Understanding.

* **Formula 3: 4 Steps of Consultation/Coaching (Vũ khí tối thượng xử lý vấn nạn):**
  This billion-dollar formula is used for 1-on-1 consultations and group coaching. It lights a lamp of awareness so adversities disappear.
  * **Step 1: Know you are hearing, know you are seeing.** When the client shares their suffering, the expert keeps their inner self at truth, separating mind and emotion, merely "knowing they are hearing/seeing." If you think of solutions immediately, their adversity will enter you (taking their karma).
  * **Step 2: Naturally know what to say/do.** By keeping truth in Step 1, stage 4 wisdom is activated; the expert naturally speaks the wisest words. *(Performing Step 1 and Step 2 qualifies the expert as wise)*.
  * **Step 3: Elevate their awareness to realize that life's root stems from their own mental images, inner states, and source concepts.**
  * **Step 4: Elevate their awareness to accept, tolerate people, and appreciate the lesson.** Instantly, old karma finishes, adversities dissolve, and positive seeds are sown. *(Performing Step 3 and Step 4 qualifies the expert as having true wisdom)*.
  The 21-day "Understanding Inner Self - Creating Peace" roadmap is the practical execution of this 4-step formula.

* **Formula 4: Rhythm in Coaching & Consultation (Nhịp điệu trong TVHL):**
  To leverage knowledge without resistance, the expert must follow the Circle of Knowledge rhythm:
  * Speak for **Knowing** ➔ rely on the listener's **Belief**.
  * Speak for **Understanding** ➔ rely on the listener's **Knowing**.
  * Speak for **Comprehending** ➔ rely on the listener's **Queries**.
  * Speak for **Transformation** ➔ rely on the listener's **Desires**.
  * *Example:* You cannot make someone comprehend if they haven't raised queries. You cannot help someone transform if it is not their ultimate desire. Mastering this rhythm makes the expert a "wise communicator."

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
**2.1. Core Concepts**
* **Consultation (Tư vấn):** A "retail" interaction, directly addressing the specific desires and adversities of an individual or small group.
* **Coaching (Huấn luyện):** A "wholesale" process, offering general awareness so listeners can resolve their own problems.
* **Role Attachment (Sĩ thân):** Attaching titles or roles to oneself and living bound by them.
* **Self-anchoring (Định thân):** Inner cognition of who one is, focusing all resources to become that person.

**2.2. The 4 Coaching and Consultation Formulas**
1. **Release - Self-anchor - Release - Self-anchor:** "Being nobody ➔ Self-anchoring who you are ➔ Being nobody."
2. **Transferring Knowing - Believing - Understanding:** Lecture design loop: Topic ➔ Definition ➔ Explanation ➔ Example ➔ Summary ➔ Discussion ➔ Share ➔ Query ➔ New Topic.
3. **4 Steps of Consultation/Coaching:**
   * Step 1: Know you are hearing/seeing (An vui state, cuts karma attachment).
   * Step 2: Naturally know what to say/do (Wise state).
   * Step 3: Elevate awareness of Life Source (Life stems from myself).
   * Step 4: Accept, Tolerate people, and Appreciate the lesson (Wisdom state).
4. **Rhythm in Coaching & Consultation:** Aligning content with Belief (for Knowing), Knowing (for Understanding), Queries (for Comprehending), and Desires (for Transformation).

**2.3. Key Awarenesss**
* **Insight 1: Release Role Attachment to activate Wisdom.** By releasing titles, we prevent ego attachment, and the total karma corresponding to our self-anchoring role will flow naturally.
* **Insight 2: Do not carry others' karma.** Step 1 is the shield. Knowing one is hearing/seeing prevents others' adversities from invading one's inner self.
* **Insight 3: Speak based on the listener's state.** Always follow the Circle of Knowledge rhythm to ensure smooth transfer without resistance.

**2.4. Personal Application**
When conducting consultations, I apply Formula 1: before stepping into the room, I release my title (being nobody). I self-anchor as a compassionate helper. This allows untaught wisdom to flow. After the session, I immediately release the role, keeping my mind peaceful and free from arrogance.

**2.5. Family Application**
Applying Formula 4 (Rhythm) in communication with my spouse and children. I do not lecture them when they are not ready. I wait for their queries to speak for comprehension, and align with their desires when guiding them to transform, creating harmony.

**2.6. Organizational Application**
Training leaders in my company to use Formula 1 (Self-anchoring). When they lead, they anchor as leaders. When they return home or interact as peers, they release the leader role to maintain healthy relationships, avoiding the friction caused by rigid role attachment.

**2.7. Social Impact**
If educators apply the 4-step formula (Formula 3), they can lift communities out of suffering. Instead of solving individual complaints, they elevate collective awareness, helping people realize their life source and find peace.
