**B. In-Depth Content on Outstanding Wisdom (Establishment, Assembly, Operation, and Empowerment)**
**Introduction: Awakening to the limits of the individual and the great power of collective strength**
Throughout the long history of human development, countless great projects and organizations expected to rise to the height of their era have shattered because of one subtle inner error within the person at the top: arrogance. Many leaders deceive themselves and don the cloak of an all-powerful "superhuman," shouldering every responsibility with the blind belief that their personal capability can turn the universe. However, the principle of nature has clearly shown that the strength and wisdom of a single individual, no matter how exceptional, are only finite before aspirations of national or global scale.
To permanently break through this stifling barrier, we need to thoroughly awaken and establish an awareness that carries the weight of truth: **"There is no exceptional individual, only an exceptional team."** True greatness never originates from a single individual standing above ten thousand people; rather, it arises when a group of people **let go of personal pride, share the same destination, share the same thinking, and share the same action**. When this consensus unites, they create an entity of matchless strength called the **Outstanding Wisdom Group**. This topic is precisely the greatest blueprint, giving us the power to borrow strength and support, through a standard roadmap, to turn human aspirations into indestructible legacies.
**Development: Decoding the personnel structure and the art of deploying people within Outstanding Wisdom**
Previously, when it came to gathering people, ordinary thinking stopped at merely searching for "talents" to shoulder the work. But the strength of an Outstanding Wisdom Group will be extremely fragile and easily broken if it lacks intellectual direction and supporting resources. Every human dream is only truly and fully realized when we know how to fully gather **9 types of people**, and skillfully arrange them into three core, inseparable role groups.
The first group is **the Directing Mind (the Advisory Group)**, where the Guiding Master who leads the direction, the Eminent Person who points things out, the Teacher who passes on moral principle, the Expert who shapes thinking, and the Wise Companion who opens up wisdom all converge. The second group is **the Supporting Resources (the Mutual Support Group)**, including the Noble Benefactor who is ready to help, the Connector who links enormous pieces together, and the Fortune Bringer who brings smooth flow. Finally, to turn theory into reality, we need **the Executing Hands (the Burden-Bearing Group)**, precisely the Talents who dedicate themselves day and night for the common mission.
However, in the initial stage of establishing the nucleus of the organization, the crucial, life-and-death principle is that **the initiator must prioritize finding, by all means, the "Left Assistant – Right Supporter."** The Left Assistants execute effectively and the Right Supporters specialize in building concepts and communicating the image. With a **Guiding Master** as a solid foundation to rely on, and **Talents who bear the burden** serving as Left Assistant and Right Supporter on both sides to support execution — when these factors are all present, the organization has officially planted a solid foundation that cannot be broken.
**Analysis: The 5-step roadmap of Establish – Assemble – Operate – Empower for an everlasting organization**
The birth of an Outstanding Wisdom Group does not follow luck or chance, but is operated by a standard 5-step cause-and-effect roadmap, requiring the leader to walk correctly through each stage one at a time.
**Step 1: Choose the target for creating value.** A great organization begins by clearly identifying: Whom are we serving? Is the recipient of our value women, the entrepreneurial class, or 25 million Vietnamese children? **Clearly identifying the target we serve is precisely what determines the scale of the Outstanding Wisdom Group we need to establish.** If you only want to solve a problem for a small group, you will attract mid-tier talents. But if you set your destination as elevating the awareness of 25 million Vietnamese children, or building an ecosystem of thousands of billion-dollar enterprises worldwide, that great field will automatically summon Eminent People and Guiding Masters of international stature to converge, and the right Noble Benefactors, Experts, or Wise Companions will appear.
**Step 2: Awareness of mission, or Continuation of mission.** Once we have clearly identified the target receiving our value, we need to thoroughly grasp a profound awareness: depending on which target our mission creates value for, that will determine how we gather people and the Outstanding Wisdom Group. **Eminent People who point things out** or **Guiding Masters who lead the way** will never gather together if we only work based on a "motive" of selfish personal benefit.
To gather exceptional people, we must **erase personal motives** and work based on **mission, passion, and the 4 driving forces of survival** (Self, Family, Organization, Society). This awareness must be expanded to encompass the **4 Great Debts of Gratitude**: repaying the debt to the nation, repaying the debt to the organization, repaying the debt to the family, and repaying the debt to oneself.
Only when a person raises a heartfelt wish, becoming aware that **they themselves must be the one to do it** for the benefit of the organization, the nation, or human life, will the banner of gathering righteous people truly be raised. Once we **prove to society the passion and mission** aimed at the transformation and maturation of people, that is precisely the most powerful field to draw in every resource of society and every great mind to converge and support us in turning our dream into reality.
**Step 3: Do outreach for the project and attract the Outstanding Wisdom Group.** No matter how great a mission is, if it is only hidden deep in the mind, it will forever remain merely an illusion. We do not go around "begging" talented people to come and work. Instead, we **repeat again and again the picture of the project's benefit, clarifying the image and the source concept with the energy source of Appreciation and Gratitude.** Through relentless outreach capability, we create a **resonance of energy vibration frequency**. At this point, kindred souls will receive the signal; the Guiding Master, the Talent, and the Fortune Bringer will automatically be attracted and willingly step into the ranks of those who bear the burden.
**Step 4: Operate through Treatment and Upgrading of relationships.** Very many organizations manage to mobilize an exceptional workforce only to collapse from within. The thorough awareness here is: **the essence of operating an Outstanding Wisdom Group does not lie in rules of governance, but in the art of treating and upgrading the relationship with the 9 types of people.** We do not use iron-fisted power to force people. Elite operation means using acknowledgment, appreciation, and persistent cultivation to **upgrade the social relationship to the level of "Trust" and "Closeness."** When the people within the organization reach the state of **Shared Language, Consensus, and Companionship**, the entire machine will automatically operate smoothly without any need for forcing.
**Step 5: Empower through Virtue that conducts the world (Transferring the seat).** This is the step that determines the greatness and everlasting nature of the organization. If the leader clutches tightly onto power and the secrets of success, that organization will be limited by their own lifespan. To empower strength to a limitless degree, we must carry out **the act of Transferring the seat — wholeheartedly handing over this entire formula of establishment, assembly, and operation to the Talents in the lower ranks.** The realm of "Virtue that conducts the world" is achieved when every talent thoroughly comprehends the formula and personally replicates it into thousands of other Outstanding Wisdom Groups. From a single original source, tens of thousands of projects will blossom.
**Conclusion: The root of attraction lies in cultivating oneself**
Throughout that great 5-step roadmap, there is always a hidden principle governing all things: **the root of gathering Outstanding Wisdom never lies in how many talented people we rush out into society to recruit, but the root lies in WHO WE OURSELVES ARE.**
Before craving a Guiding Master to show the way or gathering enough of the 9 types of people, each individual must quietly and diligently **enrich the 7 kinds of comprehensive wealth.** We must ourselves light the lamp of Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Character, Capacity, Physical Body, and Material Wealth. When you cultivate yourself to the point of maturity, carrying within you **the continuous energy source of Joy, Tolerance, and Appreciation-Gratitude**, you will automatically become a giant magnet attracting every good connection.
The journey of thoroughly understanding and operating Outstanding Wisdom is, in the end, a great transformation of the inner being. By correctly shaping the target audience, nurturing a great mission, mastering the art of outreach, and upgrading human relationships with one's whole heart, we are not only building exceptional teams in the present, but are also creating everlasting family clans and great organizations that leave a legacy for generation after generation to come.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
**2.1. Concepts**
**1. Outstanding Wisdom Group**
Definition: A group of people with no exceptional individual, only an exceptional team; they share the same thinking, the same action, and head toward some shared common purpose together.
Meaning: It brings out the power of collective wisdom, turning every great idea into reality that the strength of a single individual could never shoulder alone.
Transformative role: Releases the leader from the pressure of having to become a "superhuman," eliminates personal arrogance, and helps learners thoroughly grasp the mindset of "borrowing strength – gaining support."
**2. Target for creating value (Value recipient)**
Definition: A specific group of people in society that we choose to serve and bring value to (for example: entrepreneurs, 25 million Vietnamese children, women...).
Meaning: This is Step 1 in the roadmap for establishing the Outstanding Wisdom Group. Depending on who the value recipient is, we will decide the scale and nature of the Outstanding Wisdom Group we need to gather.
Transformative role: Helps learners avoid feeling lost when starting a venture or a project, always knowing clearly the picture of the destination and who they need to head toward before calling for companionship.
**3. Mission (Awareness of mission / Continuation of mission)**
Definition: Standing before the target for creating value, we raise a heartfelt wish, taking on the responsibility to help them (Awareness of mission), or continuing the good aspiration from previous generations (Continuation of mission).
Meaning: This is Step 2 and is the core magnet of every organization.
Transformative role: Establishes the thorough awareness: if one cannot raise the intention to do something for society, for the nation, or for the world, one can never gather a great Outstanding Wisdom Group.
**4. The structure of the 9 types of people within Outstanding Wisdom**
Definition: Within an Outstanding Wisdom Group, the 9 types of people are distributed into 3 core role groups to coordinate together:
The Advisory Group: Includes the Guiding Master (leading), the Eminent Person (pointing things out), the Teacher (giving moral principle), the Expert (giving thinking), and the Wise Companion (opening up wisdom).
The Mutual Support Group: Includes the Noble Benefactor (helping), the Connector (connecting), and the Fortune Bringer (mutual support).
The Burden-Bearing Group (Execution): Precisely the Talent who bears the mission's burden.
Meaning: Avoids arranging people's roles in the organization haphazardly.
Transformative role: Helps the founder know who to prioritize attracting first (the Guiding Master and the Talent), thereby knowing how to treat each group of people according to the correct standard.
**5. Left Assistant – Right Supporter**
Definition: These are assistants, companions standing shoulder to shoulder alongside. "Left Assistant" means supporting, assisting, executing by all means everything the leader wants to do; "Right Supporter" means lifting up.
Meaning: Any project that wants to begin and develop must have a Left Assistant and Right Supporter (one leaning toward execution, one leaning toward building concepts/outreach).
Transformative role: Affirms the principle of borrowing strength: do not open a project alone by yourself if you do not yet have a Left Assistant and Right Supporter. (Note: This concept is intimately connected to forming a strong Ecosystem.)
**6. Operation (Treatment and Upgrading of relationships)**
Definition: The essence of the "Operation" stage of an organization is precisely applying the principles of treatment for each of the 9 types of people and upgrading the social relationship with them to the level of Trust and Closeness.
Meaning: This is Step 4 in the roadmap. The leader does not use power to force people, but uses standard treatment to retain them.
Transformative role: Helps learners realize that the deepest management of people is precisely the management and upgrading of human relationships.
**7. Virtue that conducts the world (Transferring the seat)**
Definition: This is the act of transferring (handing over the seat) the entire formula and roadmap of establishing, assembling, and operating the Outstanding Wisdom Group to the Talents in the lower ranks.
Meaning: This is Step 5 (Empowerment) in the roadmap.
Transformative role: The only way to replicate strength. When the transfer succeeds, one organization will give birth to thousands of other Outstanding Wisdom Groups — this is precisely the key to building an Everlasting Family Clan and an Everlasting Enterprise.
**2.2. Key Knowledge: The 5-step roadmap for establishing, assembling, operating, and empowering the Outstanding Wisdom Group**
**Step 1: Choose the target for creating value.**
We must know precisely who the recipient of our value is (women, entrepreneurs, 25 million Vietnamese children, or all of humankind...). Based on that target, we will decide how to gather the wisdom group.
**Step 2: Awareness of mission or Continuation of mission.**
Standing before that target, we become aware of what responsibility (mission) we have, or continue the mission from those who came before us (for example: spreading the Philosophy of Education at the Root).

**Step 4: Treat and upgrade the relationship with them.**
After gathering people, the "Operation" stage is precisely where we apply the principles of treatment and upgrade the relationship with the 9 types of people so that they share the same language, reach consensus, and walk alongside us.
**Step 5: Virtue that conducts the world (Transferring the seat).**
"Empower" the Outstanding Wisdom Group by Transferring the seat (handing over) this entire formula: the way of establishing, assembling, operating, and empowering, to the Talents below, so they can continue to replicate tens of thousands of other Outstanding Wisdom Groups.
