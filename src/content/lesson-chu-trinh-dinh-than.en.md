## TOPIC: THE SELF-ANCHORING CYCLE

### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

**A. Topic Objectives**

**Value of the topic:** Helps learners thoroughly comprehend the operating law of establishing one's own position, thereby actively building a roadmap of growth through **correctly Self-Anchoring** in order to achieve the desired portrait.

**Significance for learners:** Removes the shackles of "Ego-Identification" (attaching oneself to a temporary, borrowed title), teaching how to "be nobody" so as to be able to become anybody, thereby activating the total karma of consciousness matching the role that has been set.

**The transformation brought:** Shifts from a state of blindly exerting effort into a state of **inner cognition that converges every resource** to make dreams and career portraits real in the shortest possible time.

**B. In-depth Content (Refined Version)**

**1. Introduction: The Power of Intention and the Destination.** Every great transformation begins with a balanced intention and a solid anchor point. To truly master one's life and become an outstanding "Portrait," we need to thoroughly comprehend the law of the **Self-Anchoring Cycle** — the key that releases the barriers of cognition, helping us "be nobody" so that we can become "anybody."

**2. From "Ego-Identification" to "Self-Anchoring."**

**Ego-Identification – The Chains of Growth:** This is the act of attaching a position or status to oneself and then mistakenly believing oneself to be that person. Ego-Identification makes the successful person become arrogant and the not-yet-successful person fall into low self-esteem, strangling the ability to create value in other roles. The first step is the capacity for **"Letting Go"** to bring the inner self back to a state of being nobody.

**Self-Anchoring – A State That Converges Resources:** This is an inner awareness of who one truly is, combined with focusing the entirety of one's resources to become that person. When Self-Anchoring is done correctly, one's words will carry "divine spiritual power," and the matching total karma of consciousness will automatically be called forth.

**3. The Structure of the Standard Self-Anchoring Cycle.** A roadmap anchored in **Knowledge** as its foundation:

**Self-Anchoring:** Establishing the awareness of who one is.

**Choice:** Choosing a life philosophy/path (for example, the Education-at-the-Root Philosophy).

**Action:** Spreading value, building merits and virtues.

**Consent (Affinity):** Attracting people's support.

**Self-Belief (Cause):** Reinforcing belief once the image and the concept are clear.

**Attaining the Portrait (Result):** Making the desired portrait a reality.

**Hear – See – Speak – Know:** Immersing oneself in an environment to continue cultivating and absorbing.

**4. The Operating Logic: The Forward Loop and the Reverse Loop.**

**The Forward Loop (achieving results in under 3 years):** Self-Anchoring immediately -> Choosing a philosophy -> Taking decisive action. This is the roadmap for people with great courage and accelerated comprehension.

**The Reverse Loop (achieving results in 5 – 10 years):** Immersing oneself in an environment -> Hear-See-Speak-Know -> A clear image (Belief) -> Consent -> Action -> Successful Self-Anchoring.

**5. The Formula "Let Go – Anchor" for Infinite Growth.**

**Letting go of oneself – Anchoring others:** Letting go of one's own personal status to recognize and be grateful for the superiority of others.

**Letting go of others – Anchoring oneself:** Letting go of negative images of others so as not to be attached, while at the same time re-anchoring good values for oneself.

At the outset of this journey of inner transformation, source knowledge establishes that every great and sustainable change in a person's life originates from a balanced, upward-facing intention and a solid anchor point within one's consciousness. The **Self-Anchoring Cycle** is precisely the optimal operating law that helps a person move from being an ordinary individual to becoming an outstanding portrait, through a methodically established roadmap that is both sequential and cyclical in nature. In society's ordinary way of thinking, when wanting to achieve a certain position, people usually try to name themselves first, and only afterward struggle to build belief and seek support from those around them. However, this approach often leads to a mid-way collapse if the inner self does not yet have enough inner strength or if self-belief is still vague. Inner knowledge points to a deeper cycle, beginning with establishing the inner awareness of who one truly is, with the entirety of one's resources focused on it, while at the same time choosing a life philosophy or a standard reference framework as the most stable anchor point on which to base one's capacity for action.

To truly and fully step into this cycle, the greatest barrier that each individual must have the courage to step over is precisely the invisible chains of **"Ego-Identification."** Ego-Identification is understood as a person attaching temporary positions, statuses, or titles to themselves and then mistakenly believing themselves to be that very entity, living with it constantly. When a person becomes too attached to labels such as "director," "expert," or even narrower titles such as "housewife," they unknowingly strangle their ability to create value in other roles and make their inner self heavy, prone to arrogance upon success or low self-esteem upon failure. Therefore, the first and most important step in the cycle is precisely the capacity for **"Letting Go"** to bring the inner self back to a state of **"being nobody."** Only upon reaching the state of "being nobody" is a person in a state of Empty Nature, creating the conditions for the treasury of Total Karma of Consciousness to open up and flow powerfully, helping the individual learn new things and call forth the knowledge matching the newly established role.

The structure of a standard Self-Anchoring Cycle does not stop at mere wishing but must pass through seven rigorous steps of transformation. Beginning with **Self-Anchoring** (establishing the awareness of who one is), the individual moves on to **Choice** (choosing a path or a life philosophy such as the Education-at-the-Root Philosophy), which leads to **Action** that creates value in order to attract the **Consent** of the affinities around them. Once people's consent reaches a state of ripeness, **Self-Belief** will be absolutely reinforced on the foundation of a clear image and a clear concept, leading the individual to make the desired **Portrait** a reality and to keep continuously cultivating through the process of **Hear – See – Speak – Know**. The special feature of this roadmap is that it anchors itself in the knowledge of human life instead of relying on external factors that are prone to fluctuation, because knowledge is precisely the deep, sustainable "cause" that helps one manage every result in life.

The Master has established two flexible directions so that anyone, whatever their foundation, can immerse themselves in this cycle to transform. The **Forward Loop** is the accelerated roadmap for those with great comprehension and courage; they anchor themselves immediately, choose a life philosophy, and act decisively to reap sustainable results in a short time, usually under 3 years. Conversely, the **Reverse Loop** is the path for those who feel they are still weak or do not yet have a clear mental image; they choose to immerse themselves in a good environment, daily practicing Hear-See-Speak-Know to clarify the source concepts and the image of the portrait. Over a period of 5 to 10 years, once belief and consent have become full, Self-Anchoring will automatically be accomplished as an inevitable ripe fruit. Whichever direction one follows, the operating crux still lies in the release-and-attach formula of **"Let Go – Anchor"**: a person needs to practice "letting go of self – anchoring others" to revere and be grateful for the presence of those around them, while also practicing "letting go of others – anchoring self" to reinforce, through society's recognition, the inner value of oneself.

The final result of the Self-Anchoring Cycle lies not only in attaining a personal title, but in ultimate maturity oriented toward the **7 dimensions of holistic wealth** and building **Everlasting Organizations** or an **Everlasting Family Clan** for future generations. When an individual thoroughly comprehends this law, they no longer struggle in exhaustion but shift into a state of living with a philosophy, where every action follows the principle of the rhythm and cycles of nature. The most important statement of awareness that learners need to engrave in their minds is: *"If you have chosen it, consider that you already have it"* — because once the mental image is clear and Self-Anchoring has been established, the actual result is only a matter of time and accumulated merit. The Self-Anchoring Cycle is, therefore, precisely the key that opens the door to a thorough realization of human life, helping people master their own lives at ease and build humanistic legacy values that transcend time.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT

**2.1. Concepts**

**Self-Anchoring:** The state of inner awareness of who one is, combined with **focusing the entirety of one's resources** to become that person.

**Ego-Identification:** Attaching a position, status, or title to oneself and **mistakenly believing oneself to be that person**, then living with it constantly.

**Anchoring the Mind:** A state of firmly binding one's inner self to a determined goal or person.

**2.2. Key Knowledge**

**The 7-step cycle:** Self-Anchoring -> Choice -> Action -> Consent -> Belief -> Portrait -> Hear-See-Speak-Know.

**The Anchor Point of Knowledge:** Knowledge is the most stable anchor point for Self-Anchoring, rather than relying on external circumstances.

**The Mechanism of Calling Forth Total Karma:** Self-Anchoring decides the retrieval of information from the treasury of total karma of consciousness accumulated in this life and in many previous lives.

**2.3. Key Awareness**

**"Being nobody so as to become anybody":** Once Ego-Identification is let go, the inner self is in a state of Empty Nature, ready to receive infinite growth.

**Having chosen is as good as already having:** Establishing the awareness "A clear image is the Result" to reinforce self-belief.

**Achievement through others – Origin from oneself:** Self-Anchoring in order to become a "good affinity" that supports others, but always remembering that the origin of life comes from oneself.

**2.4. Personal Application (3 examples)**

**Transitioning from housewife to Expert:** Letting go of the title of "just a housewife" to anchor oneself as an Inner-Self Expert; the result is that one's words and demeanor change immediately, even before graduating.

**Self-Anchoring physically (Pickleball):** A person who has never played sports anchors themselves as an "athlete"; by letting go of the ego of "knowing nothing," they achieve threshold-breaking results in a short time.

**Managing daily roles:** Letting go of the role of speaker after a class session to return to being an ordinary person within the family, helping the inner self stay at peace without clinging to fame.

**2.5. Family Application (3 examples)**

**Parents anchoring themselves as an Enlightened Teacher:** Instead of imposing, parents anchor themselves in guiding their children through knowledge, helping their children reach their own realization and act on their own initiative.

**Husband and wife consenting together on Self-Anchoring:** Together anchoring themselves in building an "Everlasting Family Clan," choosing the Education-at-the-Root Philosophy as the foundation, helping the family become harmonious.

**Healing through "Let Go – Anchor":** The wife lets go of the image of "the husband is at fault," re-anchoring the image of "the husband is a talented person"; the relationship immediately shifts in a positive direction.

**2.6. Organizational Application (3 examples)**

**Personnel who shoulder the burden:** The leader positions the employee as "a person who shoulders the mission"; the employee automatically calls forth creative solutions from their total karma of consciousness.

**Building a Director of Human Life:** Anchoring oneself in a new position specializing in caring for the ultimate maturity of employees, helping the organization become everlasting.

**Replicating leadership:** The leader anchors themselves as a simple person so that employees can easily replicate them, creating tens of thousands of "extended arms."

**2.7. Social Impact (3 examples)**

**Promoting the portrait of the Expert:** The organization anchors itself and promotes the dream career; once society consents, great resources flow in to build a joyful environment.

**Positioning a Nation of Cognition:** Vietnam orients itself to become a powerhouse of cognition; millions of people anchoring themselves together to spread the root philosophy helps elevate the nation's standing.

**The Iron WiT community:** Tens of thousands of people anchor themselves to transform their physiques; creating a new wave of awareness about health throughout the population.
