### CURRICULUM: ELEVATING INNER COGNITION
#### TOPIC: SELF-ANCHORING CYCLE (CHU TRÌNH ĐỊNH THÂN)

### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION
**1A. Objectives & Target Audience:**
* **Value of the topic:** Explores the natural law of establishing one's position, helping learners actively create a growth pathway by **Self-Anchoring (Định thân)** correctly to achieve their desired portrait.
* **Significance for learners:** Shakes off the chains of **Ego-identification (Sĩ thân)** (attaching to temporary roles/titles), teaching how to "be nobody" to become "anybody," which unlocks the corresponding total karma of knowledge.
* **Brought transformation:** Shifts from blind, struggling actions to an **inner awareness that converges all resources** to manifest career portraits and dreams in the shortest possible time.
* **Learner's Outcomes:**
  * Learners thấu suốt the 7-step Self-Anchoring Cycle. They distinguish between Ego-identification (Sĩ thân) and Self-Anchoring (Định thân), using the "Letting go - Anchoring" (Buông - Định) formulas to cultivate wisdom.

**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**SELF-ANCHORING VS. EGO-IDENTIFICATION – THE PATH TO INFINITE GROWTH**

**1. Ego-identification: The Chains of Growth**
* **Ego-identification (Sĩ thân):** The act of attaching titles, status, or temporary roles (e.g., "Director," "Expert," or "Housewife") to yourself and believing you *are* that role.
* **The Danger:** It causes successful people to become arrogant and unsuccessful people to fall into low self-esteem, locking their ability to create value in other roles.
* **The Solution:** The capacity to **"Let Go" (Buông)** to return the inner state to **"being nobody."** Only when you are nobody (Tánh không) can your store of Total Karma of Knowledge open, allowing you to absorb new things and speak with power.

**2. Self-Anchoring: The Convergence of Resources**
* **Self-Anchoring (Định thân):** The inner awareness of who you are, with the **focus of all resources** to become that person. When you self-anchor correctly, your words carry divine force, and matching karmic wisdom is summoned automatically.

**3. The 7-Step Self-Anchoring Cycle**
The cycle anchors on **Wisdom** as the ultimate stable foundation:
1. **Self-Anchor (Định thân):** Establish the inner awareness of who you are.
2. **Choose (Lựa chọn):** Select the path or philosophy (e.g., Education at the Root).
3. **Act (Hành động):** Radiate values and build merit & virtue.
4. **Agree (Đồng thuận):** Attract support and agreement from people (Duyên).
5. **Belief (Niềm tin):** Solidify self-belief via clear images and concepts (Nhân).
6. **Portrait (Chân dung):** Materialize the desired outcome (Quả).
7. **Hear - See - Speak - Know (Nghe - Thấy - Nói - Biết):** Immerse in the environment for continuous huân tập.

**4. The Forward Loop vs. The Reverse Loop**
* **The Forward Loop (Vòng xuôi - Less than 3 years to fruit):**
  * *Sequence:* Self-Anchor immediately ➔ Choose philosophy ➔ Act decisively.
  * *Profile:* For individuals with great courage and rapid comprehension.
* **The Reverse Loop (Vòng ngược - 5 to 10 years to fruit):**
  * *Sequence:* Immerse in good environment ➔ Hear-See-Speak-Know ➔ Clarify image (Belief) ➔ Gain agreement ➔ Act ➔ Achieve Self-Anchoring.
  * *Profile:* For those who need to build their mental images and concepts gradually.

**5. The "Let Go - Anchor" (Buông - Định) Formulas**
* **Letting go of self, anchoring others (Buông mình - Định người):** Let go of personal status to recognize and appreciate the superiority of others.
* **Letting go of others, anchoring self (Buông người - Định mình):** Let go of negative images about others to remain free from attachment, while anchoring positive values for yourself.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
**2.1. Concepts:**
* **Self-Anchoring (Định thân):** The inner awareness of who you are, focusing all resources to manifest that identity.
* **Ego-identification (Sĩ thân):** Attaching a temporary title or role to oneself and mistaking it as one's true identity.
* **Anchor Intent (Định tâm):** Firmly linking one's inner-self with a target or a person.

**2.2. Key Knowledge:**
* **The 7-Step Cycle:** Self-Anchor ➔ Choose ➔ Act ➔ Agree ➔ Belief ➔ Portrait ➔ Hear-See-Speak-Know.
* **Summoning Karma:** Correct self-anchoring determines the extraction of knowledge from the store of total karma across lifetimes.
* **Achievements from others, source from self:** We anchor to become a "good catalyst" for others, yet understand that reality originates from within ourselves.

**2.3. Key Awarenesss:**
* **Insight 1: "Be nobody to become anybody."** Letting go of Ego-identification places the mind in Tánh Không (emptiness), allowing infinite growth.
* **Insight 2: What is chosen is already possessed.** Conceptualize "Clear image is the Result" to support absolute self-belief.
* **Insight 3: Wisdom is the anchor.** Aligning with life philosophies is more stable than anchoring on moving external factors (money, power).

**2.4. Personal Applications:**
* **Role Management:** Shifting from "housewife" to "Inner Coach" by letting go of the old role and self-anchoring as an educator.
* **Ego Release:** Letting go of the "speaker" identity after a session, returning to a normal family member to keep the mind joyful and free of pride.
* **Marital Healing:** Letting go of the negative image of a spouse, anchoring them as a "talent," shifting the marriage positive.
