**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**FORMULA FOR ACTIVATING DESIRABLE TOTAL KARMA**

**INTRODUCTION – FROM VICTIM OF FATE TO ARCHITECT OF DESTINY**
Facing life's ups and downs, humans tend to assign everything to "Fate." Believing in fate strips us of the power to master our lives, leading to resignation or blame.
However, the Human Structure framework indicates that: there is no fixed fate, but our lives are directed by **Karma Destiny** (Total Karma). We establish a key awareness:
**"Before having thoughts, a human's life is decided by Total Karma. When having thoughts, it is still decided by Total Karma, ****but**** humans HAVE THE RIGHT TO CHOOSE Total Karma according to their desires."**
Our journey is to master and "call out" the best total karma.

**OPERATING PRINCIPLES OF THE "GARDEN OF SUBCONSCIOUSNESS"**
To choose total karma, we must understand how it is stored. Imagine that our Subconsciousness is like a **fertile garden**.
* **Three types of seeds in the garden:** Through countless lifetimes, we have sown seeds via Hearing - Seeing - Speaking - Knowing. They are divided into:
  * **Total Karma of Knowledge (Thức):** All understandings, wisdom, and intellect.
  * **Total Karma of Connection (Duyên):** All connections and relationships with people.
  * **Total Karma of Outcome (Quả):** All life results and achievements.
* **The watering mechanism of "Master Ý":** In this garden, there is a master named **"Ý" (Intent/Thought)**. Daily, Master Ý visits the seeds. What decides which seed grows is the **inner electromagnetic vibration state** Master Ý carries:
  * If Master Ý carries **Negative** electricity (anger, blame, anxiety): watering negative energy leads to **Undesirable Outcomes (Bất Như Ý)**.
  * If Master Ý carries **Positive** electricity (excitement, appreciation, love): watering positive energy leads to **Desirable Outcomes (Như Ý)**.
  * If Master Ý carries **Balanced** electricity (Tánh Không): the seed stops growing.

**DEEP ANALYSIS – CUTTING OFF WATER AND THE POWER OF INTENT**
When an undesirable outcome occurs (disease, bankruptcy, family issues), the natural human reaction is pain and blame. But this negative reaction (negative electricity) continues to water the undesirable seed, making it grow stronger.
* **The Root Solution: Balance.** We apply the balanced state: **Tánh Không**. When adversity occurs, instead of reacting or analyzing, remain at truth: *"merely knowing I am hearing, merely knowing I am seeing."* Master Ý visits the tree without negative or positive electricity. Watering is cut. The bad outcome withers and falls. Adversity dissolves.
* **"It is only a thought":** When the garden is cleared of weeds, it becomes pure. When your merit accumulator (Công đức - Phước đức) is large, your inner state remains balanced and positive. You only need to raise a thought (place intent) without attachment, and the universe will gather connections to manifest it.

**THE 5-STEP FORMULA TO ACTIVATE DESIRABLE TOTAL KARMA**
1. **Elevate inner awareness to Level 1, 2, 3:** Especially Level 3 (Tánh Không) to cut negative outcomes.
2. **Rotate Mental State:** Using wisdom energy:
   * Keep **Joy (An vui)** to call **Knowledge (Thức)**.
   * Keep **Tolerance (Bao dung)** to call **Connection (Duyên)**.
   * Keep **Appreciation & Gratitude** to call **Outcome (Quả)**.
3. **Raise ultimate desire:** Define what you want to Become, Possess, Comprehend, or Connect.
4. **Cultivate (Clear images and concepts):** Control hearing/seeing to clarify mental images.
5. **Appretell (Quảng bá):** Repeat the benefit picture with appreciation/gratitude to get agreement and align energy frequencies.

**DAILY ACTION CODE:**
* **Free? Stay peaceful (An vui):** Prevent the mind from falling into negative electricity.
* **Thinking? Think of the 7 Dimensions of Holistic Wealth:** Focus desires in the light.
* **Doing? Practice the 7 Almsgivings (Bố thí):** Sow good connections.
