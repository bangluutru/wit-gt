In humanity's journey of development, traditional education has usually placed great expectations on cultivating logical thinking, analytical intelligence, and practical skills. We constantly strive to mold behavior, drill habits, and use discipline to force ourselves or others (such as our children or personnel) to change. However, the masters have pointed out a profound truth: all behavioral change achieved through the imposition of willpower is only temporary. Without acting upon the root of awareness, once discipline is relaxed, every habit will return to its original state. That is why the higher-order education of society does not focus on the branches of behavior, but goes directly deep into the core foundation: **Education of Awareness**.

Education of Awareness operates based on an extremely subtle mechanism called **"Sufficient Awareness."** A person only truly achieves permanent transformation when they attain three factors with regard to any aspect of human life: clarity about the mental image, thorough understanding of the beneficial source concepts, and maintaining a balanced, positive inner state. Here, we need to establish a pivotal awareness: **When awareness is present, there is no need for behavioral thinking.** Once the inner world already contains good seeds and vibrates at the same frequency as the light, all external behaviors and habits will automatically be redirected and changed naturally and gently, without any need for forcing or strained effort.

The power of Education of Awareness does not stop at the individual level but creates an explosive effect when elevated into **Collective Consciousness**. Imagine a family or a business where all members are synchronized to the same standard frame of reference and share the same beneficial source concepts. When that happens, they achieve "shared language" (the same inner language) and "consensus" (looking in the same direction). Any individual who steps into an environment with this powerful collective consciousness, even if they previously had less-than-good habits, will automatically be assimilated and change their behavior to become compatible with the energy source of Joy, Tolerance, and Gratitude. This is precisely the core secret to building families and organizations that endure across generations.

If Education of Awareness is the path and the vehicle, then **Education at the Root** is the ultimate destination. The destination of education is never merely a diploma or survival skills, but orienting a person to reach ultimate maturity. This maturity is defined by simultaneously lighting up **7 forms of complete richness**: Rich in Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Character, Capacity, Physical Body, and Material Wealth. Among these, prioritizing the cultivation of the non-material factors (Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Character) infuses infinite value into a person, helping them rise above every problem that arises in all 4 aspects: Inner Being, Health, Relationships, and Finance.

In summary, the journey of shifting from an education focused on behavior to the **Philosophy of Education at the Root** is a revolution of the inner being. It frees people from the toil of "handling problems" to leisurely walk the path of "illuminating human life." When each individual, each family thoroughly understands and lives by this philosophy, we not only completely master our own lives but are also together carrying out a great mission: turning Vietnam into a powerhouse of awareness education, exporting wisdom and core human values to the entire world.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
#### 2.1. Concepts
**Education of Awareness:** A higher-order education of society that directly acts upon human awareness instead of focusing solely on logical intelligence or skills. Only humans possess awareness, therefore the journey of human development must go hand in hand with education of awareness.
**Education at the Root:** The effect (destination) of education of awareness. It is the orientation of a person toward ultimate human maturity: enriching the 7 forms of complete richness (Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Character, Capacity, Physical Body, Material Wealth). *(Note: This content will be explored in depth and clarified in the topic: The Philosophy of Education at the Root.)*
**Sufficient Awareness:** Whenever anything is mentioned, if a person has a balanced, positive inner state, a clear mental image, and a clear source concept about it, then they are said to have sufficient awareness.

#### 2.2. Key Knowledge
**The difference between ordinary education and education of awareness:** The world's current education mainly expects people to develop in thinking, logical intelligence, and skills (science). However, if education of awareness is missing, human maturity will be limited. Education of awareness goes into the root of thinking and behavior.
**The importance of Collective Consciousness:** Collective consciousness has the strongest impact on awareness. When an environment (such as a nation or an organization) establishes a shared collective consciousness, people who step into it will automatically change their behavior without being forced (for example: arriving in a clean country, people automatically become aware not to litter).
**The 3-outlook roadmap of awareness:** For a person to develop fully, they need sufficient awareness of: the Outlook on Human Life (the existence and development of human beings), the Worldview (all things around us), and the Cosmic View (invisible laws). Among these, education of awareness prioritizes clarifying the Outlook on Human Life and the Worldview first, so that people do not lose their way.
**Sufficient awareness in every aspect (Bringing education of awareness into movement/exercise):** Education of awareness can permeate every corner of life. For example, in swimming or health, if one only trains skill, one will become tired; but if one has "sufficient awareness about exercise," a person will know how to bring wisdom and a joyful mental state into their movement, and from there health will be maximized.

#### 2.3. Key Awareness
**Establishing Awareness 1:** *Any education that does not act upon a person's awareness will return education to how it was before — it will never change.* We would like to establish an important awareness: any effort to change behavior (of ourselves, our children, or our personnel) that does not change their inner awareness is only a temporary forcing. Once relaxed, the old habit will immediately return.
**Establishing Awareness 2:** *When there is awareness, there is no need for behavioral thinking.* If you want your child to be well-behaved, do not force their behavior. Instead, build "sufficient awareness" for your child. Once a person has a clear image, a clear beneficial source concept, and a vibrational frequency that resonates with what is good, every bad habit and cycle will be broken in an instant.
**Establishing Awareness 3:** *The destination of education is not a diploma, but ultimate maturity.* Education at the root is the effect of education of awareness. A person is only truly considered to have education at the root when they head toward the 7 forms of complete richness and thoroughly understand Cause - Condition - Effect.

#### 2.4. Personal Application
When applying Education of Awareness to myself, instead of using willpower to force myself into discipline or stressful studying, I place myself into a state of "enjoying learning." I equip myself with beneficial source concepts and clarify the mental image of transformation. When the inner awareness changes, outer behavior automatically changes gently.

#### 2.5. Family Application
As a Family Educator, parents do not educate their children through imposed expectations or cramming skills, but through **Living Example (Thân giáo — teaching by embodiment)**. Parents must be people who live with a philosophy, who have sufficient awareness about health, marriage, and their inner being. From that context, children will automatically absorb correct awareness and reach ultimate maturity in a naturally flowing way.

#### 2.6. Organizational Application
In a business, leaders apply education of awareness by "synchronizing inner awareness" across all personnel. When 1,000 or 10,000 personnel share the same standard frame of reference, the same source concepts, and the same language, that organization creates a "Collective Consciousness." At that point, the organization's strength will explode, easily moving toward the model of an everlasting organization.

#### 2.7. Social Impact
From a macro perspective, spreading education of awareness is precisely building a generation of Vietnamese people with scholarly intelligence and thorough understanding of human life. The great mission of experts is to turn Vietnam into "the capital of education of awareness," "the world's center of wisdom" within the next 10-15 years, exporting the philosophy of education to the whole world.
