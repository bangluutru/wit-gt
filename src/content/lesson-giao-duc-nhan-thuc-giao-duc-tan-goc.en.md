### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION
**1A. Objectives & Target Audience:**
* **Value of the topic:** Redefines the core nature of education. Instead of focusing solely on transferring logic-based knowledge or training surface-level skills, it directs learners to a high-level societal education: directly impacting human root awareness.
* **Significance for learners:** Explains why conventional behavioral changes are unsustainable. It provides the key to lifelong transformation by equipping learners with **"full awareness" (nhận thức đủ đầy)** across all aspects of life.
* **Brought transformation:** Shifts the mindset from forcing oneself or others to change behaviors, to clarifying mental images, adopting beneficial source concepts, and maintaining a balanced-positive inner state so that growth occurs naturally.
* **Learner's Outcomes:**
  * Learners master the roadmap of education at the root to self-educate, guide future generations, and cultivate collective consciousness, aiming to position their nation as a global hub of wisdom and cognitive education.

**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**COGNITIVE EDUCATION VS. BEHAVIORAL EDUCATION – THE ROOT SOURCE OF CHANGE**

**1. The Fallacy of Behavioral Training:**
Traditional education focuses on logic, analysis, and practical skills. We try to mold behaviors and enforce habits through discipline. However, all behavioral changes enforced through conscious effort are temporary. When discipline relaxes, old habits return. Therefore, high-level education targets the core root: **Cognitive Education (Giáo dục nhận thức)**.

**2. The Mechanism of Full Awareness:**
A person transforms permanently only when they attain three dimensions regarding any aspect of life:
1. **Clear Mental Images (Rõ hình ảnh tâm trí).**
2. **Beneficial Source Concepts (Thấu suốt khái niệm nguồn có lợi).**
3. **Balanced-Positive Inner Energy Frequency (Trạng thái nội tâm cân bằng, hướng dương).**

* *The Axiom:* **"When awareness is active, behavioral thinking is obsolete."** Once the inner world aligns with beneficial seeds and light, external habits adjust naturally without forced effort.

**3. Collective Consciousness (Tâm thức tập thể):**
When a family or enterprise synchronizes their reference frameworks and source concepts, they achieve **Shared Language (Đồng ngôn)** and **Agreement (Đồng thuận)**. Any newcomer entering this environment—regardless of their old habits—automatically adjusts their behavior to match the collective energy of Joy, Tolerance, and Gratitude. This is the secret to building eternal organizations.

**4. Education at the Root (Giáo dục tận gốc) as the Destination:**
The ultimate goal of education is not degrees or survival skills, but **ultimate maturity (trưởng thành tận cùng)**. This is defined by thắp sáng (illuminating) the **7 Dimensions of Holistic Wealth** (Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Qualities, Capacity, Physical, Material). Nurturing non-material elements (Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Qualities) breathes infinite value into human beings, allowing them to rise above adversities.

**2.2. Key Knowledge:**
* **The 3 Views (Lộ trình 3 quan):** Full awareness of Life view (Nhân sinh quan), World view (Thế giới quan), and Universe view (Vũ trụ quan).
* **Collective Impact:** Establishing a shared collective consciousness that automatically refines individual actions.
* **Role Modeling (Thân giáo):** Parents and leaders live the philosophy, allowing descendants to absorb correct awareness naturally.
