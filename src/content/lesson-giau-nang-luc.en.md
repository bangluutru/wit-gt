# CURRICULUM: UNDERSTANDING INNER BEING – CREATING INNER PEACE

## TOPIC: WEALTH OF CAPACITY (MATURITY EXCEEDING OTHERS)

---

## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** Shifting awareness from purely professional skills to holistic maturity to construct sustainable success. Specifically:
  - Redefining Capacity as a combination of **Concepts, Social Relationships, and Professional Expertise**.
  - Chasing maturity to guarantee success. Mastering the Capacity Cycle.
  - Shifting focus to **being a human**: establishing that success and maturity consist of **80% being a human** (Concepts and Social Relationships) and only **20% doing work** (Expertise).
- **Significance for learners:** Helps learners thoroughly understand the path to achieve "maturity exceeding others" by cultivating Concepts, Social Relationships, and Professional Expertise simultaneously.
- **Brought transformation:** Learners stop chasing external success and focus on internal maturity, because: *"Chasing success does not guarantee success, but chasing maturity guarantees success."*

### B. In-depth Content

A person's capacity is essentially represented by their **maturity**. While professional expertise is limited, maturity is infinite. A person is called **Wealthy in Capacity** when they possess Concepts, Social Relationships, and Professional Expertise that exceed their physical age, aligning with the maturity of successful individuals recognized by society.

The core of the topic lies in **The Capacity Cycle (Vòng tuần hoàn năng lực)**:
- **Maturity in Concepts (40%):** Decided by the richness of Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Qualities, and Physical Body. A person mature in concepts holds balanced, positive ideas, prioritizing others' benefits without lowering their own.
- **Maturity in Social Relationships (40%):** Upgrading relationships from acquaintance to close and full trust, through a complete awareness of humans and practicing the 7 offerings.
- **Maturity in Professional Expertise (20%):** Requiring comprehension of the 15 source concepts of the industry, anchoring destination thinking, and executing the **6 golden words**: *Simple, Joyful, Trusting, Easy, Regular, and Mindful*.

The formula for sustainable success is **80% being a human (Concepts & Relationships) and 20% doing work (Expertise)**. When focusing on the "being a human" part, professional capacity will automatically be elevated through the mutually supportive cycle.
