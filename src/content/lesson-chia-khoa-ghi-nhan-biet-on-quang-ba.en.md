### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

**2.1. Concepts**

**Reverence - Gratitude:** The state of being inwardly touched in the face of things, events, phenomena, circumstances, or people. It is the state in which obviousness does not exist. It is the state where we recognize the reason for someone's presence, the importance of a person, and the usefulness of all things.

**Being Inwardly Touched:** An inner feeling state — something we feel we do not deserve to have, yet we have it; something we receive that goes beyond the greed and desires of reality regarding wealth, beauty, fame, food, or sleep.

**Recognition - Gratitude:** The choice to record what brings positive emotion, the good values we get to enjoy, in a balanced, upward-facing direction.

**Outreach:** Repeating again and again what someone else has done for us, or how something or some event has benefited us, with an attitude of reverence and gratitude.

**The Art of Outreach:** A marvelous art that creates, in a person's mind, a contrasting picture of benefit, so that they come to desire that picture of benefit as their own.

**The Operating Law of Reverence - Gratitude:**

Being touched appears -> Obviousness disappears -> Reverence - Gratitude manifests -> Resentment is no more.

*Introduction:* Gives people information (but no energy).

*Outreach:* Gives the listener a clear image, a clear concept, and a balanced, upward-facing inner state. It consists of: Conceptualization (words/information) + Visualization (material) + State-forming (the energy of Reverence - Gratitude).

Self-belief is the life force of the outreach expert. People's consent is the food of the outreach expert.

The outreach expert helps the recipient of the outreach with Conceptualization, State-forming, and Visualization, but does NOT decide on the recipient's behalf (respecting their total karma).

The outreach expert does not sell anything at all; receiving money is only the first step of helping.

**The Formula for Carrying Out Outreach:**

Step 1: Generate the energy source of Reverence - Gratitude.

**Erasing self-resentment:** Using the key of Recognition - Gratitude to record the Lesson - Impression - Realisation (LIR) every day. This is a way of recognizing one's own value, helping the inner self always maintain a state of being touched, without being tormented by past mistakes.
