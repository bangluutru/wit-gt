**THE APPRETELLER'S CODES – SIMPLIFYING HUMAN WISDOM**

* **9 Objects of Appretelling (9 Đối tượng quảng bá):** Wisdom, People, Dreams (plans to use money), Environment, Classes, Projects, Businesses, Products/Services, Books.

* **9 Perfected Personalities (9 Nhân cách kiện toàn).**
* **9 Elements of a Good Environment (9 Yếu tố môi trường tốt).**

**3. Code 6: The Rituals and Ceremonies of Connection**
Whether an appretelling touches awareness depends on the 6 steps of Rituals and Ceremonies:
$$\text{Listen} \rightarrow \text{Empathize} \rightarrow \text{Appretell} \rightarrow \text{Listen to Feedback} \rightarrow \text{Verify Satisfaction} \rightarrow \text{Repeat}$$
* *Critical Detail:* Skipping **Listening** and **Empathizing** to rush into appretelling closes the listener's "cup" (ego) and wastes connection merit.

**5. The 15 Source Concepts of the Industry**
By mapping codes into the 15 source concepts (Rules, Principles, Keys, Formulas, Methods, Principles, Standard Concepts, Right Mental States, Suitable Capacities, Reference Frameworks, Source Concepts, Codes, Rituals/Ceremonies, Environments, Tools/Vehicles), a coach gains **Free Trade in Consciousness (Tự do thương mại trong tâm thức)**.
