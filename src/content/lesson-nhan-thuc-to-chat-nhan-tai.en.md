### CURRICULUM: ELEVATING INNER CONSCIOUSNESS AWARENESS
#### TOPIC: AWARENESS OF THE QUALITIES OF TALENT

### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION
**A. Topic Objectives**
**Value of the topic:** Provides an accurate and tolerant perspective on the 8 qualities of talent hidden deep within every person, especially children. Helps learners possess a tool to recognize and magnify bright spots instead of focusing on darkness.
**Significance for learners:** Eliminates judgment, irritation, and mental turmoil when facing other people's behaviors that seem "problematic." Protects the inner electromagnetic vibration state so it always stays balanced and positive-facing.
**Brought transformation:** Shifts from the awareness "people are the problem" to the awareness "everyone carries qualities of talent." From someone who habitually corrects other people's faults, the learner becomes someone who creates an environment that contains and cultivates talent.
**Output result:** Learners become proficient at applying the 8 qualities of talent to encourage those around them, understand the importance of humility and appreciation-gratitude for retaining talent, thereby building an enduring organization and family.

### B. IN-DEPTH CONTENT: AWARENESS OF THE QUALITIES OF TALENT – LIGHT THAT PROTECTS THE INNER BEING AND BUILDS PEOPLE
**1. Introduction: Breaking the barrier of judgment in human life.** On the journey of elevating inner awareness, one of the biggest barriers that causes people to lose their peace and joy is the habit of focusing on other people's shortcomings. Through an ordinary lens, we very easily see weakness, stubbornness, or undesirable behavior from those around us, especially children or subordinate staff. This habit of "hunting for darkness" causes our inner electromagnetic state to fall into the negative range (irritation, suffering, torment).
To break this cycle of resentment and thoroughly implement the Principle of Light, the Master transmitted a groundbreaking piece of knowledge that completely changes how we view people: **Awareness of the qualities of talent**.
**2. Redefining Talent and the 8 Core Qualities.** According to the organization's standard frame of reference, we need to strip away the grandiose, exaggerated coat draped over the two words "talent." Talent is not a distant concept, exclusive to individuals with innate IQ or EQ. The Master offers a plain and tolerant definition: **"A talent is someone who shoulders some aspect of our life."**
For an ordinary person to mature and be able to shoulder great responsibilities, they need to be recognized and cultivated through **8 qualities of talent**, including: *Courage to admit mistakes, Courage to change, Capacity for learning, Contribution, Willingness to shoulder responsibility, Appreciation-gratitude, Humility,* and *Perseverance*.
**3. The pivot point of awareness: Shifting perspective – Protecting the inner being.** The philosophical core of this topic does not lie in memorizing the 8 keywords above, but in the process of **shifting perspective in order to protect the state of inner electromagnetic vibration**.
Let us borrow a practical argument from the lecture: When we see a child playing video games day and night, or wailing to demand a toy even after being scolded and spanked. The ordinary viewpoint will immediately label that child as "stubborn, obstinate, hard to teach." The very moment that judgment arises, the adult's inner electromagnetic state immediately shifts to Negative, giving rise to anger and violent behavior.
But if we bring the lens of the 8 qualities of talent to bear, we will be startled to realize: That child is not "obstinate" at all — they possess an extremely powerful quality — that is the **Quality of Perseverance**. The moment the awareness *"this is perseverance"* appears, our inner electromagnetic state is immediately pulled back to Balance and Positive-facing. The anger dissolves. We realize: **People are not the problem; it is only that their quality is not yet being applied in the right place.**
Instead of punishing, an inner-being educator will acknowledge: *"You are very persevering,"* then use guidance to redirect that perseverance from playing games toward practicing sports, learning a foreign language, or pursuing a passion. By naming and magnifying the light, the flaw automatically hides itself. Here, the Master establishes a great awareness: **When we have the right awareness, every child, every person beside us carries within them the quality of a talent.**
**4. The trap of capability and the key to retaining Talent.** Once talent has been recognized, the cultivation process opens up an extremely subtle karmic trap within organizations and families. If we only focus on encouraging work capability, extolling **"Contribution"** and **"Shouldering responsibility"** while forgetting to cultivate the lamp of Character, that talent will soon step onto the path of self-destruction.
The logic of cause and effect makes it clear: A person who shoulders too much but lacks **"Humility"** (lacking the thinking of *achievement is due to other people*) and is missing the energy source of **"Appreciation - Gratitude,"** will quickly develop the disease of inner arrogance. They begin to have delusions of grandeur, boast of their contributions, demand excessive rights, resent the organization, and ultimately leave or sabotage the collective.
Therefore, the core key to protecting and retaining talent is to cultivate in them Humility and a grateful heart. At the same time, the organization must be an environment overflowing with recognition, gratitude, and readiness to acknowledge others who excel beyond oneself. Wherever there is appreciation and gratitude for the willingness to shoulder responsibility, talent automatically gathers there.
**5. Conclusion: A macro vision beyond the concept of "using people."** The Master's guiding journey of awareness closes with a statement that elevates management thinking: Our organization finds the formula for cultivating talent **not in order to exploit their capacity to shoulder responsibility for personal gain**.
If we only nurture talent to "squeeze dry" their labor, that is a mindset of appropriation. On the contrary, we create a naturally favorable environment where talent is cultivated toward **7 kinds of comprehensive wealth** (Wisdom, Mental State, Character, Quality, Capability, Physical Body, Material Wealth). The highest path is to help them mature to the fullest, going from a person with the quality of shouldering responsibility to becoming **9 types of people in 1** (the Eminent Person, the Noble Benefactor, the Connector, the Wealth God, the Talented Person, the Enlightened Master, the Teacher, the Expert, the Wise Friend).
**Core message:** Awareness of the qualities of talent is, in essence, a "wondrous method" for protecting one's own peace and joy in the face of the myriad affairs of human life. When you see all people as talents, your inner being will forever be at ease, and you will officially become a machine that attracts, contains, and lights up great people.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
**2.1. Concepts**
**Talent:** A person who shoulders some aspect of our life.
**Quality of talent:** The seeds of character and attitude within a person that, if encouraged and cultivated, will make that person a true talent who shoulders great responsibility in the future.
**8 Qualities of Talent:** Including: (1) Courage to admit mistakes, (2) Courage to change, (3) Capacity for learning, (4) Contribution, (5) Shouldering responsibility, (6) Appreciation - gratitude, (7) Humility, (8) Perseverance.
**2.2. Key Knowledge**
**The power of "Encouraging the Cause" instead of "Praising the Effect":** Instead of only praising when a person wins a gold medal (Effect), the inner-being educator looks at the process and encourages "Perseverance," "Capacity for learning" (Cause). Cultivating the Cause helps that quality spread into every other aspect of life.
**The trap in using talent:** If an organization only focuses on praising contribution and willingness to shoulder responsibility without cultivating the Energy of Appreciation - Gratitude and Humility, the talent will mistakenly believe they are the best. Arrogance arises, they will demand entitlements, resent the organization, and leave.
**The secret to retaining talent:** An environment that can contain talent is one with Recognition, Gratitude, Acknowledgment of others who excel beyond oneself, and a culture of "achievement is due to other people."
**2.3. Key Awareness**
**Establishing awareness 1 - Transforming perspective, protecting the inner being:** The Master establishes an extremely important awareness: Understanding the 8 qualities of talent is not only for training people, but first and foremost so as to **avoid mental turmoil** in the face of others. When we see behavior that appears negative (such as stubbornness), if we recognize it as "the quality of perseverance," the inner electromagnetic state immediately shifts toward the positive. People are not the problem; it is only that the quality is not yet being used in the right place.
**Establishing awareness 2 - Everyone is a talent:** Through the lens of these 8 qualities, we will realize that every child, everyone around us, carries within them the quality of talent. We only need to find 1 quality and magnify it using the Principle of Light.
**Establishing awareness 3 - Beyond even Talent itself:** We do not create the formula for cultivating talent merely so they will work for us. We create the setting for them to move toward the 7 kinds of comprehensive wealth; from a Talent who shoulders responsibility, they will mature to become an Enlightened Master, an Expert, a Wise Friend (9 types of people in 1).
**2.4. 3 Personal Application Examples**
**Example 1: Overcoming self-doubt about capability (Capacity for learning)**
*Context before transformation:* Starting to learn a new skill (e.g., a foreign language/calligraphy) but absorbing it very slowly.
*Old awareness:* Labeling myself as inept, without talent, giving rise to discouragement and wanting to give up (looking at the darkness).
*Knowledge applied:* The qualities of Capacity for learning and Perseverance.
*New action:* Stopped comparing myself to others. Acknowledged my own "Perseverance" in still sitting down to study every day even when not yet skilled. Magnified my own capacity for learning.
*Result after transformation:* Inner being at peace, no longer under pressure. Thanks to persistent cultivation, capability automatically opened up and I achieved results far beyond my initial expectations.
**Example 2: Facing a personal mistake (Courage to admit mistakes & change)**
*Context before transformation:* Caused a major mistake affecting personal and family finances.
*Old awareness:* Fear, avoiding responsibility, finding reasons to blame circumstances to protect the ego.
*Knowledge applied:* The qualities of Courage to admit mistakes and Courage to change.
*New action:* Recognized the root cause came from myself, faced my family to admit the mistake candidly, while immediately drawing up a new action plan (courage to change).
*Result after transformation:* Freed myself from self-torment. Not only did the family not resent me, they also acknowledged my growth, helping me quickly rebuild my fortunes.
**Example 3: Eliminating inner arrogance (Humility)**
*Context before transformation:* Achieved a certain level of success in my career, praised by many people.
*Old awareness:* Believing myself to be highly capable, becoming complacent, beginning to judge and look down on those slower than me.
*Knowledge applied:* The quality of Humility and the philosophy "achievement is due to other people."
*New action:* Reflected on the entire journey, acknowledged and gave thanks to the noble benefactors, connectors, and talents who had supported me. Proactively acknowledged the excellence of others.
*Result after transformation:* Inner vibration became noble and composed. No longer controlled by the trap of fame, I continued to attract even greater talents to accompany me.
**2.5. 3 Family Application Examples**
**Example 1: Transforming the perspective on a "stubborn" child**
*Context before transformation:* Son frequently played video games forgetting to eat and sleep, demanding to buy toys by crying for hours without giving up.
*Old awareness:* Anger, scolding and beating the child, labeling him a "spoiled, stubborn, hard-to-teach" child (negative electromagnetic energy).
*Knowledge applied:* Shifting the awareness of the behavior into the "Perseverance" Quality.
*New action:* Recognized the child's pursuit of his goal as the "Perseverance" quality. Inner state returned to balance. Proactively redirected that perseverance toward studying sports or reading, and encouraged: "You are very persevering."
*Result after transformation:* The child no longer resisted his parents. Perseverance used in the right place helped him achieve impressive records in academics and physical fitness.
**Example 2: Cultivating "Capacity for learning" instead of pressure over grades**
*Context before transformation:* Child received a poor grade on an important exam.
*Old awareness:* Focused on the effect (the grade), disappointed, scolded and compared the child to "other people's children."
*Knowledge applied:* Encouraging the Cause (8 qualities) instead of Praising/Blaming the Effect.
*New action:* Set aside the grade. Recognized the effort the child had put in staying up late reviewing, and encouraged: "Mom recognizes your capacity for learning and your dedication of time to studying."
*Result after transformation:* The child was not psychologically harmed, maintained love for studying, courageously admitted the mistake themselves, changed method, and improved their results on the next exam.
**Example 3: Acknowledging a spouse's "Willingness to shoulder responsibility"**
*Context before transformation:* Husband/wife only did minor household chores, sometimes doing them wrong or not to one's liking.
*Old awareness:* Criticizing, taking over everything oneself out of the belief the other was useless, leading to being overburdened and exhausted.
*Knowledge applied:* The qualities of Contribution and Shouldering responsibility.
*New action:* Even though the other person swept the floor imperfectly, still acknowledged: "Grateful that you shoulder the housework to help the family." Encouraged even the smallest actions.
*Result after transformation:* The other person felt acknowledged, automatically found the courage to change and do better; the family became harmonious, and the couple achieved common language and consensus.
**2.6. 3 Organizational Application Examples**
**Example 1: Handling a star employee with arrogance disease**
*Context before transformation:* A talented employee bringing 50% of company revenue began to act condescending, make demands, and threaten to quit.
*Old awareness:* Fear of losing the person, yielding to unreasonable demands, or firing them out of anger, causing losses to the company.
*Knowledge applied:* The qualities of Appreciation - Gratitude and Humility in cultivating talent.
*New action:* Met with the employee, acknowledged their contribution, while also cultivating in them the thinking "achievement is due to other people" (pointing out the support of other departments that made that revenue possible).
*Result after transformation:* The employee recognized their own limits, giving rise to Appreciation - Gratitude toward the organization. The disease of arrogance disappeared; they continued contributing with absolute loyalty.
**Example 2: Giving opportunity to new staff (Contribution, Shouldering responsibility)**
*Context before transformation:* Hired a new fresher without experience; leadership dared not assign major work for fear of mistakes.
*Old awareness:* Only evaluating people through current "Capability" (effect), believing they could not do the work.
*Knowledge applied:* Seeing through to the 8 qualities of talent.
*New action:* Observed the new staff member's attitude. Seeing they had "Capacity for learning" and a spirit of "Courage to admit mistakes," leadership proactively assigned small projects for them to "Contribute and shoulder responsibility" and acknowledged every effort.
*Result after transformation:* The staff member matured remarkably in a short time, fully compensating for their lack of experience, and became a loyal core force of the organization.
**Example 3: Building organizational culture (Appreciation-gratitude)**
*Context before transformation:* The company focused only on KPIs; internal toxic competition; employees jealous when colleagues were promoted.
*Old awareness:* Using money and material benefits as the sole motivation to retain talent.
*Knowledge applied:* An environment that contains talent (Recognition, gratitude, acknowledgment).
*New action:* Established a culture of "Gratitude for shouldering responsibility." Organized recognition events not just for sales figures but for cross-department support.
*Result after transformation:* Eliminated jealousy. The organization created an ecosystem overflowing with positive energy, attracting countless outside talents who voluntarily asked to join.
**2.7. 3 Social Application Examples**
**Example 1: Changing the community's view of the younger generation (Gen Z)**
*Context before transformation:* Media and society often label Gen Z as a "rebellious generation, high on ego, lacking patience."
*Old awareness:* Older people judge, react negatively, creating a deep generational gap.
*Knowledge applied:* Using the 8 qualities of talent to explain behavior.
*New action:* Educators analyzed that "rebelliousness" is precisely the quality of "Courage to change" and "Capacity for learning" absorbing the new. Brought this awareness into media campaigns.
*Result after transformation:* Society became more tolerant of young people. The generational gap narrowed; organizations learned to harness Gen Z's strengths for innovation.
**Example 2: Addressing the problem of school/juvenile delinquency**
*Context before transformation:* Youths who violated discipline were shunned by society, disciplined by school, with families feeling helpless.
*Old awareness:* Society used punishment and isolation (taking out the darkness) to address the problem.
*Knowledge applied:* Magnifying the quality of talent to overpower the darkness.
*New action:* Created community social projects for the youths to participate in. Encouraged their "Courage to admit mistakes," and assigned them to "Shoulder responsibility" for helping people worse off than themselves.
*Result after transformation:* When given opportunity and recognition, the lamp of their character lit up. Light pushed back the darkness of violation; they reintegrated into society and became productive citizens. *(Note: The connection between protecting the community and maintaining merits and virtues will be clarified in the topic: Desired Outcomes in Human Life.)*
**Example 3: Building a nation of talents**
*Context before transformation:* The national education system chased achievement, ranking students based on scores in natural/social science subjects.
*Old awareness:* Using test scores as the sole measure to evaluate a person's talent.
*Knowledge applied:* Bringing the 8 qualities of talent into the foundation of root-level educational philosophy.
*New action:* The education system renewed its evaluation criteria: honoring children with Capacity for learning, with Appreciation-gratitude, who know how to Contribute and shoulder responsibility for the community.
*Result after transformation:* The nation produced a generation of citizens who are not only professionally skilled but also have well-rounded character and outstanding quality, creating a solid foundation for the nation to rise to prosperity on the international stage.
