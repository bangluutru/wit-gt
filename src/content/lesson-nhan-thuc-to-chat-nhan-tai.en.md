## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** Provides a precise and tolerant view of the 8 qualities of a talent hidden deep inside every human, especially children. It equips learners with tools to identify and magnify bright spots instead of focusing on darkness.
- **Significance for learners:** Dissolves judgment, annoyance, and mental disturbance when facing others' seemingly "problematic" behaviors. It protects the inner electromagnetic vibration state, keeping it balanced and positive.
- **Brought transformation:** Shifts awareness from "people have problems" to "everyone carries the qualities of a talent." Instead of fixing others' errors, learners become creators of environments that embrace and nurture talents.
- **Outcomes:** Learners become proficient in applying the 8 qualities of a talent to encourage others, understanding the importance of humility and gratitude to retain talents, thereby building enduring organizations and families.

### B. In-depth Content

One of the greatest barriers to inner peace is the habit of focusing on other people's faults. Under a conventional lens, we easily see weakness, stubbornness, or unpleasant behaviors in those around us, especially children or subordinates. This habit of "seeking darkness" drops our inner electromagnetic state into negative frequencies (irritation, suffering, regret). To break this cycle and execute the **Principle of Light**, sages transfer a key awareness: **the qualities of a talent**.

Under standard reference systems, we strip away the grandiose label of "talent." Talent is not an exclusive concept for individuals with high IQ or EQ. We define it simply: **"A talent is one who carries a certain aspect of our life."** For a regular person to mature and carry major responsibilities, they must be recognized and nurtured through the **8 qualities of a talent**:
1. **Courage to admit mistakes** (Dũng cảm nhận lỗi)
2. **Courage to change** (Dũng cảm thay đổi)
3. **Extraordinary learning capacity** (Sức học tập)
4. **Dedication** (Cống hiến)
5. **Carriage** (Gánh vác)
6. **Honouring & Gratitude** (Trân trọng biết ơn)
7. **Humility** (Khiêm tốn)
8. **Perseverance** (Kiên trì)

The core philosophy does not lie in memorizing these words, but in **shifting our perspective to protect our electromagnetic vibration state**. Consider a practical example: when a child plays video games all day, or cries for a toy despite being scolded. A conventional view labels the child as "stubborn and disobedient." The moment this judgment arises, the adult's inner vibration turns negative, bringing anger.

But when we apply the lens of the 8 qualities of a talent, we realize: the child is not "stubborn," but holds a strong quality—**Perseverance**. Once we recognize *"this is perseverance,"* our inner electromagnetic vibration returns to balanced or positive. Anger vanishes. We realize: **"People have no problems; their qualities are just placed in the wrong spot."** Instead of scolding, an inner educator acknowledges: *"You are very persevering,"* and guides that perseverance toward sports, language learning, or passions. By magnifying the light, darkness automatically fades. **When we hold the correct awareness, everyone around us carries the qualities of a talent.**

Once talent is identified, nurturing opens a subtle trap. If we only praise work capacity, **"Dedication,"** and **"Carriage"** while forgetting to light the lamp of Personality, the talent will soon enter self-destruction. The logic of cause and effect shows: one who carries much but lacks **Humility** (the awareness that *achievements are brought by others*) and **Honouring & Gratitude** will develop inner arrogance. They demand excessive privileges, complain, and eventually leave or damage the collective.

Therefore, the key to protecting and retaining talents is cultivating Humility and Gratitude. Simultaneously, the organization must be an environment of appreciation and ready to admit others' superiority. Where carriage is honoured and appreciated, talents gather.

We do not nurture talents to exploit their capacity for selfish gains. Exploiting them is a consumerist mindset. Instead, we construct an environment where they are nurtured toward the **7 dimensions of holistic wealth** (Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Qualities, Capacity, Physical Body, Material Assets). The highest path is helping them mature, transforming from one who carries tasks to the **9 types of people in 1**.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Talent (Nhân tài):** One who carries a certain aspect of our life.
- **Qualities of a Talent (Tố chất nhân tài):** Seeds of character and attitude that, if encouraged and nurtured, enable a person to become a true talent carrying heavy responsibilities.
- **8 Qualities of a Talent:** Courage to admit mistakes, Courage to change, Learning capacity, Dedication, Carriage, Honouring & Gratitude, Humility, and Perseverance.

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

- **Encouraging the Cause vs. Praising the Outcome:** Instead of only praising when someone wins a gold medal (Outcome), encourage their "Perseverance" and "Learning capacity" (Cause). Cultivating the cause spreads that quality to all aspects of life.
- **The Trap of Talent Usage:** If an organization only praises dedication and carriage without cultivating Gratitude and Humility, the talent will think they are the best. Arrogance arises, leading to demands and resentment.
- **Retention Secret:** An environment that retains talents is one with Recognition, Gratitude, admitting others' superiority, and a culture of "Achievements are brought by others."

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **Shift View to Protect the Mind:** Understanding the 8 qualities is not just for training others, but primarily **to keep one's own mind undisturbed** (không bị loạn tâm). Seeing a stubborn behavior as "perseverance" turns the inner vibration positive.
- **Everyone is a Talent:** With this lens, everyone around us carries talent qualities. Find one quality and magnify it using the Principle of Light.
- **Beyond Talent:** We do not build a formula just to exploit talents. We create a context for them to reach the 7 dimensions of holistic wealth and become a Leader (9 types of people in 1).

### 2.4. Applications (Personal, Family, Organizational, Social)

#### Personal Application
- **Overcoming Self-Doubt (Learning Capacity):** When learning a slow skill, stop self-labeling as "untalented." Acknowledge your "Perseverance" and "Learning Capacity" daily. The mind remains peaceful, and capacity opens.
- **Facing Mistakes (Courage to Admit & Change):** When making a financial error, accept the cause is yourself, face others with courage to admit errors, and immediately change. Self-regret disappears.

#### Family Application
- **Transforming the "Stubborn" Child:** View a child's persistence as "Perseverance." Redirect that perseverance toward sports or reading, acknowledging: *"You are very persevering."* The child stops resisting and achieves records.
- **Appreciating the Spouse's Carriage:** Even if the spouse cleans the house imperfectly, acknowledge: *"Thank you for carrying the household chores."* The spouse feels recognized and changes to do better.

#### Organizational Application
- **Managing Arrogant Star Employees:** Acknowledge their carriage, but bồi dưỡng them with "Achievements are brought by others" by pointing out the support of other departments. Arrogance fades, replaced by loyalty.
- **Creating a Culture of Gratitude:** Establish a culture of "Gratitude for Carriage." Praise team support rather than just individual sales, eliminating jealousy.

#### Social Impact
- **Shifting Views on Gen Z:** Reframe "rebellious Gen Z" as possessing "Courage to Change" and "Learning Capacity." Society becomes tolerant, and organizations innovate.
- **Rehabilitating Youth Offending:** Instead of isolating them, engage them in community projects. Encourage "Courage to Admit Mistakes" and give them responsibilities to help others. They rebuild character and reintegrate.
