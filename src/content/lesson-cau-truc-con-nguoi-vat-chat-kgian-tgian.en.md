- **EXTREMELY FAST movement:** Particles lose their fixed form, creating waves. They exceed human sensory perception and become invisible, merging into **Space** (e.g., telephone waves, wifi, or non-material energy like emotions and inner peace).

> 📌 **[DIAGRAM 1: PARTICLE MOVEMENT]**
> - **Slow:** Particles → Condensation → Form → **MATTER** (Visible)
> - **Extremely Fast:** Particles → Wave → Invisible → **SPACE** (Invisible)

> 📌 **[DIAGRAM 2: OBSERVER AND OBSERVED]**

Time is not a fixed, linear constraint. It is the change in vibration frequency to experience different positions in space. Thus, time can be stretched or compressed based on the observer's choice of inner vibration state, releasing the fear of aging and lack of time.

#### 4. Feeling vs. Value of Time (Nghịch lý Cảm giác và Giá trị Thời gian)
