### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION
**1A. Objectives & Target Audience:**
* **Value of the topic:** Identifies the 11 core questions that define the entire roadmap of human life. It reveals the blind spot of using surface metrics or logic to solve human problems, presenting the ultimate solutions anchored in Triết lý Giáo dục Tận gốc (Education at the Root).
* **Significance for learners:** Relieves learners of endless struggling in loops (sầu quần) trying to find their life's purpose from superficial angles. It empowers them to step into the role of an Inner-Self Consultant/Trainer to solve life's challenges.
* **Brought transformation:** Shifts the state from confusion or feeling lost to absolute confidence, naturalness, and self-control. Learners thấu triệt their life's treasured methods (Pháp bảo), knowing how to attract and treat the 9 types of people to build good relationships (Duyên lành).
* **Learner's Outcomes:**
  * Learners thấu triệt and solve the 11 challenging questions. They understand why only Education at the Root provides the wisest answer to human existence.

**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**RESOLVING THE 11 GREAT QUESTIONS OF LIFE**

**1. The 11 Challenging Questions:**
Throughout history, humans have struggled in loops searching for their true purpose by asking these 11 questions:
1. **Core Mindset (Tâm niệm):** What is my deep intention?
2. **Mission (Sứ mệnh):** What is my life's mission?
3. **Passion (Đam mê):** What is my passion?
4. **Drive (Động lực):** Where does my motivation come from?
5. **Goal (Mục tiêu):** Where am I heading?
6. **Action (Hành động):** How do I act?
7. **Mental State (Tâm thái):** What is my internal state?
8. **Principle (Nguyên tắc):** What principles do I build upon?
9. **Treasured Method (Pháp bảo):** What is my ultimate tool?
10. **Desire (Mong muốn):** What is my ultimate desire?
11. **Foundation (Nền tảng):** What is my core foundation?

**2. Why are they "Challenging"?**
The challenge lies in using the wrong reference framework. Most people apply physical sciences, material accumulation, or superficial logic to answer these questions. Without anchoring in **Education at the Root (Giáo dục tận gốc)**, these questions remain unanswered, causing endless confusion.

**3. The Cognitive Shift: Stepping into the Role of a Coach**
To solve these questions, you cannot act as an ordinary worker, housewife, or manager. You must **Self-Anchor as an Inner-Self Consultant/Trainer (Mentor/Master)**.
By taking this role:
* Your **Foundation** becomes the *3 Standard Reference Frameworks*.
* Your **Mental State** becomes *Joy, Tolerance, and Gratitude*.
* Your **Treasured Method (Pháp bảo)** becomes *Appretell, Coordination, and Lead* (Quảng bá, Phối hợp, Dẫn dắt).

With the Treasured Methods in hand, you break the limits of personal desire. Your mission naturally attracts the **9 Types of People (9 dạng người)** to assist:
1. **Noble Master (Cao nhân - People who point out path):** We Tri phúc (know blessing) - Trọng phúc (value blessing) - Tạo phúc (create blessing).
2. **Benefactor (Quý nhân - People who help):** We Tri ân (know gratitude) - Trọng ân (value gratitude) - Báo ân (repay gratitude by helping them fulfill their dreams).
3. **Connector (Nhân mạch - People who connect):** We support and appretell their image, helping them connect relationships.
4. **Talisman / Fortune Bringer (Thần tài - People who multiply assets/virtues):** We appreciate and value their presence.
5. **Talent (Nhân tài - People who gánh vác / contribute):** We design a growth roadmap to train them.
6. **The Master (Minh sư - People who lead):** We trust absolutely, accompany, and respect their lead.
7. **Teacher (Thầy cô - People who give principles/teachings):** We respect and treasure their life principles.
8. **Expert (Chuyên gia - People who give mindset/methods):** We value their thinking and provide environments for them to excel.
9. **Wise Friend / Spiritual Master (Thiện tri thức - People who open wisdom):** We apply their teachings and spread values to humanity.
