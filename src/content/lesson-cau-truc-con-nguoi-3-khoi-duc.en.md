- **Value of the topic:** This is a vital component of the Religious (Belief) perspective in the 3 standard reference systems. The topic decodes the root causes of luck and adversity in life through the operation of 3 electromagnetic blocks: Bad merit, Good merit, and Balanced merit.

### B. In-depth Content

Human life is currently dominated by three core perspectives: Scientific, Religious (Belief), and Ethical. While the Formula for the Root Cause of Life provides the Scientific view (Mental Images), the Human Structure provides the Religious lens to explain the source of human life. Deep within the shell of human Nature and Body, our inner being is shaped by the vibration state of the 3 blocks of electromagnetism: Bad merit, Good merit, and Balanced merit.

The presence of these 3 electromagnetic blocks is not abstract theory; they are the root that decides all human experiences, world views, and cosmic views:
- **Bad merit (Ác đức - Negative Electromagnetism):** Causes humans to experience undesirable outcomes, suffering, poverty, and sickness.
- **Good merit (Phước đức - Positive Electromagnetism):** Brings humans abundance in material life, luck, prosperity, and beautiful relationships.
- **Balanced merit (Công đức - Luminous/Balanced Electromagnetism):** Helps humans open wisdom, achieve tranquil Inner Peace, understand human life, and find the Truth within.

The greatest paradox of humanity is chasing external material values without knowing that when we lose this physical body, assets, fame, and power dissolve. The only things we carry in the cycle of reincarnation are our blocks of Balanced merit, Good merit, Bad merit, and our Total Karma.

Therefore, an outstanding manager of cause and effect does not focus on dealing with superficial symptoms, but on managing the root through the following principles:
- **Accumulation:** Comprehending that Good merit is created when making others happy (satisfying their wants regarding Wealth, Form, Fame, Eating, Sleep), and Balanced merit is created when helping others elevate their inner awareness (receiving Truth).
- **Protection:** Comprehending the principle: "Good merit is like a mountain of wood; a single matchstick can burn it down." That matchstick is resentment, pride, anger, and sexual misconduct. Just one minute of unconscious resentment can burn down merits accumulated over many years.
- **Transformation:** Using the energy of Honouring - Gratitude to dissolve resentment, Humbleness to dissolve pride, and Inner Peace and Tolerance to extinguish anger.

When we fully realise the operation of the 3 blocks of merit, we recognize a great truth: Without humans, we cannot create Good or Balanced merits. From there, we stop seeing people as problems, but instead look at every person who appears in our life as a **"merit-accumulating machine" (cỗ máy Công đức - Phước đức)**. Our attitude towards this machine decides whether the rest of our life is spent in poverty and resentment, or stepping onto the peak of Sixfold Life Fulfillment and Holistic Wealth.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Core Concepts

According to the Human Structure reference system, wrapping the 16 natures are 3 electromagnetic blocks:
- **Bad merit (Ác đức - Negative Electromagnetism):** Wrapped in the human nature shell. Created when we make others suffer. This block initiates undesirable outcomes in life.
- **Good merit (Phước đức - Positive Electromagnetism):** Wrapped in the human nature shell. Created when we make others happy (helping them satisfy wants regarding Wealth, Form, Fame, Eating, Sleep). It dominates material life and the ease/flow of our path.
- **Balanced merit (Công đức - Luminous / Balanced Electromagnetism):** Wrapped in the shell of Truth. Created when we help others raise their inner awareness and elevate their level of wisdom (especially level 3 wisdom of Emptiness of Nature), helping them receive their own Truth. It opens untaught knowledge (vô sư trí) and brings mental peace.

### 2.2. Key Knowledge

The operation of the 3 blocks of merit is not random but follows the law of Cause and Effect. We must know how to protect our merits from the "enemies" inside:
- **Protecting Good merit:** Good merit is like a mountain of wood; a single matchstick can burn it down. The four "matchsticks" that destroy good merit fastest are: **Resentment (Oán trách), Pride (Ngạo mạn), Anger (Sân si), and Sexual misconduct (Tà dâm)**. When a wife resents her husband, or a leader shows pride towards staff, they burn their own mountain of merits, leading to plunges in finance, health, and relationships.
- **Protecting Balanced merit:** Balanced merit is like a mountain of gold; it is hard to burn down but very easy to be clouded (bôi đen) by **Dogmatic Views (Chấp kiến - clinging to one's own knowing and denying others)**. When balanced merit is clouded, human wisdom is obscured.
- **Accessing Total Karma:** Total Karma (Knowledge - Conditions - Outcomes) lies still. The vibration frequency of our inner being (decided by the 3 blocks of merit) "calls" total karma out. Negative electromagnetism calls undesirable total karma; positive electromagnetism calls desirable total karma; balanced electromagnetism accesses wisdom and avoids attachment.

### 2.3. Deep Insights

- **Insight 1: "Humans are merit-accumulating machines."**
  - *The Paradox:* We often view others as burdens, sources of trouble, or only seek them for personal leverage.
  - *Root Awareness:* Without humans, you cannot create a single seed of Good or Balanced merits! From parents to partners to clients, they are the "machines" for us to accumulate assets for our soul's journey. Comprehending this, we stop blaming and naturally show Honouring - Gratitude towards every person.
- **Insight 2: What do we carry when leaving the world?**
  - *Root Awareness:* When leaving the body, all wealth and titles stay behind. The only baggage that decides our destination in reincarnation is our blocks of Balanced merit, Good merit, and Bad merit. Mastery of cause and effect is not about earning the most money, but accumulating merits. "High virtue commands respect from all realms (Đức trọng quỷ thần kinh)" – one with abundant merits lives in prosperity and is protected by all forces.
- **Insight 3: Honouring - Gratitude is the "fire extinguisher."**
  - Since resentment is the fire that burns good merit, a leader must use Honouring - Gratitude to dissolve resentment, Tolerance to dissolve anger, and Humbleness to dissolve pride. Holding these three states is wearing the eternal armor of merit protection.
