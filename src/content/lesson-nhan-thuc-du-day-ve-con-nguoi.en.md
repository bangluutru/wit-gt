### CURRICULUM: ELEVATING INNER CONSCIOUSNESS AWARENESS
### TOPIC: FULL AWARENESS OF HUMAN BEINGS

### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION
**A. Topic Objectives**
**Value of the topic:** This is a hinge topic in the path of thoroughly grasping "Good Affinity" in human life. It helps learners completely break apart prejudices, barriers, and grievances toward people, thereby establishing a system of beneficial source concepts to attract and preserve good relationships and conditions.
**Significance for learners:** Helps learners realize that every achievement, suffering, or happiness in life (finances, health, career) is brought about by people. When we master our awareness of people, we master our destiny and the outcomes of our lives.
**Brought transformation:** Shifting from the state of "seeing people as a problem" (blaming, seeking to make others change) to the state of "people are capital, a machine of merits and virtues." Learners attain a source of energy of Peace and Joy, Tolerance, and Appreciation and gratitude toward every presence around them.
**Output result:** Learners *understand* the 5 inner awarenesses about people and 9 types of people; *realize* the principle of "Common Language – Consensus – Companionship"; and *apply* the upgrading of the 5 levels of social relationships to create good affinity.
**B. In-depth content:** In the human-life worldview, people are the center of the cycle of karma and affinity. We seek after a great many "desired outcomes" (career, happiness, freedom of time), but forget that every sweet outcome must pass through a threshold called "Affinity" — and Affinity is precisely People. If we get entangled, hold grievances, or view people as the problem, we are unknowingly closing the door to our own blessings with our own hands.
The topic **"Full Awareness of Human Beings"** is a revolution that changes the lens through which we view human life. Starting from clarifying what "full awareness" means (a balanced, positive-facing inner state + a clear mental image + a clear source concept), the lecture leads us to reinstall 5 core awarenesses: People are Capital, are a Teacher, are a Bridge - a Path, are a Machine of Merits and Virtues, and are a source to borrow strength and support from. From seeing everyone as a benefactor, we will spontaneously give rise to Appreciation and Gratitude.
Not stopping at theory, the Master transfers a strategic vision: recognizing and treating 9 types of people (the Eminent Person, the Noble Benefactor, the Connector, the Wealth God, the Talented Person, the Enlightened Master, the Teacher, the Expert, the Wise Friend). The deepest message is not to *search for* these 9 types of people, but the process of cultivating Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, and Character so that we ourselves *become* the 9 types of people in 1. When that happens, we automatically become the "Good Affinity" that attracts every resource of society, achieving absolute "Common Language – Consensus – Companionship" to make every dream come true.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
#### 2.1. Concepts
**Full awareness:** Whenever anything is mentioned, we have a *balanced, positive-facing inner state*, together with a *clear mental image* and a *clear, beneficial source concept* about it.
**Common Language – Consensus – Companionship:**
*Common Language:* Sharing the same frame of reference, the same source concept about information.
*Consensus:* Matching energy-vibration frequencies, heading toward the same goal.
*Companionship:* Converging into material form, working together and staying bonded long-term. (Note: Without common language and consensus, companionship will easily break apart.)
**9 Types of People:** The set of people who bring key value in human life, including: the Eminent Person, the Noble Benefactor, the Connector, the Wealth God, the Talented Person, the Enlightened Master, the Teacher, the Expert, and the Wise Friend.
#### 2.2. Key Knowledge
**5 Inner Awarenesses about People:**
**People are Capital:** Capital is not only money, but assets, wisdom, technology, and especially the storehouse of Total Karma (Consciousness - Affinity - Effect).
**People are a Bridge or a Path:** If they are a bridge, they help us connect with other affinities/conditions; if they are a path, they walk alongside us to the destination.
**People are a Machine of Merits - Virtues:** A person's presence gives us the opportunity to create value, accumulating radiant and positive electromagnetic energy.
**People are a Teacher:** They bring us lessons to help us mature in every circumstance.
**Borrowing Strength and Support:** A person's achievement is brought about by other people; knowing how to borrow others' strength makes everything simple and effortless.
**5 Levels of Social Relationships:** Acquaintance -> Familiarity -> Fondness -> Trust -> Closeness. People need to proactively use the *7 Important Forms of Giving in Life* to upgrade relationships instead of waiting for it to happen naturally.
#### 2.3. Key Awareness
**Establishing 1 - Breaking the paradox of "People are the problem":** In society, when adversity occurs, we usually demand that other people change. *The Master would like to establish this awareness: "People are not the problem."* The secret here is that all suffering or dissatisfaction comes from the seed of our own mental image. When we understand that "the root cause comes from me" and use Tolerance to treat others, every human-to-human problem in life will spontaneously dissolve.
**Establishing 2 - Decoding blind "Companionship":** We often want our family or personnel to "walk alongside" us while forgetting the foundation. The Master points out: *"Companionship truly becomes sustainable only when it originates from Common Language and Consensus."* The secret of a great leader/promoter is not to force others to work alongside them, but to provide Source Concepts and Mental Images so that they *reach consensus* themselves, after which *companionship* becomes self-evident.
**Establishing 3 - The path to becoming the 9 types of people in 1:** People often hope that upon starting a venture they will meet a Noble Benefactor or a Wealth God to help them (a mindset of seeking/expecting). The paradox is, if we do not have the cause, we cannot reap the effect. Instead of searching, the Master establishes the destination: *"Become the 9 types of people in 1."* When we proactively act as a Connector linking people together, as an Eminent Person offering guidance, as a Wealth God supporting others... we have made ourselves into the root source of good affinity, and every social resource will automatically converge toward us.
#### 2.4. 3 Personal Application Examples
**Example 1: Work Aspect (Borrowing strength and support)**
*Context:* Starting a business, taking on everything myself from sales to accounting to marketing because I didn't trust anyone, leading to exhaustion.
*Old awareness:* Afraid others would mess things up, thinking "doing it myself is best." Seeing people as a risk.
*Knowledge applied:* People are capital; Borrowing strength and support.
*New action:* Delegating work and trusting others. Placing people in the right position, viewing their contribution as "capital" of Total Karma that offsets my own weaknesses.
*Result:* Work flowed smoothly, I became at ease and relaxed, and the business expanded in scale thanks to collective strength.
**Example 2: Inner Aspect (Letting go of grievances)**
*Context:* Was defrauded of a large sum of money by a close friend. Suffered and resented this for years.
*Old awareness:* That person is an enemy who brought me suffering; seeing people as the problem.
*Knowledge applied:* The root cause comes from me; People are a Teacher.
*New action:* Recognized the fault as coming from my own seed of greed and lack of financial understanding. Viewed that friend as a "Teacher" teaching me a lesson about financial management. Gave rise to Tolerance.
*Result:* Cut the thread of resentment (negative electromagnetic energy); inner being returned to peace and joy, which in turn made business run smoothly and I recovered many times the amount lost.
**Example 3: Career Aspect (Becoming the 9 types of people)**
*Context:* Worked as an office employee for 10 years, always complaining about not meeting a good boss (Enlightened Master) or someone to lift me up (Noble Benefactor).
*Old awareness:* Looking outward, seeking for others to help me first.
*Knowledge applied:* Becoming the 9 types of people in 1.
*New action:* Stopped seeking. Proactively became a "Talented Person" shouldering the department's difficult work, became a "Connector" linking customers for colleagues.
*Result:* Became an indispensable core staff member. Naturally attracted the attention of the Board of Directors (Noble Benefactor/Enlightened Master) and was promoted to a senior leadership position.
#### 2.5. 3 Family Application Examples
**Example 1: Husband-Wife Relationship**
*Context:* Husband wanted to start a company; wife fiercely opposed out of fear of risk. The couple argued and had a cold war.
*Old awareness:* Husband forced his wife to "go along" (sign papers, contribute capital) through imposition. Wife saw her husband as unrealistic.
*Knowledge applied:* Common Language - Consensus - Companionship.
*New action:* The husband stopped forcing companionship. Instead, he sat down and shared with his wife a *clear image* of the company's future, providing a *source concept* about financial safety so she could feel secure (Common Language).
*Result:* The wife's inner state shifted to support (Consensus), after which she voluntarily contributed money and helped with the work (sustainable Companionship).
**Example 2: Parents and Children**
*Context:* Child performed poorly at school and frequently played games. Mother got angry, used corporal punishment and prohibitions.
*Old awareness:* Saw the child as the problem, as a "burden," and demanded the child change according to her wishes.
*Knowledge applied:* People are not the problem; People are a machine of merits and virtues.
*New action:* Mother acknowledged her own lack of companionship. Recognized that accompanying her child through this stage was an opportunity for her to train patience and Tolerance (accumulating blessings).
*Result:* The pressure was lifted. The mother's unconditional love moved the child inwardly; he voluntarily reduced gaming and returned to studying.
**Example 3: Extended Family Relations**
*Context:* Lived a closed-off life, rarely interacting with extended relatives out of fear of trouble, leading to family isolation during important life events.
*Old awareness:* Leaving everything to "however conditions fall out," seeing no need to be close with distant relatives.
*Knowledge applied:* Proactively recognizing and upgrading social relationships.
*New action:* Proactively used the Gift of Smiles, the Gift of Words (asking after others), and the Gift of Heart (appreciation and gratitude) when meeting relatives to upgrade the relationship from "Acquaintance" to "Fondness."
*Result:* The extended family became bonded and harmonious. When the family faced a crisis, the whole extended family pitched in to help like true Noble Benefactors.
#### 2.6. 3 Organizational Application Examples
**Example 1: Leadership**
*Context:* The company recruited many skilled staff, but they worked disconnectedly with no cohesion, and the turnover rate was high.
*Old awareness:* The CEO used salary and bonuses to buy loyalty, viewing staff as tools for generating money.
*Knowledge applied:* People are a Bridge / a Path; Full Awareness of People.
*New action:* The CEO changed their mindset, valuing the presence of staff. Determined they were a "Path" walking alongside the company. Cared for the staff's ultimate growth (Wisdom, Mental State, Capability).
*Result:* Built a lasting Organization. Staff became absolutely loyal, viewing the company as their second home; revenue grew dramatically.
**Example 2: Organizational Culture**
*Context:* Departments within the company constantly blamed each other whenever a project failed.
*Old awareness:* The Marketing department saw the Sales department as the problem, and vice versa.
*Knowledge applied:* People are not the problem.
*New action:* The Leadership Board trained staff in inner-being knowledge, helping everyone understand "the root cause comes from me." Established a culture of recognition, gratitude, and acknowledging each other's achievements.
*Result:* Eliminated the culture of blame. The team coordinated smoothly, tolerating each other's mistakes to move toward the common goal together.
**Example 3: Human Development**
*Context:* An employee repeatedly made mistakes in the Accounting department; the manager was preparing to fire them.
*Old awareness:* Labeled the employee as useless, incompetent.
*Knowledge applied:* The 9 types of people (recognizing the Talented Person).
*New action:* The manager dropped the prejudice and talked with the employee to find out their true wishes. Discovered they had good communication skills and transferred them to the Customer Care department.
*Result:* The employee transformed, becoming the company's Best Seller (properly deploying the Talented Person's capacity to shoulder responsibility).
#### 2.7. 3 Social Application Examples
**Example 1: Community**
*Context:* Residents of an apartment complex were cold to each other; no one greeted anyone in the elevator.
*Old awareness:* Afraid of trouble, thinking "everyone minds their own business."
*Knowledge applied:* People are a machine of Merits and Virtues.
*New action:* One individual proactively smiled, greeted others, held the elevator, and helped elderly residents (proactively creating good affinity).
*Result:* Broke the indifference. The whole building became friendly and bonded, creating a civilized, safe living environment for children.
**Example 2: Humanistic Value (Volunteering)**
*Context:* Doing charity work but being criticized by the online community, suspected of embezzling funds, leading to resentment and a desire to quit.
*Old awareness:* Seeking gratitude from others. Seeing the online community as cruel.
*Knowledge applied:* The recipients help us accumulate blessings. (They are a machine of merits and virtues.)
*New action:* Recognized that giving is one's own opportunity, and society's judgment is a lesson for training Peace and Joy. Continued contributing without seeking fame.
*Result:* Inner being became settled; the charity project spread wider and stronger, overcoming all gossip and helping tens of thousands of people.
**Example 3: Social Responsibility (Sustainable Development)**
*Context:* A business discharged waste into the environment and was boycotted by residents.
*Old awareness:* The business only cared about profit, viewing residents as a force obstructing business.
*Knowledge applied:* The Four Great Debts of Gratitude (Self - Family - Organization - Society).
*New action:* The corporation's chairman awoke, understanding that fortune comes from society. Decided to invest in an internationally-standard waste treatment system and provided scholarships for local children.
*Result:* Shifted from the risk of bankruptcy to becoming the most beloved business in the region. Society's consensus helped the brand develop sustainably for hundreds of years.
