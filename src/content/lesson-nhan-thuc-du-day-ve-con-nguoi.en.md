# CURRICULUM: UNDERSTANDING INNER BEING – CREATING INNER PEACE

## TOPIC: FULL AWARENESS OF HUMAN BEINGS

---

## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** A cornerstone topic in understanding "Good Connections" in human life. It helps learners break prejudices, barriers, and resentment toward humans, establishing a beneficial source concept system to attract and preserve good connections.
- **Significance for learners:** Helps learners recognize that all achievements, suffering, or happiness in life (finance, health, career) are brought by humans. By mastering awareness of humans, we master our destiny.
- **Brought transformation:** Shifts state from "seeing humans as problems" (blaming, demanding others change) to "humans are capital, engines of merits and virtues." Learners acquire a state of Inner Peace, Tolerance, and Honouring & Gratitude.
- **Outcomes:** Learners comprehend 5 inner awarenesses of humans and the 9 types of people; realize the principle of "Shared Language - Shared Agreement - Shared Journey"; and apply the 5 levels of relationship upgrades.

### B. In-depth Content

In human life, humans are the center of karmic connections. We chase "Desirable Outcomes" (career, happiness, time freedom), forgetting that every sweet fruit must pass through the gate of "Connections"—and Connections are Humans. If we hold resentment or see humans as problems, we close our own gate of phước báu.

The topic **"Full Awareness of Human Beings"** is a revolution in perspective. Starting by defining "full awareness" (balanced-positive inner state + clear mental image + clear source concepts), it guides us to install 5 core awarenesses: Humans are Capital, Teachers, Bridges/Roads, Merits & Virtues Engines, and borrowing strength. Seeing everyone as a benefactor sparks Honouring & Gratitude.

Furthermore, sages transfer a strategic vision: identifying and treating the 9 types of people. The deepest message is not to *seek* them, but to cultivate Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, and Qualities to *become* the 9 types of people in 1. In doing so, we naturally become a "Good Connection" attracting social resources and achieving absolute alignment to realize dreams.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Full Awareness (Nhận thức đủ đầy):** Mentioning a subject evokes a balanced-positive inner state, a clear mental image, and clear beneficial source concepts.
- **Shared Language - Shared Agreement - Shared Journey (Đồng ngôn - Đồng thuận - Đồng hành):**
  - **Shared Language (Đồng ngôn):** Sharing the same reference systems and source concepts.
  - **Shared Agreement (Đồng thuận):** Aligning energy vibrations toward the same goal.
  - **Shared Journey (Đồng hành):** Gathering into physical form, working and staying together long-term.
- **9 Types of People:** Sages, Benefactors, Connectors, Gods of Wealth, Talents, Mentors, Teachers, Experts, and Virtuous Knowers.

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

- **5 Inner Awarenesses of Human Beings:**
  1. **Humans are Capital (Con người là Vốn):** Capital is not just money; it is assets, wisdom, technology, and especially the Total Karma warehouse (Wisdom - Connections - Fruits).
  2. **Humans are Bridges or Roads (Cây cầu hoặc Con đường):** As bridges, they connect us to other relationships; as roads, they walk with us to the destination.
  3. **Humans are Merits & Virtues Engines (Cỗ máy Công đức - Phước đức):** Their presence gives us opportunities to create value and accumulate positive electricity.
  4. **Humans are Teachers (Con người là Thầy):** Bringing lessons for our maturity in all circumstances.
  5. **Leveraging and Supporting (Mượn sức trợ lực):** One's achievements are brought by others; leveraging strength makes work simple and light.
- **5 Levels of Social Relationships:** Acquaintance $\rightarrow$ Familiarity $\rightarrow$ Appreciation $\rightarrow$ Trust $\rightarrow$ Intimacy. Proactively use the 7 important offerings to upgrade relationships instead of waiting.

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **Breaking "People have Problems":** In adversity, we demand others change. Sages establish: *"Humans have no problems."* Suffering comes from our own mental images. Taking responsibility and using Tolerance dissolves problems.
- **Demystifying Blind "Shared Journey":** Demanding family or staff "walk along" without foundations fails. Sages point out: *"A shared journey originates from shared language and agreement."* Leaders provide concepts and images for agreement first.
- **Becoming the 9 Types in 1:** Stop seeking benefactors; strive to become the 9 types of people in 1. Act as a connector, sage, or supporter for others to become a magnet for good connections.

### 2.4. Applications (Personal, Family, Organizational, Social)

#### Personal Application
- **Work (Leveraging Strength):** When starting a business, instead of micro-managing, view colleagues' contributions as "capital" from total karma. Delegating and trusting simplifies work.
- **Inner Peace (Ceasing Blame):** When cheated of money, recognize the cause is your greed and lack of financial concept. View the person as a "Teacher" of finance, practicing Tolerance to restore inner peace.

#### Family Application
- **Spousal Harmony:** When a spouse objects to a business plan, stop forcing a shared journey. Share future images and provide safe financial concepts (Shared Language) to achieve Shared Agreement.
- **Parenting:** Stop viewing children as burdens. View supporting them as an opportunity to practice patience and Tolerance (accumulating merits and virtues).

#### Organizational Application
- **Leadership:** Care for staff's ultimate maturity (Wisdom, Mental State) instead of treating them as tools. They walk with you as "Roads," ensuring organizational longevity.
- **Culture:** Stop departments from blaming each other. Cultivate "cội nguồn từ tôi" and build appreciation to dissolve conflicts.

#### Social Impact
- **Community:** Proactively smile, greet, and support neighbors (making engines of merits). It breaks coldness and builds a safe living environment.
- **Philanthropy:** Understand that receiving help is a gift to the giver to accumulate phước báu. Continue dedication without seeking praise, maintaining an undisturbed mind.
