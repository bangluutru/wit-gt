**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**THE PHILOSOPHY OF EDUCATION AT THE ROOT – THE SEED OF ETERNAL LIFE**

**1. The Search for an Anchor:**
Throughout history, humans have searched for the true meaning of life, often chasing material targets like fame, wealth, or status. However, when these vanish, people feel empty and insecure because they lack a solid "philosophy of life" as an anchor for their soul. Without philosophy, we are like ships floating without a compass, easily capsized by storms.

**2. The Four Pillars of the Philosophy:**
To address this aimlessness, the **Philosophy of Education at the Root (Triết lý Giáo dục Tận gốc)** serves as a guiding sun, built on 4 solid pillars:
* **Purpose:** To elevate inner awareness and wisdom level.
* **Orientation:** To guide human beings to the ultimate maturity—**7 Dimensions of Holistic Wealth**.
* **Energy:** Nurtured by the pure energy of **Joy, Tolerance, and Gratitude**.
* **Results:** A life that is **simpler, happier, trusting, and lighter**.

**3. The 11 Compass Guides of Life:**
To keep the philosophy active, practitioners align with the 11 declarations:
* *Intent:* Take helping others, helping others be happy as intent.
* *Mission:* Take spreading the Philosophy of Education at the Root as mission.
* *Passion:* Take the transformation and growth of human beings as passion.
* *Motivation:* Take the Four Gratitudes (Self, Family, Organization, Society) as motivation.
* *Goal:* Take building eternal organizations / families as goal.
* *Action:* Take the Seven Important Offerings (Bảy bố thí) as action.
* *Mental State:* Take Joy, Tolerance, Gratitude as mental state.
* *Principle:* Take alignment with the Three Realms (Tam giác giới) as principle.
* *Treasure:* Take Appretell, Coordination, Lead as treasures.
* *Desire:* Take holistic wealth and mastery of all life aspects as desire.
* *Foundation:* Take the Philosophy of Education at the Root as foundation.
