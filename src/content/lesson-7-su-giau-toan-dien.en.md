## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** Provides the ultimate direction of growth, helping learners escape the cycle of solving life problems by lighting the 7 inner lamps simultaneously. It provides the mindset of "Materializing" and "Non-materializing" to elevate the value of self and organizations infinitely.
- **Significance for learners:** Helps learners stop chasing only material things, money, and capacity. It gives them the tools to make invisible values (happiness, peace, wisdom) visible and possessable, while making their physical values priceless in the eyes of others.
- **Brought transformation:** Shifts completely from "coping with circumstances" to "proactively constructing the 7 dimensions of holistic wealth." Shifts from being valued finitely by society to self-valuing as priceless through non-materializing.
- **Outcomes:**
  - *Understand:* The structure of the 7 lamps of holistic wealth (material and non-material groups).
  - *Realise:* The danger of lighting only a single lamp; understand the cross-illumination principle and the multiplying nature of non-material elements.
  - *Apply:* Master "non-materializing oneself" and "materializing invisible values" in life, marriage, parenting, and business.

### B. In-depth Content

Human life from ancient times has revolved around seeking self-value, chasing happiness, and trying to solve problems. However, the greatest tragedy of modern society is that we value ourselves and others based on extremely limited "Material" measures. We box a person's value inside the money they earn, the degrees they own, their work capacity, or physical strength. When bound to these physical measures, human value becomes fragile, easily broken, easily discarded, and easily replaced by technology or time.

To break these boundaries and stand above life problems, the inner being curriculum hands down a great philosophy: **The ultimate destination of growth is not accumulating material assets or dealing with darkness, but directing toward the 7 Dimensions of Holistic Wealth.** Alongside this, mastering the art of **"Materializing the non-material"** and **"Non-materializing the material"** is the ultimate key to make a life priceless and eternal.

The diagram of the 7 dimensions of holistic wealth is pictured as seven sacred lamps to be lit in each person's "House of Life." These seven lamps are divided into two core groups:
1. **The Non-material foundation (invisible):** Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, and Qualities. These shape our destiny.
2. **The Material expression (visible):** Capacity, Physical Body, and Material Assets.

A natural law points out that: *Under any lamp, no matter how bright, there is always a shadow directly beneath it.* This explains a sad paradox: some people have a brilliant lamp of Material Assets, but beneath it is the darkness of inner anxiety, family breakdown, or physical exhaustion. Others strain to light Capacity and Physical Body, but beneath them remains the shadow of poverty and financial block.

Because of this truth, we must thắp sáng all seven lamps simultaneously to create the **Cross-illumination Principle**. When all seven lamps shine together, Wisdom lights up the corner of Mental State, Personality shines for Qualities, and Capacity guides the way for Material Assets. This multi-dimensional light eliminates all shadows, leaving no room for problems to root. Prioritizing the four Non-material lamps builds a foundation as solid as a rock. When the inner world is filled with Wisdom and Inner Peace, the external capacity, physical body, and material assets gather sustainably and naturally.

From this structure, we touch the peak art of humanity: **The Art of Non-materializing**. The physical world is limited and decays over time. A handbag or product sold purely for its physical utility has low value and is easily replaced. But when a creator "Non-materializes" it—breathing in brand stories, philosophy, artisan love, or cultural depth—its value breaks physical limits, multiplying thousands of times. People no longer buy a physical object; they pay to possess a story, an emotion, a spiritual value.

The greatest application is **"Non-materializing oneself."** Society values us based on hours worked, products made, or degrees owned. Accepting this game makes us replaceable gears.Sages establish: *Do not let society value you with limited labor or degrees. Reclaim the right to value yourself by cultivating Wisdom, Inner Peace, Tolerance, Honouring & Gratitude, Personality, and Qualities.* A person who non-materializes themselves holds a natural magnet, making anyone near feel warm, safe, and connected. Their value becomes priceless.

Conversely, we step into **"Materializing the non-material."** Humans long for Happiness, Inner Peace, or Wisdom, but box them as abstract, floating concepts depending on luck. Sages provide a philosophical lens: *"What is invisible in the eyes of others becomes visible in our awareness."* We materialize them by defining and clarifying beneficial source concepts. When Inner Peace or Happiness is defined with core information, energy, and physical behaviors, we can easily possess and package them to transfer to others. You cannot transfer something invisible, but once you materialize peace, you can pass it to your children, spouse, or team. This capacity to package and transfer reality is the peak competence of an inner coach.

Finally, there is the **Mathematics of Sharing**. For physical matter (money, food), the more we share, the less we have. But for non-material elements (Wisdom, Mental State, Personality), **the more we share, the more they multiply**. When we share gratitude, peace, or wisdom, that energy within us does not decrease; it resonates and grows thousands of times, protecting our life and creating a cỗ máy of merits and virtues.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **7 Dimensions of Holistic Wealth (7 Sự giàu toàn diện):** Bảy ngọn đèn to be lit in the House of Life to dissolve all problems.
  - *Non-material Group (Invisible):* Wealth of Wisdom, Wealth of Mental State, Wealth of Personality, Wealth of Qualities.
  - *Material Group (Visible):* Wealth of Capacity, Wealth of Physical Body, Wealth of Material Assets.
- **Materializing the Non-material (Vật chất hóa phi vật chất):** Clarifying, defining, and quantifying invisible inner values (Peace, Happiness, Tolerance, Wisdom) to make them visible, possessable, and transferable.
- **Non-materializing the Material (Phi vật chất hóa vật chất):** Infusing non-material elements (love, history, meaning, culture) into physical objects/persons to multiply their value infinitely.
- **Non-materializing Oneself (Phi vật chất hóa bản thân):** Cultivating Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, and Qualities to elevate self-value beyond finite labor or degrees.

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

- **Priority in Wealth:** Accumulating only money is fragile. Accumulating Non-material wealth (Wisdom, Mental State) builds the foundation. When the inner world is filled, material wealth gathers automatically.
- **Cross-illumination Principle:** Lighting a single lamp leaves a shadow beneath it. We must light all seven lamps together to eliminate all dark corners of life problems.
- **Miracle of Sharing:** Material diminishes when shared; non-material multiplies when shared.

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **Do not let society value you with Material:** Reclaim the right to value yourself by non-materializing yourself.
- **From Invisible to Visible:** *"What is invisible in the eyes of others becomes visible in our awareness"* through beneficial source concepts.
- **Business is Building People:** True entrepreneurs do not just non-materialize products; they non-materialize themselves and build people (7 dimensions of holistic wealth), and those people build the business.

### 2.4. Personal Application

I used to chase money, defining myself by income and capacity, which led to exhaustion and health erosion (building a house from the roof). Since understanding the 7 dimensions of holistic wealth, I shifted my focus: I return to non-materializing myself. Daily, I study Lessons - Impressions - Realisations to enrich Wisdom, and maintain Peace, Tolerance, and Gratitude. As my inner value grows, financial opportunities and quality relationships are attracted naturally and thong dong.

### 2.5. Social Impact

- **In Parenting:** Investing only in capacity (degrees) and physical body leaves the child vulnerable to low valuations. Infusing gratitude, qualities, and personality (non-materializing) makes the child a precious talent anywhere.
- **In Business:** Selling products purely physically yields low margins. Non-materializing products with brand stories and artisan dedication multiplies their value sustainably.
