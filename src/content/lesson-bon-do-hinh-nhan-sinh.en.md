**FOUR DIAGRAMS OF HUMAN LIFE – Blueprints for Eternity**

**INTRODUCTION – THE GRAND BLUEPRINT**
In human training and development, the greatest barrier to replicating talents is the lack of a comprehensive blueprint. The Philosophy of Education at the Root resolves this by systemizing the **Four Diagrams of Human Life**, allowing us to "conceptualize and visualize" the entire training model.

* **Diagram 1 (Enrich - Master - Become - Transform):** Establishing the destination, playing the role of "anchoring desires" (cố định tham tưởng). When a person anchors their desires towards the 7 dimensions of holistic wealth, mastering 4 aspects of life, becoming wise, and transforming into 9 types of people in 1, they never lose direction. This is the lighthouse gathering the shared language, agreement, and accompaniment of thousands.
* **Diagram 2 (Realities of Human Life / Hiện thực nhân sinh):** Building the inner foundation. It requires "possessing realizations," starting from the core of Truth (Hearing-Seeing-Speaking-Knowing), thấu suốt the 3 standard reference frameworks, accumulating the 3 blocks of merits/virtues, and enriching non-material elements to manifest Dream Career, Dream Life, and Realm of Life.
* **Diagram 3 (Comprehension of Human Life / Thấu suốt nhân sinh):** Serving others. By mastering the house of human life with 4 aspects (Inner self, Health, Relations, Finance) via the 15 Source Concepts, we possess the capacity of a true expert.
* **Diagram 4 (Portrait of Cultural Entrepreneur - Omnipotent Leader):** The external manifestation. This is the set of 16 recognition factors (Scholar's Wisdom, Joyful Mental State, Elite Quality... to Virtue conducting the world and Sixfold Life Fulfillment).
Applying these 4 diagrams allows us to build business ecosystems, nurturing generations of Omnipotent Leaders.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
**2.1. Core Concepts**
* **Diagram 1 (Destination Diagram): Enrich - Master - Become - Transform.**
  * *Definition:* The roadmap establishing the ultimate destination of human life. **Enrich** (7 dimensions of wealth) ➔ **Master** (4 aspects: Inner self, Health, Relations, Finance) ➔ **Become** (wiser, more intelligent, more scholarly) ➔ **Transform** (becoming 9 types of people in 1: Mentor, Benefactor, Expert, etc.).
  * *Significance:* Anchoring desires to gather talents.
* **Diagram 2 (Foundational Diagram): Realities of Human Life.**
  * *Definition:* Concentric circles describing the inner world. Core is Truth (Hearing-Seeing-Speaking-Knowing), wrapped by the 3 frameworks, 3 blocks of merits/virtues, 7 dimensions of wealth, and outer rings (Dream Career, Dream Life, Realm of Life).
  * *Significance:* Requires "Possessing Realizations" (làm cho mình) to protect inner peace.
* **Diagram 3 (Expertise Diagram): Comprehension of Human Life.**
  * *Definition:* Drawn as a house with Inner self as foundation, Health and Relations as pillars, Finance as roof, operated by **15 Source Concepts**.
  * *Significance:* Requires "Comprehension" to serve and help others.
* **Diagram 4 (Manifestation Diagram): Portrait of Cultural Entrepreneur - Omnipotent Leader.**
  * *Definition:* The 16 factors phô diễn to society: Scholar's Wisdom, Joyful Mental State, Perfect Personality, Elite Qualities, Outstanding Capacity, Model Body, Iron Health, Superb Appretell, Wise Communication, Inner Iron Law, Clear Vision, Comprehending Life, Benevolent Leadership, Virtue conducting the world, Sixfold Life Fulfillment, and Omnipotent Leadership / Cultural Entrepreneurship.
  * *Significance:* Creating invisible attraction for social agreement.

**2.2. Key Knowledge**
* **Nurturing Sequence:** Start by **Anchoring desires (Diagram 1)**, then help them **Possess Realizations (Diagram 2)**, followed by providing 15 concepts for **Comprehension (Diagram 3)**, and finally polishing traits for **Manifestation (Diagram 4)**.
* **4-Step Maturity Roadmap:**
  1. **Awareness:** Having images and balanced positive inner state.
  2. **Management:** Having clear images/concepts and aligning energy frequencies.
  3. **Training:** Helping others clarify images and concepts.
  4. **Leadership:** Training omnipotent trainers.

**2.3. Key Awarenesss**
* **Insight 1: Anchoring Desires.** Why do companions drift apart? Because their desires (tham tưởng) changed. Desires determine reincarnation and assembly. Diagram 1 anchors desires to keep organizations eternal.
* **Insight 2: "Lifting the bottle, lifting the cup."** Do not ask students to empty their cups. Use appreciation and appretell to show the benefit picture, lifting their cups naturally to receive light.
* **Insight 3: Hide the inner, reveal the outer.** Keep Diagram 1 and 2 (inner wealth) hidden. Reveal Diagram 4 (16 portraits) to attract natural social agreement.
