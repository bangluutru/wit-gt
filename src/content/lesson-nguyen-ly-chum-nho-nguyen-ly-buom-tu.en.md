## TOPIC: THE PRINCIPLE OF THE GRAPE BUNCH - THE PRINCIPLE OF THE GATHERING BUTTERFLIES IN HUMAN LIFE

### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

**A. Topic Objectives**

**Value of the topic:** This topic provides the core principle in the art of "gathering people," building a team, and developing an organization. It helps learners decode the secret of how to naturally attract talent (Gathering Butterflies) and how to elevate an entire system in the gentlest way possible (the Grape Bunch).

**Significance for learners:** It helps learners immediately stop exhausting themselves "hunting" for people or micro-managing each individual. It equips them with the mindset of an all-around leader: using inherent self-worth to attract, and using the synchronization of awareness to elevate the collective.

**Transformation brought by the topic:** A shift from a state of "pursuing, dragging people along" to "self-cultivation, radiating light." Learners realize that: to lift an entire group, you only need to lift the right "grape stem" (the leader/the core philosophy), instead of trying to hold onto every single grape.

**Output results:** Skillful application of the 8 attraction factors to create a "fragrant flower garden" within oneself. A thorough comprehension of the formula "Ten thousand people share the same language, a thousand people share the same consensus, a hundred people walk side by side" in order to build an organization that lasts forever.

**B. In-Depth Content: The Principle of the Grape Bunch - The Principle of Gathering Butterflies in Human Life**

On the journey of building a harmonious family, a business that lasts forever, or a great community, the greatest barrier people usually encounter is exhaustion from searching for and managing people. We chase after personnel, drag customers along, and strive to control every individual in the organization. That exhaustion comes from the fact that we are going against natural law. The inner-being reference framework offers two principles that completely free us from this stagnation: **the Principle of Gathering Butterflies and the Principle of the Grape Bunch**.

**The Principle of Gathering Butterflies** originates from a profound folk philosophy: *"Do not carry a net to go catch butterflies; spend your time planting a flower garden instead. When the flowers bloom and their fragrance spreads, the butterflies and bees will naturally come to you."* In human life, the "butterfly" is precisely a person, a talent, a good opportunity. Chasing after and dragging people along (managing their behavior) will only bring resistance and departure. Instead, an inner-being expert focuses on creating distinction and distance in themselves through 8 factors: Health, Beauty, Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Character, Capacity, and Realm of Life. When you light up the 7 forms of holistic wealth within yourself, you become a giant magnet. Every resource and talent will automatically gather because of the similarity in vibrational frequency of energy.

However, attracting people is only the starting step. How can we manage and elevate an organization of tens of thousands of people without burning out? That is when **the Principle of the Grape Bunch** comes into play.

Picture yourself wanting to lift a bunch of grapes off a table. If you try to pick up each individual grape, they will scatter and get crushed. The only intelligent way is to grasp the "grape stem" and lift it, and the entire hundreds of grapes will automatically be lifted along through their natural connection. In an organization, the "grape stem" is precisely the person at the head, the educational philosophy, the standard reference framework, and the core awareness. Instead of going to solve the problems of each individual subordinate, a leader only needs to "promote the person at the top of the grape bunch," synchronizing the awareness of the elite tier, and from there the light automatically spreads down through the entire system.

The combination of Gathering Butterflies and the Grape Bunch is precisely the pinnacle of managing cause and connection. Plant flowers to attract talent (Shared Language), and lift the grape stem to guide the organization (Shared Consensus, Walking Side by Side). Once you grasp these two principles, building an organization of tens of thousands of people suddenly becomes leisurely, simple, joyful, trusting, and gentler than ever before.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

**2.1. Concepts (20%)**

**The Principle of Gathering Butterflies (The Law of Attraction):** This is the principle showing that instead of using energy to pursue and drag people along, we focus on creating inherent self-worth (distinction, distance) in order to naturally attract people, resources, and connections that share a similar vibrational frequency of energy.

**The Principle of the Grape Bunch (The Law of Gathering People & Synchronization):** This is the principle of managing and guiding an organization by focusing one's effort on the core nucleus (the "grape stem" – the leader/the root philosophy), thereby indirectly elevating the entire system through the organization's invisible connecting thread, instead of handling each department separately.

**2.2. Key Knowledge (30%)**

**1. The 8 Attraction Factors (Planting the Flower Garden):** To achieve "Gathering Butterflies," a person needs to create distinction and distance in a positive direction compared to society. The reference framework points out 8 ultimate attraction factors, comprising:

External factors: *Health, Beauty.*

Internal factors (Non-material): *Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Character.*

Action & outcome factors: *Capacity, Realm of Life.* When you possess these things, tens of thousands of people will dream of coming close to and walking alongside you.

**2. Lifting the "Grape Stem" – Synchronizing a Business's Awareness:** If you want to change a company of thousands of people, do not go and fix each employee. Instead, "lift the grape stem" by acting directly on the Owner (the person at the head). Elevate the leader's awareness, synchronizing their awareness of Inner Being – Health – Relationships – Finance from top to bottom. When the person at the head thoroughly comprehends the philosophy, the entire system automatically changes.

**3. The Formula for the Connecting Thread (Shared Language – Shared Consensus – Walking Side by Side):** A bunch of grapes can only be lifted when the grapes are firmly attached to the stem. The thread that binds people together is precisely:

*Shared Language:* Sharing the same reference framework, the same inner-being vocabulary, the same destination.

*Shared Consensus:* Similarity in terms of energy (Cherishing Gratitude, Tolerance, Peaceful Joy).

*Walking Side by Side:* Similarity in terms of the material (acting together). Ten thousand people share the same language, a thousand people share the same consensus, a hundred people walk side by side — that is the formula for building an organization that lasts forever.

**2.3. Key Awareness (30%)**

**Establishing Awareness 1 – Do not interfere; instead, radiate light:** People commonly make the mistake of always wanting others to change (spouse/children/employees). The Principle of Gathering Butterflies cuts off that expectation entirely. The key is to stop trying to change other people, and return to cultivating yourself into a "flower garden." The world around you will automatically change to match you.

**Establishing Awareness 2 – Promote the person at the top of the grape bunch:** The Teacher has established: do not use the "4 gatherings," use the Principle of the Grape Bunch instead. Promote the person at the top of the grape bunch. Protect the merit and honor the guide, honor the Philosophy of Education from the Root. When the philosophy (the grape stem) is elevated, everyone who follows that philosophy is automatically elevated in value within society.

**Establishing Awareness 3 – The rain always falls, but only a tree with roots can absorb it:** During the process of attracting people, do not become attached to the question of why the flower has bloomed yet this particular butterfly has not come. You must understand the principle: a person's transformation depends on their own total accumulated karma and their own merits and virtues. We are merely a connecting condition. Maintain a state of Peaceful Joy to gather people, without forcing or coercing them.

**2.4. Three Examples of Practical Application (10%)**

**Example 1: Family (Educating Children Using Gathering Butterflies)**

*Context:* Parents force their child to study and yell at them to keep them in line (carrying a net to catch butterflies). The child becomes exhausted and rebels.

*Application:* Apply the Principle of Gathering Butterflies. The parents themselves enrich their Wisdom and Mental State, keeping the family harmonious and at peace. When the parents become a shining example (a flower garden), the child automatically "gathers" around them, admiring them, listening to them, and naturally following their example.

**Example 2: Organization (Rescuing a Business Using the Grape Bunch)**

*Context:* A business of 1,000 employees is falling apart. HR management goes department by department resolving conflicts, firing people, and constantly punishing mistakes (picking up each fallen grape), and the system is exhausted.

*Application:* A consultant applies the Principle of the Grape Bunch. They invite the Board of Directors (the grape stem) to study and elevate their inner-being awareness. Once the Board shares the same language about the philosophy and holds a mental state of Peaceful Joy and Tolerance, they radiate energy down through the ranks. The system automatically re-establishes order without needing piecemeal intervention.

**Example 3: Society (Attracting Talent)**

*Context:* A startup project spends a great deal of money on headhunters to drag experts in, but they work for a few months and then quit because they do not fit the culture.

*Application:* The founder applies Gathering Butterflies. They cultivate themselves to reach Realm of Life level 6 or 7. They become a person of complete Personality and outstanding Character. This very distinction automatically attracts talented people (people of wisdom and knowledge, personal connections, benefactors) to voluntarily come and contribute, carrying the burden together, without needing money to drag them in.
