### B. In-depth Content

For generations, the greatest human desire has been to change life realities for a better future. We constantly strive for physical strength, abundance of finance, or family harmony. However, most people remain stuck in frustration due to an old habit: rushing outward to seek and shape the physical world. This is like plucked sour fruits off a tree hoping it will grow sweet fruits next season, while forgetting that the root lies deep underground.

From the perspective of quantum physics and foundational knowledge, every existing matter in this world contains energy and holds an internal information structure. Gold and lead differ physically not by chance, but because their deep information structures are completely different. Different information structures lead to different emitted energy, manifesting in different material states. Theoretically, if we change the internal information structure of lead to match that of gold, lead can turn into gold. This principle opens an enlightening gate for humanity: to change external reality, we do not need to alter the surface, we only need to change the internal information structure.

A person's life is also a material manifestation. The difference between a suffering person and a happy person is not decided by fate, but by their information structure and energy state. To decode this information structure, sages pointed us to a profound diagram: **The Reality Triangle**, representing the Moral perspective. This triangle consists of 3 corners: **Information**, **Energy**, and **Matter**, corresponding to what we **Know**, **Believe**, and **Understand**. From this diagram, a ground-breaking definition emerged: *"Information inside us is what we Know, what we Believe, what we Understand, representing the Information inside us, representing our life reality."*

This solution holds the power to shatter illusions. It asserts an absolute operating principle: when mentioning anything in life, how you **Know** about it, how you **Believe** about it, and how you **Understand** about it represents your reality of it. The concept that encompasses the knowing, believing, and understanding is called the **Source Concept**. If you hold negative source concepts about marriage, your marriage will break. If you hold beneficial source concepts about money, prosperity will naturally manifest. Shifting life reality becomes extremely simple: a process of purifying your own operating system by accumulating **Standard Reference Systems** (the source concepts of source concepts).

The peak of the Reality Triangle is not just changing matter, but allowing us to **"Materialize the non-material."** Abstract values like Happiness, Inner Peace, or Success are hard to hold. But when we put them into the Reality Triangle, defining clearly what the Information is, what the Energy is, and what the Matter is, these invisible concepts immediately become visible in our awareness. We can then easily possess them and, more importantly, **"Receive and Transfer Reality"** to anyone. A person's life becomes truly free when they realise: simply master the internal Reality Triangle, and everything external will naturally fall into place.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **1. The Reality Triangle (Tam giác hiện thực):** The reference system representing the Moral perspective, showing that all realities are formed by 3 corners: Information (Knowing) - Energy (Believing) - Matter (Understanding).
- **2. Source Concept (Khái niệm nguồn):** Information inside us is what we Know, what we Believe, what we Understand, representing the Information inside us, representing our life reality. It acts as the "software operating system" of a person. Shifting it to beneficial concepts automatically changes life reality.
- **3. Standard Reference System (Hệ quy chiếu chuẩn):** The source concept of source concepts; the foundational concepts helping us develop beneficial source concepts. The three standard reference systems governing human life are: The Formula for the Root Cause of Life (Science), Human Structure (Religion/Belief), and The Reality Triangle (Morality).

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

#### 1. Correlation between Knowing - Believing - Understanding & Information - Energy - Matter
- **Moral Angle (Information):** What we **Know** forms our internal information system.
- **Religious Angle (Energy):** What we **Believe** creates the corresponding energy vibration frequency.
- **Scientific Angle (Matter):** What we **Understand** decides the material manifestation.
Any reality must converge all 3 corners to manifest.

#### 2. The Reality Triangle of "Good Cause" (Nhân tốt)
To create a desirable life, we must plant a "Good Cause." Under the Reality Triangle, a Good Cause consists of:
- **Information:** Beneficial source concepts.
- **Energy:** Merit and Virtue (Công đức, Phước đức).
- **Matter:** Beneficial mental images.
Enriching these 3 corners manages the root cause of life.

#### 3. The Art of Materializing the Non-Material
We use the Reality Triangle to turn invisible things visible. For example, the Reality Triangle of "Success":
- *Information:* Achieving what one desires.
- *Energy:* Others like being near us.
- *Matter:* Life Realm Level 4 (Career + Growth).
Once materialized, one can easily transfer the reality of "Success" to others by providing the Information and aligning the Energy.

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **Cognitive Shift:** *"When mentioning anything, how we Know, Believe, and Understand about it represents our reality of it."* This removes external blame. Wealth status is not decided by the economy, but by your source concept of "Money" (knowing, believing, or understanding wrongly).
- **The Human Paradox:** People spend their lives chasing "Matter" (the fruit) but ignore enriching "Information" and "Energy" (the root). Shifting thinking means: stop trying to change external matter; purify and install "Beneficial Source Concepts" within.
- **Transferring Reality instead of Theory:** Peak training is not stuffing knowledge, but "Transferring Reality." When you transfer enough Information and Energy of Tolerance or Inner Peace, the Material manifestation of Tolerance and Inner Peace will naturally grow within them.
