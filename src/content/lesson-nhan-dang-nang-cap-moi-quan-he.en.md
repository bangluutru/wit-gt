## TOPIC: IDENTIFYING AND UPGRADING RELATIONSHIPS

---

## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** Provides a reference system and a precise yardstick to evaluate the current state of social relationships. It breaks the painful illusion of "role attachment / social labeling" (dính mắc nhân xưng) that humans struggle with.
- **Significance for learners:** Helps learners stop demanding trust or intimacy based solely on titles like marriage, blood ties, or positions. It equips them with tools to proactively upgrade any relationship at will.
- **Brought transformation:** Shifts perspective from a passive stance (complaining about others being cold) to a proactive stance of creating joy, trust, and value. Shifts from passive "tùy duyên" to proactively mastering connections.
- **Outcomes:** Learners become proficient in identifying the 5 levels of social relationships. They master the keys of "proactivity" and "value creation" to guide those around them into shared language, agreement, and journey.

### B. In-depth Content

In human life, we establish a foundation: *"Humans are capital, bridges, and roads."* Yet, a painful paradox occurs: many live surrounded by family and organizations but feel lonely, hurt, and resentful.

The teaching starts by decoding the root of this suffering: the **Illusion of Social Labeling (Lầm tưởng về nhân xưng)**. Humanity attaches heavily to labels like "Husband-Wife," "Parent-Child," "Boss-Employee," or "Siblings." We assume that being husband and wife automatically means "intimacy"; being parent and child automatically means "trust" and mutual understanding.

In reality, some couples share a bed for decades but remain at the level of "knowing lots of info about each other" (familiarity), defending against each other when trouble strikes. This illusion of labeling breeds unreasonable expectations, demanding the other person treat us at the highest level. When unmet, resentment immediately ignires.

To break this cycle of pain, sages establish a revolutionary awareness: **Set aside all social labels and measure relationships purely based on two indicators: "Information" and "Feeling."**

Strip away the labels, and all relationships fall into 5 distinct levels:
1. **Acquaintance (Quen biết):** Knowing **1 piece of information** about the person (e.g., name or job).
2. **Familiarity (Quen thuộc):** Knowing **multiple pieces of information** about them (many colleagues, partners, or even spouses are stuck here).
3. **Appreciation (Quý mến):** Having a **feeling of joy** when being near them.
4. **Trust (Tin tưởng):** Having a **feeling of safety** when being near them.
5. **Intimacy (Thân thiết):** Possessing **comprehensive trust** between both sides.

Once we locate the true level via "Feeling," our mind is freed. We realize: *"Ah, my child and I are only at the Familiarity level. The child does not feel safe (Trust) near me, so keeping secrets is natural."* See the truth to stop demanding, stop blaming, and start acting.

How do we upgrade? The master key is: **PROACTIVITY (CHỦ ĐỘNG)**.
Many use "Tùy duyên" (allowing nature to take its course) to excuse laziness and passivity. Sages point out: *"Tùy duyên is only used when the other person rejects the connection; once the opportunity connects, we must fix our desire and be PROACTIVE."*
- **Acquaintance $\rightarrow$ Familiarity:** Proactively increase contact methods, meeting regularly.
- **Familiarity $\rightarrow$ Appreciation:** Humans often wait for others to make them happy to appreciate them. The correct principle is: **Proactively bring joy**, connecting on a shared frequency.
- **Appreciation $\rightarrow$ Trust:** Joy is only the launchpad. For absolute safety, we must **Proactively trust and create value**. Relationships without value creation (helping, dedicating, bringing benefits) remain superficial. Value creation is the application of the 7 important offerings.

To guide a relationship from "Trust" to "Intimacy" (comprehensive trust), the key action is **Proactively aligning goals**. Sages quantify absolute intimacy using the **"11 Shared Elements" (11 chữ Cùng)**:
*Shared foundation, shared desire, shared magic treasure, shared principle, shared mental state, shared action, shared goal, shared motivation, shared passion, shared mission, and shared conviction.*

The moment these 11 Shared Elements are established, two independent individuals unite, achieving: **Shared Language** (common inner language), **Shared Agreement** (aligned energy frequencies), and **Shared Journey** (common mental images and destination). This is the highest realm to retain talents and build enduring organizations.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Acquaintance (Quen biết):** Knowing **1 piece of info** about the person.
- **Familiarity (Quen thuộc):** Knowing **multiple pieces of info** about them.
- **Appreciation (Quý mến):** Having a **feeling of joy** when being near them.
- **Trust (Tin tưởng):** Having a **feeling of safety** when being near them.
- **Intimacy (Thân thiết):** Having **comprehensive trust** between both sides.

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

- **Illusion of Social Labeling:** Titles do not represent relationship levels. Evaluate relationships via "Feeling" (joy, safety) and "Information" (1 or many).
- **Upgrading Relationships:**
  - *Acquaintance $\rightarrow$ Familiarity:* Frequent contact, meeting regularly.
  - *Familiarity $\rightarrow$ Appreciation:* **Proactive joyfulness**, frequency alignment.
  - *Appreciation $\rightarrow$ Trust:* **Proactive trust, value creation**.
  - *Trust $\rightarrow$ Intimacy:* **Comprehensive trust**, aligning goals.
- **11 Shared Elements (11 chữ Cùng):** Shared foundation, desire, magic treasure, principle, mental state, action, goal, motivation, passion, mission, and conviction. These lead to Shared Language, Agreement, and Journey.

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **Dissolve Role Attachment to Cease Blame:** Intimacy does not spring from a marriage certificate or birth certificate. Couples living together with mutual suspicion are only at "Familiarity." See this to stop demanding.
- **Power of Proactivity:** Tùy duyên does not mean giving up. It is only used when connections are rejected. When the opportunity arises, be **Proactive**.
- **Value Creation is the Glue:** To step into "Trust," there must be continuous value creation.

### 2.4. Applications (Personal, Family, Organizational, Social)

#### Personal Application
- **Overcoming Isolation at Work:** Stop waiting for colleagues to approach. Smile, greet with energy, and proactively bring small joys (Familiarity $\rightarrow$ Appreciation).
- **Receiving a Mentor:** Instead of maintaining distance, volunteer to carry tasks for the mentor, helping spread their philosophy (Appreciation $\rightarrow$ Trust $\rightarrow$ Intimacy).

#### Family Application
- **Rejuvenating Spousal Relationship:** Stop assuming marriage guarantees intimacy. Proactively delegate finance management to the spouse (Proactive Trust) and help with chores. Align family goals using the 11 Shared Elements.
- **Parent-Child Generation Gap:** Realize the child lacks safety near you. Step back to "Proactive joyfulness," creating fun family moments instead of interrogations. The child will open up.

#### Organizational Application
- **Leadership and Retention:** Avoid micromanagement. Proactively trust staff by delegating decisions, and create value through career paths. Staff feel safe and dedicate beyond expectations.
- **Silo Effect Solution:** Align marketing and sales departments with the 11 Shared Elements (common goal, vision). Request cross-value creation.

#### Social Impact
- **Community Connection:** Proactively create community groups and share small gifts. Neighbors upgrade from Familiarity to Appreciation, creating a safe neighborhood.
- **Spreading Education Philosophy:** Stop waiting. Share the 11 Shared Elements with educators, creating value unconditionally to align thousands of experts.
