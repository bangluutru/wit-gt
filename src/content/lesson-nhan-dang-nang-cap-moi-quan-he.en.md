### CURRICULUM: ELEVATING INNER AWARENESS
#### TOPIC: IDENTIFYING AND UPGRADING RELATIONSHIPS

### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION (GENERAL INFORMATION)
**A. Topic Objectives**
**Value of the topic:** Provides an accurate reference system and standard of measurement to correctly assess the current state of social relationships. Breaks the painful misconception about "titles" (forms of address) that people often get trapped in.
**Significance for learners:** Helps learners stop seeking trust or closeness based solely on the label of family, blood ties, or position. Equips a toolkit to proactively upgrade any relationship at will.
**Brought transformation:** Shifts from a passive position of resenting others for being indifferent to an active position of creating joy, trust, and value. Moves from passively "leaving it to fate" to actively seizing the opportunities life presents.
**Outcomes:** Learners become fluent in distinguishing the 5 levels of relationships. They firmly grasp the keys of "being proactive" and "creating value" to turn the people around them into companions who share the same language, the same consensus, and walk the same path in depth.
**B. IN-DEPTH CONTENT: IDENTIFYING AND UPGRADING RELATIONSHIPS – FROM THE MISCONCEPTION OF TITLES TO BECOMING SOULMATES**
**1. Introduction: Dissecting the Root of Suffering in Communication – The Misconception of Titles** On the path of elevating inner awareness, when we entered the work of cultivating "Good Affinity", we established a foundational awareness: *"People are capital, people are the bridge and the road."* However, there is a heartbreaking paradox occurring in human life: many people live surrounded by a sea of others, live within their own family and organization, yet constantly feel lonely, hurt, and full of resentment.
The journey of guiding awareness begins by dissecting the root of this suffering. It stems from an invisible barrier called: **the misconception of titles**. Humanity often clings heavily to labels such as "Husband - Wife", "Parent - Child", "Boss - Employee", or "Blood Siblings". We default, under an illusion, to believing that: once bearing the title of husband and wife, one must naturally be "close"; once being parent and child, one must naturally "trust" and understand one another.
Yet, harsh reality shows otherwise: there are couples who have shared the same bed for decades but remain stuck at merely "knowing a lot of information about each other" (familiarity) — when something happens, they meet with suspicion and defensiveness. This misconception of titles breeds unreasonable expectations, demanding that the other person treat us at the highest level, and when that is not met, resentment arises immediately.
To break this cycle of suffering, a revolutionary awareness must be established: **Cast aside every mask of titles, and measure the relationship purely based on two yardsticks: "Information" and "Feeling".**
**2. Expanding the Knowledge: The 5 Levels of Social Relationships** Once we strip away the shell of titles, every human relationship in this world falls into just 5 clear and transparent levels:
**Level 1 - Acquaintance:** The state when we only *know 1 piece of information* about that person (for example: only knowing their name, or only knowing their occupation).
**Level 2 - Familiarity:** The state when we *know a lot of information* about that person. (Many colleagues, drinking buddies, or even spouses are stuck at this level).
**Level 3 - Fondness:** An inner state where, being next to that person, we have a *feeling of joy*.
**Level 4 - Trust:** An inner state where, being next to that person, we have a *feeling of safety*.
**Level 5 - Closeness:** The peak state where both sides have *complete, all-around trust*.
Once we correctly locate the level through "Feeling", our inner being is immediately liberated. We calmly realize: *"Ah, it turns out my child and I are only at the level of Familiarity — my child does not yet have the feeling of safety (Trust) around me, so of course they hide things from me."* Seeing the truth clearly allows us to stop demanding, stop resenting, and start acting.
**3. Analyzing the Principle of Transformation: The Power of "Being Proactive" and "Creating Value"** Once we have correctly identified the level, how do we upgrade the relationship? The master key that unlocks every level is two words: **BEING PROACTIVE**.
Many people often use the phrase "leave it to fate" to justify laziness and passivity in communication. A profound principle is pointed out: *Leaving it to fate only applies when the other person does not want to connect with us; but once the opportunity for connection exists, we must fix our intention and BE PROACTIVE.*
**From Acquaintance to Familiarity:** We need to proactively increase our *methods of contact* and meet regularly.
**From Familiarity to Fondness:** People commonly make the mistake of waiting for others to make them happy before they feel fond of them. The correct principle is that we must **proactively be joyful**, bringing joy and connecting on the same frequency with them.
**From Fondness to Trust:** The feeling of joy is only the initial launchpad. For the other person to have an absolute "feeling of safety", we must **proactively trust and create value**. A relationship without the creation of value (helping, contributing, bringing real benefit) will forever remain at the level of passing entertainment. This process of creating value is precisely where we skillfully apply the 7 Important Forms of Giving in Life. *(Note: This content will be explored further and clarified in the topic: The 7 Important Forms of Giving in Life.)*
**4. The Peak of Awareness: Building Ultimate Closeness with the "11 Words of Togetherness"** To bring a relationship from "Trust" up to the peak of "Closeness" (complete, all-around trust), the key action is to **proactively walk together toward a shared goal**.
Closeness is not a vague, emotional concept. It has been quantified through **the "11 Words of Togetherness"**. When two people — whether husband and wife, business partners, or teacher and student — sit down together and establish: *the same foundation, the same aspirations, the same guiding principles (sacred tools), the same rules, the same mindset, the same actions, the same goal, the same motivation, the same passion, the same mission, the same convictions.*
The very moment these "11 Words of Togetherness" are established and blended together, the two independent individuals officially unite, becoming part of one another with 3 eternal traits: **Shared Language** (sharing the same inner language), **Shared Consensus** (sharing the same vibrational frequency of energy), and **Shared Journey** (sharing the same mental image and destination). This is the highest realm for retaining talent and building an enduring family clan or organization.
**5. Conclusion: Shifting Position – Mastering One's Own Destiny** To close this topic, thoroughly understanding the knowledge of "Identifying and Upgrading Relationships" creates a revolution in the way people treat one another. It decisively ends the era of passivity, self-pity, and waiting for others to bring us happiness.
By clearly understanding the 5 levels and wielding the weapon of "proactively creating value", we officially hold in our hands the power to design the network of relationships in our own life. We are no longer victims of broken relationships, but become creators of good affinity — where people who share the same language, the same consensus, and the same journey automatically gather together to build a life of complete fortune and smooth flow.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
**2.1. Concepts (Concepts)**
**The 5 Levels of Social Relationships:**
**Acquaintance:** The state when we only *know 1 piece of information* about that person (for example: name, or occupation).
**Familiarity:** The state when we *know a lot of information* about that person.
**Fondness:** The state when, being next to that person, we have a *feeling of joy*.
**Trust:** The state when, being next to that person, we have a *feeling of safety*.
**Closeness:** The state where both sides have *complete, all-around trust*.
**2.2. Key Knowledge (Key Knowledge)**
**The misconception of titles:** A title (Family, spouse, long-term partner) does not represent the level of a relationship. The level must be assessed through "Feeling" (joy, safety) and "Information" (knowing 1 piece or many).
**How to upgrade a relationship:**
*From Acquaintance -> Familiarity:* Methods of contact, meeting regularly.
*From Familiarity -> Fondness:* **Proactively being joyful**, connecting on the same frequency.
*From Fondness -> Trust:* **Proactively trusting, creating value**.
*From Trust -> Closeness:* **Proactively trusting completely**, walking together toward a shared goal.
**The 11 Words of "Togetherness" that build Closeness:** The same foundation, the same aspirations, the same guiding principles, the same rules, the same mindset, the same actions, the same goal, the same motivation, the same passion, the same mission, the same convictions. When these are present, people arrive at "Shared Language, Shared Consensus, Shared Journey".
**2.3. Key Awareness (Key Awareness)**
**Establishing Awareness 1 - Removing the barrier of titles to end resentment:** Clearly recognize that closeness does not naturally arise from a marriage certificate or a birth certificate. A husband and wife may live under the same roof, but if the moment something happens they meet with suspicion and no feeling of safety, they are in fact only at the level of "Familiarity". Seeing this clearly allows us to stop demanding and stop resenting.
**Establishing Awareness 2 - The power of Being Proactive:** Leaving it to fate does not mean giving up. Leaving it to fate only applies when someone refuses to connect. But once the opportunity exists, we must be the one who is **Proactive**. Waiting for others to make us happy before we feel happy is passive; we must "proactively be joyful" for others to feel Fondness toward us.
**Establishing Awareness 3 - Creating value is the glue that binds:** For a relationship to step into the state of "Trust" (feeling of safety), there must be continuous giving and "creating value" for one another. Without creating value, a relationship will forever remain at the level of passing entertainment (Fondness).
**2.4. 03 Personal Application Examples (Personal Application)**
**Example 1: Overcoming loneliness and the barrier to communication**
*Situation before transformation:* Starting work at a new company, keeping to myself, waiting for others to come and talk to me, and complaining that my colleagues were cold.
*Old awareness:* Believing that since I was the new person, everyone else had a responsibility to be friendly with me.
*Knowledge applied:* To move from "Familiarity" to "Fondness" requires "proactively being joyful".
*New action:* I stopped waiting. Every morning I arrived at the company, I proactively smiled, greeted people with energy, and proactively brought small moments of joy to my colleagues.
*Result after transformation:* Everyone immediately felt "Fondness" toward me. I quickly integrated and gained a happy working environment, extinguishing my inner loneliness.
**Example 2: Receiving a Teacher/Mentor in life**
*Situation before transformation:* Joining courses, admiring a Teacher but always keeping a distance, finishing the course and going home.
*Old awareness:* Thinking I was just an ordinary student (the misconception of titles), that the Teacher was too far above to reach, and waiting for the Teacher to pay attention to me first.
*Knowledge applied:* Upgrading to "Trust" and "Closeness" through "proactively creating value" and "walking together toward a shared goal".
*New action:* I proactively volunteered to take on tasks serving the class, walking alongside to spread the Teacher's philosophy (creating value).
*Result after transformation:* I earned the Teacher's trust and was entrusted with important responsibilities; the relationship upgraded to Closeness, and I officially received direct guidance for outstanding growth.
**Example 3: Clearing the confusion in relationship orientation**
*Situation before transformation:* Having many friends on social media, frequently going out drinking, but when a crisis hit, no one helped.
*Old awareness:* Believing that frequent drinking together and knowing everything about each other meant we were already "Close" and could rely on one another.
*Knowledge applied:* Relocating the level of the relationship through feeling.
*New action:* Upon reflection, I realized these drinking relationships only brought a "feeling of joy" (the level of Fondness), and never carried a "feeling of safety" or "creation of value" (Trust). I stopped fantasizing and stopped resenting them for not helping me. Instead, I focused on truly creating value for those who deserved it.
*Result after transformation:* I protected my inner being from disappointment and rebuilt a leaner but higher-quality network of relationships with complete trust.
**2.5. 03 Family Application Examples (Family Application)**
**Example 1: Transforming a cold Husband-and-Wife relationship**
*Situation before transformation:* A couple who had lived together for 10 years but each went their own way, kept separate secret funds, and frequently suspected each other.
*Old awareness:* Believing that since they were husband and wife (a title), they must automatically understand and trust each other without needing to do anything.
*Knowledge applied:* Escaping the misconception of titles, upgrading from Fondness to Trust.
*New action:* Accepting the truth that the couple was only at the level of "Familiarity/Fondness". I began to "proactively trust" by handing over the finances for my wife to manage, and "created value" by taking on housework to help her.
*Result after transformation:* My wife felt safety (Trust). The couple began sharing the 11 Words of "Togetherness" (the same family goals, the same mindset), moving toward Closeness and complete marital happiness.
**Example 2: Closing the Generation Gap between Parents and Children**
*Situation before transformation:* A child entering puberty, closing their bedroom door, not speaking to their parents, and getting irritable whenever the parents asked questions.
*Old awareness:* The parents used the authority of titles: "I am your parent, I have the right to know, you must trust me."
*Knowledge applied:* Identifying the level and "proactively being joyful".
*New action:* Realizing the child did not have a "feeling of safety" around them, the parents stepped back to step 3: "proactively being joyful". They stopped interrogating and instead created joyful family settings, talking about things on the same frequency as their child.
*Result after transformation:* The child gradually let down their guard because they had a "feeling of joy", then began opening up and confiding (moving toward safety/trust). The family regained its deep connection.
**Example 3: Building the Mother-in-law – Daughter-in-law relationship**
*Situation before transformation:* The daughter-in-law and mother-in-law were constantly defensive, scrutinizing each other's every word.
*Old awareness:* Trapped by social prejudice and the title "Mother-in-law – Daughter-in-law", believing they were always naturally at odds.
*Knowledge applied:* Proactively creating value to move toward Trust.
*New action:* The daughter-in-law cast aside the title and viewed her mother-in-law as a person who needed to be connected with. She proactively created value by caring about her mother-in-law's health, buying gifts she liked, and sincerely listening to her advice.
*Result after transformation:* The feeling of defensiveness disappeared, replaced by a feeling of safety. The mother-in-law came to see her daughter-in-law as her own daughter (Closeness), and the family became warm and harmonious inside and out.
**2.6. 03 Organizational Application Examples (Organizational Application)**
**Example 1: Leadership and Human Resources Management**
*Situation before transformation:* A boss who assigned tasks but constantly scrutinized employees through cameras; employees worked half-heartedly and kept quitting.
*Old awareness:* The boss believed that since he paid salaries (the title of "owner"), employees naturally had to be loyal and give their all.
*Knowledge applied:* Moving from Fondness to Trust (proactively trusting, creating value).
*New action:* The leader realized his staff did not yet have a "feeling of safety". He began "proactively trusting" by delegating decision-making power to employees, and created value through a clear career advancement path.
*Result after transformation:* The staff felt safe and valued. They developed trust in return, contributing and taking on responsibility for the company far beyond their salary.
**Example 2: Breaking down barriers between Departments (Silo Effect)**
*Situation before transformation:* The Marketing and Sales departments constantly blamed each other whenever revenue fell, refusing to cooperate.
*Old awareness:* The departments viewed each other as competitors for benefits, only doing their own strict duties (the level of Acquaintance/Familiarity).
*Knowledge applied:* Walking together toward a shared goal to reach the level of Closeness.
*New action:* The board of directors organized workshops to establish the "11 Words of Togetherness" (the same company goal, the same vision, the same mission). They required the two departments to "proactively create value" for each other (Marketing making materials easier for Sales to use, Sales feeding back customer insights to Marketing).
*Result after transformation:* The two departments became "Shared Language, Shared Consensus, Shared Journey", turning into one family, driving the company's revenue up sharply.
**Example 3: Building a base of loyal Customers**
*Situation before transformation:* The business focused only on closing a one-time sale; customers never returned after purchase.
*Old awareness:* Viewing the buying-selling relationship as a purely material exchange (the level of Acquaintance).
*Knowledge applied:* Proactively being joyful and creating value to rise to the levels of Fondness and Trust.
*New action:* Instead of just selling, the business cared for customers with a respectful attitude, provided free knowledge (proactively being joyful, connecting), and offered unconditional support when products had defects (creating value).
*Result after transformation:* Customers felt absolute safety when using the product. They not only bought repeatedly but became brand ambassadors, referring many new customers.
**2.7. 03 Social Impact Examples (Social Impact)**
**Example 1: Eliminating indifference in the Community**
*Situation before transformation:* Neighbors in an apartment complex kept their doors tightly shut, no one knew anyone, indifferent to each other's hardships.
*Old awareness:* Believing that living in a city means everyone looks after themselves — leaving it to fate, nodding if you happen to meet, and letting it go if not.
*Knowledge applied:* Turning Acquaintance into Familiarity and Fondness.
*New action:* A few individuals with inner awareness began to "proactively" create a neighborhood social group, regularly organizing gatherings and giving each other small gifts (methods of contact, proactively being joyful).
*Result after transformation:* The apartment complex became warm; everyone felt fondness for each other and was willing to help watch each other's children and look out for security, creating a civilized community culture.
**Example 2: Spreading an Educational Philosophy Globally**
*Situation before transformation:* An organization had a great philosophy, but it only spread within a small group; society had not widely embraced it.
*Old awareness:* Simply opening classes (leaving it to fate) — whoever wants to learn, learns; whoever doesn't, doesn't.
*Knowledge applied:* Proactively trusting completely, walking together toward a great shared goal (Closeness).
*New action:* The organization sought "human consensus", sharing the "11 Words of Togetherness" (the same mission of elevating the awareness of the Vietnamese people). It proactively created unconditional value for businesses and schools.
*Result after transformation:* It attracted tens of thousands of experts and educators (the Elite Intellectual Group) to gather. From there, it successfully built a massive educational ecosystem, powerfully impacting the nation's educational landscape.
**Example 3: Rescuing socially isolated individuals**
*Situation before transformation:* Depressed teenagers, or vulnerable people, isolating themselves from society, feeling the world was full of danger.
*Old awareness:* Society believed they were "problem" people, hard to approach, so best avoided.
*Knowledge applied:* Using "proactively being joyful" and "creating value" to bring a feeling of safety.
*New action:* Inner-being experts approached without a judgmental mindset, without demanding they change. The experts simply "proactively remained joyful", giving out a source of peaceful, tolerant energy, and creating small spiritual values every day.
*Result after transformation:* The vulnerable individuals felt a rare "feeling of safety" (Trust). Their defensive shell shattered, they opened up to reconnect with the world, healed their souls, and reintegrated into the community.
