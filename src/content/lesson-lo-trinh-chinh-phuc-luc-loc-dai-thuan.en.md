### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION
**1A. Objectives & Target Audience:**
* **Value of the topic:** This topic helps learners thoroughly comprehend the concept of a "Dream Life" through the symbol of Sixfold Life Fulfillment (Lục Lộc Đại Thuận - six grand favorable aspects of human life). It provides a clear map, pointing out the exact "starting point" so that humans do not lose direction when creating a fulfilling life.
* **Significance for learners:** It dismantles the misconception that merely chasing money leads to a good life. Learners realize that if a person's life is sufficient and balanced in all 6 aspects of Sixfold Life Fulfillment, life adversities will no longer feel like major disruptions, similar to a fully inflated wheel that can smoothly roll over any pothole on the road of life.
* **Brought transformation:** Learners shift from chasing careers aimlessly to anchoring in a value-creation tool, optimizing that tool to achieve Sixfold Life Fulfillment. They establish a correct awareness of financial freedom and the value of experiencing the world.

**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**ROADMAP TO ACHIEVE SIXFOLD LIFE FULFILLMENT – THE MAP FOR CREATING A FULFILLING LIFE**

**INTRODUCTION – THE PARADOX OF PURSUIT AND THE VISION OF THE DESTINATION**
If we step out into society and ask someone busy working: *"What is the ultimate goal of your daily efforts?"*, the majority of answers will be money, wealth, or success.
But there is a painful paradox in human life: many people who reach the peak of wealth suffer from depleted health, broken families, inner insecurity, and lack of true relationships. We wander around seeking a "good life" but have never actually defined: **What is a Dream Life?**
Masters have pointed out a clear and complete vision of the destination, called **Sixfold Life Fulfillment** (Lục Lộc Đại Thuận) – six grand favorable aspects of life. Comprehending and creating Sixfold Life Fulfillment is having the map to conquer a fulfilling life.

**DEVELOPMENT – THE STRUCTURE OF THE SIXFOLD LIFE FULFILLMENT WHEEL**
Sixfold Life Fulfillment is not an abstract concept; it is the convergence of the 6 most critical aspects of human life:
1. **Time Freedom:** The ability to master and freely allocate one's own time.
2. **Financial Freedom:** Not merely owning a mountain of money. *We define financial freedom as the state where we work the least but still have enough passive income (automatic income) to satisfy the lifestyle we desire.*
3. **Harmonious Family:** Husband and wife share the same language, agreement, and accompaniment; children love and respect parents.
4. **World Travel:** Not simply packing a suitcase to travel and spend money. World Travel is a journey to experience life perspectives, expand worldviews, and bring value to exchange with the world.
5. **Personal & Relational Development:** Continuously nurturing wisdom, mental state, personality, qualities, capacity, and associating with outstanding people.
6. **Good Health:** Achieving a state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being.

**The Wheel of Life Principle:** These six aspects are like 6 spokes of a wheel. If we only focus on making money (a very long financial spoke) but neglect health and family (short spokes), the wheel will be distorted. When rolling on the road of life, at the first "pothole" (adversity), this distorted wheel will break.
But if we nurture them equally, keeping this wheel **fully inflated across all 6 aspects**, it will roll smoothly forward. At that point, any life adversity that falls upon us will no longer be a major crisis; the fully inflated wheel will glide effortlessly over any pothole.

**IN-DEPTH ANALYSIS – THE STARTING POINT AND ROADMAP**
* **Energy Orientation in the Reality Triangle of Human Life:**
  Under the Reality Triangle perspective of life destination:
  * The Information corner is the **Dream Career**.
  * The Energy corner is the **Dream Life (Sixfold Life Fulfillment)**.
  * The Material corner is the **Realm of Life**.
  * *We must establish a critical awareness:* **Working hard at a career without knowing how to direct that energy toward a Dream Life to elevate the Realm of Life makes all efforts incomplete in meaning.** If we only work without aiming for Sixfold Life Fulfillment, we are merely tools for the job instead of letting the job serve our life.

* **The Starting Point – Where do we jump in?**
  This is the decision that determines the success or failure of a person's life. The natural human tendency is to jump straight into "Finance" to solve everything. But that is a trap.
  The secret pointed out by masters is: The most stable, secure, and wise starting point is jumping into **Personal & Relational Development**.
  Why? Because finance is the outcome (Material). If the inner self (Non-material) lacks Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, and Qualities, and relationships are not of high quality, a sudden influx of money will be a curse rather than a blessing. When we start by **"non-materializing ourselves"** (enriching mental state, wisdom, personality) and associating with outstanding people, our health naturally improves, our family naturally becomes harmonious, and by anchoring in a value-creation tool, finance automatically converges.

* **7-Step Roadmap to Achieve a Dream Life (Sustainable Wealth Formula):**
  To realize the Sixfold Life Fulfillment Wheel, especially to achieve financial freedom, we follow a rigorous 7-step roadmap:
  1. **Non-materialize ourselves:** Nurturing Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, and Qualities. This is the foundational core that cannot be bypassed.
  2. **Train the Physical Body:** Having ironman-like health as the vehicle for our dreams.
  3. **Elevate Appretell - Cooperation - Guidance capacities:** Leveraging support from people.
  4. **Anchor in a value-creation tool:** The tool must satisfy the "Long-lasting - Large-scale - Human-benefiting" criteria and "Alignment with the Three Realms".
  5. **Realize the 6 Golden Words in action:** Doing things with simplicity, joy, trust, ease, frequency, and mindfulness.
  6. **Apply the a^n formula:** Replicating value (1 - 2 - 20 - 500 - 10,000).
  7. **Virtue conducts the world (Đức hành thiên hạ):** Spreading philosophy and values globally.
  *(Note: The sequence and techniques for implementing these 7 steps will be elaborated in the topic: Formula for Material Wealth).*

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
**2.1. Core Concepts**
* **Sixfold Life Fulfillment (Lục Lộc Đại Thuận):** Represents the Dream Life, consisting of 6 key aspects: Time Freedom, Financial Freedom, Harmonious Family, World Travel, Personal & Relational Development, and Good Health.
* **Wheel of Life Principle:** A visual metaphor where the 6 aspects form a wheel. A balanced and fully inflated wheel allows a person to sail smoothly through life's adversities.
* **The Wise Starting Point:** Starting not from finance, but from **Personal & Relational Development** (non-materializing ourselves first).

**2.2. Key Knowledge**
* **Destination Reality Triangle:** Dream Career (Information) ➔ Dream Life / Sixfold Life Fulfillment (Energy) ➔ Realm of Life (Material).
* **7 Steps to Sixfold Life Fulfillment:**
  1. Non-materialize ourselves (Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Qualities).
  2. Physical training.
  3. Master Appretell, Cooperation, Guidance.
  4. Anchor in a Long-lasting, Large-scale, Human-benefiting tool.
  5. Act with simplicity, joy, trust, ease, frequency, and mindfulness.
  6. Replicate values (a^n).
  7. Spread virtue globally.

**2.3. Deep Insights**
* **Financial Freedom redefined:** Working the least while earning sufficient passive income to support the desired lifestyle.
* **World Travel redefined:** Traveling not to spend money, but to expand life perspectives, worldviews, and create value at every destination.

**2.4. Personal Application**
Instead of worrying about "how to get rich," I proactively choose "Personal Development" as my starting point. I focus on non-materializing myself by studying Inner self knowledge and doing physical exercises. By nurturing my mental state and personality, my relationships naturally improve, and my career/finance flow smoothly.

**2.5. Family Application**
Using Sixfold Life Fulfillment as a guide for family culture. My spouse and I focus on creating a Harmonious Family. We establish a habit of traveling the world together to expand our children's horizons, guiding them on the balance of these 6 elements to build a sustainable family legacy.

**2.6. Organizational Application**
As a corporate advisor, I guide business owners to plan Sixfold Life Fulfillment for themselves and their key staff. Through the 7-step roadmap, employees work with an ownership mindset, contributing fully, making the business strong while achieving a fulfilling life.

**2.7. Social Impact**
If millions of people apply Sixfold Life Fulfillment, society will be highly prosperous. People work to create value, travel the world carrying national cultural essence, and create a community that is physically healthy and spiritually rich.
