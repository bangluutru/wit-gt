## TOPIC: ROADMAP TO CONQUERING A LIFE OF SIXFOLD LIFE FULFILLMENT

### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

**A. TOPIC OBJECTIVES:**

**Value of the topic:** This topic helps learners thoroughly comprehend the concept of a "Dream Life" through the image of Sixfold Life Fulfillment (Lục Lộc Đại Thuận – 6 grand favorable aspects of life). It provides a clear map, pinpointing precisely the "starting point" so that people do not lose direction while creating a fulfilling life.

**Significance for learners:** It shatters the misconception that merely chasing money will bring a good life. Learners realize that if a person's life is sufficient and balanced across all 6 aspects of Sixfold Life Fulfillment, then almost every adversity that comes will no longer feel like a crisis at all — just like a fully inflated wheel that can smoothly roll over every pothole on the road of life.

**Brought transformation:** Learners shift from chasing a career aimlessly to anchoring themselves in a value-creation tool, optimizing that tool to achieve Sixfold Life Fulfillment. They establish a correct awareness of financial freedom and the value of experiencing the world.

**B. IN-DEPTH CONTENT: ROADMAP TO CONQUERING A LIFE OF SIXFOLD LIFE FULFILLMENT – THE MAP FOR CREATING A FULFILLING LIFE**

**1. INTRODUCTION – THE PARADOX OF PURSUIT AND THE PICTURE OF THE DESTINATION**

If we go out into society and ask someone who is constantly rushing about: *"What is the ultimate purpose of your daily efforts?"*, most answers we get back will be about pursuing money, wealth, or success.

But there is a painful paradox in human life: very many people, upon reaching the peak of wealth, find their health depleted, their family broken, their inner being anxious, and not a single true relationship around them. We are engrossed in searching for a "good life" but have never truly named and clarified: **What actually is a Dream Life?**

Masters have pointed out to us an extremely clear and complete picture of the destination, called **Sixfold Life Fulfillment** – six grand favorable aspects of a person's life. Thoroughly comprehending and creating Sixfold Life Fulfillment is the moment we hold in our hands the map to conquer a fulfilling life.

**2. DEVELOPMENT – THE STRUCTURE OF THE SIXFOLD LIFE FULFILLMENT WHEEL**

Sixfold Life Fulfillment is not some distant abstract concept; it is the convergence of the 6 most critical aspects of human life, including:

**Time Freedom:** The capacity to master and freely allocate one's own time.

**Financial Freedom:** Not about owning a mountain of money and locking it away. *Toan would like to establish this awareness: Financial freedom is the state where we work the least yet still have a passive income (automatic income) sufficient to satisfy the lifestyle we desire.*

**Harmonious Family:** Husband and wife share the same language, the same consensus, and walk side by side; children love and respect their parents.

**World Travel:** Not simply packing a suitcase to travel and spend money. World Travel is a journey to experience life perspectives, expand one's worldview, and bring value to exchange with the five continents.

**Personal Development & Upgrading Social Relationships:** Continuously cultivating wisdom, mental state, personality, and character, and associating with outstanding people.

**Good Health:** Achieving a state of complete well-being physically, mentally, and socially.

**The Wheel of Life Principle:** These six aspects are likened to the 6 spokes of a wheel. If we only bury ourselves in earning money (a very long financial spoke) while neglecting health and family (short spokes), the wheel will become distorted. When rolling along the road of life, it only takes one "pothole" (an adversity) for that distorted wheel to break down.

But if we cultivate all of them evenly, keeping this wheel **fully inflated across all 6 aspects**, the wheel will roll smoothly forward. At that point, the adversities of life that fall upon us will almost no longer be adversities at all; the fully inflated wheel will glide gently over every pothole of life.

**3. IN-DEPTH ANALYSIS – THE STARTING POINT AND THE ROADMAP FOR CREATION**

**3.1. Orienting energy within the Reality Triangle of human life**

Before we set to work, we need to thoroughly understand the position of Sixfold Life Fulfillment within the structure of human life. From the perspective of the Reality Triangle:

The Information corner is precisely the **Dream Career**.

The Energy corner is precisely the **Dream Life (Sixfold Life Fulfillment)**.

The Material corner is precisely the **Realm of Life**.

*Let us together establish an extremely important awareness:* **Working at a career with your head down, without knowing how to direct that source of energy to become the Dream Life, in order to elevate the Realm of Life, means all effort lacks complete meaning.** If we only know how to work without aiming toward Sixfold Life Fulfillment, we are merely tools for the job, instead of letting the job serve our life.

**3.2. The starting point – Facing the 6 aspects, where do we jump in?**

This is the question that decides the success or failure of a person's life. The human tendency is to jump straight into the "Finance" segment hoping to solve everything. But that is a trap.

The secret pointed out by the masters is: The sturdiest, safest, and wisest starting point is to jump into the segment of **Personal Development and Upgrading Social Relationships**.

Why? Because finance is an outcome (Material). If the inside (Non-material) is not yet sufficiently full of Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Character; if capacity is not yet adequate and relationships are not yet of quality, and money suddenly floods in, that is not a blessing but a curse. When we begin with **"non-materializing ourselves"** (enriching our mental state, wisdom, personality) and manage to associate with outstanding people, then health naturally improves, family naturally becomes harmonious, and from there, by anchoring in a value-creation tool, finance will automatically converge.

**3.3. The 7-step roadmap to conquering the Dream Life (The Sustainable Wealth Formula)**

To make the Sixfold Life Fulfillment Wheel a reality, especially to reach Financial Freedom, we do not follow instinct but follow a rigorous roadmap of 7 steps:

**Non-materialize ourselves:** Cultivate Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, and Character. This is the core foundation that cannot skip stages.

**Train the Physical Body:** Have ironman-strong health to serve as the vehicle carrying our dreams.

**Elevate the capacity for Outreach - Coordination - Guidance:** Borrow supporting strength from people.

**Anchor in a value-creation tool:** That tool must satisfy the criteria of "Long-lasting - Large-scale - Wholesome" and "Alignment with the Three Realms" (benefiting oneself, benefiting others, benefiting all beings).

**Realize the 6 golden words in action:** Act simply, joyfully, with trust, gently, regularly, and mindfully.

**Apply the a^n formula:** Replicate value (1 - 2 - 20 - 500 - 10,000).

**Virtue that conducts the world:** Carry philosophy and values to spread globally.

*(Note: The sequence of transition and the execution techniques for these 7 steps will be explored in depth and clarified in the topic: The Formula for Material Wealth).*

**4. CONCLUSION & TRANSFORMATIVE MESSAGE**

Dear friends, the journey of conquering **Sixfold Life Fulfillment** is, in essence, the journey by which we reclaim mastery over our own lives.

Instead of letting the current of life push us around — today worrying about health, tomorrow grieving over our children, the day after stuck because of money — we choose to stop, to take a step back to "non-materialize ourselves." When the light of understanding human life is lit, we calmly anchor ourselves in a value-creation tool, working with a peaceful, joyful mental state and love for people.

Just by sticking closely to this map, the 6 spokes of the wheel of life will gradually be pumped full. One day, not far off, we will not only have a free, fulfilling life for ourselves, but will also become a role model, a legacy of awareness to pass down to our family clan and future generations.

That is the true destination of a life lived with a philosophy!
