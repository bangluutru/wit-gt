# CURRICULUM: UNDERSTANDING INNER BEING – CREATING INNER PEACE

## TOPIC: DESIRABLE OUTCOME IN HUMAN LIFE

---

## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** Provides the final piece and destination of the formula: **Good Cause + Good Connection = Desirable Outcome**. It helps learners shape a clear picture of the achievements a human being truly needs to pursue.
- **Significance for learners:** Enables learners to stop chasing short-term material targets or straining after surface outcomes. Instead, they lock in the ultimate destination of life and step back to cultivate roots.
- **Brought transformation:** Shifts awareness from "Sentient beings concern themselves with outcomes" to "Bodhisattvas focus on causes." Learners master how to "Activate Outcomes" via Appretell and "Preserve Outcomes" sustainably via cultivating Qualities.
- **Outcomes:** Learners clarify the 3 pillars of Desirable Outcomes: Dream Career, Dream Life, and Life Realms. They learn how to activate outcomes via Appretell and preserve them via the 5 Qualities.

### B. In-depth Content

In our journey to light up the lamps of awareness, sages summarize life operation into a formula: **Good Cause + Good Connection = Desirable Outcome**. We build the Good Cause by clarifying mental images, gathering beneficial concepts, and accumulating phước báu. We call forth Good Connections via correct inner awareness of humans. Today, we clarify the final piece: **the Desirable Outcome in Human Life (Quả Như Ý)**.

In society, most live under "Sentient beings concern themselves with outcomes." From youth, we are guided to study hard, buy a house, get a car, and marry. These are surface fruits. People run outward straining to harvest fruits at all costs, forgetting to cultivate causes and connections. This is like wanting a garden full of sweet fruits while refusing to plant seeds and nurture the soil. Sages show that when Cause and Connection are set, the Desirable Outcome manifests naturally.

What is the ultimate Desirable Outcome in human life? Sages summarize it into three grand pillars: **Dream Career, Dream Life, and Life Realms**. Possessing these prevents us from being lost in petty desires, directing our life toward ultimate maturity.

Once seeds are planted and soil is fertile, how do we harvest and preserve the fruit? Sages transfer two keys: **Activating Outcomes (Kích Quả) and Preserving Outcomes (Giữ Quả)**.
- **Activating Outcomes:** The tool is **Appretell (Quảng bá)**. Using the energy of Honouring & Gratitude, we repeatedly promote the benefit of what we do, causing the outcome to manifest.
- **Preserving Outcomes:** To keep outcomes from fading or being lost, cultivate **Qualities (Phẩm chất)**—specifically *Benevolence, Ritual (Propriety), Righteousness, Wisdom, and Trustworthiness* (Nhân - Lễ - Nghĩa - Trí - Tín). A person of outstanding qualities protects all material and spiritual achievements permanently.

Ultimately, understanding the Desirable Outcome is not to breed greed, but to identify the destination. Once the target is clear, return to cultivating Causes and Connections, using Appretell to activate outcomes, and Qualities to preserve them. Life becomes simple, joyful, and peaceful.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Desirable Outcome (Quả Như Ý):** The achievements and good outcomes humans desire, represented by: **Dream Career, Dream Life, and Life Realms**.
- **Activating Outcomes (Kích Quả):** Actions to call forth good outcomes; the key tool is **Appretell (Quảng bá)**.
- **Preserving Outcomes (Giữ Quả):** Actions to protect and maintain achievements sustainably; the key tool is cultivating **Qualities (Phẩm chất)**.

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

- **Mouth of Operation (Nhân - Duyên - Quả):**
  - **Good Cause:** Activated by elevating inner awareness; Preserved by Mental State.
  - **Good Connection:** Activated by the 7 Offerings; Preserved by Personality.
  - **Desirable Outcome:** Activated by Appretell; Preserved by Qualities.
- **Reality Triangle of Desirable Outcomes:**
  - **Information:** Dream Career (Nghề Ước Mơ).
  - **Energy:** Dream Life / Lục lộc đại thuận (Cuộc Sống Ước Mơ).
  - **Material:** Life Realms / Cảnh Giới Cuộc Sống.

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **Sentient Beings Concern Outcomes, Bodhisattvas Focus on Causes:** Stop running after surface fruits. Once the target is clear, return to cultivate causes and connections.
- **No Qualities, No Preservation:** If success comes from luck (phước báu ripening) but you lack Qualities (Benevolence, Ritual, Righteousness, Wisdom, Trustworthiness), the fruits will fade.
- **Appretell is the Key to Activate Outcomes:** Promoting the benefit of your work with gratitude attracts agreement, ripening the outcomes.

### 2.4. Applications (Personal, Family, Organizational, Social)

#### Personal Application
- **Career & Finance:** Instead of working hard just for salary (Outcome), cultivate capacity, qualities, and inner awareness (Cause). High-value work and financial growth follow naturally.
- **Health Preservation:** After achieving health (Fruit), maintain it by building Trustworthiness (Tín) with yourself to sustain healthy habits (Quality).

#### Family Application
- **Guiding Children:** Stop forcing grades or elite schools (Fruits). Focus on cultivating their inner causes (concepts) and connections (morals), ensuring future success.
- **Spousal Harmony:** Protect family happiness (Fruit) by cultivating Benevolence (Love) and Ritual (Respect) to prevent decay.
- **Family Joy:** Use Appretell to promote family harmony: repeatedly praise the positive deeds of your spouse and children with gratitude.

#### Organizational Application
- **Business Growth:** Focus on building a joyful work environment and raising employee awareness (Cause) rather than just chasing KPIs. Sales follow.
- **Retention:** Leaders perfect their own Qualities to create a safe, appreciative environment where talents volunteer to stay.

#### Social Impact
- **Educational Spread:** Before launching public advertisements, train thousands of teachers in inner being concepts (Cause). Society embraces it naturally.
- **Charity:** Move from giving money (surface fruit) to providing education and skills (Cause) so people stand on their own feet.
