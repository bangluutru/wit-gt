### THE FULFILLED DESTINATION OF HUMAN LIFE AND THE ART OF ACTIVATING – PRESERVING THE DESIRABLE OUTCOME

Dear friends, over the past days, we have together lit up the lamps of awareness to enrich the root inside ourselves. The sages have pointed out for us a great formula that sums up the entire operation of life: Good Cause plus Good Connection equals Desirable Outcome. We have already learned how to build the Good Cause by clarifying mental images, cultivating beneficial source concepts, and accumulating merits and virtues. We have also learned how to call forth Good Connections through accurate inner awareness about people. Today, we officially step into clarifying the final piece — the destination that tens of thousands of people yearn for: the Desirable Outcome in human life.

There is a reality that most people in society are living with the mindset of "Sentient beings debate the outcome." From childhood, we have been directed to study hard so we can have a stable job, buy a house, get a car, get married. All of these are surface Outcomes. People rush outward, straining to harvest outcomes, harvesting at any cost, pursuing results while forgetting to cultivate the Cause and build the Connection. This is like wanting to pick a garden heavy with fruit while refusing to plant the seed and tend the soil. Precious knowledge points out that once we already have the Good Cause and the Good Connection, the Desirable Outcome will be harvested naturally by everyone — it is simply the manifestation of a natural law.

But the question arises: if we set aside surface material things, what is the ultimate essence of the Desirable Outcome in human life? When speaking of a person's achievements, the experts have distilled the Desirable Outcome around three great pillars: having a Dream Career, possessing a Dream Life, and rising to a high Life Realm. (Note: The detailed content on the Dream Career, the Dream Life (Sixfold Life Fulfillment), and the 7 Life Realms will be explored and clarified in depth in the upcoming topics: Topics 32, 33, and 34). Thoroughly grasping these three pillars helps us avoid getting lost in trivial cravings, and instead directs our own life straight toward fulfilled maturity.

So once the seed has been sown, the soil of cause and connection has become fertile, and the fruit is waiting to ripen, how do we make that fruit fall right into our hands and stay with us forever? The sages continue to hand us two ultimate techniques: Activating the Outcome and Preserving the Outcome. Just like a tree that has reached harvest season, we must take action to stimulate the fruit to ripen fully. In human life, the one and only, most powerful tool to Activate the Outcome is the capacity for Outreach. Using the energy source of honouring and gratitude, we promote the picture of benefit about what we are doing, and from there the desirable outcome will manifest. However, once we have the fruit but do not know how to preserve it, the fruit will also rot away or fall into someone else's hands. To Preserve the Outcome for the long term, the ultimate root is that we must cultivate Outstanding Qualities (Benevolence, Ritual, Righteousness, Wisdom, Trustworthiness). A person of outstanding qualities has all their achievements, all their material and non-material assets, permanently protected once they arrive.

In summary, thoroughly grasping the topic of the Desirable Outcome is not so that we develop a greedy mind chasing after results, but so that we know precisely what our own ultimate destination is. Once the picture of the Outcome is clear, we return to unhurriedly cultivating the Good Cause, building the Good Connection, using Outreach to activate the outcome, and using Qualities to preserve the outcome. When this entire operating chain is fully applied, a person's life will naturally become unhurried, simple, joyful, trusting, and will gently arrive at ultimate fulfillment.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Desirable Outcome:** The achievements and good results that people wish to harvest in human life, represented by three factors: Dream Career, Dream Life, and Life Realm.
- **Activating the Outcome:** The proactive actions taken to stimulate and call forth a good result to surface in reality; the key tool for activating the outcome is Outreach.
- **Preserving the Outcome:** Protecting and sustainably maintaining the achievements already attained; the key tool for preserving the outcome is cultivating Outstanding Qualities.

### 2.2. Key Knowledge

- **The Operating Chain of Cause – Connection – Outcome:**
  - **Good Cause:** Activated by Elevating Inner Awareness; Preserved by Mental State.
  - **Good Connection:** Activated by the 7 Important Offerings of Human Life; Preserved by Personality.
  - **Desirable Outcome:** Activated by Outreach; Preserved by Outstanding Qualities.
- **The Reality Triangle of the Desirable Outcome:** (Note: These concepts will be analyzed in detail in Topics 32, 33, 34).
  - Information Corner: Dream Career.
  - Energy Corner: Dream Life (Sixfold Life Fulfillment).
  - Material Corner: Life Realm (aiming toward Realm 6, Realm 7).

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **Bodhisattvas debate the cause, sentient beings debate the outcome:** Stop chasing surface results. Once the destination is clear, return to cultivating the Cause and the Connection.
- **Without Qualities, the Outcome cannot be preserved:** If an achievement comes from luck (merit ripening) while the person themselves has not cultivated Outstanding Qualities (Benevolence – Ritual – Righteousness – Wisdom – Trustworthiness), that outcome will sooner or later dissolve away.
- **Outreach is the key to Activating the Outcome:** Every dream, every product, every project that wants to become reality must use the capacity for Outreach (repeatedly reminding people of the picture of benefit, using the energy source of honouring and gratitude) to activate the outcome.

### 2.4. Personal Application Examples (03 Examples)

- **Example 1 (Finance & Career):** Instead of striving to work hard merely to receive a surface-level salary (debating the Outcome), one returns to cultivating capacity, qualities, and elevating inner awareness (cultivating the Cause). As a natural result, jobs that truly match one's passion are attracted, and finances automatically grow.
- **Example 2 (Preserving Health):** Having achieved good health through a training process but afterward becoming complacent and neglecting it. Applying the knowledge "Preserve the outcome through Qualities," one cultivates the quality of Trustworthiness toward oneself (committing to keep up a healthy daily routine), thereby sustainably protecting an ironman-like physique.
- **Example 3 (Applying Outreach):** Launching a personal business project. Instead of worrying that no one will buy, one uses honouring and gratitude to "reach out" about the value of the project to the people around them. Consensus appears, and the business outcome ripens automatically.

### 2.5. Family Application Examples (03 Examples)

- **Example 1 (Guiding Children):** Stop forcing children to achieve high scores or get into a prestigious school (forcing the Outcome). Parents shift to cultivating the Good Cause (life awareness) and the Good Connection (how to treat people) for their children; naturally, the child's future will reap solid success.
- **Example 2 (Preserving a Marriage):** A husband and wife are living a happy family life (a Desirable Outcome). To preserve this outcome, both consciously cultivate their Qualities (such as Benevolence — love, and Ritual — mutual honouring and gratitude), keeping the marriage close-knit and avoiding the law of Formation – Stasis – Decay – Dissolution.
- **Example 3 (Family Atmosphere):** Wanting a joyful family atmosphere, the wife uses "Outreach" to activate the outcome: regularly and repeatedly reminding everyone of the good deeds of her husband and children, using the energy source of honouring and gratitude. Naturally, the family becomes harmonious.

### 2.6. Organizational Application Examples (03 Examples)

- **Example 1 (Business Development):** The business owner does not only chase KPIs or revenue (the Outcome), but focuses on building a joyful working environment, elevating employees' inner awareness (the Cause), and connecting the team (the Connection); as a result, revenue automatically breaks through.
- **Example 2 (Retaining Talent):** A business achieves great success, but talented people leave due to declining company culture. Applying the perfecting of the leadership board's Qualities (Preserving the Outcome) creates a peaceful, joyful environment, and talented people voluntarily stay to contribute.
- **Example 3 (Activating a Project's Outcome):** A company has a very good product but cannot sell it. All personnel apply the principle of Outreach (repeatedly reminding customers of the product's picture of benefit with a sense of honouring and gratitude toward them), and the product quickly captures the market.

### 2.7. Social Impact Examples (03 Examples)

- **Example 1 (Spreading Educational Philosophy):** An organization wants to bring a root-level educational philosophy to reach the world (the Outcome). Instead of rushing into loud advertising, they patiently cultivate tens of thousands of experts who thoroughly understand human life (the Cause); as a result, society embraces it naturally.
- **Example 2 (Charity and Helping Others):** Instead of merely giving money to the poor (solving the surface outcome), society focuses on educating awareness, teaching a trade, and awakening self-belief for them (cultivating the Cause), helping them stand on their own two feet.
- **Example 3 (Building an Elite Community):** A community of accomplished people wants to endure. Instead of flaunting wealth, they focus on sharing values of Wisdom, Mental State, and Qualities (an Outreach of the Cause), attracting thousands of people who wish to rise toward a higher life realm to gather together.
