## TOPIC: THE SUCCESS PROCESS OF THE EXTRAORDINARY LEADER

### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

**A. Topic Objectives**

**Value of the topic:** This topic provides a mindset map that breaks through every rut of ordinary management and leadership. It shifts the learner's inner consciousness from pursuing surface-level results (sales figures, achievements) to building a solid foundation of human life (environment, concepts, mental state, capacity).

**Significance for learners:** Helps learners stop the hasty pursuit of results, and learn how to build the root of a sustainable organization or family by establishing "Point - Line - Plane" through perfecting one's personality.

**Transformation brought by the topic:** Transforms a manager who only knows how to demand sales figures into an "Extraordinary Leader" — someone who dares to focus wholeheartedly on building a good Environment and nurturing people, thoroughly understanding the principle of "Do well first, then grow big."

**What learners will understand, realize, and apply:** Realize the core difference between the ordinary success process and the extraordinary success process. Apply this immediately to their family or organization by seeking out "left-hand and right-hand supporters" to build a successful environment before seeking any achievement.

**B. In-depth Content**

In modern life and business, people are often swept into the spiral of results. An individual or a startup business usually immediately goes looking for "Population" (recruiting people), forcing out "Revenue," to achieve "Performance," under the illusion that this is "Success." However, this is a castle built on sand. Its consequence is the breakdown of relationships, a lack of culture, and the depletion of blessings.

This topic brings the **Success Process of the Extraordinary Leader** (for individuals) or the **Success Process of the Extraordinary Enterprise** (for organizations). "Extraordinary" here is not magic or the ability to fly, but courage: *Daring to do what others dare not do, thinking what others dare not think, persistently pursuing and shouldering what others run away from*. This extraordinary quality is expressed in the fact that the leader accepts swimming against the current, returning to build from foundation step 1 instead of leaping straight to step 5.

This process consists of 8 closed-loop steps, going from Root (Cause) to Fruit (Effect):

**Environment (Good)**

**Concept (Standard)**

**Mental State (Right)**

**Capacity (Suitable)**

**Population (Cultural)**

**Revenue (Sustainable)**

**Performance (High)**

**Success (Fulfilled)**

This journey begins with the leader orienting toward **Perfecting their own Personality** (the Point). Then, they seek out and go together with 2 "left-hand and right-hand supporters" who are also perfecting their personalities (the Line). The convergence of these 3 people creates a (Plane) — precisely the **Good Environment**. A good environment is a machine that generates 9 factors: Joy, Hope, Belief, Wisdom, Cherishing Gratitude, Love, Tolerance, Humility, and Truthfulness.

When living in a Good Environment, people automatically form Standard Concepts (about being a person and about working), from which the Right Mental State arises, and this crystallizes into Suitable Capacity. Only when these 4 non-material foundations are solid will the extraordinary leader attract Population (of a Cultural nature), from which the flow of Revenue, Performance, and Fulfilled Success automatically manifests without needing to be pursued exhaustingly. *(Note: The method for this leader to train and replicate capacity will be explored and clarified in depth in the topic: The Roadmap to Becoming an All-Around Leader - The Cultural Entrepreneur.)*

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

**2.1. Concepts**

**1. The Extraordinary Leader**

**Definition:** "Extraordinary is daring to do what others dare not do, daring to think what others dare not think."

**Meaning within the topic:** Positions the portrait and temperament of a person who leads. The "extraordinary" quality here is not magic, but the courage to dare to swim against the tide of society, choosing the path of building a sustainable foundation instead of chasing surface-level results.

**Transformative role:** Activates the courage within the learner, helping them break through mental limits and the pressure of short-term achievement in order to remain steadfast toward long-term goals.

**2. The 8-Step Success Process**

**Definition:** A chain of links operating from cause to effect, comprising: (1) Good Environment -> (2) Standard Concept -> (3) Right Mental State -> (4) Suitable Capacity -> (5) Cultural Population -> (6) Sustainable Revenue -> (7) High Performance -> (8) Fulfilled Success.

**Meaning within the topic:** Provides an absolutely accurate and clear mindset map of the steps that must be present in order to create enduring success for both individuals and organizations.

**Transformative role:** Helps learners stop rushing, know exactly where they stand on the roadmap, and know what to do next so they do not "skip steps," leading to collapse.

**3. Good Environment**

**Definition:** An environment where people feel comfortable and are encouraged to develop comprehensively. This environment brings 9 factors: Joy, Hope, Belief, Wisdom, Cherishing - Gratitude, Love, Tolerance, Humility, Truthfulness.

**Meaning within the topic:** This is "step number 1," the root source and foundation from which every other factor in the process can sprout and develop.

**Transformative role:** Shifts the focus of management from controlling tasks to building a living and working space that nurtures human personality.

**4. Point - Line - Plane**

**Definition:** The law of building a good environment. The "Point" is 1 person orienting toward perfecting their personality. This person finds 2 "left-hand and right-hand supporters" who are also perfecting their personalities, to form the "Line." 3 people with perfected personalities combine to form a "Plane" (precisely the Good Environment).

**Meaning within the topic:** Points out the practical method for beginning to build a strong organization from the smallest possible factors.

**Transformative role:** Helps learners realize the power of consensus and gathering. Leadership is not done alone, but must begin from perfecting oneself and finding the right companions.

**2.2. Key Knowledge**

*(Illustrative diagram: The instructor draws on the board a process diagram of 8 closed circles that progress step by step. The first 4 circles (Environment, Concept, Mental State, Capacity) are drawn in blue ink, symbolizing the "Root - Cause" part. An arrow extends from Capacity to circle number 5. The last 4 circles (Population, Revenue, Performance, Success) are drawn in red ink, symbolizing the "Flower and Fruit - Effect" part that manifests externally.)*

The key knowledge of this topic focuses on dissecting the operating structure of success through the Law of Cause and Effect:

**The division of Cause - Effect in the Process:**

The 8-step process is not a set of disconnected pieces, but a continuous flow of Cause and Effect.

**The CAUSE part (Inner - Invisible):** Comprises 4 foundational steps: Environment, Concept, Mental State, Capacity. This is the process of "Doing Well."

**The EFFECT part (Outer - Visible):** Comprises 4 surface-level steps: Population, Revenue, Performance, Success. This is the process of "Growing Big."

**The internal circulation of the CAUSE part:**

Without a Good Environment, people cannot shape their thinking, therefore **a Good Environment gives birth to Standard Concepts**.

From Standard Concepts in how one conducts oneself and works, people then attain a **Right Mental State** (Peaceful Joy, Tolerance, Cherishing Gratitude).

When the Right Mental State is present, capacity is automatically unlocked, creating **Suitable Capacity** for the organization's requirements.

**The natural blossoming of the EFFECT part:**

An organization with Suitable Capacity will attract a **Population (of a Cultural nature)**.

From people who deeply embody culture, **the Revenue generated will be Sustainable**.

Sustainable Revenue creates momentum for **High Performance**, and finally blossoms and bears fruit as a **Fulfilled Success**.

**The eternal loop:** Fulfilled Success is not the finishing point. The people at step 8 will return to immerse themselves back at step 1, continuing to reinforce the Good Environment, creating an infinite loop of development for the organization.

**2.3. Key Awareness**

*(Illustrative diagram: The instructor draws an image of two people climbing an 8-story tower. The first person uses force to leap straight to the 5th floor; the tower's foundation shakes, bricks and tiles crumble. The second person patiently builds brick by brick on the 1st floor, the 2nd floor... radiating brilliant light, the tower stands firm and solid amid the storm.)*

This is the core section of the lecture. Grasping the process is not as important as breaking through humanity's age-old misconception about how to create success.

**1. Dissecting the paradox of human life: The collapse of "Discarding the Root for the Branch"**

**Establishing awareness:** Master Toàn establishes an awareness: Society and ordinary managers usually begin right at number 5. They jump straight into recruiting "Population," forcing out "Revenue," demanding "Performance," and hold the illusion that this is "Success."

**Inner interaction:** Question yourself and your organization. Does your mind immediately panic when revenue drops? Do personnel keep coming and going, with the team always in a patched-up state? Why is that? Because we are building a castle on sand! We seek the "Flower and Fruit" (Effect) but refuse to tend to the "Root" (Cause).

**Transformation:** Once one thoroughly understands this, the learner "realizes" that every collapse in business or family stems from a lack of the first 4 foundations. This awareness cuts off the habit of chasing illusory achievement, bringing a person back to a calm state to build the foundation.

**2. Extraordinary courage: Accepting swimming against the current**

**Establishing awareness:** The extraordinary leader is someone who "dares to do what others dare not do." Not daring here means daring to refuse the pressure of surface-level revenue, in order to return to the starting point: Number 1 - Building the environment.

**Counter-argument:** Many people say "If there's no revenue, what will feed the troops?" The extraordinary leader clearly understands that money is information and energy. When the Environment holds the 9 elements of light (Joy, Hope, Belief, Wisdom...), gathered energy will automatically draw finance back in.

**Real-life example:** Over the past 15 years, the WiT community has not chased external achievement, focusing only on cycling through the first 4 steps: Bringing people into an environment, cultivating concepts, mental state, and personality. The result is that all-around leaders have appeared and effortlessly created enormous ecosystems on their own.

**3. The eternal power of Environment (The Principle of Osmosis)**

**Establishing awareness:** *A rock is inert, but if soaked in ginseng, it will come out smelling of ginseng too.* People are the same — simply by being immersed in a good environment, one's concepts will gradually become standard, one's mental state will gradually become right.

**Transformation:** Instead of exerting effort to bend, discipline, or correct the mistakes of each employee or each child (the mindset of chasing darkness), the extraordinary leader only needs to focus all their strength on lighting up the Environment with Cherishing - Gratitude, Tolerance, and Peaceful Joy (bringing in light). A good environment will automatically take on the task of educating people.

**4. Point - Line - Plane: You yourself are the origin of the Environment**

**Establishing awareness:** One cannot borrow or seek a good Environment from outside; a leader must begin from number 1 — precisely **Perfecting one's own Personality** (the Point).

**Transformation:** You do not need to change the whole world or the whole company right away. Start with yourself, then seek 2 "left-hand and right-hand supporters" to go together with (the Line). When 3 people with perfected personalities converge, a bright zone (the Plane - a Good Environment) will automatically open up. This awareness helps release pressure for the learner, bringing the belief that anyone, no matter how small, can build an extraordinary empire if they follow the law correctly.

**2.4. 03 Personal Application Examples**

**Example 1: Application to Career and Inner Being**

**Context before transformation:** As a startup founder, I was constantly in a state of extreme stress, suffering from chronic insomnia due to the pressure of hitting monthly revenue KPIs.

**Old awareness:** I believed that a successful career meant quickly recruiting many people (Population) and forcing out money (Revenue). I measured my own self-worth by the profit figure.

**Knowledge applied:** The 8-step success process and the principle of "Do well first, then grow big" (Focus on the Root: Environment, Concept, Mental State, Capacity before seeking the Effect).

**New action:** I stopped forcing sales targets onto my employees. I slowed down, focusing on building the "Point" — myself — by perfecting my personality (cultivating joyfulness, truthfulness, cherishing gratitude). I spent time training my core team in "Standard Concepts" and "Right Mental State" at work.

**Result after transformation:** My inner being became peaceful and light. The pressure disappeared. The core team bonded deeply, their capacity automatically flourished, which in turn led "Population" and "Revenue" to grow naturally and sustainably without needing to force anything.

**Example 2: Application to Raising Children**

**Context before transformation:** I always scolded and pressured my child to achieve excellent grades at school, making the family atmosphere always stifling, and the child grew fearful and withdrawn.

**Old awareness:** I mistakenly believed "Performance" (grades) was the only measure of a child's "Success," neglecting to nurture their soul.

**Knowledge applied:** The Success Process of the Extraordinary Leader — Starting from Step 1: Building a "Good Environment."

**New action:** I stopped demanding grades. Becoming an "extraordinary leader" within my own home, my spouse and I (the Line) established a family environment filled with 9 elements: Joy, Hope, Belief, Tolerance, Love... I cultivated in my child a "Standard Concept" that studying is for growth, not for grades.

**Result after transformation:** The mother-child relationship became close and trusting. Living in a good environment, the child automatically formed a "Right Mental State," becoming proactive and self-disciplined in studying. Academic results (Performance) automatically improved with ease.

**Example 3: Application to Work and the Journey of Self-Transformation**

**Context before transformation:** I worked independently, taking on everything because I trusted no one, leading to exhaustion and stagnant work performance.

**Old awareness:** I thought I had to do everything myself to get the best results, viewing my career as a solitary battle.

**Knowledge applied:** The law of "Point - Line - Plane" in building an outstanding work environment.

**New action:** I changed my thinking, stepping out to find 2 colleagues who shared the same aspiration of perfecting their personality, to serve as "left-hand and right-hand supporters" (forming the Line). Together we spread positive energy, creating a symbiotic working space (the Plane).

**Result after transformation:** The workload was shared. Team energy soared. The resonance of these 3 people created an environment that attracted more talented people to join, marking a major turning point in my journey of self-transformation from "hired worker" to "environment builder."

They apply the principle of: Acknowledgment, Gratitude, Recognizing and Promoting the excellence of subordinates. They turn the company into a "school of human life," nurturing personnel to ultimate maturity.
