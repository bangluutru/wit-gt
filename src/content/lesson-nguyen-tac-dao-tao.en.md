### CURRICULUM: ELEVATING INNER CONSCIOUSNESS AWARENESS
#### TOPIC: PRINCIPLES IN THE TRAINING OF THE INNER-BEING COACHING CONSULTANT

### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION
**A. Topic Objectives**
**Value of the topic:** This topic establishes a "protective fence" for the Inner-Being Coaching Consultant (IBCC). The principles set out are not meant to be restrictive, but to ensure the trainer does not violate the natural laws and principles governing human life. Following these principles protects the Merits and Virtues of the Expert and keeps knowledge pure as it is transmitted.
**Significance for learners:** Helps learners eliminate the pressure of "having to make other people transform." Understanding clearly that transformation is the responsibility and the blessing of the listener themselves allows the trainer to reach a state of ease, freedom, and non-attachment to outcomes.
**Brought transformation:** Learners shift from the mindset of "someone who saves humankind" (which easily breeds arrogance or self-blame) to the mindset of "someone who sows good affinity." The Expert deeply understands the role of Total Karma and Merits-Virtues in determining the outcome of training.
**Output result:** Learners thoroughly grasp the set of principles that "rain always falls"; they establish self-belief and understand that human consensus is the life force and the sustenance for the career of promoting/outreaching knowledge.

### B. IN-DEPTH CONTENT
**THE PRINCIPLES OF THE MASTER TRAINER: FLOWING WITH CAUSE AND EFFECT AND PROTECTING THE INNER BEING**
Among the 15 Source Concepts of the inner-being coaching consulting profession, **Principles** act as the "precepts" that help the Expert stand firm on the path of service without being swept into the whirlpool of human life's problems. The Master leads us to the awareness that principles exist to protect us from violating natural laws and principles. When an IBCC thoroughly grasps these principles, they not only protect themselves but also create a training environment full of humanity and wisdom.
#### I. The Principle of the "Rain Shower" and Human Transformation
The Master establishes a liberating awareness for every trainer: *"Rain always falls; a tree with roots will draw it in by itself."* Human knowledge is like a rain shower falling broadly over the world, without discriminating among recipients. However, whether or not a person transforms, whether or not they receive the knowledge, depends entirely on their root system (capacity for absorption) and their own Total Karma.
A master Expert understands that whether the listener transforms is governed by their **Total Karma** and their store of **Merits and Virtues**. We are only the "good affinity" that supports them — we must never become attached or arrogant when we see a learner change, nor should we feel sad when we see they have not yet grasped it. This awareness helps the Expert let go of every desire to change other people, and instead return to cultivating themselves so as to become the highest-quality "good affinity."
#### II. Humility in Role: "Without me, there will be someone else better"
To eliminate arrogance — the "weapon" that destroys a teacher's blessings the fastest — the Master hands us a golden principle: *"If we did not exist, there would be others who could help them tens of thousands of times better than we can."*
The fact that we are given the support and opportunity to share knowledge with someone is due to **fitting causes and conditions**. We are not the sole saviour. This principle helps the Expert always maintain an attitude of eagerness to learn, humility, and appreciation for the opportunity to create value. When the trainer is not attached to their ego (self-image/status), knowledge will be transmitted objectively, powerfully, and will go straight into the listener's inner being.
#### III. The Life Force and Sustenance of the Knowledge Promoter
The IBCC is, in essence, a great Promoter of human-life knowledge. For the training career to endure, the Expert must firmly grasp the principle: *"Self-belief is the life force; human consensus is the sustenance."*
The Expert's capability does not come from speaking technique but from **Self-Belief** based on having a clear mental image, a clear concept, and an inner electromagnetic vibration frequency that resonates with the knowledge. Besides that, the learners' consensus is not meant to satisfy the ego, but is the nourishing source of energy that allows the knowledge to spread. The Expert helps the listener gain a clear mental image and a clear concept so they can decide their own life for themselves — the Expert must never decide on behalf of the recipient.
#### IV. Recognizing Needs: From Hidden Needs to Human-Life Solutions
The modern training principle emphasizes: *"Human beings are never without needs; needs can only be hidden or manifest."* The Expert's task is not to force others to learn, but through outreach to **activate the hidden needs** within them into manifest needs. Once the need has manifested, the Expert then provides the complete human-life solution to help them thoroughly grasp cause-condition-effect. This is a "supply" process that follows "demand," helping the trainer avoid wasting resources and always receive appreciation from learners.
#### V. Conclusion: Peaceful Service Built on the Foundation of Principles
Adhering to the training principles helps the Expert become a blazing "furnace," lighting up their own life and warming the world. When they understand that they are "no one" — merely one link in the network of causes and conditions that helps others accumulate merits and virtues — the Expert officially reaches the realm of effortless, easeful training.
**Core message:** Remember that knowledge belongs to humanity, transformation belongs to the learner, and the opportunity to share is the Expert's privilege. When you hold fast to these principles, every class you teach becomes an act of accumulating boundless merit, contributing to spreading the philosophy of root-level education across the five continents.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
#### 2.1. Concepts
**Principle:** Guidelines for behavior set in place to help an individual avoid violating natural laws and principles.
**The "Rain Shower" Principle:** A concept indicating that knowledge (rain) is always broadly present; whether it is received depends entirely on each person's root system (capacity for absorption and Total Karma).
**Consensus:** The "sustenance" of the Promoter, which helps sustain and expand the training energy field.
#### 2.2. Key Knowledge
**The 4 core principles of the trainer:** (1) Self-belief is the life force, consensus is the sustenance. (2) Help the listener gain a clear mental image and clear concept — do not decide on their behalf. (3) Sell nothing; receiving payment is the beginning of helping. (4) The listener's transformation is determined by their own Total Karma and Merits-Virtues.
**The role of Affinity/Conditions in training:** The Expert is supported in helping the listener due to fitting causes and conditions. If not for us, someone else, tens of thousands of times better, would support them.
**The mechanism for activating needs:** The trainer does not search for new needs but instead promotes/reaches out so that a person's hidden needs surface, then provides the solution.
**The 1-2-20-500-10,000 diagram:** This is the principle of replicating experts, focusing on cultivating 500 all-around leaders to spread the knowledge to millions of people.
#### 2.3. Key Awareness
**Establishing awareness 1 - Transformation is not caused by the Expert:** The Master establishes: *"The learner's transformation is determined by their Total Karma."* The Expert is only the good affinity that supports the setting.
**Establishing awareness 2 - Transcending attachment:** The Expert needs to clearly recognize: *"If not for me, the learner will meet someone else better."* This helps eliminate the mindset of possessiveness and arrogance.
**Establishing awareness 3 - The Supply-Demand process:** Recognizing that human needs are always present in a hidden form. The Expert does not go "sell" knowledge but goes to "promote/reach out" reality in order to activate the need.
**Establishing awareness 4 - The listener's responsibility:** The Expert helps them gain a clear image and concept, but life and decisions belong to the listener. The Expert does not decide on their behalf, in order to avoid "carrying their karma."
#### 2.4. 3 Personal Application Examples
**Example 1 (The Rain Shower Principle):** When I shared inner-being knowledge with a group of friends, some changed immediately while others reacted negatively. Instead of feeling sad or feeling like I had failed, I applied the principle "rain always falls," understanding that that friend did not yet have enough roots (their Total Karma had not yet been activated) to receive it. This awareness helped me maintain peace and joy and continue sowing affinity without losing energy in the face of other people's rejection.
**Example 2 (Not deciding on someone's behalf):** During a 1:1 consultation with a colleague about a marriage issue, instead of giving advice such as "you should divorce" or "you should stay together," I focused only on helping them clarify the mental image of a happy family and provided source concepts about tolerance. When they became clear on the image and the concept themselves, they made the decision that best suited them. I felt at ease because I had correctly followed the principle of not deciding on the listener's behalf.
**Example 3 (Belief is the life force):** Before standing up to share about the topic "7 kinds of comprehensive wealth," I took time to center myself and re-establish my self-belief by clarifying the destination image of 10,000 experts. When my inner belief was solid, my words naturally carried weight and drew the consensus of the entire hall. I realized that when the life force (belief) is strong, the "sustenance" (consensus) will automatically come to find you.
#### 2.5. 3 Family Application Examples
**Example 1 (Raising children):** Instead of forcing a child to excel at Math, the parents apply the outreach principle to activate the child's hidden needs. When the child sees for themselves the image of becoming a technology expert in the future (clear image, clear concept), they become automatically proactive about studying without their parents needing to push them.
**Example 2 (Husband-wife relations):** Applying the principle "if not for me, there will be someone else better" to reduce control over one's spouse. When recognizing that one is merely the good affinity for accumulating merits and virtues together, appreciation for each other's presence replaces taking each other for granted, helping the marriage stay harmonious and lasting.
**Example 3 (Mutual understanding):** In a family, when a relative faces a problem, other members only play the role of a "furnace" transmitting peaceful energy, rather than going to solve the problem for them. Following the principle of respecting each person's Total Karma helps the family have fewer grievances and less inappropriate interference in each other's private affairs.
#### 2.6. 3 Organizational Application Examples
**Example 1 (Training leadership):** A leader cultivating talent within a company always adheres closely to the 1-2-20-500 principle. Instead of doing everything themselves, the leader focuses on cultivating 20 core personnel to become trainers themselves, thereby creating a resonating power for the entire 10,000-person workforce.
**Example 2 (Organizational culture):** Building a working environment based on the principle of consensus. Every new project has its picture of benefits (mind-wealth-strength) clearly promoted so employees thoroughly grasp it and voluntarily take it on, instead of using authority to manipulate behavior.
**Example 3 (Human resource management):** When an employee fails to complete their work, the manager views it from the perspective of "they are lacking a beneficial source concept" rather than labeling them "incompetent." The organization provides a path for cultivating awareness so the employee transforms themselves following the principle of flowing with the current.
#### 2.7. 3 Social Application Examples
**Example 1 (Spreading knowledge):** Society will become more civilized when human-life knowledge is "immersed" into the community following the rain-shower principle. Those with the right roots and conditions will automatically receive and transform, creating a global network of people rich in inner attitude.
**Example 2 (Social responsibility):** Businesses carry out the principle "receiving money is the beginning of helping." When they earn profit from society, businesses invest that profit back into awareness-education programs, helping the community thoroughly grasp cause-condition-effect and creating sustainable national development.
**Example 3 (Humanistic value):** Establishing 10,000 inner-being coaching consultants is a principle for "reshaping the world." When there is a sufficient number of experts with the right attitude and correct principles, a state of world peace and wisdom will officially be created.
