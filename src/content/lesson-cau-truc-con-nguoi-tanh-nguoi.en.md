## 16 HUMAN NATURES
### (THE KEY TO OPERATING THE INNER BEING AND MASTERING HUMAN LIFE)

#### I. POSITION OF HUMAN NATURE IN HUMAN STRUCTURE
In the roadmap of mastering life through understanding the inner being, after recognizing Truth, we must comprehend the second layer wrapping the Mind: the Nature layer. This layer contains 16 natures wrapped in a polar electromagnetic field, creating continuous fluctuations in our awareness and emotions. According to different reference systems, this layer has its own names: folk terms call it Nature; science calls it personality or emotional states; and Buddhism calls it the 16 Human Natures. Understanding its structure and operation helps us shift from being unconsciously led by destiny to proactively directing our cause and effect.

The 16 human natures are grouped into specific operating systems:
- **Information Processing (4 natures):**
  - **Reception (Thọ):** The process of receiving and absorbing into the inner being.
  - **Imagination (Tưởng):** Contains three mốc (milestones) of time: *Recollecting the past*, *Imagining the present*, and *Projecting the future*.
  - **Expression (Hành):** The process of executing actions or expressing outward.
  - **Cognition (Thức):** The knowing, awareness, or understanding.
- **Basic Human Needs (5 natures):**
  - **Wealth (Tài):** Money/assets and talent/abilities.
  - **Form (Sắc):** *Physical body/appearance* (sắc thân) and *detail-oriented/visual form* (sắc tướng).
  - **Fame (Danh):** Reputation and social recognition.
  - **Eating (Thực):** The need for food and drink.
  - **Sleep (Thùy):** The need for sleep and rest.
- **Electromagnetic Fluctuation (7 natures):**
  - **Want (Tham):** Ranging from greed (wanting for oneself - negative) to generosity (wanting for others - positive).
  - **Temperament (Sân):** Ranging from anger (negative) to satisfaction (positive).
  - **Ignorance (Si):** Ranging from delusion (negative) to clarity (positive).
  - **Pride (Mạn):** Ranging from inferiority (negative) to superiority (positive).
  - **Discernment (Nghi):** Ranging from doubt (negative) to trust (positive).
  - **Ethos (Ác):** Ranging from cruelty (negative) to benevolence (positive).
  - **View (Kiến):** Ranging from stubbornness (negative) to acceptance (positive).

The focus of human nature is Want and Imagination (Tham & Tưởng). Humans without want and imagination are no longer human. Our want and imagination regarding the 5 basic needs (Wealth, Form, Fame, Eating, Sleep) decide the level and grade of our Temperament, Ignorance, Pride, Discernment, Ethos, and View, and direct what we Receive, how we Express, and how our Cognition expands.

Because it is wrapped in a polar electromagnetic field, human nature constantly fluctuates between negative and positive. The mixture of these 16 natures with different amplitudes at any given moment creates our unique character or "Ego," just like mixing colors in different ratios. To master life, we must manage want and imagination. Instead of letting human nature run wild like a driverless car, we proactively direct our wants toward positive targets (like the 7 dimensions of holistic wealth and creating value). When the inner being fluctuates excessively, returning to the Truth through the state of "knowing oneself is hearing, knowing oneself is seeing" (Emptiness of Nature) stops want and imagination, bringing balance and activating serene Inner Peace.

#### II. THE SYSTEM OF 16 HUMAN NATURES
- **Reception - Imagination - Expression - Cognition:**
  - *Reception:* Receiving and absorbing.
  - *Imagination:* Recollecting (past), Imagining (present), Projecting (future).
  - *Expression:* Executing actions outward after receiving and imagining.
  - *Cognition:* Knowing, awareness, or understanding.
- **Wealth - Form - Fame - Eating - Sleep:**
  - *Wealth:* Money/assets and talent/abilities.
  - *Form:* Appearance (sắc thân) and detail-oriented form (sắc tướng).
  - *Fame:* Reputation, titles, and recognition.
  - *Eating:* Food and drink.
  - *Sleep:* Sleep and rest.
- **Want - Temperament - Ignorance - Pride - Discernment - Ethos - View:** Natures that show the level and grade of inner electromagnetic fluctuations.

#### III. WANT AND IMAGINATION – THE CORE FOCUS OF OPERATION
"Want and Imagination are the focus of human nature." They act as the engine that directs the other natures:
- **Directing emotions:** Want and imagination regarding Wealth, Form, Fame, Eating, and Sleep decide the level and grade of Temperament, Ignorance, Pride, Discernment, Ethos, and View.
- **Directing reception and action:** Want and imagination decide what we Receive, how we Express, and how our Cognition expands.
- *Example:* A person with a want and imagination regarding physical appearance will naturally receive (Thọ) knowledge about health and beauty, and their Cognition (Thức) in this area will exceed others.

#### IV. POLAR ELECTROMAGNETIC AMPLITUDE OF HUMAN NATURE (NEGATIVE - POSITIVE)
Wrapped in a Polar Electromagnetic Field, the 16 natures fluctuate from Negative (-) to Positive (+):
- **Want:** Greed (for oneself - Negative) ↔ Generosity (for others - Positive).
- **Temperament:** Anger (when want is not met - Negative) ↔ Satisfaction (when want is met - Positive).
- **Ignorance:** Delusion (-) ↔ Clarity (+).
- **Pride:** Inferiority (-) ↔ Superiority (+).
- **Discernment:** Doubt (-) ↔ Trust (+).
- **Ethos:** Cruelty (-) ↔ Benevolence (+).
- **View:** Stubbornness (-) ↔ Acceptance (+).

#### V. THE PIVOT: MASTERING WANT AND IMAGINATION TO CREATE CAUSE AND EFFECT
Understanding the 16 natures is understanding "destiny" and how humans direct their own lives. To change outcomes from undesirable to desirable, we must master our inner being:
- **Anchor want and imagination:** Proactively anchor wants toward beautiful goals (Holistic Wealth, mastering the 4 aspects of life).
- **Clarify desires:** Continuously cultivate beneficial concepts and mental images to direct wants toward the Positive.
- **Use One-Word Zen (Release - Stop - Cease - End):** When wants regarding Wealth, Form, Fame, Eating, and Sleep go out of control or cause negative electromagnetic fluctuations, proactively apply "Release - Stop - Cease - End" to bring the inner being back to balance.
- **Anchor in Truth:** Receive messages with Truth (knowing oneself is hearing, knowing oneself is seeing) to avoid attachment to Nature and Emotion layers, standing above all adversities.

#### VI. SUMMARY
Comprehending the 16 natures helps us understand the source of our own and others' personalities and behaviors. When an individual proactively directs want and imagination and shifts inner awareness appropriately, they rise above all cognitive barriers and construct a life of holistic wealth that is simple, joyful, trusting, and light.
