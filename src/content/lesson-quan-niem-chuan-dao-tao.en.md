## TOPIC: STANDARD CONCEPTS IN THE TRAINING OF AN INNER COACHING & CONSULTING EXPERT

### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

**A. Objectives of the topic:** This topic provides a system of 9 standard concepts serving as a compass and a solid foundation of thinking for anyone stepping onto the path of becoming an Inner Coaching & Consulting Expert. It helps learners understand that the essence of these concepts is not confined to how to practice a profession, but at its deep root, is about how to be a human being.

**Value and significance for learners:** It helps learners have correct, positive thinking and attitudes about human life and worldview. Once they firmly grasp these concepts, learners will equip themselves with the "seed of the eternal fruit," creating a machine that protects merit and blessings and activates a source of energy that orients a sustainable future.

**The transformation brought about:** Learners shift from being attached to status and position toward a state of "being nobody," so as to be qualified to guide anyone. The ultimate transformation is that learners thoroughly comprehend the perfect combination between the "concept of being a human" and the "concept of doing work," from which they position themselves and live a life with a philosophy, without being deflected or falling into "idleness that breeds mischief."

**Output results:** Learners memorize and thoroughly comprehend the 9 Standard Concepts. They know how to use the law of cyclical cause and effect to apply the concepts to real training practice, thereby attracting people, elevating their own value, building a good environment, and heading toward an everlasting career.

**B. IN-DEPTH CONTENT:**

**1. Concept**

**What is a concept?** A concept is precisely the standard way of thinking, the standard attitude toward the outside world. A person's concepts represent the maturity of their Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, and Qualities.

**A standard concept:** A standard concept is composed of two core elements: **The concept of how to be a human + The standard concept in professional work.**

**Conceptualizing, visualizing, and state-aligning:** Regarding the concepts of an Expert, learners are required to bring them into awareness through conceptualization, visualization, and state-alignment (maintaining a balanced, sun-facing inner state) toward this profession.

**2. Key knowledge points** The career of an Inner Coaching & Consulting Expert must be built upon the 9 Standard Concepts. This is the root system of reasoning that shapes all behavior and thinking in training:

**Teach courage and courage will appear; teach the market and the market will appear; teach assets and assets will appear:** The process of sharing and training is precisely the tool to activate karmic consciousness, karmic connections, and karmic results, creating both material and non-material assets.

**Being nobody is precisely what qualifies us to guide people in elevating their inner awareness:** Letting go of "the standing of the self" helps us avoid attachment, and from there we gain the capacity to guide every stratum of society.

**Having the opportunity to help others elevate their inner awareness means all of life's troubles will dissolve on their own:** Helping others light up their inner lamp is precisely the way we light up our own life.

**Every desire is clarified when we help others thoroughly comprehend Cause – Connection – Effect:** The shortest path to attaining our own desires is to go and help others thoroughly comprehend the root source of life.

**Learners are the ones who give us the opportunity to accumulate merits and virtues:** We take the transformation of people as our life's mission. Learners are not people who bring us trouble; they are the engine that helps us accumulate blessings.

**Give ourselves the opportunity to understand others, and others will understand us. Help others understand themselves, and we will understand ourselves better:** The process of inner training is a two-way process of interaction, reflecting the inner being of the expert themselves.

**The simplest way to become comprehensively wealthy is to do outreach and transform the reality of mastering one's inner self:** Use the tool of outreach to spread the wisdom of human life, thereby automatically enriching 7 aspects of oneself.

**Outreach and transforming the reality of mastering one's inner self helps elevate personal value and upgrade relationships:** The wisdom of human life is the perfect bridge to upgrade every relationship from mere acquaintance to trust and intimacy.

**Inner coaching and consulting is a career that endures through time:** This is not seasonal work, but an educational career of human-life philosophy that spans across many generations.

**3. Key awareness points**

**Establishing awareness of "the Seed of the Eternal Fruit":** The Master would like to establish an awareness: These nine standard concepts are precisely **the seed of an "eternal fruit"** named the Inner Coaching & Consulting Expert. As long as a person grasps the marrow of these nine concepts, their becoming an expert with a standard mental state and suitable capacity is only a matter of time. Once they taste and savor this "eternal fruit," they can live at ease, creating everything they want in human life.

**The shift from "Doing work" to "Being a human":** The Master clarifies a paradox: People often only focus on building concepts about doing work while forgetting the concept of being a human. If one does not anchor oneself in a value-creation tool, and lacks a standard concept of how to be a human, one can easily fall into "idleness that breeds mischief" (idle time gives rise to recklessness and mischief-making in the community). Therefore, a good environment must cultivate both the concept of being a human (Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Qualities) and the concept of doing work (Professional Expertise).

**Applying cyclical Cause – Connection – Effect in training:** When training, learners do not naturally absorb these 9 concepts. The secret is that we must show them the **Effect** (the Dream Life, the Dream Career, the mastery over one's own life of an Expert). When they crave that Effect, they will ask themselves what **Cause** they need to plant. At this point, we hand them the 9 standard concepts (which are precisely the Cause). This law of "cyclical Cause – Connection – Effect" allows wisdom to go straight into the listener's awareness without encountering any barrier.
