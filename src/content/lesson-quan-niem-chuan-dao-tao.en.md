### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION
**1A. Objectives & Target Audience:**
* **Value of the topic:** It provides a system of 9 standard concepts serving as a compass and cognitive foundation for anyone entering the path of becoming an Inner Coach. It helps learners realize that standard concepts are not just about professional practice, but at the root, they are about how to be a human.
* **Significance for learners:** Equips learners with correct thoughts and attitudes about life and worldview. Mastering these concepts provides them with the "Seeds of the Eternal Fruit," protecting merits and orienting a sustainable future.
* **Brought transformation:** Shifts learners from being attached to status or titles to the state of "being nobody" to be qualified to guide anyone. Learners integrate "how to be a human" and "how to work," anchoring their lives in philosophy.
* **Learner's Outcomes:**
  * *Understand:* Master the 9 Standard Concepts.
  * *Realize:* Understand how to apply these concepts using cause-and-effect loop to attract people, raise personal value, and build an eternal career.

**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**STANDARD CONCEPTS IN TRAINING**

**INTRODUCTION – DEFINING STANDARD CONCEPTS**
What is a concept? A concept is a standard way of thinking and a standard attitude about the world. A person's concepts represent their maturity in Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, and Qualities.
A **Standard Concept** is composed of two core elements: **Concepts in how to be a human + Standard concepts in professional work.**
For an Inner Coach, we must bring these concepts into awareness through conceptualization, visualization, and state alignment (maintaining a balanced/positive inner electromagnetic state).

**THE 9 STANDARD CONCEPTS OF THE INNER COACH**
The career of an Inner Coach is anchored in 9 Standard Concepts, shaping all behavior and training thoughts:
1. **Teaching courage, and courage manifests; teaching market, and market manifests; teaching assets, and assets manifest:** Sharing and training are tools to activate total karma of knowledge, connection, and outcomes, creating material and non-material assets.
2. **Being nobody, therefore qualified to guide people in elevating their inner awareness:** Releasing role attachment (sĩ thân) frees us from dính mắc, allowing us to guide all classes of society.
3. **Having the opportunity to help others elevate inner awareness, all life adversities will dissolve:** Lighting others' inner lamps is the way to light our own lives.
4. **All desires are clarified when helping others comprehend Cause-Connection-Outcome:** The shortest path to achieve your desires is helping others comprehend their life source.
5. **Learners give us the opportunity to accumulate merit and virtue:** We take the transformation of people as our life-mission. Learners do not bring trouble; they are the engines helping us accumulate phước báu.
6. **Giving ourselves the opportunity to understand others, others will understand us. Helping others understand themselves, we understand ourselves better:** Inner self training is a two-way reflection.
7. **The simplest way to be holistically wealthy is to appretell and transfer the realization of mastering the inner self:** Spreading human life wisdom via appretell automatically enriches our 7 dimensions of wealth.
8. **Appretelling and transferring the realization of mastering the inner self elevates personal value and upgrades relationships:** Wisdom is the bridge to upgrade relations to trust and intimacy.
9. **Inner coaching and consultation is an eternal career across time:** This is not seasonal work, but a lifelong educational career spanning generations.

**DEEP INSIGHTS**
* **The Seeds of the Eternal Fruit:** We establish an awareness that these 9 standard concepts are the **seeds of an "eternal fruit"** named the Inner Coach. Once a person grasps the essence of these 9 concepts, becoming a coach with a standard mental state and suitable capacity is only a matter of time. Enjoying this "eternal fruit" allows them to live peacefully and manifest whatever they want.
* **Shift from Working to Being a Human:** Humans often build concepts for work but forget how to be a human. Without a value-creation tool and standard human concepts, people fall into "idleness breeds mischief" (nhàn cư vi bất thiện). A good environment must cultivate both human concepts (Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Qualities) and working concepts (Specialization).
* **Applying Cause-Connection-Outcome Loop in Training:** Learners do not automatically receive these concepts. The secret is showing them the **Outcome** (Dream Life, Dream Career, the life of an Inner Coach). When they crave that Outcome, they will seek the **Cause**. Then we hand them the 9 standard concepts (the Cause). This loop ensures knowledge flows directly into their awareness without barriers.
