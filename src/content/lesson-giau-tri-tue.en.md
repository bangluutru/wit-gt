## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** Wisdom has traditionally been viewed as mysterious, reserved only for enlightened beings or erudite scholars. This topic shatters that barrier by "materializing" this non-material element into clear, measurable, and transferable concepts through a simple definition: *Wisdom is simply a state of inner awareness*. It is the core and most important lamp to be lit in the house of Human Life to dispel darkness and guide humans toward the 7 dimensions of holistic wealth.
- **Significance for learners:** Helps learners master their inner being, eliminating habits of blaming, complaining, or clinging to external circumstances. It equips learners with a stable "lighthouse" of awareness, helping them thong dong rise above all adversities and master the four key aspects of life: Inner Being, Health, Relationships, and Finance.
- **Brought transformation:** Shifts learners from "passive" reaction to adversities to "proactive" creation of life. Instead of desiring others to change (the root of suffering), they turn back to change the mental seeds inside themselves (the root of happiness). Thus, they stand firm in their proper pursuits: *enriching, mastering, becoming, and achieving*.
- **Outcomes:**
  - **Understand:** Grasp the Reality Triangle of Wisdom (Information - Energy - Matter) and the 5 levels of inner awareness states.
  - **Realise:** Realise that the peak of a wise person is not holding a massive block of academic knowledge or clinging to any single state, but the capacity to *proactively shift* between levels of inner awareness to fit each real-life context.
  - **Apply:** Fully apply the material manifestation of wisdom through "Diligence" in life. This means practicing the 6 golden words in action: doing everything in a *Simple, Joyful, Trusting, and Easy* manner, combined with *Regular* action and *Mindful* execution to achieve desirable realities.

### B. In-depth Content: Lit the Lamp of Wisdom

Since ancient times, humanity has associated "Wisdom" with enlightened masters, sages, or erudite scholars, making it seem abstract and out of reach for ordinary people. However, the inner being reference system shatters this barrier with a simple and ground-breaking definition: **Wisdom is simply a concept indicating a state of inner awareness**.

By defining wisdom as an inner state, anyone—as long as they understand and manage their own inner being—can become a wise person. The key point is: *A wise person is one whose state of inner awareness rises above any arising problems*. The path of wisdom development is summarized in 5 levels corresponding to 5 states of awareness.

#### 1. Wisdom Level 1: Proactively Choosing Light
Daily, when facing people or events, our inner being automatically reacts to identify right/wrong, true/false, good/bad, should/should not. However, if we wait for circumstances to happen before reacting, we are merely being passive due to the seeds of our total karma. The difference of a person with Level 1 Wisdom lies in: **Proactivity**. Instead of waiting passively, they proactively choose to focus on others' virtues, and proactively use "generous eyes" (nhãn thí) to enlarge the virtues of their spouse, children, and colleagues. According to the Principle of Light, when we light the lamp of virtues, the darkness of defects automatically recedes.

#### 2. Wisdom Level 2: Power of Creating from the Root
If we only use Level 1 awareness, we easily cling to right and wrong, breeding complaints when undesirable events occur. To rise above, we must elevate our awareness to Level 2: thoroughly comprehending that **the root cause of life originates from myself**. All current results come from the mental images in our own minds. A person with Level 2 wisdom lives by the philosophy: *"Desiring others to change is the beginning of suffering; changing oneself is the beginning of happiness."* Thanks to this awareness, they stop blaming, turn back to change their mental images to "shift images, shift reality," and proactively build their future. This is the capacity of "envisioning and manifesting"—clarifying images and concepts and keeping a balanced, positive inner state regarding their desires.

#### 3. Wisdom Level 3: The Raft of Emptiness of Nature and Rebirth
There are life crises where taking responsibility still leaves the inner being heavy. That is when we need to borrow Level 3: **Emptiness of Nature of the inner being (Tánh không)**. A person with Level 3 wisdom always perceives that events, circumstances, and people have no inherent problems. When an undesirable message arrives, they live in Truth, only "knowing they are hearing, knowing they are seeing, speaking, and knowing," without letting the Nature layer (analyzing) or the Emotion layer (reacting with sadness/anger) intervene. Immediately, the cause-and-effect chain of suffering is broken, and the inner being returns to a state of peace and purity. The greatest application of Level 3 is dropping self-identification (sĩ thân), anchoring oneself as *"being no one to become anyone,"* unlocking infinite potential.

*(When Emptiness of Nature is achieved, Level 4 Wisdom—Natural Knowing—opens automatically to guide us on what to say or do most appropriately. However, the absolute taboo is seeking or greedily desiring to open Level 4 or 5, as initiating greed and desire instantly destroys Emptiness of Nature).*

#### 4. The Peak of Wisdom is Shifting
A common illusion is that a wise person must always live in Emptiness of Nature or retreat to high mountains. The truth is, no level is absolutely perfect for all contexts. The peak of a wise person lies in the capacity to **proactively shift** between levels of awareness. Facing crises, use Level 3 to protect peace; resolving conflicts, use Level 2 to take responsibility and recreate mental images; executing tasks or guiding children, use Level 1 to distinguish details and proactively enlarge virtues.

#### 5. Materializing Wisdom through "Diligence"
Wisdom does not reside in empty theories. All non-material values can be materialized. If the Information of wisdom consists of 5 levels of awareness, and the Energy is the protection of Inner Peace - Tolerance - Honouring & Gratitude, then the Material manifestation of Wisdom is summarized in: **Diligence (Cần mẫn)**.
Diligence in the inner being reference system is not working exhaustingly, but doing everything using the 6 golden words: *Simple, Joyful, Trusting, Easy, Regular, and Mindful*. As the Teacher taught: *"Complex tasks made simple makes you an expert. Simple tasks done regularly makes you a professional. Regular tasks done mindfully makes you a winner."*

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Wisdom (Trí tuệ):** Wisdom is simply a concept indicating a state of inner awareness. There are 5 states of inner awareness corresponding to 5 levels of wisdom.
- **A Wise Person (Người có trí tuệ):** A person whose state of inner awareness rises above any arising problems.
- **A Person of Holistic Wisdom (Người giàu trí tuệ):** A person who maintains a state of inner awareness rising above arising problems in all areas of life.
- **The 5 Levels of Inner Awareness:**
  - *Level 1:* The inner state of distinguishing right/wrong, true/false, good/bad, should/should not regarding events, circumstances, or people.
  - *Level 2:* The inner state of realizing that the root cause of life originates from oneself.
  - *Level 3:* The state of Emptiness of Nature (Tánh không) of the inner being.
  - *Level 4:* The state of Natural Knowing (Tự nhiên biết) of the inner being.
  - *Level 5:* The state of Omnipresence (Trùm khắp) of the inner being.
- *Related Concepts:*
  - **An Insightful Person (Người thông thái):** A person with the capacity to resolve current practical problems.
  - **A Clever Person (Người khôn ngoan):** A person who knows how to utilize natural and social laws to achieve life goals.
  - **An Erudite Person (Người uyên bác):** A person with deep and wide academic knowledge across many fields.

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

#### 1. The Reality Triangle of Wisdom
Every non-material element can be materialized using the Reality Triangle. The Wealth of Wisdom is formed by:
- **Information Corner:** The 5 states of inner awareness (Level 1 to Level 5).
- **Energy Corner:** The presence of Honouring & Gratitude (at Emotion/Tình), Tolerance (at Nature/Tánh), and Inner Peace (at Mind/Tâm). *(Detailed in the topic: Wealth of Mental State)*.
- **Matter Corner:** Doing everything in a **Simple, Joyful, Trusting, and Easy** manner. Combining this with **Regular** and **Mindful** action creates the material state of **Diligence**.

#### 2. Shifting from Passive Awareness to Proactive Wisdom
Awareness is often a passive reflex governed by total karma. When facing circumstances, seeing someone as "good or bad" is an automatic reflex (Level 1 awareness). But **Wisdom is proactive**. A person with Level 1 Wisdom does not wait for circumstances; they *proactively* choose to see and enlarge others' virtues, letting defects fade into the dark.

#### 3. Separating "Belief" (Tin) from "Imagination" (Tưởng)
The material manifestation of wisdom is "Trust." However, for negative past experiences, a wise person separates "Belief" from "Imagination/Expectation." They treat past negative memories as "Imagination" (Tưởng) and reserve "Trust" (Tin tưởng) for positive mental images. For the future, trust in positive outcomes is called "envisioning and manifesting."

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **Cognitive Shift 1: The Peak is Shifting.** No single level of wisdom is superior for all situations. The peak of a wise person is the capacity to **proactively and flexibly shift** between levels of awareness (1, 2, 3) to suit real-life contexts and stay above problems.
- **Cognitive Shift 2: Root cause is from me, but not my fault.** At Level 2 Wisdom, we understand: *"Desiring others to change is the beginning of suffering; changing oneself is the beginning of happiness."* We accept that the result comes from our own mental seeds, but show tolerance to ourselves because that choice was the best we could make in the past.
- **Cognitive Shift 3: The Saving Power of Emptiness of Nature.** When major crises strike, Level 3 Wisdom acts as a shield. The first priority is to bring the inner being back to Truth: *"Only knowing I am hearing and seeing,"* without letting nature (analysis) or emotion (reaction) interfere. In that split second, suffering is broken.
- **Cognitive Shift 4: The Simplicity of a Wise Person.** Complex tasks made simple makes you an expert. Simple tasks done regularly makes you a professional. Regular tasks done mindfully makes you a winner. A truly wise person appears simple and light, utilizing Diligence to act rather than complicating human life.

### 2.4. 03 Practical Application Examples

- **Example 1: Relationship Harmony (Hôn nhân)**
  - *Context:* Husband and wife have fierce arguments over finance or parenting.
  - *Applied knowledge:* Shifting between Level 2 and Level 1 Wisdom.
  - *Action:* The spouse immediately lifts awareness to Level 2: "Root cause is from me; desiring the other to change brings suffering." Once resentment dissolves, they shift to Level 1, proactively looking for and enlarging the partner's virtues.
  - *Outcome:* The conflict recedes, restoring mutual understanding, respect, and tolerance.
- **Example 2: Inner Being & Health (Facing Crisis)**
  - *Context:* Receiving unexpected bad news about health.
  - *Applied knowledge:* Level 3 Wisdom (Emptiness of Nature) as a protective shield.
  - *Action:* Cut off all negative imagination immediately. Use Level 3: "Only know I am hearing this news." Do not let anxiety or dark visions of a worst-case future enter the mind.
  - *Outcome:* The inner being stays balanced and calm, allowing the intellect to find the best medical solutions.
- **Example 3: Career & Project Management**
  - *Context:* Facing a massive project that leads to exhaustion and stress.
  - *Applied knowledge:* The Matter corner of Wisdom - "Diligence."
  - *Action:* Simplify complex processes. Execute with the mindset of the 6 golden words: simple to do, joyful to do, trusting the outcome, easy and light, done regularly and mindfully.
  - *Outcome:* Dissolved all pressure. Efficiency increased, leading to smooth career success.
