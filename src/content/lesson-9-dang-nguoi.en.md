## TOPIC: 9 TYPES OF PEOPLE TO IDENTIFY, ATTRACT, TREAT, AND BECOME

---

## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** Provides comprehensive knowledge about the 9 key types of people who govern success in each person's life. This topic equips learners with precise tools to identify, treat, and attract each type, while mapping out a path to "Become" those 9 types of people.
- **Significance for learners:** Helps learners master the principle: **"Achievements are brought by others" (Thành tựu do người)**, thereby dissolving arrogance, and learning to cherish and seize every connection in life. It helps parents and leaders understand the most optimal way to support their children and organizations by perfecting themselves.
- **Brought transformation:** Shifts focus from rushing outward to "seek" benefactors or mentors to turning inward, proactively striving to "become" a Good Connection (9 types of people in 1) to attract outstanding connections naturally.
- **Outcomes:** Learners master the traits and proper treatment for the 9 types of people, applying the process of "Enriching $\rightarrow$ Mastering $\rightarrow$ Becoming $\rightarrow$ Transforming/Being" to personal growth, career, and building a sustainable family clan.

### B. In-depth Content

Human life is a constant search for success, fulfillment, and happiness. Yet, the tragedy of most of humanity is struggling alone, self-deluded by individual strength, and falling into arrogance or feeling too small and lacking resources. To break this barrier and master destiny, the inner being curriculum opens a great philosophy: **"Achievements are brought by others."** No brilliant achievement on this earth bears the independent mark of a single individual. Every step of glory or storm-riding is supported by humans.

To construct an outstanding network of minds, the first key is to identify, treat, attract, and ultimately rise to **"Become" the 9 key types of people in life**:
1. When life is blocked and awareness is dark, we need a **Cao nhân** to point the way and elevate our awareness.
2. When facing difficulties, we need a **Quý nhân** to stretch out a helping hand.
3. To expand our reach and influence, we borrow the connection strength of a **Nhân mạch**.
4. To multiply what we have, we need the supporting energy of a **Thần tài**.
5. To realize grand visions, we long for the dedication and carriage of a **Nhân tài**.
6. To keep our life on track, we need a **Minh sư** to guide us with a dream that encompasses our own dream.
7. To live with moral ethics and understand principles, we seek a **Teacher (Thầy Cô)**—one who speaks, acts, and educates excellently.
8. To simplify complex issues, we need the sharp logic of an **Expert (Chuyên gia)**.
9. To understand human, world, and cosmic outlooks, we need the wisdom opening from a **Thiện tri thức**.

Recognizing these 9 types of people smashes subtle arrogance, replacing it with Honouring & Gratitude for humans. Every connection holds vital significance in our cause-and-effect chain.

However, identification is not enough. The miracle of relationships lies in **Treating and Attracting**. Sages point out that talents are like autumn leaves; if we use money or material rewards to recruit them, they will leave when a larger reward appears. The ultimate way to retain Talents sustainably is to cultivate their **7 dimensions of holistic wealth**, helping them light up wisdom, mental state, and realize their own dreams. To attract a Minh sư, we must demonstrate the 8 traits of talent, respecting and accompanying their mission. To attract a Nhân mạch, we must show appreciation, proactively promoting their image. Each type of person requires different energy and inner nourishment.

If we only run outward to "seek" them, we remain in attach and exhaustion. Sages establish a life-shifting awareness: **"Instead of spending your youth and life searching for the 9 types of people, focus your mind and energy to become the 9 types of people in 1."** This is a shift from "seeking externally" to "constructing internally." Under the law of frequency alignment, when you become a Good Connection, elite people are naturally attracted to you.

To realize this, the curriculum designs a 4-step cause-and-effect ladder: **Enriching $\rightarrow$ Mastering $\rightarrow$ Becoming $\rightarrow$ Transforming/Being**. We cannot skip steps.
- **Enriching (Làm giàu):** Lighting all 7 lamps (Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Qualities, Capacity, Physical Body, Material Assets).
- **Mastering (Làm chủ):** Mastering the 4 key areas: Inner Being, Health, Relationships, and Finance.
- **Becoming (Trở nên):** Shifting energy to become wiser, more insightful, and erudite.
- **Transforming/Being (Trở thành):** Manifesting as the 9 types of people in 1.

Applying this in family and leadership constructs legacy. Parents who master this do not worry about finding external tutors; they perfect themselves. When the child needs guidance, the parent is a Cao nhân; when the child needs logic, the parent is an Expert; when the child needs mentoring, the parent is a Minh sư. This parenting acts as the greatest cradle of good connections, shortening the child's growth path. In business, a leader representing the 9 types of people in 1 gathers talents and builds a sustainable organization.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Cao nhân (Sage / Insightful Person):** One who gives us a concept, a lesson, or an understanding that elevates our awareness; their core role is **Pointing (Chỉ điểm)**.
- **Quý nhân (Benefactor):** One who helps us in a certain aspect or stage of life; their core role is **Helping (Giúp đỡ)**.
- **Nhân mạch (Connector):** One who holds a key role in their connections, possessing a quality network and weight in their voice; their core role is **Connecting (Kết nối)**.
- **Thần tài (God of Wealth):** One whose presence multiplies what we currently possess; their core role is **Supporting (Tương trợ)**.
- **Nhân tài (Talent):** One who dedicates and carries tasks in an aspect of our life or organization; their core role is **Carrying (Gánh vác)**.
- **Minh sư (Mentor):** One whose dream encompasses our dream, possessing distinct wisdom, mental state, personality, qualities, capacity, or life realm; their core role is **Guiding (Dẫn dắt)**.
- **Teacher (Thầy Cô):** One who produces, manages, and educates excellently; speaks, acts, and teaches; makes others listen, believe, follow, and spread; their core role is **Providing morals/ethics (Cho đạo lý)**.
- **Expert (Chuyên gia):** One who gives us logic/thinking to simplify complex issues; their core role is **Providing thinking (Cho tư duy)**.
- **Thiện tri thức (Virtuous Knower / Spiritual Friend):** One who masterfully understands human, world, and cosmic outlooks; their core role is **Opening wisdom (Khai mở trí tuệ)**.

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

- **Treating and Attracting a Nhân mạch:** Treat them with respect and proactively connect and promote their image. Attract them by showing a habit of connecting people and helping others borrow strength from your relationships.
- **Treating and Attracting a Nhân tài (Talent):** When a talent carries tasks for you, do not just pay them; cultivate their 7 dimensions of holistic wealth to help them achieve their dreams. Attract them by possessing a clear, large dream and pointing toward ultimate maturity.
- **Treating and Attracting Minh sư & Teachers:** Treat a Minh sư with respect and accompany their mission; attract them with the 8 traits of talent. Treat a Teacher by listening, believing, following, and spreading; attract them with humility and a learning spirit.
- **Ladder to Become the 9 Types of People in 1:** Enriching (7 dimensions of wealth) $\rightarrow$ Mastering (4 areas) $\rightarrow$ Becoming (wise, erudite) $\rightarrow$ Transforming/Being (9 types in 1).

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **Achievements are Brought by Others:** Smashes arrogance. Every step is supported by the 9 types of people. Gratitude is the root to nurture these connections.
- **Shift from "Seeking" to "Becoming":** *"Don't search for the 9 types of people; strive to become the 9 types of people in 1."* When you become a Good Connection, elite people gather.
- **Shorten Growth Path for Next Generation:** Parents perfecting themselves to represent the 9 types of people in 1 acts as a shield and guide for children, making them "ancestors of nobility."

### 2.4. Personal Application

- **In Organizations:** Leaders bồi dưỡng talents with the 7 dimensions of holistic wealth. Defining oneself as the 9 types of people in 1 and gathering Left & Right supporters ensures organizational longevity.
- **In Family:** Parents apply the ladder to become the 9 types of people in 1, creating a cỗ máy of merits and virtues, protecting descendants for generations.
