## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Inner Consciousness (Tâm thức):** Simply defined, it is inner awareness (nhận thức nội tâm). Inner consciousness contains 3 core elements: **mental images**, **inner electromagnetic states**, and **source concepts**.
- **Operating Principle of Inner Consciousness:** The sequential operation: Inner Awareness determines the Internal Electromagnetic Vibration State (Negative, Positive, Balanced) → determines the Energy Vibration Frequency (Low, High, Super High) → determines the human experience of the human world view, world view, and cosmic view.
- **Management of Inner Consciousness:** Proactively managing the resulting mental images of our desires, managing our inner electromagnetic state when thinking of our desires, and managing our source concepts about our desires.

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

- **The Operating Chain from Consciousness to Life Outcomes:** All human outcomes originate from behaviors, behaviors are shaped by thoughts, and thoughts originate from the deep layer of **inner consciousness**. When we manage our consciousness (images, concepts, inner states), analytical thinking is bypassed or automatically takes place in positive ways, leading to standard behaviors and desirable outcomes.
- **The Core Interconnection in Consciousness:** Consciousness operates through the interaction of 3 elements, with the **inner electromagnetic state at the center**. When the inner state is kept Balanced or Positive, it is simple to obtain positive mental images and beneficial source concepts. Conversely, if the inner state is Negative, it drags in negative images and disadvantageous source concepts, leading to poor thinking and undesirable outcomes.
- **The Connection between Electromagnetism and Experiences:**
  - *Negative Inner Awareness* → *Negative Inner State* → *Low Energy Vibration Frequency* → *Undesirable Life Experiences*.
  - *Positive Inner Awareness* → *Positive Inner State* → *High Energy Vibration Frequency* → *Desirable Material & Mental Experiences*.
  - *Balanced Inner Awareness* → *Balanced Inner State* → *Super High Energy Vibration Frequency* → *Fulfilling Spiritual Experiences (Inner Peace)*.

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **The Peerless Power of Merit & Virtue (Công đức - Phước đức):** The Teacher establishes this core awareness: **"The key to managing inner consciousness is accumulating and protecting Merit and Virtue."** Inner awareness combined with the block of merit and virtue is required to maintain a balanced, positive inner state. Without accumulating merit and virtue in our desired area, we cannot shift our inner state, and thus our mental images and source concepts remain stuck.
- **Do not force surface thinking:** Instead of straining your intellect to find solutions, remember: *"If consciousness is in a good state, proper thinking will naturally arise."* What we need to do is focus on clarifying mental images and source concepts of our desires, and borrow Merit and Virtue to maintain our energy state.
- **Facing and transforming adversities:** When an undesirable event occurs, if we let our inner electromagnetism drop to Negative, our frequency drops and attracts more suffering. The wisest way is to immediately elevate awareness to Level 3 (Emptiness of Nature—knowing we are hearing/seeing) using the energy of Virtue (Công đức) to establish balance. Then, initiate positive wants using the energy of Merit (Phước đức) to clarify positive images and concepts, recreating desirable outcomes.

### 2.4. Personal Application
The Operating Principle of Inner Consciousness is the master key for my self-understanding. In daily life, when facing obstacles in career, declining health, or marital conflict, instead of panicking or complaining, I immediately turn back to check my inner consciousness. By stopping busy thinking and bringing the inner state to Balance (Truth), then activating Honouring - Gratitude to shift to Positive, conflicts are naturally resolved. Setting goals is no longer a physical struggle, but a gentle, joyful, trusting, and natural process of accumulating beneficial images and source concepts.

### 2.5. Social Impact
When this principle is applied widely in corporations, organizations, and family education, it will create a breakthrough in human management. Leaders will stop managing staff through behavioral control or forced thinking, but will construct environments that help the collective accumulate merit and virtue. By shifting inner states and sharing beneficial source concepts, organizations will automatically produce talents who think creatively, behave properly, and contribute fully. This root knowledge is the foundation to build sustainable family clans and turn the nation into a global center of wisdom and education.
