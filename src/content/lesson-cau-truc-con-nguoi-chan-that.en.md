## TOPIC: HUMAN STRUCTURE - TRUTH

---

## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** Borrows the religious/spiritual reference system to provide a lens for decoding the deepest inner seeds ("Inner Core") of human beings. This is the most precious human knowledge, helping individuals trace back to their life source and comprehend their own subtle design.
- **Significance for learners:** Eliminates attachment and identification with external, temporary values (such as wealth, appearance, fame, food, sleep, or the physical body). It helps learners find a solid, eternal anchor amidst a constantly changing life.
- **Brought transformation:** Shifts learners from being manipulated by external circumstances and emotions to holding complete mastery over their inner being. It activates the energy of serene Inner Peace, cutting off attachment and resentment.
- **Outcomes:** Learners understand the 4-layer structure (Mind - Nature - Emotion - Body); realise that "Truth does not come from thinking"; and apply the Mind to receive messages and create Emptiness of Nature.

### B. In-depth Content

The human journey is often a continuous chase for happiness from external things: houses, wealth, status, or love. However, all things in this world follow the law of "arising, staying, decaying, and dissolving," constantly changing across space and time. When anchored to what changes, our inner being fluctuates, fearing loss and giving rise to suffering. This topic borrows the story of "how the demons hid human truth" to awaken us: The greatest treasure is not in the sky or under the sea, but hidden deep within ourselves.

The lecture dissects human structure into 4 layers from the outside in: The Physical Body (made of the 4 elements), the Emotion layer (84,000 bubbles of illusion), the Nature layer (16 human natures constantly shifting along the polar electromagnetic amplitude), and the innermost core: the Mind. The subtle and crucial focus of this topic is the clear distinction between "The perceived" (sound, image - constantly changing) and "The Intention of awareness" (Intention to hear, Intention to see - never arising or dissolving). That is Truth, wrapped in Luminous Electromagnetism (Radiant Energy).

The core message the Teacher wants to transfer is: "Truth does not come from thinking." The moment we initiate a thought of analysis or differentiation, we are already caught in the Nature and Emotion layers. Comprehending this, individuals will know how to "create a delay" when receiving messages from external environments. They use the Mind layer to "simply know they are hearing, simply know they are seeing," touching the Emptiness of Nature of the inner being and activating the eternal energy of Inner Peace.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Truth:** What does not change across space and time. This is the only anchor that is not dominated by physical laws or reincarnation.
- **Mind (True Mind / Buddha Nature):** According to folk terms it is the Mind, science calls it Truth, and Buddhism calls it Buddha Nature. It contains the "Intention to Hear - See - Speak - Know" wrapped in Radiant Energy (Luminous Electromagnetism).
- **Inner Being:** Consists of 3 layers: Mind - Nature - Emotion (excluding the external Physical Body).
- **Mastery of Inner Being:** Proactively choosing and taking responsibility for the inner choices (using Mind, Nature, or Emotion) to master external situations.

### 2.2. Key Knowledge

- **The 4-layer Structure of Human Beings (From inside out):**
  - **Mind Layer:** Contains "Intention to Hear - See - Speak - Know," wrapped in Radiant Energy (luminous electromagnetism - non-fluctuating, eternal light).
  - **Nature Layer:** 16 human natures (Reception, Imagination, Expression, Cognition; Wealth, Form, Fame, Eating, Sleep; Want, Temperament, Ignorance, Pride, Discernment, Ethos, View). The focus is Want and Imagination (Tham & Tưởng). Wrapped in polar electromagnetic fields (positive/negative).
  - **Emotion Layer:** 84,000 bubbles of illusion (fluctuating emotions).
  - **Body Layer:** Physical Body made of 4 elements (Earth - nutrition, Water, Air - breath, Fire - warmth) - the physical manifestation.
- **Inner Operating Principle when receiving a message:**
  - Use **Emotion** to **React/Interact**.
  - Use **Nature** to **Analyze - Differentiate**.
  - Use **Mind** to **Receive**.

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **Awareness 1 - The Solution to the Paradox of Seeking Happiness:** Humans often mistake happiness as coming from possessions (Wealth, Form, Fame). The paradox is using constantly changing things to seek eternal peace. The Teacher establishes this awareness: "Truth is what does not change across space and time." Comprehending this helps us stop clinging to external conditions and turn back to rely on the Luminous Electromagnetism inside.
- **Awareness 2 - "Truth does not come from thinking":** This is the key anchor that the Teacher repeats constantly. The moment we initiate a thought of analysis, right or wrong, we fall into the human Nature layer and lose the primal truth. The secret to touching Inner Peace is simply using the Mind layer to Receive: "Knowing oneself is hearing, knowing oneself is seeing."
- **Awareness 3 - Intention to Hear and See is the "raft" to rise above adversities:** What is heard (praise or blame) always changes, but the "Intention to hear" is ever-present. When an undesirable message arrives, the secret is using the true Intention to hear to create an inner "delay" before Nature and Emotion interfere. The Emptiness of Nature emerges, immediately cutting off all suffering and attachment.

### 2.4. 03 Personal Application Examples

- **Example 1: Inner Being (Emotional Mastery)**
  - *Context:* Being unfairly reprimanded by a superior in front of colleagues.
  - *Old awareness:* Used Emotion to react (resentment, humiliation) or Nature to analyze (where I went wrong, how unreasonable the boss is), leading to suffering.
  - *Applied knowledge:* Truth does not come from thinking; Use the Mind to receive.
  - *New action:* Right at the moment of hearing the reprimand, created an inner delay by simply "knowing I am hearing." Did not initiate any thoughts or interpretations.
  - *Outcome:* Activated the inner Emptiness of Nature. Anger could not rise, protecting personal energy frequency and maintaining composure.
- **Example 2: Health (Facing Sickness/Aging)**
  - *Context:* Discovering wrinkles on my face and being diagnosed with a chronic illness.
  - *Old awareness:* Clung to the physical body, feeling fear and sadness seeing the body enter the "decaying and dissolving" cycle.
  - *Applied knowledge:* Human structure - the Physical Body is temporary, only the Truth (Buddha Nature) is eternal.
  - *New action:* Stopped using Nature to cling to the physical body. Received the diagnosis with Inner Peace, used Emotion to show gratitude to the body for warning me, and used Nature to find suitable nutritional solutions.
  - *Outcome:* Rose above the fear of aging and death. High vibration frequency helped the immune system work efficiently, leading to miraculous recovery.
- **Example 3: Career/Occupation (Releasing Identity Attachment)**
  - *Context:* Startup failed, losing the Director title and all personal assets.
  - *Old awareness:* Attached to the Nature of Fame and Wealth (Identity/Label attachment). Felt useless and lost the reason to exist.
  - *Applied knowledge:* Wealth and titles change over time and are not truth. The Truth within is never lost.
  - *New action:* Understood clearly that titles are temporary "coats." Proactively released the label, returning to the state of "being nobody."
  - *Outcome:* Dissolved all painful torment. When "being nobody," one immediately has the strength to start over and the potential to become anybody.
