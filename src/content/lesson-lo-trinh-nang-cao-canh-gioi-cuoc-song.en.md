## TOPIC: ROADMAP TO ELEVATE THE REALM OF LIFE

### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

**A. TOPIC OBJECTIVES:**

**Value of the topic:** Helps learners clearly establish the ultimate destination of a person's life. Provides a map that precisely locates one's current position and a specific roadmap for climbing each rung of the ladder of fulfillment.

**Significance for learners:** Eliminates confusion and loss of direction in career and life. Helps people escape the loop of "working just to survive" to move toward a life of value, leaving an enduring legacy for later generations.

**Brought transformation:** Learners will shift their consciousness from pursuing momentary personal "success" to pursuing "maturity," creating "happiness," and reaching the realms of "service" and "transfer/culture."

**B. IN-DEPTH CONTENT: ROADMAP TO ELEVATE THE REALM OF LIFE – LOCATING THE DESTINATION AND CREATING A HUMAN LEGACY**

**1. INTRODUCTION – THE PARADOX OF PURSUIT AND THE DESTINATION OF LIFE**

Dear friends, on the journey of human life, the vast majority of us often struggle with the question: *"How do I succeed?"* or *"How do I make a lot of money?"*. But very few people ask themselves a question that gets to the very root: *"Where truly is the ultimate destination of a person's life?"*

The consequence of not identifying that destination is that there are people who work hard their entire lives, reaching the peak of wealth, only to lose their health and family. There are others who wake up confused, not knowing why they go to work each day other than to earn a living to survive.

To thoroughly resolve this confusion, the masters have pointed out to us an extremely precious map: **The Roadmap of the 7 Realms of Life**. Thoroughly comprehending this roadmap, we not only know exactly where we stand, but we also hold in our hands the key to stepping up onto the most fulfilling rungs of human life, moving from the state of "survival" to the state of "service" and creating a legacy.

**2. DEVELOPMENT – THE STRUCTURE OF THE 7 RUNGS OF THE REALM OF LIFE**

The Realm of Life represents the level of development and the living state of a person. From the perspective of Inner Being knowledge, the masters have distilled the entire human journey through the following 7 equations:

**Going to work + Salary = JOB**

**Job + Ownership = BUSINESS**

**Business + Profit (Outreach) = CAREER**

**Career + Maturity = SUCCESS**

**Success + Good Health + Harmonious Family = HAPPINESS**

**Happiness + Helping others be happy = LIFE VALUE**

**Life Value + Transfer = CULTURE**

These seven equations are the 7 rungs. Anyone, no matter what profession they work in, must climb through these rungs to reach the summit of human life.

**3. IN-DEPTH ANALYSIS – THE KEY TRANSITIONAL KEYS**

Knowing the 7 equations is not enough; an inner coaching expert needs to thoroughly understand the **"keys"** to open the doors from one rung to the next.

**1. From Job to Business: The "Ownership" key**

Many people mistakenly believe that going into business means putting up capital to open a company. *Let us together establish this awareness: Ownership here comprises two elements — Actively Choosing and Actively Taking Responsibility.* Whether you are a janitor or a director, if you do your work with a 100% proactive mental state and take ultimate responsibility for the outcome, you have officially entered the 2nd realm: Business.

**2. From Business to Career: The "Outreach" key**

A shop that opens and is run entirely by your own hands is called business. But when we know how to apply the capacity for **Outreach** — promoting the value-creation tool widely throughout society and training people to work alongside us — only then does the profit become sustainable, and we officially build a Career.

**3. From Career to Success: The "Maturity" key**

Even a vast fortune cannot yet be called success if it lacks maturity. A person's ultimate maturity must encompass 3 elements: Maturity in **Concepts** (cultivating Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Character), maturity in **Social Relationships** (recognizing the 9 types of people), and maturity in **Professional Expertise** (thoroughly understanding the 15 source concepts of the industry).

**4. From Success to Happiness: The life-and-death knot**

Very many people get stuck at realm 4. They are dazzlingly successful in society but their health is depleted and their family broken. *We need to together establish an extremely important awareness:* Do not wait until you are successful to start worrying about health and family. **Good Health** and a **Harmonious Family** are two MANDATORY elements that must be added to Success before a person can touch realm 5: Happiness.

**5. From Happiness to Life Value and Culture: The "Helping People" and "Transfer" keys**

Once a person has achieved happiness for themselves, they will feel empty if they keep that formula strictly to themselves. The higher realm is to take that formula and go **Help other people become happy** (helping them anchor in a value-creation tool, helping them become healthy, and harmonize their families). And the very summit of a person's life (Realm 7) is **Transfer** — packaging the entirety of one's reality, source concepts, formulas, and laws into a Culture (an everlasting family clan / everlasting organization) to hand down to the next generation.

**4. CONCLUSION & ESTABLISHING A TRANSFORMATIVE VISION**

**Orienting energy within the Reality Triangle of human life**

Dear friends, from the structural perspective of the Reality Triangle:

**Information** is precisely the *Dream Career*.

**Energy** is precisely the *Dream Life* (Sixfold Life Fulfillment).

And **Material** (the manifested outcome) is precisely the *Realm of Life*.

*Establish this awareness:* Working at a career with your head down, without knowing how to direct that source of energy to become the Dream Life, in order to reach a high Realm of Life, means all effort lacks complete meaning.

**The shift in one's living state (From Survival to Service)**

The journey of climbing these 7 rungs is, in essence, the journey by which a person shifts their own inner state. At realms 1 and 2, a person lives in a state of *Survival and Stability*. Stepping up to realms 4 and 5, they achieve a state of *Success and Freedom*. But only when moving toward realms 6 and 7 — using business as education, treating the transformation of people as one's very life mission — does a person truly touch the noblest state of all: unconditional **Service**.

So what is the greatest lesson today? **We do not wait until the end of our life to reach realm 7.** From the very moment we begin "going to work," fix your aspiration, and locate yourself as someone who will become a **Cultural Entrepreneur** — someone who will "Transfer" beautiful values to humanity.

When the destination is clear, all the difficulties along the path of survival, business, or career become nothing more than small lessons that light the lamp of Wisdom. Firmly holding this map, we calmly master our own lives and build family clans and organizations that endure for generations to come!

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

**2.1. Concepts**

**Realm of Life:** Represents the level of development and the living state of a person.

**The Roadmap of the 7 Realms of Life:** Distilled by the masters through 7 basic rungs:

Going to work + Salary = **JOB**

Job + Ownership = **BUSINESS**

Business + Profit (Outreach) = **CAREER**

Career + Maturity = **SUCCESS**

Success + Good Health + Harmonious Family = **HAPPINESS**

Happiness + Helping others be happy = **LIFE VALUE**

Life Value + Transfer = **CULTURE**

**2.2. Key Knowledge**

To climb each realm, a person needs to thoroughly understand the "keys" that transfer between the rungs:

**From Job to Business:** The key is **Ownership**. Ownership here does not necessarily mean opening a company, but means "Being Proactive and Taking Responsibility." Doing your current job with the mental state of an owner.

**From Business to Career:** The key is **Profit** and **Outreach**. Promoting the value-creation tool widely throughout society. The number and quality of people you help will determine the scale of your career.

**From Career to Success:** The key is **Maturity**. Not clinging to money but pursuing maturity beyond others in 3 aspects: Concepts (Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Character), Social Relationships (recognizing the 9 types of people), and Professional Expertise (thoroughly understanding the 15 source concepts).

**From Success to Happiness:** The key is **Good Health** and a **Harmonious Family**. Without these two elements, success is only a temporary shell that can collapse at any time.

**From Happiness to Life Value:** The key is **Helping People**. Making the transformation of people one's own life mission. Helping them optimize their value-creation tool, helping them become healthy, harmonious in family, and successful.

**From Life Value to Culture:** The key is **Transfer**. Packaging the entirety of reality, source concepts, formulas, and laws into a philosophy to hand down to the next generation to copy and inherit.

**2.3. Key Awareness**

**Establish the destination right from the starting line:** Do not wait until you are successful to think about how to make your family harmonious or how to get healthy. You must locate your ultimate goal at Realm 7 (Culture - Cultural Entrepreneur) right from the moment you start working.

**The Reality Triangle of the Destination:** From a structural perspective: The **Dream Career** is precisely the root of Information; when information is clear, energy is oriented and converges into the **Dream Life** (Sixfold Life Fulfillment); and when there is sufficient energy it materializes into a high **Realm of Life**. If one works a career without knowing how to direct energy to elevate the Realm of Life, all effort is meaningless.

**Awareness of the Living State:** Each realm corresponds to an inner state. People at realms 1 and 2 are in a state of *survival, stability*. People at realms 4 and 5 are in a state of *success, freedom*. Only when moving toward realms 6 and 7 does a person achieve the state of complete *service*, no longer attached to making a living or personal fame.

**2.4. Personal Application**

Applying this Roadmap to break through my own self-imposed limitations. Instead of complaining about a "job" that is just tedious wage labor, I proactively take 100% responsibility for every outcome (Ownership) to step into the realm of "Business." I stopped chasing money for its own sake and instead anchor myself in a value-creation tool (Inner Coaching Consultant), continuously enriching my Wisdom and Mental State (Maturity) to reach a sustainable Career and Success.

**2.5. Family Application**

Within the family, Realm 5 (Happiness) is the most important checkpoint. My spouse and I are conscious that earning money (success) must go hand-in-hand with protecting Health and creating a Harmonious Family (rotating through the 9 golden words in marriage). Furthermore, we package the good lessons and culture of our family clan to "Transfer" to our children and grandchildren, guiding our children to reach Realm 7 of the Realm of Life from a young age.

**2.6. Organizational Application**

An everlasting organization cannot stop merely at Business (chasing profit). The organization's leaders need to aim for Realm 7 – becoming **Cultural Entrepreneurs**. The business creates an environment where personnel not only receive a salary (Realm 1), but are given the right to own their work (Realm 2), are trained in health, and develop their concepts and social relationships (Realms 4, 5). When the entire collective reaches the state of "Happiness," that business will automatically transfer great cultural values to society.

**2.7. Social Impact**

If a nation has millions of people who thoroughly comprehend the 7 Realms of Life, that society will eliminate the toxic competition to survive. Instead, people will compete with one another to "help others be happy" and spread "life value." The vision of the Philosophy of Education at the Root is to create tens of thousands of Cultural Entrepreneurs, exporting the education of human life globally, turning Vietnam into the world's center of education, where everyone lives at the realm of Service and Culture.
