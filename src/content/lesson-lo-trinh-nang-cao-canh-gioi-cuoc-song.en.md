**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**ROADMAP TO ELEVATE THE REALM OF LIFE – LOCATING THE DESTINATION AND CREATING A HUMAN LIFE LEGACY**

**INTRODUCTION – THE PARADOX OF PURSUIT AND THE LIFE DESTINATION**
Dear friends, in the journey of human life, the vast majority of us struggle with the questions: *"How do I succeed?"* or *"How do I make a lot of money?"*. But very few ask a query that cuts to the root: *"Where is the ultimate destination of a human life?"*
The consequence of not defining this destination is that some people work hard their entire lives, reaching the peak of financial wealth but losing their health and family. Others wake up every day confused about why they go to work, other than merely seeking to survive.
To solve this confusion, masters have pointed out a precious map: **The Roadmap of 7 Realms of Life**. Thoroughly understanding this roadmap not only tells us exactly where we stand but also holds the key to stepping onto the most fulfilling rungs of human life—shifting from "survival" to "service" and creating a legacy.

**DEVELOPMENT – THE STRUCTURE OF THE 7 RUNGS OF THE REALM OF LIFE**
The Realm of Life represents the development level and living state of a human being. Under the perspective of inner self knowledge, masters have summarized the entire human journey through the following 7 equations:
1. **Work + Salary = JOB**
2. **Job + Ownership = BUSINESS**
3. **Business + Profits (Appretell) = CAREER**
4. **Career + Maturity = SUCCESS**
5. **Success + Health + Harmonious Family = HAPPINESS**
6. **Happiness + Helping others be happy = LIFE VALUE**
7. **Life Value + Transfer = CULTURE**
These seven equations are the 7 rungs. Anyone, regardless of their profession, must step through these rungs to reach the summit of human life.

**IN-DEPTH ANALYSIS – KEY TRANSITIONAL KEYS**
Knowing the 7 equations is not enough; an inner coaching expert must comprehend the **"keys"** to unlock the doors from one rung to another.
* **From Job to Business: The "Ownership" Key:**
  Many mistakenly believe that a business requires capital to start a company. *Let us establish the awareness: Ownership here includes two elements: Active Choice and Active Responsibility.* Whether you are a janitor or a director, if you do your work with a 100% active mindset and take ultimate responsibility for the results, you have officially entered the 2nd realm: Business.
* **From Business to Career: The "Appretell" Key:**
  Opening a shop and doing everything by yourself is called business. But when we apply the capacity of **Appretell** to promote our value-creation tool throughout society and train people to work with us, our profits become sustainable, and we officially build a Career.
* **From Career to Success: The "Maturity" Key:**
  Vast wealth is not yet called success without maturity. The ultimate maturity of a person must encompass 3 elements: Maturity in **Concept** (nurturing Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Qualities), maturity in **Social Relations** (identifying the 9 types of people), and maturity in **Expertise** (comprehending the 15 source concepts of the industry).
* **From Success to Happiness: The Life-and-Death Knot:**
  Many people get stuck at the 4th realm. They are brilliantly successful in society but suffer from broken health and families. *We must establish a critical awareness:* Do not wait until you are successful to care for your health and family. **Good health** and a **Harmonious Family** are two MANDATORY elements that must be added to Success for a human to touch the 5th realm: Happiness.
* **From Happiness to Life Value and Culture: The "Helping People" and "Transfer" Keys:**
  Once self-happiness is achieved, humans will feel empty if they keep it to themselves. A higher realm is taking that formula to **Help others be happy** (helping them anchor in a value-creation tool, helping them be healthy, and harmonizing their family). The summit of human life (Realm 7) is **Transfer** – packaging all realities, source concepts, formulas, and laws into a Culture (Sustainable Family / Sustainable Organization) to hand down to the next generation.

**REALIZATION – SHIFTING THE STATE OF LIFE (FROM SURVIVAL TO SERVICE)**
* **Energy Orientation in the Reality Triangle:**
  * **Information** is the *Dream Career*.
  * **Energy** is the *Dream Life* (Sixfold Life Fulfillment).
  * **Material** (Result) is the *Realm of Life*.
  * *Awareness:* Working hard without directing that energy toward a Dream Life to reach a high Realm of Life makes all efforts incomplete in meaning.
* **Shifting the Living State:**
  Climbing these 7 rungs is shifting one's inner state. In realms 1 and 2, humans live in *Survival and Stability*. Stepping into realms 4 and 5, they achieve *Success and Freedom*. But only when guiding toward realms 6 and 7, using business as education and treating human transformation as life, do they touch the noblest state: unconditional **Service**.

The greatest lesson today is: **We do not wait until the end of life to reach realm 7.** From the very moment we start working, fix your desires and locate yourself to become a **Cultural Entrepreneur**—someone who will "Transfer" beautiful values to humanity.
When the destination is clear, all difficulties in survival, business, or career are merely small lessons to light the lamp of Wisdom. Nurturing this map, we master our lives and build sustainable families and organizations for the next generation!

**2.1. Concepts**
* **Realm of Life (Cảnh giới cuộc sống):** Represents the development level and living state of a human being.
* **The 7 Realms of Life:**
  1. Work + Salary = **JOB**
  2. Job + Ownership = **BUSINESS**
  3. Business + Profits (Appretell) = **CAREER**
  4. Career + Maturity = **SUCCESS**
  5. Success + Good Health + Harmonious Family = **HAPPINESS**
  6. Happiness + Helping others be happy = **LIFE VALUE**
  7. Life Value + Transfer = **CULTURE**

**2.2. Key Knowledge**
* **From Job to Business:** Key is **Ownership** (Active Choice and Active Responsibility).
* **From Business to Career:** Key is **Profits** and **Appretell** (promoting the value-creation tool).
* **From Career to Success:** Key is **Maturity** (Concept, Social Relations, Expertise).
* **From Success to Happiness:** Key is **Good Health** and **Harmonious Family**.
* **From Happiness to Life Value:** Key is **Helping People** (guiding their transformation).
* **From Life Value to Culture:** Key is **Transfer** (packaging realities, source concepts, and formulas for replication).

**2.3. Key Awareness**
* **Define Destination from the Start:** Do not wait until success to care for health or family. Locate your ultimate goal at Realm 7 (Culture - Cultural Entrepreneur) right when starting to work.
* **Destination Reality Triangle:** Dream Career (Information) ➔ Dream Life / Sixfold Life Fulfillment (Energy) ➔ Realm of Life (Material).
* **Shifting Inner States:** Escalating from survival/stability (Realms 1, 2) to success/freedom (Realms 4, 5) and finally to unconditional service (Realms 6, 7).

**2.4. Personal Application**
Instead of complaining about a boring job, I actively take 100% responsibility (Ownership) to enter the "Business" realm. I stop chasing money alone and anchor in my value-creation tool (Inner Coaching), continuously enriching my Wisdom and Mental State (Maturity) to achieve a sustainable Career and Success.

**2.5. Family Application**
In my family, Realm 5 (Happiness) is the most critical checkpoint. My spouse and I ensure that earning money (success) goes hand-in-hand with health and family harmony. Furthermore, we package family values to "Transfer" to our children, guiding them to reach Realm 7 from a young age.

**2.6. Organizational Application**
A sustainable organization cannot stop at Business (seeking profits). Leaders must aim for Realm 7 – becoming **Cultural Entrepreneurs**. The enterprise should create an environment where staff do not just receive a salary (Realm 1) but have ownership (Realm 2), exercise health, and develop concepts/relations (Realms 4, 5).

**2.7. Social Impact**
If millions of people comprehend the 7 Realms of Life, society will eliminate toxic survival competition. Instead, people will help others be happy and spread life value. The vision of the Philosophy of Education at the Root is to create thousands of Cultural Entrepreneurs, exporting life education globally.
