### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION (GENERAL INFORMATION)
#### A. Topic Objectives
**Value of the topic:** This topic provides the supreme "blueprint" for an individual, a family clan, or an enterprise to overcome the ordinary law of arising and ceasing. It helps shape an entity that exists across many generations, based on borrowing and blending the most eternal characteristics of humankind.
**Significance for learners:** It awakens the great aspiration within each person. It helps learners realize that no matter where they start from, as long as they raise the right "concept/intention" and anchor onto the right foundation, they can fully become the one who lays the foundation for a thousand-year legacy.
**Brought transformation:** A shift from "survival" thinking (seeking short-term benefit, grasping for oneself) to "everlasting" thinking (creating value, nurturing talent). Learners automatically let go of personal arrogance to merge into a "Collective Consciousness," attracting the Group of Outstanding Wisdom to walk alongside them.
**Output result (Understand – Realize – Apply):**
*Understand:* Thoroughly grasp the definition, the 3 core characteristics, and the operating axis (Foundation – Guiding Principle – Purpose) of an everlasting organization.
*Realize:* The point of thorough realization that: an organization will never collapse if its benefit is entirely aligned with the benefit of society and humankind.
*Apply:* Know how to establish and gather the Group of Outstanding Wisdom; know how to write "Family Instructions" for a family clan or a "Culture Handbook" for an enterprise.
#### B. In-Depth Content (Refined Version)
Human history has witnessed the rise and fall of countless dynasties, powerful family clans, and enormous business empires. Most of these organizations disintegrate after only a few generations for one core reason: they were built on the foundation of control, greed, and personal benefit. When the founder passes away or when material wealth is depleted, the "thread" binding people together vanishes, dragging down the entire system with it.
Facing this harsh law, the Philosophy of Education at the Root opens up a great vision, giving us a concept with power that transcends space and time: **the Everlasting Organization**.
The first point of awakened awareness is that we do not need to invent any new model ourselves. The wisdom lies in the fact that we "borrow strength" from the 3 forms of organization with the most powerful vitality in human history: **Family, Religion (Faith), and Righteousness/Moral Principle**. Imagine a business or a community that carries within it the unconditional love and the responsibility of passing on tradition from *Family*; that possesses the ability to manage craving/desire and the cohesive, aligned communal activity of *Religion*; while at the same time absolutely upholding what is right and the law, as in *Righteousness/Moral Principle*. These three factors blend together to form an eternal "glue," making people willingly bind themselves together for lifetimes to come.
However, good characteristics are only the outer shell. The soul of the everlasting organization lies in its unshakeable "positioning axis." That organization must take **the 4 driving forces of survival (Self – Family – Organization – Society)** as its foundation. When every action of the organization brings benefit to all 4 of these aspects, it creates an extremely positive intention. At that point, society and the universe will automatically extend their arms to protect the organization, turning every enemy into a friend. Furthermore, the organization does not take profit as its final destination, but takes **the transformation and maturation of people as its guiding principle**, and takes **the realm of a cultured life as its purpose**.
Such a great machine needs exceptional people at the helm. Here gather leaders who are **9 types of person in 1**, who have an extremely close and mutually trusting relationship with one another. They do not view the organization as a place to exploit labor, but turn it into an environment where each individual can grow rich across 7 comprehensive dimensions, mastering 4 crucial domains (Inner Being, Health, Relationships, Finances).
To build this legacy, everything begins from a single **Concept/Intention**. Do not be afraid that you do not yet have resources. As long as you raise the intention to build an everlasting organization, diligently clarifying the image and the concept with the energy source of Joy, Tolerance, and Appreciation-Gratitude, the Group of Outstanding Wisdom will automatically gather. By grasping and anchoring in this knowledge, you are not merely doing business or surviving — you are planting "the Cause of the fruit of Everlasting Life," building a machine of merit and virtue that will automatically operate for thousands of years to come.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
#### 2.1. Core Concepts
Mastery begins with grasping the original definition. The concept of "Everlasting Organization" is a multi-layered blueprint, distilled with extreme rigor:
**Everlasting Organization:** Is an organization that exists and develops across many generations.
It is an organization that fully carries the core characteristics of **family, religion (faith), and righteousness/moral principle**.
It is an organization that takes **the 4 driving forces of survival as its foundation**, takes **the transformation and maturation of people as its guiding principle**, and takes **the realm of a cultured life as its purpose**.
It is an organization where the key leaders are **9 types of person in 1** and have a **close** degree of social relationship.
It is an organization where people become **more intelligent, wiser, more sagacious, more erudite**; where people attain **7 kinds of comprehensive wealth** and master 4 domains: inner being, health, relationships, and finances.
It is an organization where people are fully equipped with **Heart – Talent – Strength**, whose products and services satisfy **Long-lasting – Large-scale – Wholesome** and align with the **Three Realms**.
It is an organization that takes the standard frame of reference as its perspective, takes beneficial source concepts as its argument, takes the code as its equipment, takes a good environment as its priority, takes rites and rituals as its standard, and takes tools and means as its springboard.
#### 2.2. Key Knowledge
To successfully build this entity, we need to dissect its structural layers:
**The Material Layer (3 Core Characteristics to inherit):**
*Family-ness:* The organization is not merely a workplace, but a place with sheltering, nurturing, and unconditional love. The previous generation is responsible for guiding and passing tradition on to the next generation.
*Religion-ness (Faith):* The management of craving/desire. The organization creates periodic cohesive activities, directing people toward a shared belief (7 kinds of comprehensive wealth), thereby creating shared language, consensus, and companionship.
*Righteousness-ness (Moral Principle):* The organization's operations always rest on what is right, upholding the law and the moral standards of society.
**The Operating Layer (Core Axis):**
*Foundation of Survival:* Cling firmly to the 4 Great Debts of Gratitude (Self – Family – Organization – Society). Missing any one of these 4 legs of the tripod, the organization will self-annihilate its own blessings.
*Guiding Principle (Mission):* Borrow work/business as an educational tool. The ultimate goal is not the product, but how mature the person who makes that product becomes.
**The Convergence Layer (Roadmap for establishing the Group of Outstanding Wisdom):** No individual can build an everlasting organization alone. One must gather a group of advisors to support and share the burden, through the roadmap:
Choose the subject/target for creating value.
Realize/continue the mission.
Do outreach for the project and attract the Group of Outstanding Wisdom (prioritize attracting the Guiding Master and the Talent who bears the burden).
Treat and upgrade the relationship.
Virtue that conducts the world.
