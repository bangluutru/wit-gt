### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION
**1A. Objectives & Target Audience:**
* **Value of the topic:** Provides the ultimate "design blueprint" for an individual, a family, or an enterprise to transcend the conventional cycle of birth and decay. It shapes a multi-generational entity by blending the most enduring characteristics of humanity.
* **Significance for learners:** Awakens great aspirations. It helps learners realize that regardless of their starting point, by launching a correct "intent" and anchoring to the right foundation, they can lay the groundwork for a thousand-year legacy.
* **Brought transformation:** Shifts the mindset from "survival" (seeking short-term gains) to "eternity" (generating values, nurturing talents). Learners naturally transcend personal ego to align with a **Collective Consciousness**, attracting a **Mastermind Group**.
* **Learner's Outcomes:**
  * *Understand:* The definition, 3 core characteristics, and operating axis (Foundation - Tenet - Purpose) of an eternal organization.
  * *Realize:* An organization will never collapse if its interests align perfectly with the interests of society and humanity.
  * *Apply:* Establish a Mastermind Group, formulate family precepts, or draft organizational culture manuals.

**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**THE ETERNAL ORGANIZATION – THE BLUEPRINT OF ETERNAL MERIT**

**1. The Flaw of Traditional Systems:**
History shows the rise and fall of countless dynasties, powerful clans, and massive business empires. Most collapsed after a few generations because they were built on control, greed, and personal gain. When the material resources fade, the bond vanishes, leading to systemic collapse.

**2. Borrowing Enduring Human Forms:**
To bypass this, we borrow characteristics from the 3 most resilient organizational forms in human history: **Family, Religion (Faith), and Morality (Đạo lý)**.
* **Family:** Brings unconditional love, protection, and generational inheritance.
* **Religion (Faith):** Manages desires and implements regular, voluntary binding activities towards a shared belief.
* **Morality (Đạo lý):** Strictly adheres to truth, ethics, and social laws.

**3. The Core Operating Axis:**
An Eternal Organization operates on a fixed axis:
* **Foundation:** Built on the **4 Dynamics of Survival (Tứ trọng ân: Self, Family, Organization, Society)**. Serving all 4 protects the organization's merit shield.
* **Tenet:** **"Take the transformation and growth of human beings as the tenet (Lấy sự chuyển hóa và trưởng thành của con người làm tôn chỉ)."**
* **Purpose:** **"Take the cultural life realm as the destination (Lấy cảnh giới cuộc sống văn hóa làm mục đích)."**

**4. Leadership & Attraction:**
The core drivers of this organization are leaders who embody **"9 types of people in 1"** and share deep social bonds. By establishing this intent with the energy of Joy, Tolerance, and Gratitude, a Mastermind Group naturally gathers to turn the vision into reality.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
**2.1. Core Concepts:**
* **Eternal Organization (Tổ chức trường tồn):** An organization surviving through multiple generations. It embodies family warmth, religious alignment, and moral ethics.
* **Viability Standards:** Products and services must satisfy **Long-term, Large-scale, Good-natured (Lâu - Lớn - Lành)** and align with the **Three Realms (Tam giác giới)**. The leaders possess **Heart, Talent, and Strength (Tâm - Tài - Lực)**.

**2.2. Five Steps to Establish a Mastermind Group:**
1. Choose value-generating targets.
2. Perceive and connect to the mission.
3. Promote the project to attract the Mastermind Group (prioritizing Master Mentors & Talents).
4. Treat and upgrade social relationships to "close" (Thân thiết).
5. Act with virtue to influence the world.
