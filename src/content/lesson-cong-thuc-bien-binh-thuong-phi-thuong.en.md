### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION
**1A. Objectives & Target Audience:**
* **Value of the topic:** It provides learners with a master key to eliminate inner conflict and thoroughly comprehend the root cause of "desiring but not achieving," thereby aligning consciousness to manifest all life realities.
* **Significance for learners:** Eliminates self-doubt. It helps learners realize that past failures were not due to lack of ability, but due to conflict between conscious desire and inner belief. It equips them with the tool to reset absolute belief.
* **Brought transformation:** Shifts the state from weak "hoping/wishing" to "aiming is achieving." Learners confidently master their lives and attract the agreement of people to turn dreams into reality.

**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**FORMULA FOR SHIFTING CONSCIOUS DESIRES TO INNER BELIEF**

**INTRODUCTION – THE PARADOX OF DESIRE AND THE LAW OF MIND**
In human life, most people carry "conscious desires" oriented toward good things. A person entering marriage hopes for harmony; an entrepreneur desires prosperity; a sick person wishes for health. These are completely legitimate desires.
However, a harsh reality frequently occurs: most results people receive run completely opposite to their conscious desires. The root cause of this tragedy lies in an absolute operating principle of the mind: **When conscious desires conflict with inner belief, inner belief always wins**.
If you keep saying "I want to be rich," but your deep inner belief (shaped by past experiences of lack) is anxiety, fear, and believing you will fail, the result you receive is poverty. The mind does not operate on hollow slogans; it executes commands based on the deepest belief system. Therefore, to master life, our supreme task is to decode and align this conflict.

**DEVELOPMENT – DECODING "REAL DESIRE"**
To resolve inner conflict, we must establish a critical awareness, redefining our reference framework about desires.
According to this, **A person's real desire is the clear image lặp đi lặp lại frequently in their mind**.
The harsh secret is: negative, undesirable images are often very sharp and lặp đi lặp lại powerfully in our heads. For example, a woman says "I want my husband to stop drinking so my family can be happy" (conscious desire), but the image lặp đi lặp lại in her mind daily is her husband drunk and messy. Under the inner self definition, the subconscious and universe recognize that: **The husband being drunk is her "real desire."**
The subconscious is loyal but cannot distinguish right or wrong, good or bad. It only recognizes what is lặp đi lặp lại most clearly and immediately executes cause and effect. Realizing this, we find that much of our current suffering is due to us accidentally "desiring" it by harboring negative mental images.

**ANALYSIS – THE ROADMAP TO BUILD STABLE INNER BELIEF**
When we understand the source, how do we align the mind, turning good desires into absolute inner belief?
* First principle: When you raise a good desire but feel anxious or fearful inside (negative electricity), never use willpower to force yourself to "believe." Forcing only worsens the conflict. What we need is using the Inner Tánh Không (Level 3) to switch off negative electricity, stopping irrigation of the undesirable seed.
* Next, we construct Inner Belief based on the 3 pillars of the Reality Triangle:
  * **Information Corner (Clear source concepts):** Clarifying definitions and benefits of what you desire. You cannot achieve what you cannot clearly define.
  * **Material Corner (Clear mental images):** Cultivating Hearing - Seeing - Speaking - Knowing in alignment with your desire to create a sharp picture of the destination.
  * **Energy Corner (Energy vibration alignment):** This is the key. You must maintain a balanced and positive inner state when thinking about it. If phước báu is lacking, use the energy of Joy, Tolerance, and Appreciation/Gratitude to compensate.
When you are **clear on images, clear on concepts, and maintain a balanced/positive inner state**, your conscious desire officially becomes your Inner Belief. Conflict is eliminated.

**THE ULTIMATE SHIFT – THE AGREEMENT OF PEOPLE**
If you stop at Inner Belief, you are only an excellent but lonely individual. To create grand achievements, we follow the formula:
**"Building Inner Belief + Seeking the agreement of people = All dreams come true"**.
* **Inner Belief is life.**
* **The agreement of people is food.**
How do we get agreement? Never by force or begging. The only key is using the leverage of **APPRETELL** (Quảng bá). By repeating the benefits with appreciation and gratitude, we help those around us clarify the same images and concepts. When they share the same language, image, and energy frequency, they voluntarily agree and accompany us.

**CONCLUSION – "IT IS ONLY A THOUGHT"**
Shifting conscious desires to inner belief is not a struggle of willpower. When you comprehend the laws, protecting mental images with Appreciation/Gratitude and leveraging the agreement of people, you reach an inner realm of absolute freedom.
At that time, every grand aspiration in life becomes: **"It is only a thought."** You only need to raise a thought with a balanced/positive inner state, without attachment, and according to cause and effect, the universe will arrange good connections (Duyên lành) to manifest it as material. That is the art of mastering life!

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
**2.1. Concepts**
* **Conscious Desire (Mong muốn ý thức):** What the conscious mind aims for and wishes to possess.
* **Inner Belief (Niềm tin bên trong):** The deepest belief system shaped by the subconscious. When conscious desire conflicts with inner belief, inner belief always wins.
* **Real Desire (Mong muốn thật sự):** The clear mental image lặp đi lặp lại frequently in a person's mind.

**2.2. Key Knowledge**
* **Reality Triangle of Inner Belief:**
  * Information: Clear source concepts (clarifying definitions).
  * Material: Clear mental images (controlling hearing/seeing).
  * Energy: Balanced/positive inner state (tần số rung động năng lượng).
* **The Formula for Success:** Inner Belief + Agreement of People (via Appretell) = All dreams come true.

**2.3. Key Awarenesss**
* **Insight 1: Re-align images to change outcomes.** Stop visualizing the negative. Protect the mind from negative images using Tánh Không and Appreciation.
* **Insight 2: Compensate with phước báu.** If thinking about your desire causes anxiety, it means you lack phước báu in that area. Stop forcing, and accumulate merits through Joy, Tolerance, and Appreciation.

**2.4. Personal Application**
An investor who suffered failures applies the formula: Step 1, clarifies the concept of success. Step 2, changes their mental image by focusing on lessons (Appreciation). Step 3, maintains a balanced mind when thinking about finance, converting conscious desires into solid inner belief.
