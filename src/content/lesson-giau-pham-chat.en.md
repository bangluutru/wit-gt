## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Qualities (Phẩm chất):** The internal properties of a person's inner being.
- **Outstanding Qualities / A Person Wealthy in Qualities (Người giàu phẩm chất):** The collection of internal properties that help a person achieve a state of abundance in both material and spiritual life. Specifically, one who fully achieves the 5 qualities: **Benevolence, Ritual, Righteousness, Wisdom, and Trustworthiness** across the **4 survival dynamics**: Self, Family, Organization, and Society.
- **4 Survival Dynamics (4 Động lực sinh tồn):** The 4 target entities in human life that humans focus on to survive and develop: Self, Family, Organization, and Society.

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

- **Motto of Being a Human:** *Nurture Benevolence - Act with Ritual - Honour Righteousness - Open Wisdom - Keep Trustworthiness*. Human life is divided into being a human (80%) and doing work (20%). To be a human, one must live with these 5 qualities across the 4 survival dynamics. Our existence is already a reason; use this life to accumulate merits and virtues by practicing qualities.
- **Outstanding Qualities Expand Infinite Potential:** When a person lives and acts for the 4 survival dynamics (especially pointing toward society), their capacity is no longer a small individual strength, but borrows the strength of organizations and society. Fullness of the 5 qualities unlocks all inner barriers, attracting massive resources from the cosmos and humans.
- **Qualities Bring "Simplicity to Gain Consensus":** A person can turn any dream into reality based on two factors: Self-belief and Human Consensus. Here, qualities (especially Trustworthiness) are the key for society, organization, and family to say "yes" to what we do. Once trust is established, we easily attract benefactors, talents, and wealth to assist our goals.
- **Qualities Act as the Anchor to "Retain Outcomes" (Giữ Quả):** In the formula *Good Cause + Good Connection = Desirable Outcome*, Mental State helps "Retain the Cause," Personality helps "Retain the Connection," and **Qualities represent the capacity to "Retain the Outcome (Giữ Quả)."** A person can achieve huge success (wealth, status), but without outstanding qualities, those outcomes will erode over time. Thus, cultivating qualities is the strongest protection for all life achievements.

### 2.3. Key Awareness (Details of the 5 Qualities)

#### 1. NHÂN (Benevolence / Love)
- *Content:* Simply understood as love, related to the human nature of Greed for Wealth. Its key point is satisfying 6 needs: basic, safety, social connection, respect, self-actualization, and contribution.
- *Action:* Proactively provide for oneself and contribute to family, organization, and society's fulfillment of these needs. Expand love beyond oneself to the other three dynamics.

#### 2. LỄ (Ritual / Respect & Reason)
- *Content:* Simply understood as respect and reason, related to the human nature of Greed for Sleep. Its key point is Honouring & Gratitude.
- *Action:* Constantly show gratitude for the existence of oneself and those around. Obey laws, follow moral ethics, and construct environments for family, organization, and society to understand ethics and laws. Respect and reason govern the world; he who acts with them has no fear.

#### 3. NGHĨA (Righteousness / Responsibility)
- *Content:* Simply understood as responsibility, related to the human nature of Greed for Fame. Its key point is responsibility and recognition.
- *Action:* Recognize that the existence of oneself, family, organization, and society is for a specific reason or mission. Dedicate, take on collective tasks, and accept responsibility for the development of the survival dynamics.

#### 4. TRÍ (Wisdom / Listening)
- *Content:* Simply understood as listening, related to the human nature of Greed for Beauty/Sex (Sắc).
- *Action:* Listen to oneself and society. Hold the inner awareness that oneself, family, organization, and society need to elevate understanding and wisdom to rise above problems.

#### 5. TÍN (Trustworthiness / Keeping Word & On Time)
- *Content:* Simply understood as keeping one's word and being on time, related to the human nature of Greed for Food. Its key point is holding mental images.
- *Action:* Constantly keep commitments, promises, and be on time with self, family, organization, and society. Make sure actions are clear in image and concept, holding a balanced/positive inner state.

### BÍ QUYẾT CÂN BẰNG 5 PHẨM CHẤT BẰNG TỨ TRỌNG ÂN
(Secret to Balance 5 Qualities through the 4 Survival Dynamics)

Humans easily fall into the trap of cultivating Benevolence today and Righteousness tomorrow, or loving family while ignoring society. The supreme secret to cultivate all 5 qualities simultaneously is: Act based on the priority order of the 4 survival dynamics: **Society/Nation → Organization → Family → Self.**
Prioritize what benefits the Nation and Society first, because what is good for Society, Organization, and Family will definitely be good for Self. Acting for all 4 dynamics simultaneously expands capacity infinitely.

*Action roadmap starting from Society:*
The best way is to choose a Mission and Passion that brings great value to society, such as taking "Education at the Root" as a life philosophy, business philosophy, and education for the next generation. When you commit to helping society understand inner being and lift awareness, the 5 qualities are automatically cultivated and balanced:
- **Righteousness (Responsibility) leads:** By accepting a responsibility to carry a mission benefiting society, you live with Righteousness.
- **Ritual (Reason) is shaped:** Taking moral ethics as a guide to live and do business for society resolves Ritual completely.
- **Wisdom (Listening) opens:** Dedicated to society, you must listen to human needs to help them rise above problems, generating and perfecting Wisdom.
- **Trustworthiness (Keeping Word) is formed through Promotion:** By sharing good things with society (quảng bá), you continuously remind yourself of mental images and concepts, perfecting Trustworthiness.
- **Benevolence (Love) arises:** With the mindset of helping others be happy, love opens naturally, knowing that helping society is helping yourself and family.
