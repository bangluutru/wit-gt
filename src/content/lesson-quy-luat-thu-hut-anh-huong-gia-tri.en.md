When you continuously give value without seeking anything in return, you naturally establish influence.

**Creating Influence:** When receiving pure value, the other person senses love and voluntarily submits. Influence here does not come from power or money, but from the supreme principle that you know how to place other people's benefit above your own, while absolutely never lowering your own benefit. It is precisely this marvelous balance that makes the other person feel a pure love greater than the love they have for themselves, and from there they voluntarily listen and submit.

**Giving Rise to Attraction:** The peak of this loop is when transformation truly occurs — you yourself, and the people you have helped, will radiate a powerful attraction. ### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

**2.1. Core Concept System**

To operate the machine of human transformation smoothly, grasping and accurately shaping the 4 source concepts of this set of laws is a prerequisite condition.

The first concept is **Attraction**. In the inner frame of reference, attraction is not defined by a flashy appearance or clever, technique-based communication skills. Attraction is defined as when a person creates a *difference and distinction* compared to society across eight aspects: Health, Beauty, Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Character, Capacity, and Realm of Life. When you possess a fullness from the material to the non-material that ordinary people yearn to reach, you automatically become a "magnet" of human life without needing to use any manipulative tool.

The second concept is **Influence**. Influence is often mistaken by society as power imposed over subordinates, or financial strength used to command others. However, the law of human life defines that the power of Influence is generated when and only when a person knows how to *place other people's benefit above their own, but absolutely without lowering their own benefit*. If you only know how to sacrifice yourself for others, you will soon deplete your resources and give rise to resentment; if you only think of yourself and forget others, you turn into a selfish person. True influence is when benefits are reconciled, but the intention is always directed toward the other person's good, making them voluntarily submit out of respect.

The third concept is **Value**. Value here is not simply the utility of a product or the amount of money you give. Value is defined by the capacity to *give through the 7 important forms of giving in a person's life*. That is, you use Face-giving (joyfulness), Eye-giving (a warm, embracing gaze), Word-giving (words that bring hope and belief), Heart-giving (cherishing and gratitude), Room-giving (tolerance), Body-giving (compassionate action), and Seat-giving (transferring position or one's seat) to enrich the inner and material world of others. This value touches the deepest layers of human structure, resolving the deeply hidden desires within them at the root.

The final concept, and also the greatest destination, is **Transformation**. Transformation is entirely different from an ordinary "change" — a change can be good or bad, it can regress or progress. But Transformation carries the inherent meaning of development in the direction of light. It is defined as *placing one's heart on the ultimate transformation and maturity of a human being*. When a person is transformed, they go from an ordinary person full of attachments and suffering, to suddenly realizing the root source of life, standing above all problems, and autonomously building a life of Sixfold Life Fulfillment.

**2.2. Key Knowledge**

The subtlety of this set of laws lies in the fact that it possesses two directions of operation: the forward direction on the physical level (phenomenon) and the reverse direction on the level of awareness (essence).

Considering the forward physical direction, for a result to occur out in society, the process must go from **Attraction -> Influence -> Value -> Transformation**. That is, if you want to help someone overcome a problem (Transformation), they must first receive something useful from you (Value). But for them to be willing to open their heart to receive value, your voice must carry weight with them (Influence). And to have the opportunity to speak up, you must have something that makes them notice, become curious, and want to draw near (Attraction). If one follows this forward direction mechanically, a person will fall into the exhausting spiral of constantly putting on a show, building an external shell to create false attraction.

Therefore, the most essential point of knowledge in this topic is that one must **operate in reverse from within one's awareness**. An inner-being expert or an outstanding leader will begin from **Transformation -> Value -> Influence -> Attraction**. Every action must originate from the intention: "I am single-mindedly devoted to this person's transformation." Once you are single-minded in this way, you naturally no longer wish to take anything from them, and your mind will automatically seek out tools (the 7 forms of giving) to bring them **Value**. When the other person continuously receives value without seeing any self-interest, they sense the immense love from you, and you automatically gain absolute **Influence** over their destiny. And at precisely this moment, the energy of selfless giving, combined with your own growth and theirs, will create a natural, pure power of **Attraction**.

From here, we create a machine of **Zero-Cost Marketing** — an infinite loop. The person you helped transform will begin to possess difference and distinction. They become Attractive, and in turn go on to Influence their friends and family, giving them Value and helping them Transform. These new people then continue to become new points of attraction. This wheel of connection keeps rolling on, spreading from one person to 20 people, 500 people, 10,000 people, building a community that shares the same language, the same consensus, and walks together, which no barrier of space or time can stop.

**2.3. Key Awareness**

Hidden deep beneath the concepts and operating formulas are layers of awareness that awaken the inner being, helping learners break through every deadlock in communication and organization-building:

**Establishing Awareness 1: When does someone truly listen to another person?**

*Counter-argument:* We often use the authority of being a parent, a boss, or use money and punishment to force others to listen. The result is silent resistance or people leaving once we lose that power.

*Root awareness:* The law of human life makes it clear: **when a person senses that another's love for them is GREATER than the love they have for themselves, they will voluntarily listen**. If you truly take their transformation as your life-mission, using peaceful joy, tolerance, and cherishing gratitude as the foundation of your treatment of them, that love will permeate through every barrier. At that point, without needing a single order, countless people will still unanimously agree.

**Establishing Awareness 2: A person's transformation is one's Life-Mission, energy is one's Food.**

*Counter-argument:* Many businesspeople consider profit as their life-mission. Many speakers consider the audience's applause as their food. When profit declines or the audience criticizes, they immediately collapse and give up.

*Root awareness:* An all-around leader must clearly position themselves: **"The ultimate transformation and maturity of people is my Life-Mission. The energy source of Peaceful Joy, Tolerance, and Cherishing Gratitude is my Food."** When you hold firmly to this awareness, you become immune to every word of praise or criticism. You work not to take people's money, but for their growth. When you achieve this, the machine of merits and virtues automatically operates, and social resources will automatically flow toward you without needing to compete for them.

**Establishing Awareness 3: Provide a solution, do not sell a product.**

*The problem:* When you sell a product (such as toothpaste, an insurance package, or a course), once someone buys it the cycle ends, leading to the need to constantly find new customers.

*Root awareness:* Do not sell a product; instead **provide a comprehensive transformational solution**. No matter what service you sell, "embed" the 7 forms of comprehensive wealth into it. Customers come not just to buy things, but to be enriched in wisdom, mental state, and health. When you hold firmly to this law, customers will bond for life and automatically go on to attract other people for you.

**Establishing Awareness 4: Transformation and Maturity is a two-way path.**

*Root awareness:* We must not only give value so that others transform, but must also create conditions for them to **contribute and shoulder responsibility**. "Only by shouldering responsibility does one mature." An organization is only truly bonded when employees and customers are given the opportunity to contribute back to the shared ideal. Love is born from being sheltered and protected, but endurance is born from jointly shouldering a great shared aspiration.
