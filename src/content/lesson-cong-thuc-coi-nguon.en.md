### B. In-depth Content: The Formula for the Root Cause of Life

#### 1. Introduction: Searching for the Root of Life's Reality

In the vast ocean of human life, people always wonder: Where does the root cause of our life truly originate? Why is it that, born and raised similarly, some possess a reality of happiness, strong health, and financial abundance, while others are submerged in suffering, illness, and brokenness?

To decode this problem, instead of searching for causes from the outer environment, the wise have pointed out an extremely accurate reference system representing the Scientific perspective: The Formula for the Root Cause of Life. This is a marvelous backward retrieval journey, going from the "Outcome" displayed in the present to trace back to the deepest root hidden inside the human mind.

#### 2. The Chain of Superficial Logics and the Paradox of Change

Let us look honestly at our current life. Everything we have—our job, our spouse, our body shape—all of those OUTCOMES are the direct consequences of our own CHOICES in the past. At the moment of decision-making, the natural inner tendency of humans is to make choices because they feel they "like" it, it "fits", or it is "suitable".

But why do we make such choices? The inner logic chain continues to lead us deeper:
- Choice is dominated by **PERSONALITY / CHARACTER**,
- Personality is not born naturally; it is molded from repeated **HABITS**,
- Habits are formed from **ACTIONS**,
- And actions originate from **THOUGHTS**.

At this point, we hit a huge paradox in personal development. Society often advises: "Change your thoughts, change your actions, practice new habits, and your life will be different." But practice proves that effort to change at the level of thoughts or actions rarely brings sustainable outcomes. Like a tree bearing poisonous fruit, if we only busy ourselves picking leaves or plucking fruit, next season the tree will still bear poisonous fruit. Action and thought are only the branches, not the root.

#### 3. The Supreme Domination of Inner Belief

Standing behind and manipulating all thoughts and deciding choices is **INNER BELIEF**. Every outcome of our reality is a 100% accurate mirror reflecting our deep inner beliefs, not our superficial desires.

Between the conscious mind and the subconscious, a war always exists. When "Conscious Desire" (you crave a happy family, want success) conflicts with "Inner Belief" (you fear brokenness, fear failure), the secret of human life is: **Inner Belief always wins.** You never obtain what you want by consciousness; you only harvest what you truly believe in.

#### 4. The Seed of Mental Images and the Emptiness of All Things

So, where is that deep inner belief constructed from? The ultimate root under the Scientific perspective is the **MENTAL IMAGE**. The mental image is the initial seed that shapes and represents the entirety of human belief.

To break the attachment to fixed images and comprehend the power of mental images, we borrow the classic story of "The Pen". When we hold up an object, humans see it and call it a pen. But if we give that object to a dog, it will view it as a bone. If we place that object in an empty room, with no human and no dog, then the object is nothing.

All things in this world carry the nature of Emptiness. An object or event itself does not carry a fixed meaning until there is a cognizing subject. It becomes a pen or a bone because the mental image seed of the human or the dog projects onto it.

Applying this to human life, our spouse or our child essentially carries the nature of Emptiness. They become wonderful or terrible not from their own essence, but because of the images we hold in our minds projecting onto them. Trying to change others is like using an eraser to rub the projection screen, instead of changing the film inside the projector. Seeking the change of others is the beginning of suffering. Changing oneself (changing the mental image) is the beginning of happiness.

#### 5. Great Inner Surgery: Establishing a Critical Awareness

From this ultimate tracing, we face a major inner surgery. Let us establish a liberating awareness:
**"The root cause of life originates from myself, but it is not my fault."**

Why does the root cause originate from me? Because all outcomes are projected and created by my own choices and mental images. Accepting "root cause from me", we immediately stop blaming and complaining about circumstances. Instantly, the inner world is at peace.

But why is it not my fault? Because at that moment in the past, with that level of awareness, that character, the information, and the situation at that time, that choice was the right and best choice. If we had known a better choice, we would surely have done otherwise. Acknowledging this truth, we forgive ourselves, and the inner being is eternally liberated from self-torment.

#### 6. Conclusion: The Formula to Recreate Life

Comprehending the Formula for the Root Cause of Life hands us the supreme scepter to determine our own destiny. We do not need to take a detour by forcing changes to superficial habits or personality. The only truth is:
**"Clear image is the outcome. Change image, change life. Everything begins with the result image in the mind."**

How do we change the image? Mental images are not born naturally; they are cultivated and shaped through our daily process of Hearing, Seeing, Speaking, and Knowing. To construct a fulfilling future, we must grasp the core action principle: *"Control Hearing and Seeing - Change Speaking and Knowing."*

We need to actively block negative information, images of brokenness, and illnesses from our ears and eyes. Simultaneously, proactively use language of light, speak words of honouring-gratitude, and search for beneficial source concepts to feed into the mind. When we hold a clear, balanced, and positive destination image inside, the manifestation of a beautiful outer reality is only a matter of time. What is in the head will surely be in the hand.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **The Formula for the Root Cause of Life:** The standard reference system representing the Scientific perspective, explaining that the root cause of human life outcomes originates from the seeds of "Mental Images in the Mind".
- **The Emptiness of All Things:** Things essentially are nothing; they become what they are due to the perspective of the cognizing subject. This concept helps break attachments and fixed images.
- **Inner Belief & Conscious Desire:** Conscious desire is the surface craving; inner belief is the deep conviction shaped by mental images. When conflict arises, inner belief always wins.
- **Mental Image:** The initial seed representing belief. This image is cultivated and shaped through what humans Hear, See, Speak, and Know daily.

### 2.2. 5 Key Knowledge Points

- **The Superficial Cause-Effect Logic Chain:** All present Outcomes of life are due to our Choices. Choices are dominated by Personality → Habits → Actions → Thoughts.
- **The Paradox of Change:** Effort to only change habits, actions, or thoughts is a trap of personal development because it is not sustainable, like cutting the top of the grass without pulling the roots.
- **The Absolute Domination of Belief:** Standing behind and manipulating thoughts is Inner Belief. Belief decides choices. All reality reflects belief, not surface desires.
- **Deep Root Seed – Mental Image:** Representing belief is the mental image. The shortcut to changing destiny is going straight from the Mental Image to the Outcome.
- **The Reality Construction Diagram:** `[Hearing - Seeing - Speaking - Knowing] -> [MENTAL IMAGE] -> [Belief] -> Thoughts -> Actions -> Habits -> Personality -> [Choice] -> [OUTCOME].`

### 2.3. 5 Deep Insights

- **The root cause of life originates from myself, but it is not my fault:** Deeply breaks the habit of playing the victim. Accepting the root cause is from me "instantly brings peace to the world" (stops blaming). Accepting it is not my fault liberates the inner being from self-torment.
- **All things carry the nature of Emptiness and Infinite Potential:** The greatest illusion of humans is believing others are fixed and cannot change. Comprehending Emptiness (through the Pen Story) helps us erase the labels pasted on loved ones, realizing they are terrible or wonderful due to our projected images.
- **Seeking the change of others is the beginning of suffering; changing oneself is the beginning of happiness:** Shifts from outward-facing thinking (trying to fix others) to inward-facing thinking (turning back to modify the seed image inside oneself).
- **Clear image is the Outcome – Change image, change life:** Establishes an action principle: Every work must begin with the result image in the mind. No image, no outcome. When the image is clear inside, the outer manifestation is only a matter of time.
- **Control Hearing and Seeing, Change Speaking and Knowing:** Provides the key to action at the root. We cannot consciously force positive thinking, but we must proactively manage the input: block negative inputs (Hearing, Seeing) and construct light language (Speaking, Knowing).

### 2.4. 3 Personal Application Examples

- **Health:** A person always says "I often get minor illnesses" (conscious desire is to be healthy, but inner belief is illness). By controlling inputs—not hearing/seeing news about diseases—and changing how they speak and know about health, they change their mental image to an "Ironman", and their physical state changes naturally.
- **Inner Being:** Instead of self-torment over a past wrong decision that caused financial loss, applying the awareness "it is not my fault" to understand that at that past time, that choice was the most suitable for their awareness. The inner being immediately finds peace.
- **Finances:** Erasing the fixed negative image "earning money is very hard". Proactively cultivating beneficial source concepts about money, changing the mental image to prosperity, and financial results gradually shift.

### 2.5. 3 Family Application Examples

- **Marriage:** The wife is tired of her husband going drinking. The more they argue (seeking to change the other), the more the family suffers. When she changes her mental image, makes the husband's good points larger, and controls her complaints, her mental image of the husband changes, and the husband automatically cares for the family more.
- **Child Rearing:** Instead of labeling the child as "stubborn", parents comprehend Emptiness, realizing the child is a genius or difficult due to their own seeds. Parents change their Speaking and Knowing about the child, creating an outstanding child.
- **Family Direction:** Applying the principle "Everything begins with the result image", the family generations together clarify the image of a "Sustainable Family Clan", and all actions naturally align.

### 2.6. 3 Organizational Application Examples

- **Personnel Management:** Leaders do not use "error-fixing" thinking (changing surface actions) but cultivate the seeds of excellence. They look at employees through the lens of Emptiness to unlock their infinite potential.
- **Constructing Enterprise Culture:** Leaders actively establish the environment (Hearing, Seeing, Speaking, Knowing inputs) filled with light concepts, helping all personnel synchronize their mental images toward the company's shared goals.
- **Sales Breakthrough:** The sales director applies "Clear image is the outcome". Before launching a campaign, the entire team must have a clear mental image of success and celebration. Strong inner belief leads to breakthrough actions.

### 2.7. 3 Social Impact Examples

- **Mass Media:** Society is flooded with negative information, reducing human belief. Educators of the root spread light, helping the community "Control Hearing and Seeing" to recreate peaceful seeds for society.
- **Elevating National Position:** When the young generation is educated to have a clear image of a prosperous nation, exporting root educational philosophy to the world, the aligned mental images of millions will turn the national dream into reality.
- **Resolving Community Conflicts:** Applying the awareness "Root cause is from me", individuals in the community stop blaming circumstances or authorities, proactively take responsibility, and join hands to create value; instantly, the community is at peace.
