# CURRICULUM: UNDERSTANDING INNER BEING – CREATING INNER PEACE

## TOPIC: 7 IMPORTANT OFFERINGS IN HUMAN LIFE

---

## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** Provides the ultimate tool for anyone, regardless of financial circumstances, to proactively accumulate merits and virtues. It smashes the cognitive barrier: "I must have money to help others."
- **Significance for learners:** Enables learners to master their destiny by proactively designing and upgrading their social relationship networks. It transforms them into a magnet attracting luck and support from everyone.
- **Brought transformation:** Shifts the mind from demanding others bring benefits to proactively creating value in a natural, peaceful, and joyful way.
- **Outcomes:** Learners become proficient in applying Nhan, Nhãn, Ngôn, Tâm, Phòng, Thân, and Tọa thí in daily life; utilizing them to protect phước báu and accelerate growth.

### B. In-depth Content

In human life, we all chase "Desirable Outcomes" (success, family harmony, health, finance). Under the law of Cause and Effect: **Good Cause + Good Connection = Desirable Outcome**.

However, a cognitive barrier imprisons humanity: *poor people believe they cannot give because they lack money, waiting to be rich to help; rich people assume money alone buys social connections.* This keeps humans struggling.

Sages have pointed out a precious practice: **7 Important Offerings in Human Life (7 Bố thí quan trọng đời người)**. As long as you breathe, you hold an invaluable treasure to give. Performing these offerings does not depend on your wallet, but on your inner state.

Consider the cause-and-effect relationship. Sages shift our thinking: to harvest the **Fruit** of *a desirable life*, the **Connection** is *Merits & Virtues*. The root **Cause** generating merits and virtues is **the 7 Offerings**. Once we realize this, we stop chasing outcomes, focusing on the core Cause. Continuous practice of Nhan, Nhãn, Ngôn thí accumulates phước báu, bringing a desirable life.

Let's examine the 7 Offerings:
1. **Nhan thí (Smile Offering):** A smile of inner peace bringing life and warmth to others.
2. **Nhãn thí (Vision/Eyes Offering):** Not just a friendly look, but the **capacity to hold the positive transformation of others**. Your eyes must contain their future success, providing energy for them to grow.
3. **Ngôn thí (Speech Offering):** Words of light. An inner educator uses 8 words of light: bringing *Joy, Hope, Belief, and Wisdom* (for new acquaintances); and *Affirmation, Praise, Encouragement, and Constructive input* (for familiar ones). Words hold power when aligned with Trustworthiness (Tín).
4. **Tâm thí (Heart/Gratitude Offering):** A state of being touched when "obviousness" does not exist. Appreciating humans destroys resentment and multiplies good things.
5. **Phòng thí (Tolerance Offering):** A state where *"others' mistakes are 10 parts, but not a single part enters my mind."* Expanding the inner room to accommodate humans, protecting our energy balance.
6. **Thân thí (Action Offering):** Helping is good, but helping incorrectly harms your phước báu. Sages transfer **4 action principles to protect phước báu**:
   - *Benefits others - Harms self:* Do not do.
   - *Benefits self - Harms others:* Do not do.
   - *Benefits others - Does not harm self:* Do with reservation.
   - *Benefits others - Benefits self:* Must do.
   - Additionally, follow a strict priority: **Person who NEEDS us $\rightarrow$ Person who is CLOSE $\rightarrow$ Person who APPRECIATES $\rightarrow$ Our BENEFACTOR**.
7. **Tọa thí (Position Offering):** The highest realm of leadership. Franchising and packaging processes to transfer your position, experience, and seat to others, helping them do better than you. This is the foundation of an enduring organization.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Nhan thí (Offering of Smiles):** Smile offering, bringing joy and comfort to others.
- **Nhãn thí (Offering of Eyes/Vision):** Vision offering, holding the positive transformation of people.
- **Ngôn thí (Offering of Words/Speech):** Word offering, using 8 words of light: Joy, Hope, Belief, Wisdom, Affirmation, Praise, Encouragement, and Construction.
- **Tâm thí (Offering of Heart/Gratitude):** Heart offering, showing Honouring & Gratitude.
- **Phòng thí (Offering of Tolerance):** Tolerance offering, accommodating and not attaching to others' mistakes.
- **Thân thí (Offering of Kind Actions):** Action offering, helping others based on clear principles and priorities.
- **Tọa thí (Offering of Positions):** Position offering, transferring seats, roles, and skills so others can inherit and carry them.

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

- **Action Principles of Thân thí:**
  - Harms self - benefits others (Do not do).
  - Harms others - benefits self (Do not do).
  - Benefits others - does not harm self (Do with reservation).
  - Benefits others - benefits self (Must do).
  - **Priority of Help:** Person who NEEDS us $\rightarrow$ Person who is CLOSE $\rightarrow$ Person who APPRECIATES $\rightarrow$ Our BENEFACTOR.
- **Vibration Accumulation:** Ngôn thí (wisdom) and Tâm thí (gratitude) accumulate merits and virtues. Other offerings protect balanced vibration, accumulate phước báu, and upgrade relationships.
- **Peak of Nhãn thí:** Holding the image of others' positive future, supporting their transformation.

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **Greatest Intangible Asset:** You do not need material assets to give. Everyone possesses these 7 assets. Applying them changes the fate of the poor and protects the wealth of the rich.
- **Root of Good Connections:** Without phước báu, networking is useless. The 7 Offerings attract the 9 types of people.
- **The Receiver is the Benefactor:** Erase the mindset "I am helping them." The receiver gives us the "opportunity" to practice offerings and accumulate phước báu.

### 2.4. Applications (Personal, Family, Organizational, Social)

#### Personal Application
- **Overcoming Self-Doubt (Nhan & Nhãn thí to Self):** Smile at yourself in the mirror and hold your own image of future success. Your energy shifts, attracting others to appreciate you.
- **Work Management (Thân thí Principles):** Stop carrying others' tasks at the cost of your health (harms self - benefits others). Shift to "benefits others - benefits self" by teaching them to do it.

#### Family Application
- **Spousal Harmony (Phòng & Tâm thí):** Use Phòng thí to let go of spouse's daily errors. Actively use Tâm thí to praise their good deeds. The bad habits fade as the good is magnified.
- **Parenting (Nhãn & Ngôn thí):** Vow to hold the image of your child's success (Nhãn thí). Use Affirmation, Praise, and Encouragement (Ngôn thí). The child stops resisting and matures.

#### Organizational Application
- **Succession Planning (Tọa thí & Nhãn thí):** Hold the image of staff growth (Nhãn thí). Package your tasks and nhường positions (Tọa thí) to worthy members, freeing yourself to step higher.
- **IT/HR Resource Allocation (Thân thí Priorities):** Prioritize critical issues (NEEDS us), then core departments (CLOSE), then cooperative ones (APPRECIATES).

#### Social Impact
- **Social Media Cleansing (Ngôn & Tâm thí):** Cease negative comments. Share lessons and gratitude to shift algorithm trends toward positive energy.
- **Smart Philanthropy (Thân & Tọa thí):** Instead of just giving money (which creates dependency), provide education and skills (Tọa thí/Thân thí) for self-reliance.
