## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** Provides the core key to managing the electromagnetic vibration state of the inner being, thereby protecting merits and virtues, and constructing a desirable life.
- **Significance for learners:** Removes dependence on external conditions, helping learners master the principle: *"Mental state decides happiness or suffering."* A person wealthy in mental state sets up an energy shield protecting themselves and those around from negative energy.
- **Brought transformation:** Shifts from passively reacting to problems to proactively shifting three states: **Inner Peace at Mind, Tolerance at Nature, and Honouring & Gratitude at Emotion**. Resentment, anger, and suffering fade away.
- **Outcomes:** Learners practice shifting their mental states, understanding how to remove obviousness to invite the inner touched state, attracting good connections, and activating positive Consciousness-Connection-Outcome karma.

### B. In-depth Content

In human life, people spend their lives seeking success and happiness, but are easily tripped by negative events. A beautiful truth: **"Ways of thinking decide success or failure, mental state decides happiness or suffering."** Do not strain to solve problems in the dark; light the lamp of Mental State to dispel life's obscurity.

To understand human happiness or suffering, we must comprehend the inner structure. The inner being is formed by three core layers: **Mind (Tâm), Nature (Tánh), and Emotion (Tình)**. All messages from the world outside pass through these three layers. How we react is our mental state. Mastering one's mental state means proactively choosing and taking responsibility for inner choices to master external circumstances.

A **Person Wealthy in Mental State (Người giàu tâm thái)** is one who holds the state of Honouring & Gratitude at Emotion, Tolerance at Nature, and Inner Peace at Mind. These are the three core energies of Wisdom, helping us rise above suffering, anger, and resentment. When these three states are maintained, the inner being forms a shield protecting us from negative environments.
- A person wealthy in mental state is not one without problems.
- A person wealthy in mental state is one who stands above problems.
- They are not peaceful because life is easy; life becomes easy because they are peaceful.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Mental State (Tâm thái):** The emotional/vibrational state of the inner being.
- **Master of Mental State (Người làm chủ tâm thái):** One who proactively chooses and takes responsibility for inner choices to master external circumstances.
- **A Person Wealthy in Mental State (Người giàu tâm thái):** One who consistently maintains Honouring & Gratitude at Emotion, Tolerance at Nature, and Inner Peace at Mind.

### 2.2. Three Pillars of the Wealth of Mental State

#### A. Inner Peace (An Vui at Mind)
- *Concept:* The inner awareness state originating from the truth within oneself, hearing, seeing, speaking, and knowing without being attached to Nature and Emotion. When receiving an unpleasant message, knowing one is hearing and seeing without judging is touching the truth.
- *Energy:* Controlling the greed and imagination regarding Wealth, Beauty, Fame, Food, and Sleep using the one-word Zen: **Let go, Stop, Cease, End (Buông, Dừng, Thôi, Dứt)**.
- *Materialization:* Knowing one is hearing, seeing, speaking, and knowing. Material wealth no longer dictates one's joy. Inner Peace activates **Consciousness Karma (Nghiệp Thức)** from the total karma.

#### B. Tolerance (Bao Dung at Nature)
- *Concept:* The inner state of not being attached to anything in the mind due to external circumstances. Greed and imagination regarding others stop.
- *Four levels of treating others' mistakes:*
  - *Forgiveness (Tha thứ):* Taking 10 parts of the mistake, carrying all 10, then letting it fade slowly.
  - *Altruism (Vị tha):* Carrying 6-7 parts of the mistake.
  - *Indulgence (Khoan dung):* Carrying 4-5 parts of the mistake.
  - *Tolerance (Bao dung):* The mistake is 10 parts, but 0 parts enter our mind.
- *Significance:* Tolerance is eternal love. It removes anger and activates **Connection Karma (Nghiệp Duyên)**. Tolerating others is the simplest way to save our own future.

#### C. Honouring & Gratitude (Trân Trọng - Biết Ơn at Emotion)
- *Concept:* The inner touched state before events, things, circumstances, or people, where obviousness does not exist.
- *Materialization:* Recognizing the reason for existence, usefulness of all things, and importance of humans.
- *Inner Touched State:* Feeling that we do not deserve what we receive, yet we receive it beyond our greed and imagination.
- *Operation Law:*
  - *Touched state arises → Obviousness disappears → Gratitude manifests → Resentment is gone.*
  - *Obviousness arises → Touched state disappears → Gratitude is gone → Resentment manifests.*
- *Key motto:* *"Honouring leads to possession, gratitude leads to eternity. What we honour, we multiply."* Honouring is speed, gratitude is direction. It activates **Outcome Karma (Nghiệp Quả)**.

---

### 2.3. Shifting Inner States to Manage Cause and Effect

Our inner being has Mind, Nature, and Emotion. When a message arrives:
- We use **Honouring & Gratitude at Emotion to interact**.
- We use **Tolerance at Nature to analyze**.
- We use **Inner Peace at Mind to receive**.

If we cannot show gratitude, we take a step back to tolerate. If the mistake is too big to tolerate, we return to the root: using truth to receive with peace. Once peaceful, the mind calms, letting us rise to tolerate and find lessons to show gratitude. Shifting between: *If not peaceful, show gratitude; if not grateful, tolerate; if not tolerating, find peace* protects our electromagnetic frequency at a balanced and positive level, activating positive karma.
