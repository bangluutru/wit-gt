**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**FORMULA FOR MATERIAL WEALTH – TRANSFORMATION OF THE THREE STATES OF MONEY**

**INTRODUCTION – THE PARADOX OF PURSUIT AND THE THREE STATES OF MONEY**
Throughout most of their lives, humans run out into society with a single goal: "making money." We trade time, health, and inner peace to own banknotes or numbers in a bank account. Yet, the bitter paradox of human life is: many people work hard to earn a fortune, but after one health crisis or family breakdown, all that money disappears. Building finances on a shaky inner foundation is like building a castle on sand.
This collapse stems from a core misconception: humans only perceive money as **Material**. Masters have pointed out a shocking truth: **Money exists in three states: Information, Energy, and Material**.
Additionally, there is an immutable law of cash flow: *"Cash flow in society always shifts from those with less information/knowledge to those with more information/knowledge."* Money is created to serve human life. If an individual or organization understands human life, helping people raise their health, inner peace, and relationships, the cash flow of humanity will automatically stream there. The path of true wealth does not start by chasing material assets, but is a journey of applying the **7-step Formula for Material Wealth**, turning oneself into a magnet of unlimited prosperity.

**THE 1ST RUNGLINE – FOUNDATION OF INFORMATION (STEPS 1 & 2)**
The journey to wealth must go from the inside out.
* **Step 1: Non-materialize ourselves:** We focus entirely on enriching Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, and Qualities.
* **Step 2: Physical training:** Creating a healthy body and ironman-like health to have enough vitality to shoulder great missions.
At these two steps, we do not see cash, because **Money is being accumulated in the state of Information**. When you are peacefully studying inner self knowledge or sweating in physical exercises, proudly realize: *I am growing wealthy.* Wisdom and a healthy body are priceless informational wealth. Those who comprehend this will permanently eliminate feelings of guilt or impatience because they do not see money yet, and will peacefully build their foundation.

**THE 2ND RUNGLINE – CONVERGENCE OF ENERGY (STEPS 3 & 4)**
When information is clear, energy begins to gather.
* **Step 3: Elevate Outreach - Cooperation - Guidance capacities:** Money starts shifting into the Energy state. No matter how talented you are, if you do not know how to leverage support from people, you are forever working for yourself. Outreach is the key to attracting thousands of people sharing the same language, agreement, and accompaniment.
* **Step 4: Anchor in a value-creation tool satisfying Long-lasting - Large-scale - Human-benefiting, and Alignment with the Three Realms:** (Money in the Energy state). We do not jump jobs or chase short-term trends. We choose a tool that brings long-lasting value (Lâu), scale expansion (Lớn), and human-benefiting qualities (Lành). Especially, it must align with the Three Realms, supported by the Government, the Rich, and the People.

**THE 3RD RUNGLINE – MATERIAL DISPLAY & FINANCIAL ANCHORING (STEPS 5 & 6)**
* **Step 5: Realize the 6 Golden Words in action:** When we anchor in a tool and work with simplicity, joy, trust, ease, frequency, and mindfulness, this is the moment **Money displays as Material for the first time**. Finance begins to flow into your pocket.
But the truly wise, the outstanding billionaires in the world, never stop here to consume. They execute a key action called **"Financial Anchoring" (Khu trú tài chính):** *Using the material form of money to invest back into protecting the information and energy of money.*
* **Step 6: Apply the a^n formula (1 - 2 - 20 - 500 - 10,000):** Instead of hoarding money, they use material resources to train, copy, and replicate thousands of experts and leaders sharing the same awareness. This is the power of non-linear economics.

**THE ETERNAL PEAK – SERVICE AND GLOBAL INFLUENCE (STEP 7)**
* **Step 7: Virtue conducts the world (Đức hành thiên hạ):** When you have 10,000 experts spreading life philosophy and creating value for humanity (transferring the 15 source concepts to society), **Money displays as Material for the second time**. At this time, the financial flow is unlimited and eternal, backed by a massive merit and virtue accumulator of the collective.

From this 7-step roadmap, we establish a critical awareness: **Never let the rich guide you with their money**. If you only seek to earn money on the surface, you will become a tool of the wealthy. Our mission is to non-materialize ourselves, achieving holistic wealth (Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Qualities). When your inner power is deep, you are qualified to sit on advisory boards, guiding the elite of society. The Formula for Material Wealth not only gives you money, but hands you Sixfold Life Fulfillment and a lasting legacy.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
**2.1. Concepts**
* **Materially Wealthy Person:** Someone who possesses large assets and stable income, while maintaining balance and growth in inner self, health, relationships, and family.
* **Formula for Material Wealth:** A sequential 7-step roadmap shifting from enriching non-material causes to replicating value for the community, thereby attracting unlimited finance.
* **The 3 States of Money:** Money exists in 3 states: Information (wisdom, health), Energy (capacity, value-creation tool), and Material (tangible assets).
* **Financial Anchoring (Khu trú tài chính):** Using the material form of money to invest back into the information and energy of money (investing in the maturity of oneself, family, and organization).

**2.2. Key Knowledge**
The Formula for Material Wealth includes 7 core steps:
1. **Non-materialize ourselves:** (Information state). Enriching Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, and Qualities.
2. **Physical training:** (Information state). Nurturing a healthy body to sustain grand projects.
3. **Elevate Outreach - Cooperation - Guidance capacities:** (Energy state). Leveraging support from people.
4. **Anchor in a Long-lasting - Large-scale - Human-benefiting tool, aligning with the Three Realms:** (Energy state).
5. **Realize the 6 Golden Words in action:** (Material display 1st time). Simplicity, Joy, Trust, Ease, Frequency, Mindfulness.
6. **Apply the a^n formula (1 - 2 - 20 - 500 - 10,000):** (Investing back in Information & Energy). Replicating teams of experts.
7. **Virtue conducts the world (Đức hành thiên hạ):** (Material display 2nd time). Attracting eternal cash flow through human life legacy.

**2.3. Key Awarenesss**
* **Insight 1: Money is Information and Energy.** Do not be impatient if you don't see cash. Studying inner self and physical exercise is "making money" in the information state.
* **Insight 2: Use material to protect non-material.** Wise people use money to anchor into health, children's education, and organizational maturity. Otherwise, money will disappear due to depleted merits.
* **Insight 3: Grow holistically wealthy to guide society.** If you only chase money, you will be directed by the rich. When you are holistically wealthy, you are qualified to guide the super-rich.

The couple agreed on a life destination, converting their home into a merit accumulator (hosting book clubs, sharing inner peace). Result: Husband and wife shared the same language, attracting supportive connections for both life and finance.
