## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Total Karma (Tổng nghiệp):** The sum of all thoughts and actions across hằng hà sa số (countless) lifetimes. It includes the Total Karma of Knowledge, Total Karma of Relationships, and Total Karma of Outcomes.
- **Total Karma of Knowledge (Tổng nghiệp Thức):** The sum of all understanding across hằng hà sa số lifetimes.
- **Total Karma of Relationships (Tổng nghiệp Duyên):** The sum of all human connections and relationships across hằng hà sa số lifetimes.
- **Total Karma of Outcomes (Tổng nghiệp Quả):** The sum of all life outcomes and realities across hằng hà sa số lifetimes.
- **Subconscious (Tiềm thức / Tàng thức):** The storehouse where everything we Hear - See - Speak - Know is saved through the Nature and Emotion layers. It is compared to a fertile garden containing infinite seeds, plants, and fruits of Knowledge, Relationships, and Outcomes.
- **Habituation (Huân tập):** The process where what we Hear - See - Speak - Know through the Nature and Emotion layers is recorded and stored in the Subconscious.
- **Operating Principle of the Subconscious:** In the subconscious garden, there is a "garden owner" named **Ý (Intention)**. When "Mr. Ý" visits a seed (recalls or thinks of something), our inner state at that moment decides the watering and fertilizing:
  - Inner state at **Negative (-)**: Produces *Undesirable outcomes*.
  - Inner state at **Positive (+)**: Produces *Desirable outcomes*.
  - Inner state at **Balanced (0)**: Produces *Neither desirable nor undesirable outcomes* (stops karma).
- **Awareness of Total Karma (Thinking and Karma Destiny):** Before humans possess thinking, their life is decided by total karma. Once thinking is developed, life is still decided by total karma, but humans gain the right to choose the desired total karma.
- **Keys to Activate Total Karma:**
  - **Inner Peace (An vui)** activates *Total Karma of Knowledge* (Nghiệp thức).
  - **Tolerance (Bao dung)** activates *Total Karma of Relationships* (Nghiệp duyên).
  - **Honouring - Gratitude (Trân trọng Biết ơn)** activates *Total Karma of Outcomes* (Nghiệp quả).

### 2.4. Personal Application

Understanding Total Karma and the Subconscious is the master key for me to self-determine my own life:

If I think about finance, marriage, or health and feel anxious or fearful (Negative state), I know the undesirable seed is being watered. Immediately, I release and bring my awareness to the Balanced state (simply knowing I am hearing, simply knowing I am seeing) to cut off the nutrient source of the bitter fruit. Afterwards, I proactively think of my desires with a Positive state to bud the desirable fruits.
- **Activating the treasure of wisdom:** I am no longer limited by what I study in this single lifetime. By practicing the state of Inner Peace, I can access my "Total Karma of Knowledge," touching untaught knowledge accumulated across countless lifetimes, helping me understand human life thong dong (leisurely) and naturally.
