## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** Provides a standard reference system to redefine wealth regarding material assets. Shifting from chasing money blindly, it transfers the **"7-Step Formula for Sustainable Material Wealth Accumulation,"** helping learners thoroughly understand how to attract finance by elevating their core values.
- **Significance for learners:** Helps learners release themselves from the pressure of survival, realizing that material wealth is the natural outcome of inner peace, physical health, and expanded capacity.
- **Brought transformation:** Shifts consciousness from "seeking money" to "becoming a magnet attracting money" through non-materializing oneself and anchoring to value-creating tools.
- **Outcomes:** Learners realise the power of "materializing the non-material" and "non-materializing material objects." They understand using money to elevate quality of life and create value rather than becoming slaves to material things.

### B. In-depth Content

In human life, most people spend their youth and energy seeking "matter" (money, assets) but feel insecure and lacking. The root of this instability is going against natural law: trying to reap the material "Fruit" (Quả) externally while forgetting to plant the non-material "Cause" (Nhân) internally.

From the inner being perspective, money or material wealth is Information, Energy, and Matter. When we do not possess a certain material object, it is not because we cannot have it, but because our internal "Information" and "Energy vibration frequency" are out of alignment with it. Once you elevate your frequency through Inner Peace, Tolerance, and Honouring & Gratitude, the energy automatically condenses and materializes into what you desire.

The highlights of the wealth journey are two leverage concepts: **"Non-materializing material objects"** and **"Materializing the non-material."**
- **Non-materializing material objects:** The art of breathing soul, meaning, history, or love into a physical product to elevate its value infinitely. A mother's cooked meal containing love and dedication tastes better and holds thousands of times more value than a standard meal. A limited-edition handbag carrying brand history sells for thousands of times the cost of raw materials.
- **Materializing the non-material:** The capacity to clarify, define, and measure invisible values like Happiness, Inner Peace, or Wisdom. When we materialize inner peace into clear information and behaviors, we can easily package and transfer it to others. Physical assets diminish when shared, but non-material assets (wisdom, gratitude) multiply when shared.

For prosperity to arrive thong dong and sustainably, sages have summarized the **"7-Step Formula for Material Wealth Accumulation"**:
1. **Non-materializing oneself** (thắp sáng Trí tuệ, Tâm thái, Nhân cách, Phẩm chất). If one is not wealthy, the root cause is lacking these foundations.
2. **Lifting Physical Health**.
3. **Elevating the capacity to Promote - Cooperate - Guide**.
4. **Optimizing the value-creating tool** (satisfying Long-term, Large-scale, Wholesome criteria, and aligning with Tam Giác Giới).
5. **Executing the 6 golden words in action** (Simple, Joyful, Trusting, Easy, Regular, Mindful).
6. **Applying exponential duplication ($a^n$)**.
7. **Virtuous conduct governing the world (Đức hành thiên hạ)**.

Looking at this formula, we find absolute freedom of time and consciousness. Once you cultivate the "Good Cause" within, material wealth gathers automatically, and you achieve Sixfold Life Fulfillment (Lục lộc đại thuận) and the highest life realms.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **A Person Wealthy in Material Assets (Người giàu vật chất):** One who possesses large assets and stable income ensuring financial security, while maintaining balance and growth in their inner being, health, family harmony, and social connections. They know how to non-materialize themselves and actively create value for the community.
- **Large Assets (Tài sản lớn):** The total value of assets owned by an individual or organization that reaches a significant level.
- **Non-materializing Material Objects (Phi vật chất hóa vật chất):** Putting non-material elements (love, wisdom, meaningful stories) into physical matter to multiply its value, making it priceless.
- **Materializing the Non-material (Vật chất hóa phi vật chất):** Clarifying, defining, quantifying, and manifesting invisible values (happiness, peace, wisdom) into specific, visible expressions to possess them and transfer them to others.

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

- **The Nature of Money:** Money is Information, Energy, and Matter. It is not required to hold physical cash to have wealth. Holding correct "Information" and matching "Energy frequency" will eventually condense into "Matter."
- **The Ultimate Goal of Money:** Money is a tool to create value and elevate the quality of life (consisting of Inner Peace, good health, and quality relationships).
- **The 7-Step Formula for Material Wealth Accumulation:**
  1. Non-materializing oneself.
  2. Lifting Physical Health.
  3. Elevating the capacity to Promote - Cooperate - Guide.
  4. Optimizing the value-creating tool (Long - Large - Wholesome, aligning with Tam Giác Giới).
  5. Executing the 6 golden words in action.
  6. Applying exponential duplication ($a^n$).
  7. Virtuous conduct governing the world (Đức hành thiên hạ).

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **The Foundation of Material is Non-material:** Do not rush out to earn money when the inner world is empty. Prioritize "Non-materializing oneself" (Wisdom, Mental State, Personality, Qualities, Capacity) so that material wealth gathers sustainably.
- **Sharing Characteristics:** Physical matter decreases when shared, but non-material assets (peace, love, wisdom) multiply when shared.

### 2.4. Personal Application

- **In Family Life:** A mother cooking a meal (matter) adds love and explains the dedication behind the food (non-materializing). The meal becomes tasty, nourishing both the children's physical body and spirit.
- **In Career:** Instead of working merely for a salary with stress, an employee applies the "6 golden words": *Simple, Joyful, Trusting, Easy, Regular, and Mindful*. Efficiency rises, and frequency shifts, attracting automatic salary increments while keeping the inner being thong dong.

### 2.5. Social/Organizational Impact

- **Increasing Business Value:** A business selling physical products can "Non-materialize" them by linking them to stories of environmental protection, traditional culture, or love, attracting loyal customers who pay premium prices.
- **Constructing Sustainable Organizations:** Leaders do not use pure money to retain talents, but create environments with Inner Peace, Tolerance, and Honouring & Gratitude. Talents gather where non-material foods are strong.
