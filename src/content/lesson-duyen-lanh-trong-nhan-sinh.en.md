**1B. In-depth Content:**
**THE SECRET TO CONSTRUCTING WHOLESOME CATALYSTS TO REAP THE DESIRED EFFECT IN HUMAN LIFE**
Dear friends, on our journey of elevating inner cognition to master our lives, the wise have pointed out to us a most precious formula, a law that encompasses the whole of human life: "Good Cause combined with a Wholesome Catalyst produces the Desired Effect." In recent days, we have worked diligently together to clarify and build a Good Cause deep within ourselves — that is, constructing beautiful Mental Images, enriching beneficial Source Concepts, and accumulating a mass of Merit - Virtue. However, if the seed of a good cause already exists but we do not know how to cultivate the soil of a wholesome catalyst, that seed will remain forever dormant in the subconscious, never sprouting to bear sweet fruit for us.

So where does a wholesome catalyst in human life come from? Allow me to establish an extremely important awareness with you all: every transformation, every opportunity, every asset, or anything good that comes to us in this life — all of it is brought about by People. **The Catalyst is People.** Without people, we could not have been born, would have no knowledge to learn, no food to nourish our bodies, and could never construct any Desired Effect *(Note: content regarding the Desired Effect, the Six-fold Great Fortune, and the Realm of Life will be explored and clarified further in later topics from 31 to 34)*. Many people in society strive to find success and money, yet they get entangled with and reject people — this is like wanting to cross a river while smashing down one's own bridge with one's own hands.

To attain a wholesome catalyst, the most essential key does not lie in how skillfully we communicate outwardly, but in Elevating our inner cognition about people. Most of humanity's suffering stems from using the lens of the worldly perspective, of right-versus-wrong distinction, to scrutinize and see "people as a problem". But when we bring in the light of the standard frame of reference, we realize: People are not the problem. People are a precious asset, bringing wealth, wisdom, and opportunity. A person is either the road itself or the bridge; if they are the road, they will accompany us; if they are the bridge, they will connect us to new roads. Even more remarkable, people themselves are a machine of merit and virtue. Only by helping people elevate their cognition and bringing them joy do we have the opportunity to accumulate merit for ourselves. Their acceptance of us is our very opportunity to transform our destiny.

However, awareness is only the starting point. For wholesome catalysts to truly gather, we need an energy source to carry them. According to the operating principle of the subconscious that we have already learned, our inner state when thinking of people will determine which karmic seed rises to the surface. Cherishing and gratitude at the level of Emotion will summon good wholesome catalysts, but the greatest power to summon Karmic Catalysts is the energy of Tolerance. When we are tolerant of people, others' faults do not lodge within us, our minds stay clear, and pure catalysts and refined people will naturally be drawn towards us. When you maintain the energy source of Joy, Tolerance, and Cherishing-Gratitude towards people, then anyone who appears in your life automatically becomes a wholesome catalyst.

The pinnacle of thoroughly grasping human catalysts lies in recognizing the 9 important types of people: the Sage who points the way, the Benefactor who helps, the Connector who links, the Wealth God who supports, the Talent who shoulders responsibility, the Enlightened Mentor who guides, the Teacher who imparts principles, the Expert who provides thinking frameworks, and the Wise Friend who unlocks wisdom. In an ordinary person's whole life, having just a few of these people by their side is enough for their life to break through. But our task does not stop merely at recognizing or attracting them. Our ultimate maturity is to strive to personally Become all 9 types of people in one. When we are these 9 types of people combined into one, whoever needs guidance, we guide; whoever needs connecting, we connect; whoever needs their wisdom unlocked, we unlock it — and naturally, humanity will gather around us, with the ultimate material manifestation being tens of thousands of people who Share Our Language, thousands who Reach Consensus with us, and hundreds who Walk the Path alongside us. That is the most perfect picture of a Wholesome Catalyst in human life.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
**2.1. Concepts (Concepts)**
**Wholesome Catalyst:** The interaction and connection between people based on correct inner cognition, bringing about good transformation, and borrowing strength and support to construct desired outcomes in life.
**Inner cognition about people:** The source concepts, mental images, and electromagnetic state we hold when thinking of or interacting with people. Standard cognition includes: People are an asset; a person is either the road or the bridge; people are a machine of merit and virtue; people are teachers; people are not the problem.
**9 Types of People to recognize, treat, attract, and become:** Including the Sage, the Benefactor, the Connector, the Wealth God, the Talent, the Enlightened Mentor, the Teacher, the Expert, and the Wise Friend.
**Shared Language - Consensus - Companionship:** The material manifestation of a wholesome catalyst, when people share the same frame of reference (shared language), share the same destination and belief (consensus), and walk together on the same path (companionship).
**2.2. Key Knowledge**
**The Reality Triangle of Wholesome Catalysts:**
**The Information Corner (Cause of the Catalyst):** Cultivating accurate inner cognition in a beneficial direction about people. This means thoroughly grasping the core awarenesses: people are an asset, a machine of merit and virtue, a road or a bridge, a teacher, and "people are not the problem". At the same time, one must understand the 9 types of people in human life in order to treat them appropriately.
**The Energy Corner (Catalyst of the Catalyst):** One must continuously circulate the energy source of Wisdom, which is **Joy, Tolerance, and Cherishing-Gratitude**, towards people. This is the "magnet" that summons the rise of overall good karmic catalysts.
**The Material Corner (Effect of the Catalyst):** When the information is solid and the energy is sufficient, the wholesome catalyst will manifest into the reality of connecting with people who share the same **Language, Consensus, and Companionship**.
**The Cause-Effect Formula:** **Good Cause + Wholesome Catalyst = Desired Effect**. Having a Cause without a Catalyst means the Effect does not sprout. Having a Catalyst but lacking a Cause means missing the opportunity.
**Principle of Summoning Overall Karmic Catalysts:** The state of Tolerance is the key that unlocks the summoning of good overall karmic catalysts.
**2.3. Key Awareness**
**Achievement comes from people:** Every result, opportunity, and asset in life is brought about by people (directly or indirectly). Without people, there is no achievement of any kind.
**People are not the problem:** Every unwanted event coming from a person is, in essence, meant to teach us a lesson (People are Teachers). Recognizing this helps sever resentment and protect one's merit.
**Constructing a machine of merit and virtue:** Taking the act of helping others elevate their cognition and feel joy as the root, borrowing people to accumulate seeds of light for ourselves, and vice versa.
