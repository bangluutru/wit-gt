# CURRICULUM: UNDERSTANDING INNER BEING – CREATING INNER PEACE

## TOPIC: GOOD CONNECTION IN HUMAN LIFE

---

## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** Provides the second crucial piece in the life formula: **Good Cause + Good Connection = Desirable Outcome**. It teaches learners how to initiate, attract, and preserve social relationships by elevating their inner awareness of humans.
- **Significance for learners:** Smashes resentment toward humans and destroys the mindset of doing everything alone. It equips them with tools to identify, treat, attract, and become the 9 core types of people, realizing dreams through mượn sức and support.
- **Brought transformation:** Shifts perspective from "people have problems" to "people have no problems." Uses the energy of Honouring & Gratitude, Tolerance, and Inner Peace to call forth good connections, turning others into engines of phước báu.
- **Outcomes:** Learners master the 5 inner awarenesses of humans and the 9 types of people, realizing "Shared Language - Shared Agreement - Shared Journey" to build enduring organizations or family clans.

### B. In-depth Content

In our journey to master life, sages point out an ultimate law of the universe: **"Good Cause + Good Connection = Desirable Outcome."** While we cultivate the Good Cause internally (Positive Mental Images, Beneficial Source Concepts, and Merits/Virtues), without creating the soil of Good Connections, the cause seed remains dormant in the subconscious.

Where do Good Connections come from? Sages establish a key awareness: **Connections are Humans (Duyên chính là Con Người).** All opportunities, wealth, and good things in this life are brought by Humans. Without humans, we cannot be born, have no food, no knowledge, and can never harvest any Desirable Outcomes. Rushing to seek success while rejecting humans is like trying to cross a river while destroying your own bridge.

To construct Good Connections, the key is not outward eloquence, but elevating our inner awareness of humans. Human suffering comes from viewing people through a world outlook of right-wrong, claiming "people have problems." When we shine the light of standard reference systems, we realize: **People have no problems.** People are capital, bringing wealth, wisdom, and opportunity. A person is either a bridge (connecting us) or a road (walking with us). Furthermore, humans are engines of merits and virtues; helping them raise awareness and bringing them joy is how we accumulate phước báu.

Awareness is the start; we need energy to carry it. Our inner state regarding humans determines which karmic connection seeds surface. Gratitude calls forth good connections, but the greatest power to call forth connection karma is **Tolerance (Bao dung)**. When we tolerate others, their mistakes do not enter our minds, keeping our energy clean and naturally attracting elite people. When you maintain Inner Peace, Tolerance, and Honouring & Gratitude, anyone who appears in your life becomes a Good Connection.

The peak is identifying and becoming the 9 types of people: Cao nhân, Quý nhân, Nhân mạch, Thần tài, Nhân tài, Minh sư, Teachers, Experts, and Thiện tri thức. A regular person needs only a few of these around to transform life. But our ultimate maturity is to **Become the 9 types of people in 1**. When we embody all 9 types, ready to point, connect, or open wisdom, humanity gathers, manifesting as thousands sharing our Language, Agreement, and Journey. This is the perfect picture of Good Connections.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Good Connection (Duyên lành):** Interaction and connection between humans based on correct inner awareness, bringing positive transformation and leveraging strength to create desirable outcomes.
- **Inner Awareness of Humans:** Source concepts, mental images, and electromagnetic states when mentioning or contacting people. Standard awareness: Humans are Capital; Bridges/Roads; Merits/Virtues Engines; Teachers; and have no problems.
- **Shared Language - Shared Agreement - Shared Journey:** The physical manifestation of good connections.

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

- **Reality Triangle of Good Connections:**
  - **Information (Cause of Connection):** Cultivating beneficial inner awarenesses (capital, bridges, teachers, merits engines, no problems) and understanding the 9 types of people.
  - **Energy (Connection of Connection):** Shifting the energy of **Inner Peace, Tolerance, and Honouring & Gratitude** toward humans to attract positive connections.
  - **Material (Fruit of Connection):** Connecting with people sharing **Shared Language, Shared Agreement, and Shared Journey**.
- **Universal Law:** **Good Cause + Good Connection = Desirable Outcome**. Cause without connection does not ripen; connection without cause is a missed opportunity.
- **Calling forth Connection Karma:** Tolerance is the key to calling forth good connection karma.

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **Achievements are Brought by Others:** No achievements exist without humans.
- **Humans have no Problems:** Difficulties brought by others teach us lessons (Con người là Thầy).
- **Constructing Merits Engines:** Helping others raise awareness and feel joy accumulates positive seeds.

### 2.4. Applications (Personal, Family, Organizational, Social)

#### Personal Application
- **Inner Peace:** Stop complaining about a lack of helpers. Tri ân everyone who appeared in your life. Your energy shifts, attracting Quý nhân to guide your goals.
- **Finance (Humans are Capital):** Instead of worrying about capital, focus on high-quality connections. Investors and experts will align to support your projects.

#### Family Application
- **Spousal Harmony:** Stop finding faults in your spouse. Tolerate them and hold their positive image. The spouse changes naturally.
- **Parenting:** Stop forcing compliance. Act as a Minh sư (to guide), a Teacher (to give morals), and a Talent (to accompany). Children show respect and align.

#### Organizational Application
- **Retention:** View staff as merits engines, creating contexts for their growth. They stay loyal.
- **Sales (Bridge or Road):** Salespeople maintain joy even when rejected. The customer who rejects becomes a "bridge" introducing other major clients.
- **Culture:** Build standard reference systems for "Shared Language," leading to "Shared Agreement" and "Shared Journey." Productivity jumps.

#### Social Impact
- **Philanthropy:** Move from physical giving to becoming a Thiện tri thức who opens people's awareness to stand above problems.
- **Social Media:** Spread An vui, Bao dung, and Gratitude. This purification algorithm attracts thousands sharing a kind lifestyle.
