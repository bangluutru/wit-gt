### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION
**1A. Objectives & Target Audience:**

* **Significance for learners:** Eliminates the passive mindset of "waiting for opportunities" (waiting for demand before supplying). It empowers learners to master their circumstances by proactively creating value and using the "key" to awaken others' needs.
* **Brought transformation:** Ends the waste of resources on relationships or work that go against natural laws. It frees learners from the illusion of "competition," stepping into the realm of absolute value creation.
* **Learner's Outcomes:**
  * Learners understand the difference between the forward direction (Supply - Demand) and the reverse direction (Demand - Supply). They comprehend "Hidden vs. Manifest Needs" and master the appretell method to activate needs.

**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**LAW OF SUPPLY AND DEMAND – CREATING PROSPERITY**

**INTRODUCTION – THE ANXIETY OF CHASING DEMAND**
In life, humans desire a smooth and successful path. Yet, many struggle, wasting resources without achieving desired outcomes. This is true for businesses suffering from slow sales, spouses whose sacrifices go unrecognized, or parents unable to guide their children. Under the inner-self lens, the root of this fatigue is not a lack of effort or talent, but resisting a natural law: the **Law of Supply and Demand**.

* **The Trap of Demand - Supply:**
  We are programmed to wait for others' needs (Demand) before trying to satisfy them (Supply). This makes us reactive. In business, seeing a hot product, thousands rush to supply it, creating a red ocean of fierce competition. In family life, we blindly shower care (Supply) on a partner when they have not expressed a desire (Demand) for connection. Pushing supply when there is no demand turns love into a burden, leaving us with resentment and suffering.

* **Reversing to Supply - Demand:**
  Transformation begins when we step into the forward direction: **Supply - Demand**. A true builder of life proactively refines their capacity, values, and products (Supply) long before others seek them.
  * *We establish a key awareness: Human needs always exist; they are simply in a Hidden (Ẩn) or Manifest (Hiện) state.*
  Needs are not born externally; they stem from the desires for money, beauty, fame, food, and sleep embedded in human karma. These needs exist in humanity's consciousness, waiting to be awakened.

* **The Key of Appretell (Quảng bá):**
  A person who understands the Law of Supply and Demand never begs or forces love. They act as an **Appreteller**. Instead of pleading, they use positive language to draw a clear future benefit picture. They repeat this picture with Appreciation & Gratitude. This energy activates the seeds of positive desires in the listener's karma, converting hidden needs into manifest needs.
  Once demand is activated, supply becomes natural and appreciated. When demand exceeds supply, your value rises automatically, eliminating the concept of competition.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
**2.1. Concepts:**
* **Law of Supply and Demand:** The forward direction is Supply - Demand (proactively building value and activating needs). The reverse direction is Demand - Supply (passively waiting for needs).
* **Hidden vs. Manifest Needs:** Needs are always present in human karma, waiting to be awakened.
* **Appretell (Quảng bá):** The key to activating hidden needs.

**2.2. Key Knowledge:**
* **Reverse vs. Forward Direction:** Demand - Supply is passive and exhausting. Supply - Demand is proactive and sustainable.
* **Activating Needs:** Pushing solutions (Supply) before a person is ready (Demand) fails because their karma is not open. We must use Appretell to awaken the hidden need first.
* **Eliminating Competition:** Proactively supplying value and appretelling to activate hidden markets creates a unique space, leaving no room for competition.

**2.3. Key Awarenesss:**
* **Insight 1: Needs are always present.** The belief that "no one needs my service" is an illusion. The issue is our failure to manifest that need.
* **Insight 2: Unsolicited giving is a burden.** Forcing care (Supply) on a partner without their interest (Demand) breeds ingratitude and distance.
* **Insight 3: Prepare the Supply.** Refine your values, maintain Joy, and use Appretell. When demand arises, success follows naturally.

**2.4. Personal Applications:**
* **Example 1 (Health):** Instead of waiting to get sick (Demand) before seeing a doctor (Supply), I proactively trained my physical body (Supply) and appretelled the benefits of health to myself (Kích cầu).
* **Example 2 (Coaching):** I used to offer unsolicited advice to friends, causing friction. I shifted to refining my coaching capacity (Supply) and appretelled transformation stories. My friends now seek my counsel.
* **Example 3 (Finance):** Instead of pleading with clients to buy, I shared my product's benefit picture with Appreciation, causing clients to seek me out.
