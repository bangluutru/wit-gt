**CURRICULUM: ELEVATING INNER AWARENESS TOPIC: WEALTH OF PERSONALITY**
**PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION**
**A. Topic Objectives**
**Value of the topic:** The topic **Wealth of Personality** helps learners thoroughly understand the core foundation to become **the seed of a successful environment**. This is an important stepping stone to create natural attraction, making others enjoy being near you and accompanying you.
**Significance for learners:** Provides a roadmap for people to **upgrade relationships** and elevate their realm of life. Once personality is perfected, learners will be confident in communication and capable of borrowing the strength of social relationships to achieve their goals in life.
**Brought transformation:** Learners shift from living according to spontaneous emotional states to proactively **managing their inner awareness**, thereby generating positive and stable emotions.
**Output results:** Learners thoroughly understand and manifest **9 good states of awareness and emotion**, becoming valuable people capable of building excellent family and organizational environments.
**B. In-depth Content**
A person's personality is not merely outward expressions, but a **collection of emotional and cognitive states within the inner being**. The knowledge of Wealth of Personality helps us materialize non-material factors through 9 manifestations: **joy, hope, belief, wisdom, honoring and gratitude, love, tolerance, humbleness, and truthfulness**.

The key point in cultivating personality lies in the correlation between **awareness and emotion**. Awareness is the foundation (belonging to the layers of Mind and Nature), from which emotion is generated (belonging to the layer of Emotion). Instead of striving to change temporary emotions, an inner-consciousness training consultant focuses on **changing inner awareness**.

A person wealthy in personality (or with a perfected personality) is one who fully gathers all 9 of the above elements toward themselves and toward their social relationships. Among these, **truthful personality is the foundation**, and **wisdom personality is the key point** to develop the remaining personalities. In particular, personality plays a decisive role in **retaining good connections (giữ duyên)**. A person wealthy in personality will attract people and retain them for the long term through a source of energy that is safe and trustworthy.

Perfecting one's individual personality is the first step toward building a **good environment** according to the principle of **Point - Line - Plane**. When 3 people with perfected personalities join forces, they create a "Plane" — that is, an environment where people feel encouraged to develop holistically.

**PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)**

**2.1. Concepts**
**1. Personality and a Person Wealthy in Personality:**
**Personality:** A person's personality is the collection of emotional states and inner cognitive states manifested through their mental images and their values toward themselves and toward social relationships.
**A Person Wealthy in Personality (Perfected Personality):** One who fully gathers the emotional states and inner cognitive states manifested through joy, hope, belief, wisdom, honoring and gratitude, love, tolerance, humbleness, and truthfulness toward themselves and toward their social relationships.
**2. The Concept of the 9 Elements of Personality:**
**Joy:** Joy is the material manifestation of Pleasure and Inner Peace.
*Joy from Pleasure:* An inner state of satisfaction when one's greed and imagination regarding Wealth, Beauty, Fame, Food, and Sleep are satisfied.
*Joy from Inner Peace:* A state of inner awareness upon receiving truthfulness within oneself.
**Hope:** Hope is the state of satisfying the greed and imagination of the inner being toward what the mind wants to head toward.
*One who brings hope to others:* Is one who helps others clarify their destination. Is one who helps others recognize or optimize the tools/means to achieve what they desire.
**Belief:** Belief is the inner feeling state that oneself already has sufficient resources — or does not yet have sufficient resources — to possess what the mind is heading toward.
*One with positive belief:* Is one who has the inner feeling state that they already have sufficient resources to possess what their mind is heading toward.
*One who brings positive belief to others:* Is one who helps others realize that they already have sufficient resources to possess what their mind is heading toward.
**Wisdom:** Wisdom is simply understood as a concept referring to a state of inner awareness.
*Wisdom Personality:* Wisdom is both a state of awareness and a state of emotion. Because there is a state of awareness first, a person then manifests wisdom outwardly (levels 1+2 of wisdom manifest outward, level 3 is pure awareness).
*One who brings wisdom to others:* Is one who helps others elevate their inner awareness through concepts, stories, and lessons... so that they can rise above the problems that arise.
**Honoring - Gratitude:** Honoring-gratitude is an inner state of being moved before things, events, phenomena, circumstances, or people.
It is a state in which obviousness (taking things for granted) does not exist.
It is a state in which we recognize the reason for someone's presence, the importance of people, and the usefulness of all things.
**Love:** Love is both a state of emotion and a state of awareness.
*Informing love:* Loving oneself means giving oneself sufficiency in 6 needs: basic needs, safety needs, the need for social connection, the need to be respected, the need for self-expression, and the need to give.
*Energizing love:* A state of the inner being's desire to connect with, bond with, and possess the subject being loved.
*Materializing love:* Is respect, sharing, integrity, and tolerance.
**Tolerance:**
*According to UNESCO's definition:* Tolerance is respecting and understanding others' differences from oneself in customs, outlook on life, beliefs and religion, race, and ways of acting; accepting that others do things one does not approve of, within a certain limit, in order to guide them toward goodness of their own accord.
*According to the WiT human structure frame of reference:* Tolerance is an inner state of not being attached to anything happening in the mind due to the impact of external circumstances. It is an inner state in which the nature of greed and the nature of imagination come to a stop. A person tolerant of others is one whose inner state is not attached to anything in others' behavior.
**Humbleness:**
A humble person is one who has the inner feeling that their own achievements were brought about by others.
A humble person is one who always sees something better in others than in themselves. In other words, one who thoroughly understands the Philosophy of NGU (the philosophy of "foolishness"/humility).
A humble person is one who knows how to rise up at the right moment and bow down appropriately to specific circumstances — one who thoroughly understands the Philosophy of the Rice Plant.
**Truthfulness:** Truthfulness is that which does not change across space and time.
A person with a truthful personality is one who receives truthfulness within themselves. They are one who knows they are hearing, knows they are knowing, knows they are seeing, and knows they are speaking, without being attached to the layers of Nature and Emotion of a person.
*Manifesting a truthful personality:* There are 4 things by which a person manifests a Truthful Personality over time: 1. Knowing that one is Hearing, 2. Knowing that one is Seeing, 3. Knowing that one is Speaking, 4. Promoting/sharing what one does and doing what one promotes/shares. Over time, the words one speaks become limited by fewer stray thoughts, the eyes become limited in attaching to imagination and become simple, and actions and words become consistent. Others will judge that person as having a Truthful Personality.

**2.2. Key Knowledge**
**Personality is the Effect of Wisdom and Mental State:** The Cause is wisdom, the Condition is mental state, and the Effect is precisely personality. To manifest a holistic personality, one needs the foundation of information from wisdom and the energy from mental state.
**The relationship between awareness and emotion:** Positive awareness leads to positive emotion and vice versa. Educating awareness is the most sustainable way to build personality, rather than merely chasing surface-level emotions.
**Personality helps Retain Good Connections:** In the formula *Good Cause + Good Condition = Desired Effect*, personality plays the role of **retaining good connections**. A person wealthy in personality creates an attraction that makes people want to stay near them and accompany them for the long term.
**The Point - Line - Plane Principle in building an environment:**
**Point:** 1 person with a perfected personality.
**Line:** 2 people with perfected personalities connecting with each other.
**Plane:** 3 people (including supporters on the left and right) with perfected personalities will create a **Good Environment** and sustainable success.

**2.3. Key Awareness**
**Personality is judged by others:** Even though we cultivate it ourselves, personality is truly shaped through the recognition of the people around us.
**Achievement is brought about by others:** This is the core awareness of humble personality. When we realize this, we eliminate the mind of arrogance and win over people's hearts.
**Non-attachment to titles (the false self of status):** Removing the mask of position and status helps us return to truthfulness and become more tolerant of people.
**Learning to recognize what we already have and need to keep:** Children are born already possessing many good personality traits (such as joy and tolerance) but easily lose them as they grow up. Adults need to learn in order to **re-recognize and hold on to** these values.

**2.4. 03 Personal Application Examples**
*Each example shows a journey of inner, health, work, and career transformation when applying the knowledge of Wealth of Personality.*
**Example 1 (Inner Being & Health Aspect):**
**Context before transformation:** I regularly fell into a state of exhaustion, drained of energy after work hours, and often irritable with myself.
**Old awareness:** I believed my declining health was due to work pressure and age, and that taking supplements was enough to be healthy.
**Knowledge applied:** The personality of **Honoring-Gratitude** and **Joy** (Inner Peace).
**New action:** Every day, instead of resenting my tired body, I practiced honoring and being grateful for each part of my body (heart, liver...). I brought my inner state back to inner peace to receive the unwanted things in my work.
**Result after transformation:** A balanced, positive mental state helped me not only become happier but also improved my physical condition noticeably — my stamina increased, my complexion became radiant, and I no longer depended heavily on medicine.
**Example 2 (Work & Career Aspect):**
**Context before transformation:** I worked very well independently, but when working in a team, conflicts constantly arose, and colleagues kept their distance from me.
**Old awareness:** I believed that since I was capable and did things correctly, others had to listen to me, and that anyone who couldn't keep up was simply inferior.
**Knowledge applied:** The personality of **Humbleness**.
**New action:** I proactively recognized and highlighted my colleagues' strengths. I brought my inner awareness to the state: "The team's achievements were brought about by others." I learned to rise up and bow my head at the right moments.
**Result after transformation:** I eliminated my arrogant mind and won over people's hearts. I became someone colleagues liked to be near, work went smoothly, and I was promoted to a leadership position because I knew how to gather collective strength.
**Example 3 (Personal Transformation Aspect):**
**Context before transformation:** I was always hesitant and indecisive before new opportunities, feeling I did not have enough resources to start a business.
**Old awareness:** I thought I needed to have 100% enough money, relationships, and knowledge before I could start.
**Knowledge applied:** The personality of **Belief** and **Hope**.
**New action:** I established a clear mental image of the business results I desired. I recognized that I already possessed resources (even if not yet visible) to achieve my goal.
**Result after transformation:** I generated a source of confident energy, bravely starting the project. This very belief attracted benefactors and valuable connections to come and support me, turning my dream into reality.

**2.5. 03 Family Application Examples**
*Applying the knowledge to the relationship between husband and wife, parents and children, and building a happy family.*
**Example 1 (Husband-Wife Relationship):**
**Before transformation:** The couple often argued over small everyday mistakes, and the family atmosphere was always tense and suffocating.
**After transformation:** Applying the personality of **Tolerance** and **Truthfulness**. The husband/wife recognized that the root cause of life stems from themselves, and stopped labeling the other person's mistakes. They removed the "false self of status" (ego) to treat each other truthfully. The family atmosphere became safe, the two people understood and trusted each other absolutely, and the marriage became extremely harmonious.
**Example 2 (Parents and Children):**
**Before transformation:** Parents constantly controlled and imposed achievement expectations on their child, only seeing flaws, which made the child feel inferior, defiant, and distant from the parents.
**After transformation:** Applying the knowledge to build up the child's personality (choosing 4 key elements: **Joy, Love, Honoring-Gratitude, Humbleness**). Parents proactively recognized their child's good points and promoted the child's good actions to others. The child felt loved, confidently expressed their talents, and became more filial and deeply bonded with the family.
**Example 3 (Building an Everlasting Family Clan):**
**Before transformation:** Generations of the family lived apart, only meeting during holidays and Tet, each person looking after themselves, with the family legacy oriented only toward money and material things.
**After transformation:** The head of the family applied the "Point - Line - Plane" principle. Starting with perfecting their own personality, then spreading it to their spouse, then to their children. The family became a "Good Environment" fully possessing all 9 personality traits, passing down to the next generation a priceless non-material legacy, building an everlasting family clan.

**2.6. 03 Organizational Application Examples**
*Applying the knowledge to leadership, management, culture, and team development.*
**Example 1 (Leadership and Management):**
**Before transformation:** Leadership managed through strict regulations, personnel worked only for money, the turnover rate was high, and the organization could not retain talent.
**After transformation:** Leadership applied the knowledge: "Personality is for retaining good connections." The boss began to manifest the personality of **Joy, Wisdom, and Tolerance**. Thanks to the energy radiating from them, employees liked being near the boss, voluntarily contributed, and felt the organization was their second home. The turnover rate dropped to zero.
**Example 2 (Organizational Culture):**
**Before transformation:** The work environment was divided into factions, employees frequently picked at each other's mistakes, and resented the company during difficult times.
**After transformation:** Bringing the personality of **Honoring-Gratitude** and **Love** into the core culture. The organization held sharing sessions for employees to recognize the usefulness of the work and their colleagues' contributions. Resentment was eliminated, the corporate culture became strong, and everyone loved and supported each other's growth.
**Example 3 (Teamwork):**
**Before transformation:** The team worked in a scattered way, everyone wanted credit for themselves, and there was a lack of smooth coordination.
**After transformation:** The whole team practiced the personality of **Truthfulness** and **Humbleness** together. They eliminated judgment, creating a safe, trusting work environment where everyone freely contributed ideas without fear of being ridiculed. When KPIs were achieved, everyone recognized that the achievement was brought about by the whole team, and from there the team's strength was maximized without limit.
