## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

### A. Topic Objectives

- **Value of the topic:** The topic **Wealth of Personality** helps learners comprehend the core foundation to become **the seed of a successful environment (nhân của một môi trường thành công)**. This is an important stepping stone to create natural attraction, making others enjoy being near you and accompanying you.
- **Significance for learners:** Provides a roadmap to **upgrade relationships** and elevate life realms. By perfecting their personality, learners gain confidence in communication and the capacity to utilize social relationships to achieve human goals.
- **Brought transformation:** Learners shift from living by spontaneous emotional states to proactively **managing inner awareness**, thereby generating positive and stable emotions.
- **Outcomes:** Learners comprehend and manifest **9 states of awareness and emotion**, becoming valuable individuals with the capacity to build outstanding family and organizational environments.

### B. In-depth Content

A person's personality is not merely external behaviors, but a **collection of emotional and cognitive states within the inner being**. The knowledge of Wealth of Personality helps us materialize non-material factors through 9 manifestations: **Joy, Hope, Belief, Wisdom, Honouring & Gratitude, Love, Tolerance, Humbleness, and Truth**.

The key point in nurturing personality lies in the correlation between **awareness and emotion**. Awareness is the foundation (belonging to Mind and Nature), which generates emotion (belonging to Emotion). Instead of trying to change temporary emotions, inner coaches focus on **shifting internal awareness**.

A person wealthy in personality (or possessing a perfected personality) is one who gathers all 9 elements toward themselves and their social relationships. In this, **Truth is the foundation**, and **Wisdom is the key** to executing the remaining elements. In particular, personality plays a decisive role in **retaining connections (giữ duyên)**. A person wealthy in personality attracts people and retains them long-term by emitting a safe and trusting energy.

Perfecting individual personality is the first step toward building a **successful environment** using the principle of **Point - Line - Plane (Điểm - Tuyến - Diện)**. When 3 individuals with perfected personalities unite, they form a "Plane" (Diện)—an environment where humans feel encouraged to develop holistically.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

#### 1. Personality and a Person Wealthy in Personality
- **Personality (Nhân cách):** A person's personality is the collection of inner emotional and cognitive states manifested through their mental images and values toward themselves and social relationships.
- **A Person Wealthy in Personality (Người giàu nhân cách):** One who fully gathers the emotional and cognitive states manifested through Joy, Hope, Belief, Wisdom, Honouring & Gratitude, Love, Tolerance, Humbleness, and Truth toward themselves and social relationships.

#### 2. The 9 Elements of Personality
- **Joy (Vui vẻ):** The material manifestation of Pleasure (Khoái lạc) and Inner Peace (An vui).
  - *Joy of Pleasure:* The state of inner satisfaction when satisfying greed and imagination regarding Wealth, Beauty, Fame, Food, and Sleep.
  - *Joy of Inner Peace:* The state of inner awareness when receiving the Truth within oneself.
- **Hope (Hy vọng):** The state of satisfying inner greed and imagination regarding what the mind points toward.
  - *One who brings hope to others:* Helps others clarify their destination. Helps others recognize or optimize the tools and means to achieve their desires.
- **Belief (Niềm tin):** The inner feeling that one has accumulated sufficient resources (or insufficient resources) to possess what the mind points toward.
  - *One with positive belief:* Inner feeling of having sufficient resources to possess their desires.
  - *One who brings positive belief to others:* Helps others realize they already have sufficient resources to possess their desires.
- **Wisdom (Trí tuệ):** Concept indicating a state of inner awareness.
  - *Personality of Wisdom:* Wisdom is both a state of awareness and a state of emotion. Level 1 & 2 wisdom manifest externally, while Level 3 is pure awareness.
  - *One who brings wisdom to others:* Helps others elevate their inner awareness through concepts, stories, and lessons to rise above problems.
- **Honouring & Gratitude (Trân trọng - Biết ơn):** Inner touched state before events, circumstances, or people, where obviousness does not exist. We recognize the reason for existence, the usefulness of all things, and the importance of humans.
- **Love (Yêu thương):** Both a state of emotion and a state of awareness.
  - *Information of Love:* Loving oneself is satisfying 6 needs: basic, safety, social connection, respect, self-actualization, and contribution (giving back).
  - *Energy of Love:* The inner desire to connect, bond with, or possess the loved subject.
  - *Matter of Love:* Manifested as Respect, Sharing, Integrity (Liêm chính), and Tolerance.
- **Tolerance (Bao dung):**
  - *UNESCO Definition:* Respecting and understanding the differences of others regarding customs, viewpoints, beliefs, religion, race, and actions; accepting others' actions that we do not approve of within certain limits to guide them toward goodness.
  - *WiT Definition:* Inner state of not being attached to anything in the mind due to external circumstances. Greed and imagination stop. The inner state is not attached to any behaviors of others.
- **Humbleness (Khiêm tốn):**
  - Inner realization that one's achievements are brought by others.
  - Constantly seeing virtues in others that exceed one's own, fully comprehending **Triết lý NGU** (Philosophy of Humility).
  - Knowing when to stand tall and when to bow down to fit the context, fully comprehending **Rice Plant Philosophy (Triết lý Cây Lúa)**.
- **Truth (Chân thật):** What does not change across space and time. A person with the personality of Truth receives the Truth within themselves, knowing they are hearing, seeing, speaking, and knowing, without being attached to Nature and Emotion.
  - *Manifesting Truth:* Bộc lộ through 4 elements over time: 1. Knowing one is Hearing, 2. Knowing one is Seeing, 3. Knowing one is Speaking, 4. Promoting what one does and doing what one promotes. Over time, words restrict stray thoughts, eyes restrict imagination, and actions match words.

### 2.2. Core Knowledge

- **Personality is the Fruit of Wisdom and Mental State:** Wisdom is the Cause, Mental State is the Connection (Duyên), and Personality is the Fruit. To bộc lộ personality holistically, one needs the information from Wisdom and the energy from Mental State.
- **Correlation between Awareness and Emotion:** Positive awareness leads to positive emotions. Educating awareness is the sustainable way to construct personality, rather than chasing surface emotions.
- **Personality retains Connections (Giữ Duyên):** In the formula *Good Cause + Good Connection = Desirable Outcome*, personality plays the role of **retaining connections**. A person wealthy in personality creates attraction, making people want to stay near and accompany them.
- **Point - Line - Plane (Điểm - Tuyến - Diện) in Building Environments:**
  - *Point:* 1 person with a perfected personality.
  - *Line:* 2 people with perfected personalities connecting.
  - *Plane:* 3 people (including Tả phù - Hữu bật supporters) with perfected personalities, forming a **Successful Environment**.

### 2.3. Key Awareness

- **Personality is evaluated by others:** Although we nurture it ourselves, personality is defined through the recognition of those around us.
- **Achievements are brought by others:** The core awareness of humbleness. Realising this dissolves arrogance and wins human hearts.
- **No attachment to titles (Sĩ thân):** Releasing the mask of titles and status helps us return to truth and tolerate others more.
- **Relearning and retaining:** Children are born with many good personality traits (joy, tolerance) but lose them as they grow. Adults need to learn to **re-recognize and protect** these values.

### 2.4. 03 Personal Application Examples

- **Example 1 (Inner Being & Health):** I used to feel exhausted after work, complaining about stress. Applying **Honouring & Gratitude** and **Joy (Inner Peace)**, I showed gratitude to my body organs daily and received work challenges in peace. My mental state shifted to positive, improving my physical energy and stamina.
- **Example 2 (Career & Teamwork):** I was technically skilled but clashed with team members. Applying **Humbleness**, I focused on team members' virtues and realized: *"My achievements are brought by others."* Bowing down at the right time dissolved my arrogance, making colleagues enjoy working with me, which led to a leadership promotion.
- **Example 3 (Personal Growth):** I hesitated to start projects, thinking I lacked resources. Applying **Belief** and **Hope**, I clarified my goals, believing I possessed all needed resources. This positive energy attracted partners and resources, turning my project into reality.

### 2.5. 03 Family Application Examples

- **Example 1: Vợ chồng (Marriage Harmony)**
  - *Context:* Constant arguments over minor errors.
  - *Action:* Applied **Tolerance** and **Truth**. Stopped blaming, released self-importance, and interacted with mutual truth.
  - *Outcome:* Recreated an environment of safety, trust, and deep harmony.
- **Example 2: Parenting**
  - *Context:* Controlling parents who focused only on grades, making the child rebellious.
  - *Action:* Focused on nurturing the child's personality (using 4 pillars: **Joy, Love, Honouring & Gratitude, Humbleness**). Proactively recognized and promoted the child's virtues.
  - *Outcome:* The child felt loved, bộc lộ their potential, and bonded closely with the family.
- **Example 3: Family Legacy**
  - *Context:* Generations lived apart, connecting only on holidays with material inheritance focus.
  - *Action:* Applied Point - Line - Plane. The family head perfected his personality, aligned with his spouse, and taught the children.
  - *Outcome:* Formed a successful home environment with 9 personality traits, leaving a priceless non-material legacy.

### 2.6. 03 Organizational Application Examples

- **Example 1: Leadership and Connection**
  - *Context:* Strict rules, high staff turnover.
  - *Action:* Leader realized personality keeps connections. Proactively bộc lộ **Joy, Wisdom, and Tolerance**.
  - *Outcome:* Staff felt safe and valued, treating the company as a second home; turnover dropped to 0.
- **Example 2: Corporate Culture**
  - *Context:* Core values like "Integrity" were too abstract.
  - *Action:* Materialized "Integrity" by putting it into the Reality Triangle, defining its Information, Energy, and specific behaviors.
  - *Outcome:* Culture became visible and easy to transfer to new hires.
- **Example 3: Team Coordination**
  - *Context:* Members competed for credits, working in silos.
  - *Action:* Practiced **Truth** and **Humbleness**. Dropped judgments, created safety for sharing, and attributed success to the team.
  - *Outcome:* Cooperation grew, maximizing team strength.

### 2.7. 03 Social Impact Examples

- **Example 1: Social Responsibility**
  - *Context:* Focus only on self-interest.
  - *Action:* Extended **Love** to 4 survival dynamics (Self, Family, Organization, Society), establishing charity education projects.
  - *Outcome:* Brought value to the community, gaining social respect.
