- **Value of the topic:** Provides the standard reference system of Human Structure under the Religious (Belief) perspective, helping learners comprehend the source of all human experiences.
- **Significance for learners:** Helps learners recognize the Truth within themselves to reach true, tranquil Inner Peace without being dominated by external circumstances.
- **Brought transformation:** Shifts learners' awareness from level 1 (right – wrong) to level 3 (Emptiness of Nature), knowing how to shift inner states to stand above all emerging adversities.
- **Outcomes:** Learners understand and operate the principle "The nature of hearing and seeing is the raft," achieving sustainable Inner Peace through recognizing the Nature of Emptiness of all things.

### B. IN-DEPTH CONTENT: HUMAN STRUCTURE AND THE CODE OF EMPTINESS OF NATURE - THE JOURNEY OF FINDING TRUTH WITHIN

#### I. Truth: The Unchanging Anchor amidst fluctuating life
The journey of understanding the inner being begins with establishing a core concept: Truth. The Teacher defines: "Truth is what does not change across space and time."

Through the story of the demons and humans, the Teacher guides us to realize that humans often anchor themselves to changing things such as the physical body, wealth, houses, or emotions, leading to forever losing true peace and happiness. However, deep within each person always exists the Intention containing Hearing, Seeing, Speaking, Knowing. Whether there is sound or not, we always possess the Intention to hear; whether there is an object or not, we always possess the Intention to see. Receiving this truth is the condition to open wisdom and acts as the "raft" carrying humans beyond the attachments of the Nature and Emotion layers.

#### II. Human Structure Diagram: The layers wrapping the inner being
To comprehend the inner operations, the Teacher models human beings through a diagram consisting of energy layers and complex components:
- **Mind Layer (Innermost):** Contains the Intention to Hear - See - Speak - Know wrapped in Radiant Energy. This is the region of balanced energy, non-arising and non-dissolving, representing Buddha Nature or the true nature of each person.
- **Nature Layer (Second):** Consists of 16 human natures (Reception, Imagination, Expression, Cognition; Wealth, Form, Fame, Eating, Sleep; Want, Temperament, Ignorance, Pride, Discernment, Ethos, View). This layer is wrapped in a Polar Electromagnetic Field, constantly fluctuating across different amplitudes and levels.
- **Emotion Layer (Third):** Consists of 84,000 bubbles of illusion, representing the diverse emotional states of humans.
- **Body Layer (Outermost):** Physical Body made of Earth, Water, Air, Fire, and the Polar Electromagnetic Field wrapping the body.

*Key cognitive establishment:* Want and Imagination (Tham & Tưởng) are the focus of human nature. Our want and imagination regarding Wealth, Form, Fame, Eating, and Sleep decide the levels of the other natures (Temperament, Ignorance, Pride, Discernment, Ethos, View), thereby dominating all human experiences and the cycle of reincarnation.

#### III. Operating Principle of Emptiness of Nature (Tánh Không) and Nature of Emptiness (Tính Không)
This is the master key to achieve Inner Peace and stand above all emerging adversities.
- **Message reception mechanism:** Normally, an external message entering the inner being is immediately reacted to by the Emotion layer or analyzed/differentiated by the Nature layer.
- **Initiating Emptiness of Nature:** When we proactively use the Mind to receive the message by Truth ("knowing oneself is hearing," "knowing oneself is seeing"), the inner being creates a delay so that Nature and Emotion cannot interfere yet. This space of the inner being is the state of **Emptiness of Nature** (Tánh Không).
- **Relationship with Nature of Emptiness (Tính Không):** All things carry the Nature of Emptiness—they are what they are because the observer projects a label on them. When the inner being reaches Emptiness of Nature, one will truly see the Nature of Emptiness of all things and people around them. This awareness helps us release attachments and old definitions about ourselves and others (releasing Identity Attachment), unlocking infinite potential to become anybody.

#### IV. Managing Inner Consciousness through 3 Blocks of Merit and Total Karma
Comprehending the Human Structure helps us proactively manage our own Cause and Effect through the lens of energy:
- **Three blocks of merit:** Bad merit (negative energy - from making others suffer), Good merit (positive energy - from making others happy), and Balanced merit (luminous energy - accumulated when helping others receive Truth or elevate their level of wisdom).
- **Total Karma:** The sum of thoughts and actions across countless lifetimes (Knowledge - Conditions - Outcomes) stored in the Subconscious (storehouse consciousness).

*The Teacher asserts:* When we manage the vibration frequency of our inner being at a balanced to positive level, we will activate positive total karma and experience life as desired.

#### V. Summary: Mastering Inner Being - Creating Inner Peace
The sharing session concludes with the formula to rise above all adversities: Stand above adversities by luân chuyển (shifting) levels of awareness, especially level 3 awareness of Emptiness of Nature. A person who masters their inner being is one who "proactively chooses and takes responsibility for what occurs inside their inner being to master external situations."

*Inspirational message:* "The nature of hearing and seeing is the raft, carrying tranquil humans back to the shore of inner peace." When you receive the truth within yourself and practice "knowing it is so," you will officially possess the protective energy for yourself and become the anchor for the transformation of family, organization, and society.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **Truth:** What does not change across space and time.
- **16 Human Natures:** Reception, Imagination, Expression, Cognition; Wealth, Form, Fame, Eating, Sleep; Want, Temperament, Ignorance, Pride, Discernment, Ethos, View. Focus is Want and Imagination.
- **Inner Being:** Consists of 3 layers: Mind (Intention to hear-see-speak-know), Nature (16 human natures), and Emotion (84,000 bubbles of illusion).
- **Emptiness of Nature of the inner being:** The state of awareness when a message enters the Intention to hear/see without being attached to the Nature or Emotion layers.
- **Inner Peace (An vui):** The state of awareness originating from Truth, when Want and Imagination regarding Wealth-Form-Fame-Eating-Sleep are Released, Stopped, Ceased, or Ended.

### 2.2. Key Knowledge

- **The 4-layer structure wrapping the inner being:** Mind (luminous energy core), Nature (polar electromagnetism), Emotion (bubbles of illusion), and Body (Earth-Water-Air-Fire).
- **Want and Imagination are the focus of human nature:** Want and imagination regarding Wealth, Form, Fame, Eating, and Sleep lead and decide the levels of the other natures (Temperament, Ignorance, Pride, Discernment, Ethos, View), deciding the inner vibration frequency.
- **Inner operating mechanism:** Messages from the outside are reacted to by Emotion, analyzed by Nature, and received by Mind. To achieve balance, we proactively use the Mind to receive messages by Truth.
- **Matter – Space – Time:** Under the lens of Emptiness of Nature, matter is only the matching of vibration frequency between the observer and the observed. When the inner being reaches Emptiness of Nature, the vibration frequency is extremely high, completely changing the feeling of time and the experience of matter.
- **Raft Diagram:** Using the true nature of hearing and seeing as a "raft" to cross the fluctuating river of "natures and emotions" to return to the shore of tranquil Inner Peace.

### 2.3. Deep Insights

- **Truth does not come from thinking:** Straining the intellect to search for truth only moves us further away, because the moment we initiate a thought, Nature and Emotion interfere.
- **The Source of Inner Peace:** Inner Peace does not come from possessing matter, but from the balanced inner state when we are not attached to wants and imaginations.
- **All things carry the Nature of Emptiness:** Things and people around us are what they are because of our projected labels. When the inner being has Emptiness of Nature, we see the Nature of Emptiness of all things and release all judgements.
- **Be nobody to become anybody:** If you have defined yourself as somebody, you cannot become anybody else. When comprehending Emptiness of Nature, we release identity attachments (roles, titles) to unlock infinite potential.
- **Managing inner consciousness is the key:** Simply "knowing oneself is Hearing, knowing oneself is Seeing" is the simplest way to master the inner being. Instead of trying to fix circumstances (Emotion layer), we rely on the unchanging anchor (Mind layer) to stand above all adversities.

### 2.4. 03 Personal Application Examples

- **Example 1 (Mastery of Inner Being):** Previously, I was easily swept into others' criticism, leading to long anger and torment. Applying Emptiness of Nature, when facing negative words, I proactively bring my inner being to the state of "knowing I am hearing that, that's all," not letting Nature analyze right/wrong or Emotion react with resentment. As a result, I maintain composure and clarity to handle the situation without emotional hurt.
- **Example 2 (Health and Movement):** In physical exercise (like squats), I once felt tired and wanted to quit after 100 repetitions because my mind kept imagining muscle pain. Applying "rest within movement" and Emptiness of Nature, I focused on true breath and relaxed the physical body, releasing the want to reach a specific number. As a result, I could do thousands of squats in a light, effortless state, as the body recovered within the inner being's micro-rests.
- **Example 3 (Career and Occupation):** I was deeply attached to the label of being a manager, pressuring myself to show authority, which strained team relationships. Understanding "I am nobody" helped me drop the title, actively listening to employees with humbleness and truth. Because I held no negative images, I discovered outstanding potentials in my team that I had previously missed, boosting team performance and creating an an vui working environment.

### 2.5. 03 Family Application Examples

- **Example 1: Resolving spouse conflict by releasing Identity Attachment**
  - Previously, when my husband did something undesirable, I used level 1 awareness to analyze right/wrong and Emotion to oán trách (complain), clinging to the label that as a wife I deserved better treatment. Applying Emptiness of Nature, I focus on true Hearing and Seeing, "knowing it is so" to create a delay for inner balance. As a result, I stop clinging to my husband's mistakes, shifting my inner state to Inner Peace, making the relationship light and helping my husband feel safe to be himself.
- **Example 2: Educating children based on the Nature of Emptiness of all things**
  - I used to label my child as "game-addicted," trying to fix this behavior by imposing rules, which made the child withdraw. Applying Nature of Emptiness, I realize my child is nobody and has the potential to become anybody, and the "game-addicted" image is just a seed in my own mind. I proactively bring my inner being to Emptiness of Nature to erase the old image, then lower to level 2 to actively construct a beautiful image of my child. The child transformed positively without pressure.
- **Example 3: Building family harmony through the energy of Wisdom**
  - In a multi-generational family, different views often lead to anger and suffering because everyone clings to labels like "parents" or "children" to judge each other. Shifting levels of awareness, during conflicts, I proactively bring the inner being to Emptiness of Nature (level 3) to stop karma, then use Honouring - Gratitude (level 1) to interact. Maintaining this energy feeds the family, dissolving oán trách. The family achieves harmony and consensus to build a legacy.

### 2.6. 03 Organizational Application Examples

- **Example 1: Managing personnel with "Being Nobody" thinking**
  - As a leader, I clung to my position, demanding absolute obedience, which caused distance and blocked creativity. Applying Emptiness of Nature, I practice "being nobody" to listen to employees with truth and no judgment. This action reveals my true character, creating an environment where staff confidently take responsibility. Performance soared because people interact in an vui and trust.

I applied the formula to stand above adversities, starting by receiving messages with Truth so the inner being does not fluctuate. Keeping Emptiness of Nature helped me stand above the crisis, drawing key lessons to restructure the company. The organization not only survived but accumulated merits through serving people better.
- **Example 3: Building company culture based on Human Transformation**
  - The organization used to focus only on sales targets and behavioral control, which burned out staff. I shifted to taking human transformation as the organization's life. By cultivating 7 dimensions of holistic wealth and helping staff comprehend the Human Structure, we lit up the non-material lamps. We created a team that shares a common language and consensus, where each person proactively creates value.

### 2.7. 03 Social Impact Examples

- **Example 1: Educating the community towards "Expanding Light"**
  - Instead of condemning evils (removing darkness), the community applies the Principle of Light and Emptiness of Nature to look at humans. When seeing a person who made a mistake, we look through the lens of Nature of Emptiness, not labeling them but containing their transformation. Sharing stories of goodness raises the community's energy frequency, creating a civilized society where people trust and support each other.

  - When human life knowledge is integrated into business, entrepreneurs do not trade only for profit but aim to become Cultural Entrepreneurs. They apply Human Structure to advise people, helping them understand cause-condition-outcome. Doing social responsibility based on 4 drivers of survival accumulates merits for the nation, creating sustainable economic ecosystems.
- **Example 3: Spreading root life philosophy to build world peace**
  - Society transforms when each individual receives their own Truth and lives with Inner Peace, Tolerance, and Honouring - Gratitude. When this philosophy spreads globally, barriers of language and borders are erased by aligned human life views. People work together to build sustainable organizations and family clans, creating a peaceful and prosperous world for generations.
