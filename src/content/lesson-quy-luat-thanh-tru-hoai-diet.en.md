## TOPIC: THE LAW OF FORMATION - DURATION - DECAY - CESSATION

### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

**A. Topic Objectives**

**Value of the topic:** This topic equips learners with a thorough lens on the inevitable cyclical nature of all things in the physical world. It helps people understand that every object, event, and phenomenon (including the physical body, or a business, or a relationship) follows the process: arising, being maintained, declining, and ending.

**Significance for learners:** Cuts off the root of suffering and sorrow that stems from the habit of attachment (clinging) — seeking for all things to exist forever.

**Transformation brought by the topic:** Learners shift from a state of fear and resentment when facing decay and cessation (loss, aging, bankruptcy) to a state of **Peaceful Joy** in receiving it. At the same time, it activates **Cherishing and Gratitude** during the stages when things are "Forming" and "Enduring," to prolong the lifespan of a desired result.

**Output results:** Learners understand the nature of the 4 stages of the law; realize the difference between what is false and temporary (matter) and what is true and real (lying outside the law); and apply the appropriate inner attitude to proactively create new cycles of "Formation."

**B. In-depth Content**

People often carry within themselves a great paradox: we live in the physical world, yet we always seek for all things to last forever. We weep when a glass breaks, grieve when our beauty fades, collapse when a business goes bankrupt, and suffer to the utmost when a loved one leaves this world. All of that suffering stems from the fact that we are going against one of the greatest laws governing the universe and human life: **The Law of Formation - Duration - Decay - Cessation**.

The lecture guides learners to clearly dissect the process of all things: Beginning to form (Formation), maintaining and developing stably (Duration), beginning to weaken and wear down (Decay), and finally ending, vanishing (Cessation). In human life, this law is most clearly expressed through the cycle of **Birth - Aging - Sickness - Death** of the physical body.

The bright insight of this topic does not lie in accepting decline in order to become pessimistic or give up, but in **borrowing an understanding of the law to manage one's Inner Consciousness**. The Master points out profoundly: Anything that has form, that is constructed from positive-negative electromagnetic charge, will certainly be governed by Formation - Duration - Decay - Cessation. However, deep within a person there is something that lies outside this law, and that is precisely **the True Self** (the awareness of Hearing, Seeing, Speaking, Knowing, enveloped by the Light of Electromagnetic energy).

When one thoroughly understands this, a person facing an incident (the Decay - Cessation stage) will use the Emptiness of Nature to receive it, maintaining a Peaceful Joy inner state to protect their own electromagnetic charge and that of the people around them. They understand that "Cessation" is not a permanent end, but the inevitable clearing of the way for a new, better cycle of "Formation." From there, a person reaches a state of leisure and freedom amid the ever-changing stream of life.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

**2.1. Concepts**

**The Law of Formation - Duration - Decay - Cessation:** The inevitable law of nature and the universe, governing the life cycle of every object, event, and phenomenon. All things must go through 4 stages: Formation, Duration (Maintenance/Development), Decay (Decline/Wear), and Cessation (Vanishing/Ending).

**Birth - Aging - Sickness - Death:** The specific application and manifestation of the Law of Formation - Duration - Decay - Cessation on a person's life and physical body.

**2.2. Key Knowledge**

**Characteristics of the law:** No force can resist or prevent this law. Trying to go against the law only brings torment and depletes one's electromagnetic charge in the negative direction.

**Application of management across the 4 stages:**

**Formation:** Requires clarifying the image, clarifying the concept, and cultivating good causes and favorable conditions.

**Duration:** Requires using the energy of Cherishing - Gratitude to prolong the maintenance period, slowing the transition into Decay.

**Decay:** Requires using Tolerance and recognizing Emptiness to repair and restructure.

**Cessation:** Requires using Peaceful Joy to receive it, ending old karma, and preparing the context to initiate a new cycle of Formation.

**The boundary of this law's influence:** This law only affects the physical world, matter, and emotion (the layers of Nature, Emotion, and Body). The layer of Mind (the True Self / Buddha-nature) is eternal, neither arising nor ceasing, and is not governed by Formation - Duration - Decay - Cessation.

**2.3. Key Awareness**

**Establishing Awareness 1 - Breaking the pattern of grief (The paradox of going against the law):** The Master presents a sharp reality: "How can she act like that when her husband just died?" Society usually assumes that when a loved one passes away (Death/Cessation), the person left behind must grieve and weep in sorrow for years for it to be called faithful devotion. But the Master establishes the awareness: that sorrowful grief emits a negative electromagnetic charge, which makes the children anxious and prevents the deceased from being liberated. Conversely, a person who thoroughly understands the law, who anchors in the True Self and keeps their inner being in Peaceful Joy, may be considered "crazy" by society, but in reality they are living **in accordance with nature**, protecting the electromagnetic charge of both the person who has passed and those who remain.

**Establishing Awareness 2 - "Cessation" is the premise for rebirth:** People fear the word "Cessation" because they lack the source concept for it. The secret is, if the leaf does not fall (Cessation), the new bud cannot sprout (Formation). If an outdated business does not go bankrupt, the market cannot welcome a new model. This awareness helps people proactively "tear down and rebuild" outdated ways of thinking and processes, in order to build a new life instead of clinging to decline.

**Establishing Awareness 3 - Cherishing and Gratitude slow down Decay - Cessation:** Everything material (a house, health, love) is on the path toward Decay and Cessation. The secret to keeping the "Duration" stage for as long as possible is precisely the energy source of Cherishing and Gratitude. "Only by cherishing do you truly possess; only by being grateful does it last as long as heaven and earth." Without this energy, all things will accelerate toward decay and cessation.

**2.4. 03 Personal Application Examples**

**Example 1: The Inner Being Aspect (A Crisis of Youth)**

Context: A woman turning 40 begins to notice wrinkles and her beauty fading.

Old awareness: Fear and panic, trying every high-risk medical intervention to hold on to youth. Frustration at seeing herself age (fighting against the law of Aging - Sickness).

Knowledge applied: The Law of Formation - Duration - Decay - Cessation on the physical body.

New action: Receiving the change in her body with Peaceful Joy. Understanding that physical beauty will "Decay," but inner beauty (warmth, wisdom) is at its stage of "Formation" and "Duration." Still caring for her body but without clinging to suffering.

Result: Her demeanor radiates confidence and peace. The rich beauty of wisdom replaces the beauty of youth, and a balanced electromagnetic state helps her body age very slowly.

**Example 2: The Career Aspect (Accepting Failure)**

Context: A passionately pursued startup project of 3 years must finally declare bankruptcy due to market changes.

Old awareness: Extreme suffering, self-torment, drowning in alcohol and blaming circumstances (stuck at the stage of Cessation).

Knowledge applied: The law of cycles; "Cessation" to initiate "Formation."

New action: Using Emptiness of Nature to view the project's collapse. Drawing out the Lesson - Impression - Realisation. Letting go of attachment to the old project, calming the mind to prepare for a new business cycle.

Result: No electromagnetic damage, maintaining health and clarity of mind. Opened wisdom that reveals new opportunities many times larger.

**Example 3: The Health Aspect (Facing Illness)**

Context: Discovering that one has contracted an incurable disease.

Old awareness: Collapse, blaming fate with "why me," despairing while waiting for death.

Knowledge applied: Birth - Aging - Sickness - Death is inevitable.

New action: Using Peaceful Joy to receive the news of the illness (stopping the negative electromagnetic charge). Cherishing and being grateful to the body that has accompanied them for so many years. Following the treatment plan with the attitude of "if the condition remains, there is Formation; if the condition ends, there is Cessation."

Result: The vibrational energy frequency rises, the mind becomes at peace. The immune system recovers strongly, prolonging lifespan and quality of life far beyond the doctors' predictions.

**2.5. 03 Family Application Examples**

**Example 1: The Relationship of Life and Death (Releasing Grief)**

Context: A father suddenly passes away due to old age and frailty.

Old awareness: The whole family wails in misery, stops eating and sleeping for months, sets up a mourning altar and laments every day, casting a gloomy atmosphere over the family.

Knowledge applied: The Law of Formation - Duration - Decay - Cessation; anchoring in the True Self.

New action: The child, thoroughly understanding the law, realizes that the father's physical body has reached the time of "Cessation." Proactively maintaining a balanced, joyful inner state while arranging the funeral. Reminding siblings to raise a feeling of cherishing and gratitude for the years their father was alive.

Result: The family quickly regains peace. The positive electromagnetic charge of the children and grandchildren helps the deceased be gently liberated, and the living are not burdened by spiritual and health consequences.

**Example 2: The Husband-and-Wife Relationship (Renewing Affection)**

Context: A couple married for 10 years, whose affection has fallen into a rut, become dull, and begins to show signs of cracking (shifting into Decay).

Old awareness: Just letting things be, thinking "any couple who has been together long will get bored," and beginning to consider seeking happiness outside the marriage.

Knowledge applied: Using Cherishing and Gratitude to maintain the "Duration" cycle, building a new "Formation" cycle.

New action: The couple sits down together, reviewing each other's good qualities. Proactively studying Inner Being knowledge together, refreshing their daily habits, starting a new cycle of love.

Result: The process of "Decay - Cessation" of the marriage is halted. The affection is "reborn," and the couple understands each other and is even more affectionate than when they first fell in love.

**Example 3: Material Life (The Wear of Assets)**

Context: The family's cherished house is partially destroyed by a fire.

Old awareness: Regret over the loss, crying, the couple blaming each other for carelessness.

Knowledge applied: The Emptiness of all things, and the inevitable material law of Decay - Cessation.

New action: Receiving the incident in Peaceful Joy, comforting each other that "things are lost in place of people." Cherishing the fact that everyone is still safe.

Result: No argument occurs. The couple works together wholeheartedly to earn money to renovate the house into an even more beautiful new one.

**2.6. 03 Organizational Application Examples**

**Example 1: Enterprise Management (Restructuring)**

Context: A technology group whose core product is dominating the market (the Duration stage) begins to show signs of declining revenue.

Old awareness: Conservative, stubborn leadership pumps in money to save the old product at all costs, out of nostalgia for its former glory.

Knowledge applied: Understanding that the "Decay" stage has arrived, one must prepare for "Cessation" in order to "Form" the new.

New action: The leadership accepts the truth, proactively ending (Cessation) the old product line before it drags the company down. Concentrating R&D resources to build a new cycle with a more breakthrough product.

Result: The business does not go bankrupt but achieves a spectacular transformation, continuing to lead the market in a new technological era.

**Example 2: Personnel Development (The Departure of Talent)**

Context: An excellent business director, who has been with the company since its founding, decides to submit a resignation to start their own company.

Old awareness: The leader becomes angry, viewing it as betrayal, trying to defame the person and cut off the relationship.

Knowledge applied: Where there is Formation in a karmic connection, there will also be Cessation. A person is either a bridge or a road.

New action: The leader uses Cherishing and Gratitude for that employee's contributions during the "Formation - Duration" stage. Receiving the departure (Cessation) with Peaceful Joy and organizing a respectful farewell party.

Result: A good relationship is preserved. That employee later becomes a strategic partner (a valuable connection) bringing many large projects to the former company.

**Example 3: Organizational Culture (The Decay of Old Thinking)**

Context: A company with a long-standing, rigid, bureaucratic working culture suffers severely declining productivity.

Old awareness: Personnel are afraid of change, afraid of confrontation, and just work half-heartedly day by day.

Knowledge applied: Proactively "ending" old thinking to "form" a new environment.

New action: The board of directors introduces the fundamental educational philosophy into training. Completely tearing down the old regulations, establishing the culture of the 6 golden words (Simple - Joyful - Trusting - Gentle...).

Result: The organization is "reborn." Personnel are full of energy, cohesive, bringing the company into a new, enduring cycle.

**2.7. 03 Social Application Examples**

**Example 1: Sustainable Development (The Economic Cycle)**

Context: The world enters a period of global economic crisis, with inflation rising sharply.

Old awareness: Society panics, the media spreads pessimism and fear, businesses flee.

Knowledge applied: Crisis (Decay - Cessation) is part of the law of the Economic Cycle.

New action: Communities of entrepreneurs who understand the Inner Being maintain a calm state. They make use of this "hibernating" economic period to de-materialize themselves, conduct internal training, and upgrade technology while waiting for the new cycle.

Result: When the economy recovers (the Formation stage), these organizations rise up strongly and capture the market, because they had already prepared their resources in advance.

**Example 2: The Field of Construction/Architecture**

Context: An ancient architectural monument, hundreds of years old, begins to deteriorate, decay, and risks collapse.

Old awareness: Public opinion demands preserving the original state at all costs, opposing any renovation or demolition out of nostalgia for the past.

Knowledge applied: The Law of Formation - Duration - Decay - Cessation affects every physical object.

New action: Scholars guide public opinion to understand that decay is inevitable. The decision is made to demolish the dangerous structure (Cessation) in order to restore and rebuild it based on the original design.

Result: The structure is safely reborn, continuing to serve future generations while still preserving its cultural soul.

**Example 3: Humanistic Values (Changing Funeral Culture)**

Context: In many rural villages, funeral culture lasts for many days with loud weeping and drumming to express grief.

Old awareness: Everyone gets swept up in negative electromagnetic energy; the louder one cries, the more filial one is considered.

Knowledge applied: The awareness that "the True Self" neither arises nor ceases, and the law of human life.

New action: Family educators quietly enter these communities, spreading knowledge about Peaceful Joy and how to protect the electromagnetic charge for deceased relatives. Transforming funerals into memorial gatherings that record gratitude.

Result: Society gradually becomes more civilized. There is less toxic grief, and those attending funerals maintain purity, providing good conditions for the deceased.
