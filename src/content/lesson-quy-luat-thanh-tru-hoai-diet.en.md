### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION
**1A. Objectives & Target Audience:**
* **Value of the topic:** It provides a clear lens on the cyclical nature of all physical things. It shows that all objects, events, and phenomena (including the physical body, businesses, or relationships) follow a process: arising, staying, changing, and vanishing.
* **Significance for learners:** Cuts off the root of human suffering caused by attachment (dính mắc)—expecting physical things to remain forever.
* **Brought transformation:** Shifts from fear and blame when facing decay (loss, aging, bankruptcy) to **Joy (An vui)**. It activates **Appreciation & Gratitude (Trân trọng biết ơn)** during the "Arising" and "Staying" phases to prolong positive outcomes.
* **Learner's Outcomes:**
  * Learners understand the 4 stages of the law. They realize the difference between temporary physical things and the eternal real self (which is outside the law). They apply this awareness to initiate new "Arising" cycles.

**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**LAW OF ARISING - STAYING - CHANGING - VANISHING**

**INTRODUCTION – THE ATTACHMENT TO PERMANENCE**
Humans live in a physical world but desire permanent things. We weep when a glass breaks, grieve when beauty fades, collapse when a business fails, and suffer when loved ones depart. This suffering stems from resisting a natural law: the **Law of Arising - Staying - Changing - Vanishing (Quy luật Thành - Trụ - Hoại - Diệt)**.

* **The Four Phases:**
  1. **Arising (Thành):** Formation and launch.
  2. **Staying (Trụ):** Growth, stability, and maintenance.
  3. **Changing (Hoại):** Decay, weakening, and deterioration.
  4. **Vanishing (Diệt):** Termination, collapse, and ending.
In human life, this is represented by **Birth - Aging - Sickness - Death (Sinh - Lão - Bệnh - Tử)** of the physical body.

* **Managing Awareness Through the Law:**
  Anything with physical form or negative-positive electromagnetic structure is subject to decay. But inside us lies something beyond this cycle: **The Real Self (Sự chân thật)**—the Hearing, Seeing, Speaking, and Knowing wrapped in Light Electromagnetic Energy (Điện từ quang).
  When facing crisis (Changing-Vanishing), a person anchors in Tánh Không (Emptiness) to maintain Joy (An vui), protecting their energy. "Vanishing" is not a permanent end, but a preparation for a better "Arising" cycle.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)
**2.1. Concepts:**
* **Law of Arising - Staying - Changing - Vanishing:** The natural cycle governing physical life.
* **Birth - Aging - Sickness - Death (Sinh - Lão - Bệnh - Tử):** The application of the law to the human physical body.

**2.2. Key Knowledge:**
* **Nature of the Law:** Unstoppable. Resisting it causes internal stress and drains positive energy.
* **Managing the Phases:**
  * **Arising:** Clear vision, clear concepts, seeding right causes.
  * **Staying:** Use Appreciation & Gratitude to extend this phase and delay decay.
  * **Changing:** Use Tolerance and Tánh Không to repair and restructure.
  * **Vanishing:** Use Joy to accept, clear old karma, and prepare for a new Arising.
* **The Boundary:** The law only affects the physical, emotional, and mental levels (Body, Temper, Sentiment). The Core Self (Truth) is eternal and unaffected.

**2.3. Key Awarenesss:**
* **Insight 1: Shattering grief.** Grief over death emits negative energy, distressing the living and anchoring the departed. A person who understands the law uses Joy to maintain energy balance.
* **Insight 2: "Vanishing" precedes birth.** Leaves must fall for buds to grow. An obsolete business must dissolve to make room for innovation.
* **Insight 3: Appreciation delays decay.** Use Appreciation & Gratitude to keep the "Staying" phase long. Without it, things decay rapidly.

**2.4. Personal Applications:**
* **Example 1 (Age):** Turning 40, a woman noticed wrinkles. Instead of panicking, she accepted her body's changes with Joy. She realized physical beauty decays, but inner beauty (wisdom) rises, maintaining her peace.
* **Example 2 (Career):** A startup failed. Instead of drowning in despair, the founder used Tánh Không to view the failure, extracted lessons, and prepared for a new Arising.
* **Example 3 (Health):** Diagnosed with a chronic illness, a patient accepted it with Joy, avoiding negative energy. He appreciated his body's service, raising his energy frequency.
