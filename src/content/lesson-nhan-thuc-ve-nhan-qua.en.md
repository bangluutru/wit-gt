## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **The Law of Cause and Effect:** One of the laws that governs human life most profoundly, viewed from three perspectives: Science, Religion (Faith), and Moral Principle. Great masters often compare a person's life to a boat drifting on a river, with the laws (including the law of cause and effect) representing the water's current. If we live in accordance with the law of cause and effect, life will be like a boat going downstream, easeful and simply arriving at its destination.
- **The Formula of Cause and Effect:** Good Cause + Good Affinity = Desired Effect.
- **Cause and effect are not retroactive:** Once a cause has produced its effect, that cycle of cause and effect ends and will not repeat unless we continue to sow a new cause through resentment or self-torment.
- **The Three Planes of Cause and Effect:** Consisting of Cause and Effect of the Cosmic View, the Worldview, and the Human-Life View.

### 2.2. Key Knowledge

**The Three Planes of Cause and Effect:**

- **Cause and Effect of the Cosmic View:** Relates to the invisible factors that govern how a person lives and where they go after death. This plane is determined by 3 blocks of Electromagnetic energy (Merit, Virtue, Evil deeds) and is usually clarified by Religions and Faiths. *(Note: This content will be explored in depth and clarified in the topic: The Structure of a Human Being – the 3 Blocks of Virtue.)*
- **Cause and Effect of the Worldview:** Relates to the visible factors that govern the things around us, clarified by science. Managing the cause and effect of the worldview lies in clarifying the mental image, managing thinking and behavior (for example: planting a mango seed to harvest mangoes, or needing a design blueprint and builders in order to build a house).
- **Cause and Effect of the Human-Life View:** Relates to both invisible and visible factors. This is the plane revolving around human life with 4 key aspects: Inner Being, Health, Relationships, and Finance. Nowadays people are often quite vague about these aspects, not knowing what "cause" to start sowing in order to obtain the "desired effect."

**Managing Cause and Effect in Human Life:**

To master the cause and effect of one's life, a person needs to manage things in order of priority:

- **Managing Inner Consciousness (Priority #1)** — building the "Good Cause" within. A person's inner consciousness is formed by 3 factors:
  - *A good mental image (the scientific perspective):* Having a clear image and a clear desired outcome.
  - *The block of merits and virtues (the religious/faith perspective):* Always maintaining a balanced, positive-facing inner state.
  - *A beneficial source concept (the moral perspective):* Knowing how to Believe and Understand correct concepts that align with what one desires in that field.
- **Managing Thinking:** Forming favorable inquiries that guide action.
- **Managing Behavior:** Doing everything with a mindset that is simple, joyful, trusting, gentle, regular, and mindful.

**Clarifying the Formula "Good Cause + Good Affinity = Desired Effect":**

- **Good Cause:** The convergence of a Good mental image, a Beneficial source concept, and the Block of Merits - Virtues. *(Note: This content will be explored in depth and clarified in the topic: Good Causes in Human Life.)*
- **Good Affinity:** Every outcome is brought about by people. Good affinity comes from having correct awareness about people and from circulating a source of energy of Appreciation-gratitude, Tolerance, and Peace and Joy. *(Note: This content will be clarified in the topic: Good Affinity in Human Life.)*
- **Desired Effect:** Revolves around 3 key points: the Dream Career, the Dream Life, and the Realm of Life. *(Note: This content will be clarified in the topic: Desired Effects in Human Life.)*

### 2.3. Key Awareness

**"Bodhisattvas argue about the cause, sentient beings argue about the effect":**

A very important piece of awareness to equip oneself with is the difference in thinking: "Bodhisattvas argue about the cause, sentient beings argue about the effect."

- **Sentient beings (ordinary people):** Usually become attached to and worry about the "Effect" (wanting a big house, wanting successful children, a stable job...) while forgetting to build the root.
- **Bodhisattvas (people of wisdom):** Understand that one only needs to focus on cultivating a "Good Cause" and connecting with "Good Affinity," and the desired effect will naturally arrive without needing to be forced or sought after.

**"The root cause of life originates from myself, but it is not my fault":**

To thoroughly grasp this key point of awareness, we can understand it through two extremely profound halves:

1. **The meaning of "The root cause of life originates from myself":** Every outcome in life that we currently have — whether desired or undesired — is a direct consequence of our own past choices. Typically, these choices were simply made because, at that time, we felt we "liked" or found something "fitting/suitable." The common inner tendency of people is that when they achieve good things, they credit it to their own choice, but when facing undesired things, they blame circumstances or other people (for example: "my parents forced me," "circumstances pushed me into it"). Therefore, courageously acknowledging that the root of life comes from oneself immediately helps the learner cut off resentment toward others and blame toward external circumstances.
2. **The meaning of "But it is not my fault":** Many people, upon just realizing that the root of everything comes from themselves, fall into a state of self-torment and self-blame. However, the key point of awareness here is: at that point in the past, that choice was the best, most fitting, and most correct choice given one's level of awareness at that time. If we had known of a better choice back then, we certainly would have chosen it instead. Therefore, today's outcome is not a "fault" that we must carry a heavy sense of guilt over.
3. **The transformation this awareness brings:** When we thoroughly grasp this saying, the inner being will reach a wondrous state of balance: not resenting others, and not tormenting oneself either. This is an extremely important precondition for clearing out the inner being to become peaceful, helping us regain mastery over our own life and proactively re-plant new, more beautiful seeds of mental image for the future.

**Applying "Cause and effect are not retroactive":**

This is a wonderful characteristic of cause and effect that helps us free our inner being. When something (even something undesired) happens, that is the moment the EFFECT has ripened, the CAUSE has finished executing, and that CAUSE-EFFECT cycle is over. If, when facing adversity, we react with suffering, resenting people or circumstances, we are unknowingly SOWING a new "Seed of Evil Deed" (a bad CAUSE) into the subconscious, causing the suffering to repeat in the future. A better way is: Know and Accept the event → Show Tolerance for the person who brought the adversity → Appreciate and be Grateful for the lesson received. At this point, the negative stream of cause and effect is completely cut off, the darkness will dissolve — Light will be present. CAUSE AND EFFECT will be reestablished in a Good direction. *(Note: This content will be clarified in the topic: The Formula for Transcending Every Problem.)*
