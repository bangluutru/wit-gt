**B. In-Depth Content: The Operating Principle of the Subconscious in Human Life**

For thousands of years, humanity has always searched for the answer to this question: why is it that, born into the same world, one person carries innate intelligence (Karma of Consciousness), another is loved and helped by others wherever they go (Karma of Connection), while yet another enjoys wealth and prosperity from the moment they are born (Karma of Outcome), while countless others are sunk in suffering? If we attribute all of this to "fate" or "destiny," people will sink into resignation and stagnation.

The reference framework of the Structure of the Human Being has revealed a great secret: there is no fate, only **Karmic Destiny**. Everything we have today is the result of **Total Accumulated Karma** — the sum of all our thoughts and actions across countless lifetimes, carefully stored in a space called the Treasury of Consciousness (the Subconscious).

Picture the human subconscious as a fertile plot of land — the **"Garden of the Subconscious."** In that garden, there are countless seeds that have been planted since beginningless time: seeds of Consciousness (understanding), seeds of Connection (human causes and connections), and seeds of Outcome (the results of life). Some of these are still seeds, some have grown into trees, and some are already bearing fruit.

So where do these seeds come from? They are planted through the process of **Internalization**. Every day, whatever we **Hear – See – Speak – Know**, as it passes through the layer of Nature and the layer of Emotion within a person, gets encoded and stored into the Subconscious. If the process of internalization carries a Negative electromagnetic charge, we plant an undesirable seed. If it carries a Positive electromagnetic charge, we plant a desirable seed.

The greatest key point of this principle lies in the role of **"Mr. Intention"** (our attention, our awareness). Mr. Intention is like the owner of the garden. Whenever Mr. Intention visits any seed, he carries with him a state of inner-being electromagnetic vibration.

If Mr. Intention carries a **Negative** state (resentment, suffering, anxiety), the seed will bear an **Undesirable Fruit**.

If Mr. Intention carries a **Positive** state (joy, hope, excitement), the seed will bear a **Desirable Fruit**.

If Mr. Intention carries a **Balanced** state (peaceful joy, the nature of emptiness), the seed will **bear neither a Desirable nor an Undesirable Fruit** (halting cause and effect).

Once a person thoroughly comprehends this principle, a person rich in wisdom no longer resents their life. They understand: *"Before one has awareness, a person's life is decided by their total accumulated karma. Once one has awareness, a person's life is still decided by their total accumulated karma, but we gain the right to choose a good total accumulated karma."*

They begin to master their own garden by controlling what they Hear and See, and changing what they Speak and Know. Even more wonderfully, they hold in their hands the **"Formula for Summoning Total Karma":** bringing their inner being into a state of Peaceful Joy to summon the Karma of Consciousness, using Tolerance to summon the Karma of Connection, and using Cherishing Gratitude to activate the Karma of Outcome. All it takes is to raise an ultimate desire, internalize it to make the image and the concept clear, and align the vibrational frequency, and every treasure of countless lifetimes will automatically flood in to serve the present life, helping a person leisurely master their own destiny!
