### B. In-depth Content: The Circle of Knowledge Principle – The Key to Wise Communication and Infinite Absorption of Knowledge

---

#### 1. INTRODUCTION – THE PARADOX OF UNDERSTANDING AND COMMUNICATION

In life, we have surely experienced these two opposing inner states: There are times when standing before an expert or a highly educated person, we feel extremely small, shrink back, and carry a self-deprecating mentality. Conversely, when we know something cool, we carry an arrogant mentality, wanting to show off our understanding to others.

But paradoxically, when we carry our most impressive understanding to speak to others, the result we receive is often indifference, confused eyes, or worse, they "freeze" completely. Why is that? Why does more knowledge sometimes create barriers in communication?

Today, we will comprehend a precious principle, a master key that can solve this problem: the Circle of Knowledge Principle. Comprehending this, we not only eliminate self-deprecation or arrogance, but we also possess the capacity for "wise communication" and the infinite absorption of human knowledge.

---

#### 2. EXPANSION – THE STRUCTURE AND TRAP OF KNOWLEDGE

##### 2.1. The Structure of the 4 Circles of Human Knowledge
The wise have pointed out a truth that the knowledge of any human being in this world is composed of 4 circles from the inside out:
- **Circle 1 (Know - Know):** What we know that we know. (Example: We know how to drive, how to cook, that 1+1=2).
- **Circle 2 (Know - Forget):** What we know that we have forgotten due to long periods of non-application. (Example: Knowing we once studied integrals and derivatives in high school but have now forgotten).
- **Circle 3 (Know - Do not know):** What we know that we do not know. (Example: We know we do not know how to fly a plane, do not know Arabic).
- **Circle 4 (Do not know - Do not know):** What we do not know that we do not know. This circle is vast, infinite, and encompasses all things.

And a great truth is: My circle of knowledge has 4 circles, and your circle of knowledge also has 4 circles exactly like that.

Let us establish an extremely important awareness: **Human knowledge is equal.** Whether a wise doctorate or a farmer, their knowledge operates through 4 identical circles. The doctorate has their own "know - know", but also has countless "do not know - do not know". The farmer is the same; they have "know - know" about the fields that the doctorate completely "does not know - does not know".

When we recognize this: "I only know what I know", our inner being is immediately liberated. We no longer have any reason to be arrogant about our knowledge, nor any reason to be self-deprecating before the knowledge of others. The knowledge of all things is equal.

##### 2.2. The Trap: "Using one's own knowledge to speak"
In communication, we often make a mistake: we always take our "Knowledge" to speak to others. When we do this, according to the mechanism of the circles of knowledge, 4 reactions will occur:
- If our "Knowledge" touches their **"Know - Know"** circle: They will feel bored and close their brain, because they already know it.
- If our "Knowledge" touches their **"Know - Forget"** circle: They will not care.
- If our "Knowledge" touches their **"Know - Do not know"** circle: They will feel confused.
- If our "Knowledge" touches their **"Do not know - Do not know"** circle: They will freeze, unable to react.

This is why many highly educated, fast-talking people cannot be understood by the person opposite them. Like the story of the Buddhist monk who read many books but then chose to "clear his brain". Because the monk realized: In the temple, there are all classes from farmers to intellectuals; if he brought his own Knowledge to speak, no one would understand.

---

#### 3. IN-DEPTH ANALYSIS – SOLUTIONS FOR COMMUNICATION AND KNOWLEDGE ABSORPTION

##### 3.1. Root Communication Principle
So what is the solution? The wise point out an immutable principle:
**"Take what others know to speak to them so they understand."**

Instead of starting from ourselves, we must start from the listener's "Knowledge". By leading them from what they Know, out to the Know - Forget zone, the Know - Do not know zone, and even the Do not know - Do not know zone, and then skillfully pulling them back to their Knowledge. When that happens, their circle of knowledge is expanded, and suddenly, they will comprehend the issue.

Let us establish the second awareness in education: **There are no students who have lost their roots.** A student who cannot solve a math problem is not because they lost their roots, but because the teacher did not start from the student's current "Knowledge" to lead them. When the teacher knows how to find the student's "Knowledge" point and leads from there, all barriers of transmission will dissolve.

##### 3.2. The Golden Key: Asking with Eagerness to Learn and Humbleness
How do we know what they "Know"? The secret lies in: We must ASK with an attitude of **eagerness to learn and humbleness**.

This is the core attitude. When we lower our ego and ask humbly, the opposite person will open up and share their "Know - Know" with us. When they share, we absorb their knowledge. This action in martial arts is often compared to the "Northern Deep Divine Art" (Bắc Minh Thần Công)—absorbing the strength (knowledge) of the world to make it one's own. Through the attitude of eagerness to learn and humbleness, our "Do not know - Do not know" zone shrinks, and we absorb infinite human knowledge.

##### 3.3. Rhythm in Training and Communication (Rhythm of Education)
Human understanding does not stop at "Knowing". The core of "Wise Communication" is comprehending the 4 inner levels of the listener: Believe – Know – Inquiry – Desire.

To create deep transformation, we must apply the Rhythm Principle:
- **Take what they BELIEVE to speak to them so they KNOW.** (When they have belief, they will easily receive new knowledge).
- **Take what they KNOW to speak to them so they UNDERSTAND.** (From the foundation of what they know, we clarify so they understand deeply).
- **Take their INQUIRY to speak to them so they COMPREHEND.** (Only when they have concerns will our answers help them clarify to the end).
- **Take their DESIRE to speak to them so they TRANSFORM.** (When hitting their exact desires, they will automatically change behavior and life).

A wise communicator is not someone who talks a lot, but someone who, before opening their mouth, comprehends: What does the opposite person Believe? Know? Inquiry? And truly Desire?

---

#### 4. SUMMARY & APPLICATION (ESTABLISHING LEARNING ATTITUDES)

From this principle, we summarize an extremely important mentality in the journey of learning and personal development: **Respecting one's own freeze state (Đơ).**

In the pathway of elevating inner being awareness, there will be completely new knowledge that touches your Do not know - Do not know zone. The feeling at that time is "freezing". Please remember, that is a completely normal physiological and psychological state! When falling into a freeze, do not judge yourself, do not give up quickly. What you need to do is simply: keep listening persistently in a state of Inner Peace. After a period of cultivation, what you do not know will naturally shift into your Know zone.

In summary: The Circle of Knowledge Principle helps us reposition our entire view on learning and communication.
- **For ourselves:** Shift from arrogance/self-deprecation to a state of inner peace, eagerness to learn, and humbleness to absorb infinite knowledge.
- **For others:** Stop imposing our own knowledge. Always remember the mantra: *"Take what others know to speak to them so they understand; base it on their desires to speak so they transform."*

By adhering to this principle, everyone in the world can become our teacher, and we are also qualified to lead the transformation for everyone.

## PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

### 2.1. Concepts

- **The Circle of Knowledge:** A principle showing the limit and structure of human understanding. Everyone's knowledge includes 4 circles: Circle 1 (Know - Know), Circle 2 (Know - Forget), Circle 3 (Know - Do not know), and the vastest Circle 4 (Do not know - Do not know).
- **Wise Communication:** Communication capacity where the speaker comprehends and relies on the listener's Belief, Knowledge, Inquiry, and Desire to transmit messages, helping them achieve Knowing, Understanding, Comprehension, and Transformation.

### 2.2. 5 Key Knowledge Points

- **The Equality of Human Knowledge:** Whether a doctorate or a farmer, their knowledge operates through 4 identical circles. The doctorate has their own "know - know", but also has countless "do not know - do not know". The farmer is the same. Understanding this completely eliminates self-deprecation and arrogance.
- **The Trap of "Using one's own knowledge to speak":** When we speak from our Knowledge, 4 cases occur: touching their "Know - Know" (they get bored); touching "Know - Forget" (they do not care); touching "Know - Do not know" (they feel confused); touching "Do not know - Do not know" (they freeze).
- **The Principle of Wise Communication:** To help others understand, the immutable principle is: *"Take what others know to speak to them so they understand."*
- **The Rhythm Principle in Training and Communication:** Knowledge transfer must have a rhythm compatible with the listener's inner state:
  - Take their Belief to speak so they Know;
  - Take their Knowledge to speak so they Understand;
  - Take their Inquiry to speak so they Comprehend;
  - Take their Desire to speak so they Transform.
- **The Art of Infinite Knowledge Absorption (Northern Deep Divine Art):** By asking questions with eagerness to learn and humbleness to experts in other fields, we pull their knowledge into ours. Thus, our "Do not know - Do not know" zone shrinks, and we absorb infinite knowledge.

### 2.3. 5 Deep Insights

- **Establishing the awareness of "No self-deprecation, no arrogance":** Human knowledge is equal. The story of the young doctorate who mocked the boatman for not knowing astronomy, but ended up drowning because he did not know how to swim, is a profound proof. Comprehending this returns our inner being to a balanced state. We appreciate our knowledge and respect others' knowledge.
- **Establishing the awareness of "Wise Communicator":** The secret to becoming a wise communicator lies not in talking about how much knowledge you have, but in listening to comprehend: What does the opposite person Believe, Know, Inquiry, and Desire. Wise communication is the process of elevating others' awareness from within themselves.
- **Establishing the awareness of "No students who have lost their roots":** In education, any teacher who blames students for "losing their roots" proves they have lost their roots in pedagogical methods. A child who cannot solve a simple problem is because the teacher has not started from the child's current Knowledge.
- **Establishing the awareness of the power of "Eagerness to learn and humbleness":** Humbleness and eagerness to learn are the master keys to open the treasure of others' knowledge. When we lower our ego with true humbleness, all things and all people are ready to become great teachers.
- **Establishing the awareness of "Respecting the learner's freeze state":** When transmitting new knowledge, especially human life view, falling into a freeze state (Do not know - Do not know) is a natural law. Do not panic or complain about their absorption. Guide them patiently and peacefully; their circle of knowledge will shift to Knowing.

### 2.4. 3 Personal Application Examples

- **Overcoming self-deprecation in personal development:** Previously, when meeting successful people with high degrees, I felt small and incompetent. After understanding the Circle of Knowledge Principle, I realized "they only know what they know". My inner state immediately became peaceful and balanced. I actively asked questions with eagerness to learn, absorbing a lot of business knowledge from them without barriers.
- **Absorbing a new professional field:** When starting to learn a new language, instead of panicking before the huge vocabulary (Do not know - Do not know zone), I patiently started from the most fundamental words (my Knowledge). Gradually, the freeze disappeared, and language knowledge shifted into the "Know - Know" circle naturally.
- **Becoming a wise communicator:** In consulting work, instead of talking endlessly to give advice, I start with the question: "What is your true desire?". Based on their Desire, I share solutions. As a result, they immediately apply and transform their lives deeply.

### 2.5. 3 Family Application Examples

- **Accompanying children in learning:** Previously, when teaching my child math, I used an adult's knowledge to impose, causing the child to freeze and cry. Applying the circle of knowledge, I asked: "How do you understand this calculation?". From the child's Knowledge, I led patiently. As a result, the child is no longer afraid of learning, and I eliminated the concept of "losing roots".
- **Spousal communication:** Whenever discussing financial management, my husband and I argued because I imposed my investment knowledge. Changing my approach, I started asking what my husband Believes and Knows about keeping money. Starting from my husband's knowledge, he easily Understood the family's financial plans and happily agreed.
- **Resolving generation gaps:** Generation gaps make it hard for grandparents and grandchildren to talk. Applying this principle, I advised the children to always bring an attitude of eagerness to learn and humbleness to ask grandparents about life experiences. Grandparents happily share their Knowledge, and the family becomes connected with honouring and gratitude.

### 2.6. 3 Organizational Application Examples

- **Internal staff training:** In company training, instead of cramming procedures, the management starts by surveying what staff Believe and Know about the company culture. Using that knowledge as a foundation, the organization designs suitable lectures, helping 100% of staff Understand and Comprehend the enterprise's vision.
- **Leveraging expert capabilities:** When hiring good experts, instead of letting them work in isolation, leaders use eagerness to learn and ask questions. The organization copies and absorbs infinite knowledge of the experts, turning it into the company's shared asset.
- **Management and negotiation:** In partner negotiation, the sales team is trained in "Wise Communication". Sales always find out beforehand what the partner Inquiries about and Desires. Large contracts are signed quickly because partners feel thoroughly understood, achieving transformation in cooperation.

### 2.7. 3 Social Impact Examples

- **Erasing class boundaries of knowledge:** This principle, if spread widely, will eliminate prejudice between manual workers and intellectuals. A society where doctorates know how to bow to learn farming from farmers, and farmers confidently share their knowledge, creates a harmonious, equal, and civilized community.
- **Developing national educational philosophy:** If the educational system applies the rhythm: Believe -> Know, Know -> Understand, Inquiry -> Comprehend, Desire -> Transform, education will no longer cram theories. Students come to school with excitement because all knowledge is designed naturally, accelerating the maturity of the youth.
- **Spreading human life tri-theory globally:** To export inner being philosophy to the world, consultants must not rush to pour knowledge into foreigners (the full cup). With humbleness, using their cultural Knowledge as a foundation, leading step-by-step, human life knowledge will gently permeate, helping thousands of people in the world completely realise human life.

## APPENDIX: The Story of "The Young Doctor and the Boatman"

"There was a young Doctor who one day traveled to a rural riverside area. He wanted to go sightseeing and hired a boatman to row him around. In the process, the young Doctor said to the boatman, 'Boatman, do you know about astrology and fate analysis?' The boatman replied, 'I do not know anything about astrology and fate analysis. Since childhood, I have only lived in the countryside rowing boats. I do not know.' The young Doctor said, 'If you do not know about astrology, you have lost 1/4 of your life. Because when you know it, you will know why a person\'s life happens the way it does.' The boatman heard this and just said, 'Yes, I see.'

Going a bit further, the young Doctor asked again, 'Boatman, do you know about geology?' The boatman said, 'I told you, since childhood I have only rowed boats, I do not know about geology.' The Doctor said, 'You must know about geology to know which area is suitable for which crops. Not knowing geology means you have lost another 1/3 of your life.' The boatman just nodded and said, 'Yes.'

A bit further, the young Doctor asked again, 'Boatman, do you know about literature? Do you know poetry?' The boatman replied, 'I told you, I did not have schooling since childhood, I only rowed boats, I do not know poetry.' The young Doctor said, 'Not knowing poetry means you have lost another 1/2 of your life.'

While the young Doctor was talking endlessly and reciting poems, the boatman asked, 'Young Doctor, let me ask you: do you know how to swim?' The young Doctor replied, 'I do not know how to swim.' The boatman then said, 'If you do not know how to swim, you are about to lose your whole life. We are about to enter a whirlpool.'"
