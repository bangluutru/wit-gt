#### TOPIC: SUITABLE CAPACITY IN THE TRAINING OF THE INNER-BEING CONSULTING COACH

### PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION

**A. Topic Objectives**

**Value of the topic:** This topic establishes a standardized and distinctive capacity framework specifically for the Inner-Being Consulting Coach (IBCC). It helps learners realize that true capacity does not lie merely in raw professional knowledge but is a seamless combination of Concepts, Social Relationships, and Professional Expertise, in order to reach the state of "Maturity Beyond One's Peers."

**Significance for learners:** It removes the barrier of "I'm not good enough" or "I don't have enough knowledge." It helps learners grasp the acceleration method for absorbing the boundless human-life knowledge of the ages by acquiring and activating the total accumulated consciousness.

**Brought transformation:** Learners shift from an ordinary learning mindset to a mindset of "learning to transform reality" and "learning to replicate experts." Instead of accumulating scattered odd skills, learners focus on building the foundation of the 15 Source Concepts of the field, in order to become a true insider.

**Output results:** Learners thoroughly comprehend the capacity cycle; successfully operate the 6 golden words in action; and possess the capacity code "Leveraging Knowledge – Impacting Awareness" to guide listeners to reach ultimate transformation.

### B. IN-DEPTH CONTENT

**SUITABLE CAPACITY OF THE EXPERT: THE JOURNEY OF ULTIMATE MATURITY AND REPLICATING REALITY**

In the roadmap for cultivating the Inner-Being Consulting Coach (IBCC), establishing the awareness of **suitable capacity** plays a decisive role in what makes a master trainer distinct and sets them apart. This knowledge not only helps us dismantle the barrier of "having to be highly knowledgeable" but also provides a standardized framework so that anyone at all can accelerate their maturity and create limitless value for society.

#### I. The 40-40-20 Capacity Structure: Redefining Maturity

The capacity of an IBCC is not measured merely by professional skill, but is the synthesis of three critical aspects in the ratio of **40% Concepts, 40% Social Relationships, and 20% Professional Expertise**. The Teacher establishes that a person is called **rich in capacity** or **more mature than their peers** when they possess all three of these elements far beyond their age, approaching the level of the most successful people in society.

This maturity forms a **capacity cycle**: We use expertise as a tool to expand quality social relationships (moving from fondness to trust and closeness), and from there, through human interaction, we elevate our concepts about life. Once concepts are upgraded, they in turn leverage our professional expertise to new heights, helping us not only succeed at work but also **succeed at being a person**.

#### II. Maturing in Expertise: Thoroughly Comprehending the 15 Source Concepts

To become a true insider, the IBCC needs to thoroughly comprehend the **15 Source Concepts of the field**. This is the knowledge "backbone" that lets a trainer confidently stand on the platform without relying on pre-written lesson notes. This system comprises:

**The Guiding Trio:** Law, Principle, Key.

**The Executing Trio:** Formula, Method, Principle of Conduct.

**The Foundational Trio:** Standard Concept, Correct Mental State, Suitable Capacity.

**The Tool Trio:** Reference Framework, Source Concept, Code.

**The Supporting Trio:** Environment, Tools – Instruments, Rites – Rituals.

The Teacher emphasizes: "When you make a complex matter simple, you are an expert; when you do a simple matter regularly, you are an insider; when you do a regular matter mindfully, you are a victor." Only when you firmly grasp these 15 Source Concepts across all 4 domains — Inner Being, Health, Relationships, and Finance — do you officially have the standing and role to guide people to a thorough comprehension of human life.

#### III. The "Leveraging Knowledge" Capacity: The Code of Transformation

The peak capacity of an Expert is not to leverage knowledge using their persona or reputation, but rather **to use knowledge to leverage knowledge**. Based on the principle of rhythm in training, the Expert leverages knowledge according to the listener's states of awareness:

Rely on a person's **Belief** to promote **Knowing** to them.

Rely on a person's **Knowing** to promote **Understanding** to them.

Rely on a person's **Query** to promote **Thorough Comprehension** to them.

Rely on a person's **Desire** to promote **Transformation** to them.

A master trainer always knows how to **conceptualize, visualize, and put into a state** every message they convey. Instead of forcing learners to understand, we use **cherishing gratitude** and **promoting a picture of benefit** to activate the hidden needs within them, helping them realize things for themselves and transform for life after just a single moment of listening.

#### IV. Anchoring the Self and Mission: A Source of Limitless Energy

All capacity becomes meaningless without a clear destination. Our portrait aims toward the **7 forms of holistic wealth**, mastering our own life, and becoming **9 types of people in one** (Master, Benefactor, Personal Connection, Bringer of Fortune, Talented Person, Wise Guide, Teacher, Expert, Person of Wisdom and Knowledge).

The Teacher establishes an important awareness: **"Being no one in order to become anyone."** When we let go of "attachment to status" (rank, position), only then can we receive and transfer reality in the most complete way, through the method of **Acquisition** and **Gathering a Community**. With the mindset of "for the sake of tens of thousands of learners, teach tens of thousands of points," our capacity for self-study and absorbing knowledge will be activated at an extraordinary level.

#### V. Conclusion: Maturity Is a Journey with a Destination

The suitable capacity of an IBCC is precisely the ability to skillfully operate the **6 golden words in action**: Simple – Joyful – Trusting – Gentle – Regular – Mindful. When we persistently anchor ourselves in the value-creation tool that is human-life knowledge, not only do we ourselves achieve fulfillment, but we also build **family clans that last forever** and **organizations that last forever**.

**Inspirational message:** "Life is not afraid of repetition; it only fears having no future." Light your own lamp of wisdom and become a great Promoter, carrying the Philosophy of Education from the Root out to the world, so that every word of wisdom spoken for the nth time is as full of life as it was the very first time.

### PART 2: DETAILED CONTENT (CORE CONTENT)

#### 2.1. Concepts

**Capacity (Năng lực):** In inner-being training, capacity is understood as maturity beyond one's peers across three aspects: Concepts (40%), Social Relationships (40%), and Professional Expertise (20%).

**Maturity in Expertise:** The state in which an individual thoroughly comprehends the 15 Source Concepts of the field and has a clear-cut destination-oriented mindset about that field.

**The 6 Golden Words in Action:** Simple – Joyful – Trusting – Gentle – Regular – Mindful.

**The Capacity Cycle:** The interwoven link among expertise, social relationships, and concepts, in which expertise is the tool to expand social relationships, which then upgrades concepts, which in turn loops back to reinforce expertise to its peak.

#### 2.2. Key Knowledge

**The 40-40-20 Capacity Structure:** An outstanding IBCC focuses 80% of their effort on cultivating standard concepts and building a network of quality social relationships, because this is the foundation for attracting resources and leveraging professional value to new heights.

**The Set of 15 Source Concepts of the Field:** This is the "backbone" of a trainer's capacity, comprising: Law, Principle, Key, Formula, Method, Principle of Conduct, Standard Concept, Correct Mental State, Suitable Capacity, Reference Framework, Source Concept, Code, Environment, Tools – Instruments, Rites – Rituals.

**The Outreach Capacity Code (3-7-7-3):** Thoroughly comprehending the 3 reference frameworks, 7 forms of holistic wealth, 7 acts of giving, and 3 destinations (dream career, dream life, realm of life) helps a trainer possess language capable of carrying powerful energy.

**The Capacity to "Turn the Complex into the Simple":** A master trainer is someone with the ability to systematize complex problems of human life into simple concepts, diagrams, and codes so that anyone can grasp and follow them.

**Legitimizing One's Standing and Role:** A trainer's capacity is also shown in knowing how to organize past data and give a logical structure to their own maturation journey, in order to build trust and consensus among learners.

#### 2.3. Key Awareness

**Establishing Awareness 1 – Teaching ten thousand learners, ten thousand points:** The Teacher establishes that: *"Teaching for the sake of one learner, you teach 1 point; teaching for the sake of ten thousand learners, you teach ten thousand points."* When you set your mission as training society at large, an Expert's capacity for self-study and absorbing knowledge becomes extraordinary.

**Establishing Awareness 2 – Knowledge speaks for itself:** A trainer does not leverage knowledge using their persona or personal reputation, but rather *"uses knowledge to leverage knowledge,"* based on the listener's states of Belief – Knowing – Query – Desire.

**Establishing Awareness 3 – Being no one in order to become anyone:** The IBCC needs to recognize that they are *"no one"* in order to let go of attachment to status, and only then are they qualified to guide people and activate innate wisdom from the total accumulated consciousness.

**Establishing Awareness 4 – Hidden needs and solutions:** Peak capacity is recognizing that people never lack needs — they only have *"needs that are hidden or needs that are shown."* The Expert's task is to use outreach to trigger hidden needs and provide a complete package of life solutions.

**Establishing Awareness 5 – Ultimate maturity:** Recognizing that the final destination of capacity is not making money, but reaching the *"7 forms of holistic wealth"* and becoming the *"9 types of people in one"* in order to serve human life.

**Example 1 (Maturing in expertise):** "Previously, I studied inner-being knowledge only to solve my own personal problems, but ever since I positioned myself as an IBCC to help tens of thousands of people thoroughly comprehend cause, connection, and outcome, I found my capacity to acquire knowledge many times faster. When standing on the platform, instead of straining to memorize lesson notes, I anchor myself in the 15 Source Concepts, so my words naturally flow according to my learners' needs."

I used that expertise to create value for the community, which in turn attracted masters and benefactors. Thanks to these relationships, my concepts about human life were elevated, which then allowed me to return to advising businesses far more effectively than before."

#### 2.5. Examples of Family/Social Application

**Family:** When a mother becomes an IBCC, she does not merely teach her children knowledge but also uses her outreach capacity to create positive questions for her children, helping them find their own direction in life and understand cause and effect from an early age.

**Organization:** A leader who possesses the capacity to synchronize awareness will help all personnel share the same language and reach consensus. When an organization has a "collective consciousness" oriented toward ultimate maturity, every operational problem will dissolve on its own.

**Society:** Training 10,000 experts who thoroughly comprehend human life will create a network of "furnaces" that warm the community, turning the education of awareness into a kind of "legacy" exported to the whole world, bringing peace and wisdom to humanity.
