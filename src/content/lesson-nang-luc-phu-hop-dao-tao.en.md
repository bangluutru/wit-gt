* **Significance for learners:** Dismantles the barrier of "I am not good enough" or "I do not know enough." It guides learners to accelerate their absorption of human life wisdom through Acquisition (Thụ đắc) and activating their total karma of knowledge.
* **Brought transformation:** Shifts learners from standard learning to "learning to transfer realizations" and "learning to replicate experts." Instead of hoarding superficial skills, they build the 15 Source Concepts of the field to become true insiders.
* **Learner's Outcomes:**
  * *Understand:* Master the capacity cycle.
  * *Realize:* Run the 6 Golden Words in action successfully.
  * *Apply:* Apply the capacity of "leveraging knowledge to shift awareness" to guide listeners to ultimate transformation.

**1B. In-Depth Content:**
**SUITABLE CAPACITY OF THE EXPERT – ULTIMATE MATURITY AND REPLICATING REALIZATIONS**

**INTRODUCTION – DEFINING CAPACITY FOR INNER COACHES**
In the training of the Inner Coach, suitable capacity determines the distinction of a master trainer. This wisdom clears the barrier of "having to be a genius" and provides a standardized framework to accelerate maturity and create value.

**I. THE 40-40-20 CAPACITY STRUCTURE: REDEFINING MATURITY**
An Inner Coach's capacity is a synthesis of three areas in a **40% Concepts, 40% Social Relationships, and 20% Expertise** ratio. A person is considered **rich in capacity** or **mature beyond average** when they possess these three elements far beyond their biological age, approaching the level of the most successful.
This forms the **Capacity Cycle**: We use expertise as a tool to expand quality social relationships (from liking to trust and intimacy), then raise our life concepts through human interaction. As concepts are upgraded, they leverage professional expertise to the peak, helping us not only succeed in work but also **succeed in being a human**.

**II. MATURITY IN EXPERTISE: COMPREHENDING 15 SOURCE CONCEPTS**
To be a true insider, the Coach must comprehend the **15 Source Concepts of the Field**, which keep thoughts aligned without written notes:
* **The Steering Trio:** Law, Principle, Key.
* **The Executive Trio:** Formula, Method, Principle.
* **The Foundational Trio:** Standard Concept, Right Mental State, Suitable Capacity.
* **The Tool Trio:** Reference Framework, Source Concept, Code.
* **The Supporting Trio:** Environment, Tool-Means, Rite-Ceremony.
*We establish:* "Do complex things simply, you are an expert; do simple things frequently, you are an insider; do frequent things mindfully, you are a winner." Only when mastering these 15 concepts in all 4 areas (Inner self, Health, Relation, Finance) do you possess the qualification to guide humans.

**III. THE "KNOWLEDGE LEVERAGE" CAPACITY: DECODING TRANSFORMATION**
The ultimate capacity of an Inner Coach is using **knowledge to leverage knowledge**, based on the training rhythm:
* Rely on the listener's **Belief** to appretell for **Knowing**.
* Rely on the listener's **Knowing** to appretell for **Understanding**.
* Rely on the listener's **Queries** to appretell for **Comprehending**.
* Rely on the listener's **Desires** to appretell for **Transformation**.
A master trainer **conceptualizes, visualizes, and aligns states** for every message. Instead of forcing students to understand, we use **appreciation & gratitude** and **appretell benefits** to activate hidden needs, helping them realize and transform.

**IV. SELF-ANCHORING AND MISSION: UNLIMITED ENERGY**
All capacity is empty without a clear destination. Our portrait aims for the **7 dimensions of holistic wealth**, mastering life, and becoming **9 types of people in 1** (Master, Benefactor, Connection, Fortune Bringer, Talent, Mentor, Teacher, Expert, Wise Companion).
We establish: **"Being nobody to become anybody."** By releasing role attachment (sĩ thân), we receive and transfer realizations completely via **Acquisition (Thụ đắc)** and **Attracting a Community (Tụ chúng)**. With the mindset "study ten thousand points for ten thousand students," our capacity to learn is activated.

**V. CONCLUSION – THE 6 GOLDEN WORDS**
Suitable capacity is operated through the **6 Golden Words in action**: Simplicity - Joy - Trust - Ease - Frequency - Mindfulness. Anchoring in human life wisdom creates **eternal families** and **eternal organizations**.
