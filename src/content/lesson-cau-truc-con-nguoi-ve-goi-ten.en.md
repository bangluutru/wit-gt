(Note: Details about characteristics and amplitude of the 16 natures will be explored in the topic: Human Structure - 16 Natures).

- **Layer 4 (Outermost):** This is our physical shell. Write **Physical Body (Thân tứ đại)**, consisting of 4 elements: Earth (Nutrition), Water, Air (Breath), Fire (Warmth). Finally, wrapping the Physical Body is another outer **Polar Electromagnetic Field** protecting the entire body.

#### 3. ANALYSIS: SYNCHRONIZING COGNITION AND LABELLING BY 3 REFERENCE SYSTEMS
People in society often argue because they use words from different perspectives (Scientific, Folk, Religious). Comprehending how to label the 4 layers of Human Structure according to the 3 reference systems helps us remain flexible in communication, align our language with everyone, and understand human life thoroughly.

##### A. Labelling according to the Buddhist perspective:
Buddhism uses language oriented toward spiritual realisation. From the inside out, the 4 layers are labeled:
- **Buddha Nature / True Nature / True Mind** (Corresponding to the Intention to Hear - See - Speak - Know wrapped in Radiant Energy).
- **16 Human Natures** (Reception, Imagination, Expression, Cognition...).
- **84,000 bubbles of illusion**.
- **Physical Body (Earth, Water, Air, Fire)**.

##### B. Labelling according to the Scientific perspective:
Science uses materialistic, logical, and concrete language:
- **Truth**.
- **Character / Personality** (Science acknowledges personality is complex and constantly changes).
- **Emotion** (Scientists have measured and labeled about 34,000 different types of emotions).
- **Human Body**.

##### C. Labelling according to the Folk perspective:
This is the most simple, common, and close terminology in daily life, and is also the primary reference system we use to coach the inner being:
- **Mind (Tâm)**
- **Nature (Tánh)**
- **Emotion/Reaction (Tình)**
- **Body (Thân)**

👉 **Critical cognitive establishment on "Inner Being":**
Society mentions the word "inner being" (nội tâm) often, but very few define it accurately. Based on the folk terms on the diagram, we officially establish: **Human Inner Being includes MIND, NATURE, and EMOTION** (excluding the external Physical Body).

The journey of drawing and accurately labeling the layers in Human Structure is not just for gaining knowledge; it is a vital preparation to enter the practice of "Mastery of Inner Being."

Comprehending this diagram, humans will no longer panic before fluctuating emotions, nor be deceived by titles or superficial personalities.
